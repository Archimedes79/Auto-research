## 1. Design and prediction
**What changes (one factor, with its supervision):** the connector end is no longer the keypoint but an ATTACHED end. For every keypoint `Attach` (`training/grid/jends.py`) takes the nodes of B's frozen node head on the same batch (`qnodes.NodeHead`: the 2 m grid, existence, 8 type flags, long-link and section scores, position, direction, token; loaded from the base run `runs/e273_p_s<S>`, exactly as the E308 option does) that lie within 4 m and 45 degrees of the keypoint and exist (probability above 0.05; no change of this gate), the nearest 8 of them. A small MLP scores each candidate from (distance, along / across offset and heading agreement of the node, its existence / flag / long-link / section probabilities, its token, the kind of the keypoint, the keypoint's token); a learned 'no attachment' (from the keypoint token) competes in the softmax. The end = w0 x keypoint + sum w_m x node_m, the heading the normalised weighted mean: a convex combination of the keypoint and the candidate nodes, so it lies on a node only when one weight dominates, and it falls back to the keypoint when nothing is in reach. **It is not guaranteed to be a delivered piece end:** the candidates are raw nodes with existence above 0.05 including mid-section nodes of a lane, the weighted mean lies inside their hull, the dustbin falls back to the keypoint, and the wiring of the read-out decides which pieces the connector is joined to. Differentiable in the scores, the keypoint position and the heading; the pair logits, the keypoints and the handle scales are untouched by it.
**Supervision (written before the run):** (1) the geometry loss of E317 on the FINAL curve: `--geom-w 0.5` is part of this recipe (the attached curve must be supervised as a shape; E317's own run is held by D85, the CODE is reused): the Bezier curve with the attached ends, headings and the learned handle scales against the GT connector polyline of the pair, symmetric point-to-polyline distance, smooth L1 1 m, plus the position of every keypoint (here: its attached end) against the nearest GT end within 4 m / 30 degrees; (2) a cross entropy over the candidates + the dustbin for every keypoint that belongs to a GT end: target = the candidate node nearest to that GT end within 2.5 m, else 'no attachment'. The cross entropy is part of the geometry term and shares its weight 0.5.
**Read-out and the named hard steps:** `jends_eval.py dump` writes `att_pos`, `att_dir`, `att_p` next to the keypoints; the scorer takes the connector from the attached ends (`qends_eval.make_pairs`, the same Bezier sampling as today with the learned handle scales). Everything the loss sees is soft. **Named hard steps that remain:** the candidate set (the nearest 8 gated nodes: an index choice, the gradient flows through the values), the keypoint peak choice, the read-out threshold p >= 0.1 (as in E312), and the WIRING of the pieces at the read-out: in the PRIMARY read-out the one-piece argmin (the nearest delivered piece end within 4 m / 45 degrees per end, a rule of `add_connectors`), in the secondary the proximity rule, in the tight read-outs the proximity rule with a small radius. That the snap's geometry is gone is true for the end geometry, not for the wiring rule; E322 is the follow-up for the wiring.
**Differences to the snap that the result must be read with:** the candidates are the NODES of B's head (a node that the close-node step of the E275 spec merges away may still be chosen: the piece end is then at most 1.5 m away); the snap moves the end to the delivered LINE end after the hard close step; the training target is the node nearest to the GT end (not the delivered piece end, which does not exist inside the training loop).
Two seeds, VAL 873 frames, TEST untouched. **Prediction (a number):** topo F1 of the PRIMARY read-out of arm a is 0.006 below E312 snapped (+0.0506 / +0.0555 over the plain graph, i.e. 0.0059 / 0.0044 above u'): at the KEEP bound. On the train frames `att_acc` ends above `att_acc_nearest`; the VAL claim is the `jdiag.py kperr` line (the attached end error below the keypoint's). If a is not above u' the attachment did not learn what the snap's geometry does (the candidate target or the node source is wrong: then the delivered line ends, not B's nodes, must be the candidates, which needs the hard graph inside the forward); if a is above u' but below the bound the next card refines the candidates and the loss.
