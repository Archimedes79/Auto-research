**What it does** [checked]: Camera: EfficientViT-L2 at stride 8; heads give per pixel a depth logit vector over B uniform bins, an offset (3 values), size / orientation from the standard deviations of the depth distribution (scale k = 0.5), opacity and a 128-d feature; mean p = back-project(u, d_hat) + offset. The paper does not use a pixel footprint or cone. Radar: 7 accumulated sweeps through a lightweight Point Transformer v3, per point an offset head, a 6-value covariance (softplus-constrained eigenvalues) and opacity. Both sets of Gaussians are alpha-blended orthographically, F = sum_i f_i alpha_i prod_{j<i}(1 - alpha_j), into a 200 x 200 grid at 0.5 m (+-50 m); about 14 k Gaussians for vehicles, 24 k for map. Not specified on the page: any range / azimuth model of the radar uncertainty, time offset dt, explicit Doppler use, sensor pose beyond the calibration transform.

**Results that matter** [checked]: nuScenes BEV IoU vehicle 57.3, road 82.9, lane divider 50.1; RTX 4090, batch 1, FP32, 448 x 800: 75.6 ms (13.2 FPS) vehicle, 81.1 ms map; BEVCar 245.6 ms, SimpleBEV++ 211.3 ms (the "3.2x faster"). Ablation (vehicle IoU): GaussianLSS baseline 46.1; PTv3 radar scatter with xyz only 55.0, all radar variables 56.1; PTv3 + Gaussians (all variables) 56.9; radar sweeps 1 / 4 / 7 give 54.9 / 56.2 / 57.3. The 4x A100 80 GB training for 40 epochs is heavy.

**Transferable technique**: the unified splatting interface (every sensor produces mean, covariance, opacity, feature; one rasteriser) is the frame into which both of our blocks would fit: the cone footprint is a mean/covariance computed in closed form (see 2103.13415 note) instead of predicted from depth variance, and the radar density is a covariance computed from range / azimuth noise instead of a freely predicted 6-vector. Radar-as-Gaussians gains +0.8 IoU over the radar scatter (56.9 vs 56.1), so the covariance modelling is a small effect compared with the radar branch itself (vehicle IoU 48.0 for the camera Gaussians alone, 55.0 to 56.9 once the PTv3 radar branch is added; the table rows are not strictly one-factor ablations).

**Cost and fit**: 4090 numbers are not comparable to the 3060; PTv3 radar encoder is heavy for our budget; cheap pieces are the Gaussian rasteriser and the head layout.

**Doubts**: the radar covariance is free-form, no physical prior; no per-sweep dt; abstract and HTML give no ablation of the camera Gaussian size scale k; the camera-only part is GaussianLSS (already collected).
