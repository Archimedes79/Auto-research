# 2503.17097 R2LDM: radar points to a latent voxel grid, a mask + offset decoder back to points, diffusion in between

| verdict | watch (stage 1, the voxel autoencoder, is the useful part; the diffusion stage is heavy and aims at densification) |

**What it does**: 4D radar super-resolution against lidar. Stage 1: a voxel feature encoder (VFE) maps radar points to a latent grid F (h x w x d); the decoder (LPCR) predicts, per voxel, an occupancy MASK and an OFFSET, P_rec = (V_center + offset) * mask. The page states that it predicts only the AVERAGE position of the input points inside each non-empty voxel, so the reconstruction is voxel-level by design. Loss of stage 1: weighted mask BCE + offset L1 (weights 0.9 and 0.1). Stage 2: a latent voxel diffusion model (L2 loss) generates the dense lidar-like latent from the radar latent. Voxel size 0.125 m (GPAL) and 6.25 cm (VoD).

**Results that matter** (checked, Table I, MMD in 1e-3, comparison with R2DM): VoD CD 0.64 vs 7.36, HD 16.16 vs 23.63, F-score 0.43 vs 0.08; GPAL CD 0.79 vs 29.05, F-score 0.48 vs 0.10. Downstream (absolute points): registration recall 25.6 -> 57.3 percent (one scan); detection +24.88 AP points for cyclists in the driving corridor, +5.45 and +10.97 mAP in the two regions. 40.1 M parameters, 128 sampling steps, training 9 h + 21 h on an RTX 2080Ti; inference time NOT reported. The page does not say whether the VFE is lossless and does not report the stage-1 reconstruction error alone.

**Transferable technique**: the decoder form "occupancy logit + offset per cell, positions = cell centre + offset, masked by occupancy" with BCE (0.9) + L1 (0.1) is the smallest decodable radar-BEV head and equals the CenterNet / FFT-RadNet cell + offset code; it is also our current raster's information (mean offset) made explicit. The R2LDM stage 1 is the closest radar-specific "encode to a grid, decode to points" paper; using only stage 1 (no diffusion) as an auxiliary loss on our radar BEV is cheap.

**Cost and fit**: stage 1 is a VFE and a small decoder; stage 2 (40 M parameters, 128 steps) does not fit a few-ms budget and is not needed for our purpose.

**Doubts**: the mean-position decoder loses all but one point per voxel by construction (our collision problem again); the scale of the CD / HD columns is unclear (the CD claimed 0.19 in the 2D table ties the earlier method); no code; the gains are against lidar ground truth (densification), not a self-reconstruction recall.
