**What it does** [checked]: BEV segmentation from one 360-degree panoramic camera (Dur360BEV dataset, also tested on KITTI-360). Teacher: LiDAR (range / intensity / ambient image) + camera, soft-gated fusion, backbones ResNet-101 / EfficientNet-B0. Student: camera only, EfficientNet-B0 ("EN-b0"). Loss: student multi-task loss (focal for segmentation, balanced MSE for centerness, L1 for offset) plus channel-wise KL divergence between teacher and student dense features (each channel softmax-normalised over space with temperature T, scaled by T^2 / C) at the decoder output; a training-only Auxiliary Module (AM) fuses student camera features with teacher LiDAR features and is removed at inference. Affinity Distillation (AD) is compared with KL and reported as more stable (figure only).

**Results that matter** [checked, Dur360BEV, IoU at 100 / 50 / 20 m; FPS on an RTX 3080]: teacher 58.3 / 64.3 / 68.0 at 14.0 FPS; student without distillation (EN-b0) 32.2 / 41.8 / 58.4 at 31.2 FPS; KL at stage 3 without AM 39.4 / 49.3 / 64.2; full KD360 (stage 3 KL with AM) 41.2 / 51.7 / 67.1, still 31.2 FPS. Comparators in the same table: Simple-BEV (ResNet-101) 31.1 IoU100 at 25.7 FPS, PointBEV (EN-b4) 31.5 at 15.0 FPS. Stage ablation (Table 3), IoU100: distilling at stage 1 (after feature fusion) 20.96 without AM and 30.50 with AM; stage 2 (U-Net encoder output) 20.58 and 32.22; stage 3 (decoder output) 39.4 and 41.2. Distilling early layers without AM is worse than no distillation (20.6-21.0 vs 32.2).

**Transferable technique**: (1) a small EfficientNet-B0 student distilled from a much stronger teacher gains +7 to +9 IoU100 at identical student cost, so distillation is a real lever for a small encoder; (2) where the loss sits matters more than the loss form: supervision at the output side (decoder features) helped, matching early features hurt unless an auxiliary fusion module was added. For our plan (stride-8 student image encoder distilled from DualBEV) this is a warning to test an output-side or BEV-side distillation target (match the BEV feature or BEV logits) against matching the image-side features directly; (3) channel-wise KL over space is a one-line loss.

**Cost and fit**: student runs 31.2 FPS on a 3080 at the paper's (unread) input size, i.e. about 2.2x the teacher. The paper's numbers are on a 360-degree panoramic dataset with low absolute IoU; nothing about nuScenes front-camera lanes.

**Doubts**: different dataset and sensor; teacher contains LiDAR (privileged information), ours would be a camera teacher with a different geometry; stage names are the authors' U-Net stages, not backbone strides; input size not read; code not inspected.
