## What our design does differently
- Time is explicit. None of the three feeds a timestamp or an age to the fusion: CRN uses a fixed set of key frames (pose in geometry), GaussianCaR has no history, RaCFormer relies on the loader and a velocity per query. Ours pulls every source to t_cam with the pose and keeps an age per cell.
- A stored, warped state instead of re-running the network. CRN re-computes 3 past frames with `no_grad` each time (about 4x the backbone work, 2 to 3 s of history at 2 Hz); RaCFormer recomputes 8 frames. A recurrent state with an exact ego warp (FIERY style) costs one `grid_sample` per frame. This is the largest speed argument for design B and A.
- Object motion is a field, not absent or per query. No code base here has a cell-level velocity pull; CRT-Fusion (other scout) is the nearest.
- Confidence is an input, not only learned inside attention. CRN lets a softmax over modalities x points decide; GaussianCaR learns sigmoid gates from the features; neither sees range, age or Doppler observability as an explicit input. Our gate sees them (testable: gate map over range, plan section 4).
- Radar enters differently. CRN builds the radar BEV through the camera ray (occupancy in the perspective plane); ours is a plan-view learned density with sensor pose and dt (plan section 3), so the radar BEV is rig independent and a CRN-style fuser would meet a different radar map.
- Weaker spots of ours: untested; the pull needs a good v(x) estimate that no baseline needed; fusing three maps with ages raises the training burden (modality dropout, radar-only aux heads in plan section 4).

## What to copy as the comparison baselines (in this order)
1. Null fusion: channel concat + 3x3 conv stack (CRN `ConvFuser` / BEVFusion style); trivial, and the control every fusion claim needs.
2. Strong simple baseline: GaussianCaR's `FeatureRectifyModule` (about 30 lines, pure torch; channel gate + per-cell gate, cross-leak between the maps) as the nearest published cousin of our per-cell gate. Add our confidence channels to it as the ablation "does explicit confidence help over learned gates".
3. Strong reference: CRN `MFAFuser` as plain-torch deformable cross-attention (MIT). Use it for the claim "misalignment between radar and camera BEV is handled", and as a cost reference (6.8 ms / 1.2 M parameters at 128 x 128 x 6 layers; our grid has 12480 cells, similar order).
4. Temporal control A: FIERY ego warp (other scout) in front of RaCFormer's ConvGRU cell (MIT, about 80 lines), because RaCFormer's own GRU has no visible warp. Stateless reference: CRN's multi-frame scheme (geometry-aligned frames, concat, one 3x3 conv) as the "no recurrent state" arm if a cheap multi-frame control is wanted.
5. Do not copy: GaussianCaR's CUDA rasteriser (non-commercial licence) and RaCFormer's query decoder (detection specific). Take only their idea (opacity / covariance as footprint, radar depth + RCS columns in the lift), both already in plan sections 2.4 and 3.
