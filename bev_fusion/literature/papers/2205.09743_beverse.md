**What it does**: Shared image encoder and view transform give a BEV feature per timestamp; a spatio-temporal encoder fuses ego-motion-aligned frames. Task decoders (3D detection, semantic map, motion prediction) hang on the shared BEV, and a grid sampler resamples the BEV to a different range and cell size for each task. Future prediction uses an iterative flow module.

**Results that matter** (README model zoo, nuScenes val): BEVerse-Tiny mAP 32.1, NDS 46.6, map IoU 48.7, motion IoU 38.7; BEVerse-Small mAP 35.2, NDS 49.5, map IoU 51.7. No FPS on the pages read. The abstract claims joint training beats single-task models.

**Transferable technique**: per-task grid sampling of one shared BEV (detection on a coarse grid, lane/map head on a finer one) is the cheap answer to "0.5 m cells for thin lane markings, coarser for boxes". Same contract as `grid_sample` in SN20.

**Cost and fit**: Swin-T class backbone and temporal stack are heavier than a 3060 budget for a small system; take only the head layout.

**Doubts**: gains over single-task models are not quantified on the pages read; no loss-weighting recipe seen; map classes are coarse (the divider class is thin and low IoU in this family).

**Addendum 2026-10-26 (MTL scout, checked on https://arxiv.org/html/2205.09743v1, read through a page summariser; this closes the two doubts above)**: the loss weights are FIXED and hand-set: "we set the weights for detection, map, and motion as [1.0, 10.0, 1.0]"; no uncertainty weighting, no schedule seen. Table 4 (BEVerse-Tiny, single-task models against the joint model; the split is not stated in the text read): detection NDS / mAP 0.474 / 0.327 (BEVerse-Det) against 0.466 / 0.321 (joint); map mIoU 53.2 (divider 56.1, pedestrian crossing 44.9, boundary 58.7) against 48.7 (divider 53.2, crossing 39.0, boundary 53.9); motion IoU / VPQ 37.5 / 32.0 against 38.7 / 33.3; parameters 55.8 M / 54.9 M / 62.4 M separate against 102.5 M joint, FPS 12.6 / 14.2 / 5.6 against 4.4. The paper's own sentence: BEVerse underperforms the single-task models for 3D detection and semantic map construction. So joint training costs the thin map classes most (divider -2.9 IoU, boundary -4.8) and helps only motion: the same pattern as MATS (2607.24224: naive multi-task -3.4 mIoU on the map head) and FULLER (2307.16617).
