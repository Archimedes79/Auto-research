### What was built and trained

Order D51: the camera temporal fusion with ONE buffered frame instead of a window of three lifted images. The network `t20.base_s0` (the reference baseline of the t20 chapter) lifts the current frame and two older ones into BEV on every call (28.6 ms of its 102.1 ms) and fuses them; a stream could lift one new image per step and keep the rest in a buffer. Three versions were built, each warm-started from `t20.base_s0` and trained 1500 steps (learning rate 1e-4, same data, same seed), next to an equal-budget control that continues the three-frame window for the same 1500 steps (`t21.ctl_s0`). This chapter shows version R, the recurrent one: its buffer is the previous fused state, moved to the present by the same splat and fused with the new frame, so no older image is lifted again. In training the state is carried from keyframe to keyframe (a chain of up to nine recurrent steps, gradients through the chain); the evaluation of a validation frame starts the state cold (the last three frames), and a deployed stream carries it without limit. The velocity block still reads two raw frames, so the velocity output does not depend on the carried state. Version P (the pair) and its variant P4 follow in the next two chapters. One training seed per arm: every reading is "by hand: D51, one seed". The paired intervals come from resampling the scenes of that one seed; they say nothing about seed-to-seed spread.

### What the numbers say

- **By the point estimates no guard of the card is beyond its band.** Against the equal-budget control the velocity error at moving box centres is 1.136 against 1.084 m/s (+0.053, interval +0.036 to +0.070: separated from 0 by the scene resampling, inside the band of 0.10), the mAP in the fusion area 0.410 against 0.412 (-0.002, band 0.020) and the lane divider IoU@1 0.547 against 0.552 (-0.005, band 0.010; the interval -0.011 to +0.001 reaches just past the band). The card prediction for the velocity error was 1.35 m/s (a loss of 0.25); the measured loss is 0.053. By the written rule (point estimates) this is a tie, not a loss.
- **Small separated differences on other outputs**, found among about forty compared metrics of one seed, so read them as hints, not as findings: road divider IoU@1 -0.013, vehicle and pedestrian AP@1 over the whole area about -0.011 (in the fusion area the vehicle AP@1 is -0.008 and not separated), the mAP over the whole area -0.006, drivable area -0.004. The card has no guard on them; they are one reason the report prefers P over R on this seed (next chapter).
- **The saving is in the stream, not in the batch path.** The batch path of R unrolls three frames and costs 110.2 ms at batch 1 on an idle GPU against 102.5 ms for the window (+7.7 ms; the card set no budget for R and expected +26 ms). A deployed stream with one new image per step costs 57.7 ms at dt 0 and 78.9 ms for the three time offsets, against 85.5 and 104.7 ms for the window (synthetic timing of the same modules, random weights; all ms exclude the two frozen image encoders). The window in this comparison lifts all three frames on every call; a window that cached its lifted frames would also lift one image per step (not built).
- **The stream equals the batch unroll** at the nine steps compared up to 24 frames (identical output, same code path; one scene, scene-0003, on the CPU). Against a cold start from the last three frames the carried state differs more and more (mean difference of the semantic output 0.2 at four steps, 0.6 at 48): the carried state changes the output, the quality of the change is what the warm evaluation scores.
- **Warm whole scenes** (12 scenes, 456 frames, the state carried along the scene): velocity 1.079 against 1.019 m/s (+0.060, interval +0.025 to +0.099), mAP in the fusion area 0.450 against 0.442 (+0.008, interval contains 0); the F1 of the lane-line zero crossings is lower (-0.045, interval -0.082 to +0.004). The memory command (400 validation frames with two keyframes of history, no intervals) shows warm against cold gains outside the camera view of +0.061 (drivable area), +0.050 (walkway) and +0.067 (lane mask) at dt 0; the window control shows the same kind of gain (+0.038, +0.042, +0.056) because the world memory is shared by all arms, so this is not an effect of the new buffer.
- **Probable cause of the velocity loss.** The velocity block was trained on a 0.333 s time baseline and gets no time input; with a one-frame buffer at the same stride the baseline is 0.167 s, so the displacement it sees halves and its learned gain is miscalibrated. The result is consistent with this and P4 (two chapters on) supports it, but it is not isolated: no window arm with the velocity block on the previous frame was run (card confound 2). Further confounds: R trains at batch 2 with accumulation 3 (the chain of recurrent calls needs the memory), the other arms at batch 3 with 2; R warm-starts the temporal block on fused features it never saw.
- **Verdict (by hand: D51, one seed).** A tie with the window by the point estimates of the written guards, with a separated velocity loss; no fix attempt was triggered by the card's rule.
