**What it does** [checked]: two flows per pair, forward f^f and backward f^b. A pixel is flagged occluded in the forward direction when f^f(x) + f^b(x + f^f(x)) is large: |.|^2 > alpha1 (|f^f|^2 + |f^b(x + f^f)|^2) + alpha2 with alpha1 = 0.01, alpha2 = 0.5 (backward flag the same with f and b swapped). The data (census) term is multiplied by (1 - o); every occluded pixel pays a constant lambda_p, so the net cannot declare everything occluded; the second-order smoothness term is not masked; the forward-backward consistency term E_C is masked by (1 - o). E = E_D + lambda_S E_S + lambda_C E_C, per-scale weights 1.1 / 3.4 / 3.9 / 4.35 / 12.7 from coarse to fine.

**Results that matter** [checked, average end-point error on the KITTI training sets, 2012 / 2015, all pixels]: census, second-order smoothness, no occlusion masking 4.40 / 9.49; with occlusion masking, lambda_C = 0: 4.32 / 9.17; with lambda_C = 0.2 (best): 3.78 / 8.80. So forward-backward consistency on top of masking is worth 12 % (2012) and 4 % (2015) of the error; the table has no separate unidirectional row. Optimiser: Adam, lr 1e-4 (SYNTHIA, batch 4, 300 k iterations) and 1e-5 for 500 k iterations on KITTI, halved every 100 k.

**Transferable technique**: for our two directions dt and -dt (or the pair frame t -> t+1 and t+1 -> t): the velocity (or position) field predicted in one direction, moved by the splat, should be the negative of the field predicted in the other; cells where the sum is large are "unexplained" (occluded, new, wrong) and are excluded from the data term and from the consistency term, but pay a constant (so the mask cannot grow without bound). Our splat mass is a forward-only occlusion signal (0 = nothing landed); the backward check adds the "landed but disagrees" case (a collision, a wrong velocity). This is my adaptation (not in the paper): the check is applied to the velocity channels of the MotionHead and to the splat mass, not to photometric terms.

**Cost and fit**: a second splat per pair (the reverse direction), which for a decode at dt and -dt is already computed; no inference cost.

**Doubts**: ablation on KITTI training sets (not a held-out measure); the weights lambda_S, lambda_p, lambda_C are unreadable in the summary (best row looks like 3.0 / 12.4 / 0.2); image flow with a census photometric loss is not our setting, our supervision comes from GT boxes and the map.
