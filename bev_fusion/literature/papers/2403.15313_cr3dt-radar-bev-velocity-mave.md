**What it does**: radar points of 5 accumulated sweeps are averaged per pillar into a 128 x 128 BEV grid (0.8 m cells, 51.2 m) with 18 features per cell and concatenated with the 64-channel lifted camera features, plus a residual connection of the radar tensor to the CenterPoint head. The tracker (CC-3DT++) uses the detector's velocity in the association score.

**Results that matter** (nuScenes val): mAVE 0.47 m/s against 0.86 for camera-only BEVDet R50 (minus 45.3 %); mAP 35.1 vs 29.8, NDS 45.6 vs 37.9. AMOTA 31.2 to 38.1 with the velocity term. No radar-off ablation isolates radar's share of the velocity gain; removing the z-discretisation lowers mAVE 0.52 to 0.45.

**Transferable technique**: the simplest recipe (accumulate sweeps, pillar-average the attributes including the compensated velocity components, concatenate) already halves the camera-only velocity error; this is the baseline arm for any more elaborate radar velocity estimator. Compare with the 2305.15883 row (mAVE 0.918 to 0.421 with RC-BEVFusion).

**Cost and fit**: BEVDet-scale model; the radar block is tiny.

**Doubts**: the gain of the BEV-level velocity is from learned association inside the head, not an explicit full-velocity estimate; the velocity here is box-level, not per cell.
