## Decided

Decided: 2026-11-10
Decision: D71 (Mirko, in chat on 2026-11-10, in the Spurmitte lane chat; two messages, his words verbatim). First: "Bei Spurmitte haben wir Punkte auf den Spurmitten mit Fahrrichtung und bei Kreuzungen Eingangspunkte und Ausgangspunkte, je nach Fahrtrichtung. Diese Punkte sollten so spät wie möglich festgelegt werden, so weit noch die Spur klar durch die Umgebung und Spurmarkierungen definiert sind, und die Spurmarkierung die tatsächlichen Fahrmöglichkeiten repräsentieren." After I had answered that the stop / exit points of the lane GT are the ends of the nuScenes map lanes (a map convention, not placed by what is visible), second: "Die Punkte sollen spät sein, aber da liegen, wo das Netzwerk sie sehen kann, sowohl die Ausgänge, als auch die Eingänge". My reading, which he can correct: (1) the design rule for the junction points of the lane graph: the entry point (stop) and the exit point (exit) of a junction are placed as LATE as possible (as close to the junction as possible) but only where the network can see them, i.e. where the lane is still clearly defined by the surroundings and the lane markings and the markings show the real driving options; the same rule for entries and exits; (2) it is a rule for the ground truth (where the point is) and so for the training target, not a change of the metric or of the network; (3) "where the network can see it" is not defined yet: candidates are the camera visibility of the point (l4 `vis`), paint or boundary near the point, the end of the network's own centre-line evidence; the definition is to be measured on the stored data and the data are the GT chat's, so the wish goes to the GT chat by message with the measured numbers, not before; (4) the rule changes the GT (a new lane version) and so needs a new training in a GPU window; nothing of that starts without a card.
Consequence: no code, GT or training changes today. This chat measures on the CPU where the map's stop / exit points lie against what the network sees (signed offset of the delivered ends along the lane; visibility and paint at the map points) and writes the numbers into a card; only then a wish to the GT chat (no edit of `autogt/` here) and, with Mirko's go, a card for the retraining.
