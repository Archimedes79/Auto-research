2. THE SYSTEM THAT IS DELIVERED
It is the full camera + radar BEV network of run t16.full_s0 (trained 6000 steps on 428 nuScenes scenes, a median of 3.7 s per optimiser step, 7 hours wall-clock): two frozen image encoders with cached features (DualBEV detection features and the map-trained BEVFusion features), a depth-weighted lift into the BEV, a camera memory over 3 frames, a radar encoder on the radar detections with Doppler, concat fusion, a trunk, and heads for six map layers (drivable area, walkway, car park, pedestrian crossing, lane divider, road divider), objects (vehicles, pedestrians, two-wheelers) and a velocity field that uses two frames and the radar Doppler (motion block, velocity hurdle). The radar memory is switched off (it did not learn). It is trained on ground truth v8 from the autogt tool (the image encoders stay frozen, everything behind them is trained).
It was evaluated on 1000 validation frames spread over 82 validation scenes that it never saw (map layers are scored on the cells the front camera sees, detection on the fusion area, the cells that camera and radar both observe). One training seed per run. The TEST split was never used.

3. HOW GOOD IS IT (the table below is read from the stored evaluation files, never typed by hand)
Reading in words:
- Drivable area is good (IoU 0.78 on the 1000 frames; on the 384 frames of the GaussianCaR comparison 0.82 against 0.86, i.e. 95 % of that 360 degree six-camera system, same frames, same ground truth, same metric code). Vehicle detection in the fusion area is good (AP at 2 m 0.80).
- Walkways, car parks, pedestrian crossings and the two divider layers are clearly weaker (IoU 0.26 to 0.57); pedestrians are weak (AP at 2 m 0.38).
- Velocity is no longer the zero predictor: at moving box centres the error is about 1.1 m/s, on static cells about 0.19 m/s (details in the report, section 4).
- Lane markings: the lane divider reaches IoU 0.43 when a predicted cell within 0.5 m of a true line counts. Read as a thin line from the distance field (what a planner would use) the precision is 0.85 but the recall only 0.10 (F1 0.19, 1000 frames). So the network rarely draws a divider that is not there, but it often draws none where the map has one. The report shows twelve frames with their error maps and a reading of four of them: a dashed centre line on a straight road is found along 52 m; on a wide city street vehicles cover the lines; at an intersection no divider is predicted; and in one frame the map has a line that I cannot see painted in the camera picture, so the label asks for something the image does not show.
- Two experiments tried to improve on this system and neither did (columns "more data" and "bundle" in the table): 700 instead of 428 training scenes at the same budget (t17), and a bundle of module fixes with a smaller learning rate for the motion block (t18). The written rules gave TIE/negative and CUT. Against the baseline the bundle is worse on velocity (moving box centres 1.11 to 1.20 m/s, static cells 0.19 to 0.24 m/s) and mixed on the maps (walkway +0.010, car park -0.020, road divider IoU -0.012 and its thin-line F1 0.51 to 0.31). So the baseline stays the delivered system.

5. WHAT WAS TRIED, IN ORDER (23 experiment folders; the report has the numbers, the ground-truth version and the lesson of every one)
- First whole chain (t1, 26 Oct): frozen DualBEV heads, depth-weighted lift, 3-frame memory, radar BEV, concat fusion, trunk, heads at three time offsets. It works; a one-shot bundle of fixes was not better than the first version (the causes were never isolated).
- Block swaps (t2, t3): eleven exchanges of fusion, temporal, radar and trunk blocks for standard ones: 7 ties, 4 worse, none better, so the simplest block stays. Several early "dead" results were retracted (a camera-off arm that still had the camera on).
- State of the art in the front sector (t4): the camera path equals the DualBEV camera detector, the radar adds about +0.12 mAP over the matched camera-only arm (older ground truth).
- Why the maps are weak (t5, t6, t7, t8): a lane probe on the frozen features (inconclusive, then a tie), a maps-only run (it closes only about a tenth of the gap to GaussianCaR), a stride-4 lane branch (helps thin lines a little). Verdict by the written rule: the image features, not the competition between tasks, are the main limit.
- Map-trained BEVFusion features (t8) lift the lane divider a lot (0.19 to 0.35 on the older ground truth) but halve the object detection; using both feature sets in one chain (t10, two seeds) holds both and became the base.
- Velocity (t11, t12): the box velocity was stuck at the zero predictor because of a dead branch; the velocity hurdle (moving/static logit split from the regression) and a motion block (two frames plus radar Doppler) brought it off zero.
- Full network (t16, 2 Nov): every built block, ground truth v8, with gates and a module probe; this is the baseline that is delivered.
- More data (t17): 700 instead of 428 scenes at the same step budget: no map gain, the train-to-validation gap does not shrink. TIE/negative.
- Bundle (t18): module fixes plus a smaller learning rate for the motion block: velocity worse, maps mixed (walkway better, car park and road divider worse). CUT.
- Dead or unlearned parts found on the way: the radar memory (static map stays 1.0), the box-velocity branch, the time-mover head; the report lists the reasons.

6. WEAK SPOTS AND HONEST LIMITS
- Car parks, pedestrian crossings, the lane divider and pedestrians are the weakest outputs; the train-to-validation gap is large for them.
- One training seed per run; the seed spread of this recipe is not measured. There is no matched large-set control of the old chain (it was held when you stopped the training). 82 of the 150 validation scenes only (the rest was not downloaded).
- The comparison with GaussianCaR is like-for-like in frames, ground truth and metric code, but not in sensors (six cameras, 360 degrees, against our front camera); the published numbers of other systems are not comparable and are not used.
- Ground truth changed from v5 to v8 during the work, so numbers of old and new runs are not comparable; the report marks the version of every number.
- Two findings about my own tools: the idle-GPU guard of the timing tool refuses on this machine because nvidia-smi lists the timing process itself (worked around in a scratch wrapper, the frozen file is untouched; to be fixed under the freeze rule), and package.py / provenance give "GT version unknown" for a BEV run. Both are in bev_fusion/records/open_findings_2026-11-02.md.
