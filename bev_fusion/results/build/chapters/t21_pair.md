### What was built and trained

Version P of order D51, the pair: the buffer is the previous frame's raw camera BEV (t-1), carried in the state of a stream; the network lifts two frames (t-1 and t) instead of three, and the batch path with two frames is the same function as the stream (equivalence test). Everything else is as in the chapter before: warm start from `t20.base_s0`, 1500 steps, learning rate 1e-4, the equal-budget control is the three-frame window continued for the same steps (`t21.ctl_s0`), one seed. The velocity block reads the current frame and the buffered one, which is 0.167 s older instead of the 0.333 s of the window (see the probable cause in the chapter before).

### What the numbers say

- **By the point estimates no guard of the card is beyond its band, and the cost is lower.** Against the equal-budget control the velocity error at moving box centres is 1.132 against 1.084 m/s (+0.049, interval +0.033 to +0.066: separated from 0 by the scene resampling, inside the band of 0.10; the card predicted 1.25, a loss of 0.15), the mAP in the fusion area 0.411 against 0.412 (-0.0003) and the lane divider IoU@1 0.5496 against 0.5516 (-0.002, interval -0.005 to +0.001). The batch path takes 88.9 ms at batch 1 on an idle GPU against 102.5 ms for the window (-13.6 ms; the card allowed 92: met). A deployed stream with one new image per step takes 57.1 ms at dt 0 and 77.4 ms for the three time offsets (window 85.5 and 104.7 ms; the card allowed 80: met); synthetic timing with random weights, the ms exclude the two frozen image encoders, and the window in this comparison lifts all its frames on every call.
- **The velocity loss is the same as in R** (+0.049 against +0.053), so it does not come from the recurrence but from what both versions share: the velocity block sees a baseline of half the length. P4 (next chapter) is consistent with this; it is not isolated.
- **Fewer separated losses than R**, among about forty compared metrics of one seed (hints, not findings): walkway IoU -0.007, drivable area -0.002, moving-vehicle speed skill over the whole area -0.009, and a gain in the F1 of the lane-line zero crossings (+0.012; the same metric moves the other way in R and P4, so it is noisy).
- **Warm whole scenes** (12 scenes, 456 frames): velocity 1.079 against 1.019 m/s (+0.060, interval +0.024 to +0.102: the upper end is just past the band), mAP in the fusion area 0.444 against 0.442, lane divider IoU@1 0.543 against 0.550 (-0.007, interval -0.017 to +0.001: the lower end is past the band of 0.010). So "no guard lost" holds by the point estimates; the intervals of the warm comparison reach past two bands. Pedestrian AP@1 over the whole area is lower than the control's (-0.010, interval below 0).
- **State carried.** The memory command (400 validation frames, two keyframes of history, no intervals) shows warm against cold gains outside the camera view of +0.042 (drivable area), +0.051 (walkway) and +0.070 (lane mask) at dt 0, but the window control shows the same kind of gain, because the world memory is shared by all arms and the pair never carries its raw buffer in a batch; this is not an effect of the pair buffer. In the stream the semantic output stays within 4.5e-3 of the batch computation over 48 frames and the velocity within 3e-5 (one scene, on the CPU); this is not bit-identical (the unit test uses 1e-5), and a small systematic difference is expected, because the stream writes the world memory every frame and the batch once per keyframe. Against a cold start the stream drifts like R (mean difference of the semantic output 0.24 at the first comparable step, 0.63 at 48).
- **Verdict (by hand: D51, one seed).** By rule (1) of the card (no guard worse than its band by the point estimates, cost lower) P passes as a version to adopt; it is simpler than R (no recurrent unroll) and costs the same in a stream. The velocity loss of about 0.05 m/s remains until P4 is read, and which of P and P4 the system takes is open (next chapter).
