# 2407.14108 GaussianBeV: per-pixel 3D Gaussians splatted to BEV for map and vehicle segmentation

**What it does**: For every pixel a generator predicts a Gaussian (centre from predicted disparity-to-metric depth + a 3D offset, rotation in allocentric form, scale, opacity, embedding). A BEV rasteriser adapted from Gaussian splatting renders C-dim features orthographically into a 200x200 map (100 m, 50 cm cells); then an LSS-style or Simple-BEV backbone and heads. Losses: segmentation (BCE + centerness + offset, uncertainty-weighted), depth |log z - log z*|, and "early supervision" = the segmentation loss applied directly to the rasteriser output.

**Results that matter** (nuScenes vehicle IoU, 224x448, visibility filtering): with the BEV backbone the individual variants lie at 46.7 - 47.3, depth loss and early supervision each give no gain alone but together help; full model 47.5 (LSS) / 47.0 (Simple-BEV). Without a backbone the depth loss helps (45.4 and 45.9 in a garbled table). Speed 24 fps at 224x448 and 13 fps at 448x800 on an A100 (PointBeV 19 / 15 fps).

**Transferable technique**: the depth auxiliary loss is worth about 0.2-0.5 IoU once a BEV backbone exists, i.e. small; early supervision of the lift output is the cheap complement.

**Cost and fit**: real-time on an A100 only; relevant as evidence that an explicit depth/rendering loss helps BEV seg little when the backbone is strong.

**Doubts**: table layout is garbled in the extraction, so the mapping of the values is not reliable; lambda_depth not stated.
