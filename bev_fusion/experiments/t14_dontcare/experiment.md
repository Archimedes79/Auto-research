- **Statement.** Continuing the hurdle model for 1000 steps with no loss on the fused heads outside the camera x radar area leaves every headline number on the fusion area within its band of the same continuation with today's loss.
- **Measured before the run** (`python tests/test_fusion_area_real.py`, output stored in `results/fusion_area_real_gt.txt`: `build_layer_targets` on 40 real frames of `data/cache/gt`, signature 15efbde2d1, option off vs on): the fusion area is 62.6 % of the grid; the mean map weight falls 0.878 -> 0.546 and the object weight 0.995 -> 0.624; **supervised object centres 369 -> 197 (46.6 % dropped)**: far more than the area share, because many objects stand beside the car, outside the camera's field of view, and today only the radar supervises them. - **Mechanism.** The fused heads spend 38 % of their cells on a region the camera cannot see, where only radar and memory can speak; those cells are not scored under D37, so removing their loss frees capacity for the scored area. Against it: the shared trunk loses the supervision that radar and memory features outside the field of view received, and almost half of the object positives, including the side objects where the radar Doppler is the velocity evidence; if those features also serve the cells just inside the edge, the edge and the velocity get worse.
