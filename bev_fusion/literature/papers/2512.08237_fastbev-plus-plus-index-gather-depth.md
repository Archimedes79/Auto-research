**What it does**: The view transform is recast as Index (precomputed, sorted tables), Gather and Reshape of standard operators, so it runs in TensorRT with no custom plugin. The depth-aware step is Eq. 3: F_flat = Gather(F_img, I_spatial) (elementwise product) Gather(D_prob, I_depth), with I_spatial = {(cam, u, v)} and I_depth = {(cam, u, v, d)}; the result is reshaped to a [Z, H, W, C] volume (Eq. 4). A light pixel-wise depth head runs in parallel with the image encoder and gives a distribution over predefined depth bins; there is no explicit depth ground-truth loss, depth is trained through the detection loss. Shape of D_prob, number and spacing of bins and the interpolation are not given in the text read.

**Results that matter** (nuScenes val, detection): R50 camera-only 0.344 mAP / 0.478 NDS, with depth fusion ("C&D") 0.359 / 0.488 (R101: NDS 0.507 -> 0.522), i.e. +1.0 NDS for the depth term. Throughput 134 FPS with INT8 on a Tesla T4 (R50-CBGS); "above 100 FPS" on Orin X and T4; FP16 speedups over Fast-BEV 3.5x (Xavier), 3.9x (Orin X), 3.0x (T4) as read from a figure caption. No view-transform ms, no input resolution in the text read; no segmentation.

**Transferable technique**: the gather-then-multiply form of PD with two index tables and TensorRT-native operators (Gather, MatMul) is the deployment recipe for our lift; end-to-end depth training works without lidar (small gain). Also gives the sorted-index trick for a one-to-one assignment.

**Cost and fit**: all standard ops, Apache-2.0, built on BEVDet (mmdet3d). Hardware-named FPS is whole-network, not the lift.

**Doubts**: the page is internally inconsistent (0.359 / 0.488 / 0.489); the depth term is not isolated beyond the C vs C&D row; D_prob definition missing; the real model zoo has no numbers in the README; does not say whether uncertainty is kept beyond using the distribution; 4D / long-term variants blur what the depth term gives.
