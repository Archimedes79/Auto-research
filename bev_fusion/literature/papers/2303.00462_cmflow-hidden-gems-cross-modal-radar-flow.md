**What it does**: extends RaFlow with supervision that exists only at training time. (1) Odometer (GPS / INS) loss on the predicted rigid ego transform; static points (90.5 % of the test set) are thereby supervised implicitly. (2) A pretrained LiDAR 3D multi-object tracker gives a pseudo foreground mask and pseudo flow for moving points (motion loss only on those). (3) Pseudo optical flow from RAFT on the camera, projected onto the image; point-to-ray distance (weight 0.1) so the loss is less range-sensitive. Radial Doppler minus the Doppler expected from ego motion gives a pseudo motion label (tangential movers are not detected by this). Outputs: per-point 3D flow, a 4 x 4 ego transform and a motion mask.

**Results that matter** (View-of-Delft test): EPE 0.141 m (CMFlow) / 0.130 m (with the temporal module) against RaFlow 0.226; radial error 0.057 / 0.052 against 0.090. Ablation (temporal module off), EPE: no supervision 0.228; odometer 0.161; odometer + LiDAR 0.145; odometer + camera 0.159; all three 0.141. The odometer gives most of the gain; the camera flow gives less because of windscreen reflections. Ego motion: RTE 0.090 to 0.066 m, RAE 0.336 to 0.090 deg.

**Transferable technique**: ranking of the labels by value: ego pose (free for us, exact) >> tracked-box flow >> camera optical flow. For our synthetic gate the GT is known, but on nuScenes the ego pose alone already removes 29 % of the EPE without any object label.

**Cost and fit**: per-point network; View-of-Delft radar is denser than the nuScenes radar.

**Doubts**: dataset with a different radar; its tracker pseudo-labels carry the tracker's errors.
