# 2206.00665 MonoSDF: monocular depth and normal cues as losses for neural SDF

**What it does**: Neural SDF volume rendering with four losses: L = L_rgb + 0.1 L_eikonal + 0.1 L_depth + 0.05 L_normal. Depth: squared error after solving a per-batch scale and shift in closed form by least squares (monocular depth is only affine-correct). Normal: L1 + (1 - n.n_hat) against the monocular normal. Eikonal on uniform and near-surface points. 1024 rays per iteration, 200k iterations, Omnidata depth and normal predictors at 384x384.

**Results that matter** (Replica, MLP, Chamfer-L1 / F-score): no cues 6.75 / 66.88; depth only 4.26 / 76.42; normal only 3.19 / 85.84; both 2.94 / 86.18. Multi-resolution grid: 5.03 / 78.38 -> both 3.23 / 85.91. Predictor quality matters: depth MiDaS 68.6, LeReS 72.6, Omnidata 86.7 F-score (no cues 64.2); self-supervised depth cue 45.6. Training 6 h (grid) to 11 h (MLP) on one RTX 3090; with cues both representations beat the converged no-cue models after 10k iterations.

**Transferable technique**: shift-and-scale-invariant depth loss and a normal loss as geometry priors on a rendered SDF; useful where an up-to-scale depth prior (e.g. from a foundation depth model) is available but metric depth is not. For driving, normals would be predicted road-plane-heavy and radar gives metric depth anyway.

**Cost and fit**: per-scene optimisation, hours per scene: not a feed-forward driving model; take the loss, not the pipeline.

**Doubts**: indoor Replica / ScanNet only; no driving evidence; the gain depends strongly on the monocular predictor domain (Omnidata is trained on indoor-heavy data).
