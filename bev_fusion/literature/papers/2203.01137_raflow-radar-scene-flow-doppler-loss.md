**What it does**: per radar point (xyz + Doppler RRV + RCS + power), a multi-scale set-conv encoder, a cost-volume layer between the two scans and a flow decoder give a coarse per-point scene flow; a static-flow refinement then fixes the static points. Three losses: (1) radial displacement: the radial projection of the flow of each point must equal RRV x dt (Doppler as a one-dimensional label that is always available); (2) soft Chamfer between the warped first and the second scan (mutual nearest neighbours weighted by kernel density, outliers dropped, tolerance 0.1 m); (3) spatial smoothness of the flow (RBF-weighted neighbours). Ego motion: assume all points are static, Kabsch for a coarse transform, label points whose radial residual against RRV is <= 15 % as static, redo Kabsch on the static set; the static mask is the motion segmentation.

**Results that matter** (in-house set, HASCO LRR30 4D radar, 43.6 km, 1 test sequence): RaFlow average EPE 0.570 m, radial error 0.240 m, motion-segmentation accuracy 81.9 %, mIoU 47.9 %; the best self-supervised baseline on radial error (SLIM) 0.515 m with EPE 1.224 m. Removing RRV hurt more than removing RCS or power. No runtime reported.

**Transferable technique**: the Doppler radial-displacement loss is exactly our 'radial component is measured, the tangential must come from two sweeps' split: the second scan fixes the tangential part only through the Chamfer term. Static detection by 'radial residual vs Doppler' is a closed formula once ego velocity is known (as in our 2511.20003 note).

**Cost and fit**: point-based, our radar is the same kind of sparse point set; a BEV version would rasterise first and lose the point-wise matching.

**Verification by the dogma/flow scout (2026-10-26, ar5iv text + abs page)**: authors Fangqiang Ding, Zhijun Pan, Yimin Deng, Jianning Deng, Chris Xiaoxuan Lu; RA-L 2022, DOI 10.1109/LRA.2022.3187248; v1 2022-03-02, v4 2022-07-02. Radial loss = L1, summed over points, between (flow . unit position vector) and RRV x dt, no robust weighting; static mask thresholded at 0.15 (zeta) from the coarse flow, then Kabsch. Network: four-radius set-conv encoder (2 to 16 m), PointPWC cost-volume layer, set-conv decoder. Ablation (Table VI): without the radial loss the average radial error RNE rises 0.240 -> 0.405 m and the static / radial accuracy scores fall (SAS 0.316 -> 0.236, RAS 0.675 -> 0.375); removing the RRV input: RNE 0.362 (row assignment inferred from garbled table). Motion segmentation accuracy 81.9 %, mIoU 47.9 %. Runtime not reported.

**Doubts**: in-house data, one test sequence, no strong supervised comparison; the tangential error is still large (EPE 0.57 m against radial error 0.24 m).
