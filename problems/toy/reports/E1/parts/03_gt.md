The truth of a frame is a mask of the picture's size. A pixel belongs to the disc when at least half of its sub-pixel centres lie inside the annotated circle. It is made from the annotation of the raw shard (centre and radius), never drawn by hand. The known answers of the generator (a radius of half a pixel gives exactly one pixel, a huge disc covers the picture) and a comparison of every stored shard with a fresh one are its check. Its limit: the truth is exact by construction, so it says nothing about label noise, which a real data set has.
