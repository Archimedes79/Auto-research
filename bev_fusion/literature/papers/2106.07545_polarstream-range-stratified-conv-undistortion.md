**What it does** [checked]: Lidar detection and segmentation on streamed sectors in a polar pillar grid (delta r, delta theta). Two fixes for the distortion of polar cells: (1) range-stratified convolution and normalisation, individual kernels and statistics per range region (number of strata not stated), applied to the centre-offset regression and the shared head; (2) feature undistortion, features at Cartesian pillar locations are interpolated from the polar locations with two small networks g and q (the weights of a learned bilinear-like resampling). Multi-scale context padding joins neighbouring sectors.

**Results that matter** [checked, nuScenes val, V100, PyTorch]: detection mAP polar pillars 48.2, + range-stratified 49.1, + undistortion 48.6, both 50.3, with segmentation head 51.2. Cartesian pillars with the same head: 50.6 (so plain polar loses 2.4 mAP in detection, the two fixes close it to 50.3). Segmentation mIoU polar 73.2 vs Cartesian 72.5 (table) / 72.1 (text). Runtime 38.2 vs 38.1 ms detection, the fixes add about 0.5 ms (text). Context padding (sectors, not our case): 46.7 to 52.4 mAP at 32 sectors.

**Transferable technique**: the footprint distortion of a polar grid is real and measurable: a plain polar CNN with a fixed 3x3 kernel is 2.4 mAP worse than Cartesian for box detection, per-range kernels alone give +0.9, learned interpolation alone +0.4, both +2.1 (the gains are more than additive). For us the question is smaller: in the radar polar image the footprint is constant in bins by construction (azimuth error is a fixed number of bins), so the distortion enters only at the polar-to-Cartesian gather, where the undistortion (a learned resampling) or simply a conv in the Cartesian domain after the gather can absorb it. A per-range-band weight or a range channel (CoordConv) is the cheap equivalent of range stratification.

**Cost and fit**: plain PyTorch layers; +0.5 ms.

**Doubts**: lidar, dense points, not radar; no seeds; the polar-versus-Cartesian gap for detection is a 1-run number; the strata count is not stated; the interpolation nets are described in words only.
