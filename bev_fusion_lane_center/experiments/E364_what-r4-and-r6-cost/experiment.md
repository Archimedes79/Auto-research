## 1. Card text
- **Statement:** the long-link cover drop carries a visible amount of topo F1 on the end-to-end read-out (it acts on about three quarters of the delivered curves), and the pair mask admits about 98 % of the true connectors, of which the pair head can reach only part: R4 is a rule step with a bar, R6 an index limit with a small, measured cost.
- **Mechanism (R4):** a connector whose two ends lie within COVER_POS = 4 m of the first and the last point of one of B's long links DROPS that link (B's own long-link candidate and the connector would deliver the same lane twice: `jwire.kept_ends`, `qends_eval.add_connectors`). With the radius -1 nothing is dropped. **(R6):** `allowed` gates the K x K pair logits of the pair head and the loss is taken over `allowed` only (`qendnet.py:241`), so the logits of masked cells are untrained and R6 cannot be replayed with the mask off; a true connector whose geometry fails it can never be delivered. That is why R6 is measured on the GT.
- **Inputs (ready):** the stored dumps `runs/e324_s_s<S>/ends_val.pkl`, B's full dump `temp/e276/predfull_e273_p_s<S>.pkl`, the scorer `jends_eval.py score` (unchanged; the radius is patched in process by a new launcher), the GT loader `lane_gt_data` with `ends=True`, `qendnet.COS_TURN / MIN_CHORD`, `qends.allowed` (patched in process in the mask script only).
- **Controls:** c (stored), the fixtures (the launcher without the patch reproduces c on both seeds), the unit check of the mask function against the stored `allowed`.
