**Why:** E365 S1 is worse than the rule `lo_near` on the same tokens (0.5486 / 0.5743 against 0.6265 / 0.6281): trained from a random start on the GT, the head settles at a conservative point (precision 0.86, recall 0.40). **The question:** does a head that is first taught to BE the rule (stage A, no GT) and then trained on the GT (stage B) end above the rule and over the okayish gate, with no rule anywhere in the delivered system? **Statement, with its confound:** a rule-distilled start, schedule-confounded (15000 steps in two schedules against S1's 12000; the one-seed control `g` separates it).

**The design (two training stages):**
- **Stage A (teacher, `--teacher rule`, 3000 steps, from a random start; the rule makes LABELS only, in the loss, as matching does (D55); the forward reads only `jtoken.INPUTS`):** the teacher's nodes = the valid tokens with `e_p >= 0.5` (the field `rule_frame` uses: bit-identical to c-rule's nodes); its links = c-rule's `lo` and `lo_near` links on these nodes, computed with the SAME code as `jtoken_eval.py ctl`; wiring targets per class: the rule's single link of i in that class, or the none target when the rule has no link; **a source whose rule link lies outside the head's allowed gate (`pair_geometry`: chord and heading j, MIN_CHORD 0.3 m) is left OUT of the wiring loss** (not taught 'none'; the share of such links is measured on the TRAIN sample in the build); type targets from B' itself (GT-free): a token with e_p < 0.5 -> 0 (none), else the class of B''s own flags (sigmoid(flags) >= 0.5, the manifest's priority map split > merge > stop > exit > boundary > lane) ; no scale loss (`yscale` is not read in stage A: tested); the type CE as E365.
- **Stage B (GT, the E365 S1 loss unchanged, 12000 steps, `--init <stage-A model.pt>`):** weights from stage A, a fresh optimiser and schedule (5 % warm-up, cosine), the head's step buffer CARRIED (no second SWITCH phase: the key weights stay the head's own 1 - p_none, so the stage-B step-0 graph equals the stage-A graph); on a resume, `ckpt.pt` takes precedence over `--init`; `--teacher` is a training option only (not in `build_head`): the stage-B `model.pt` loads with the plain S1 `load_model` and has the S1 state-dict keys. Weight snapshots are kept at stage-B steps 0, 500, 1000, 3000, 6000 (readable by `load_model`).
- **The stage-A FIXTURE (written now, on the TRAIN sample = every third of the 873 evenly spaced TRAIN frames, 291 frames, both seeds):** the stage-A head reproduces c-rule on the same frames, per class, against c-rule RESTRICTED to the head's gate: at least 95 % of c-rule's short links and at least 80 % of its long links delivered, extra links at most 5 % of c-rule's, kept-node sets agreeing on at least 99 % of the valid tokens; topo F1 against c-rule (restricted and unrestricted) reported beside it. **If a seed misses:** a FRESH stage-A run of 6000 steps from a random start (`e370_a6_s<S>`, run id `E370.a6_s<S>`), for the failing seed only; its stage B starts from it and the result names the per-seed stage-A length; if that misses too, stage B starts anyway and the result says "stage A did not reach the rule" (a diagnosis, never a kill).
- **Delivered system:** the trained head of stage B alone (no rule input, no prior, no teacher): G-simple holds (one network, two training stages, the hard top-K the named budget). **E370 uses both allowed training stages:** an S4 (the joint fine-tune of B') on this head would be a third stage and fail G-simple unless stage A is folded into one run (a teacher weight decaying to 0 inside one schedule = one stage: the next card if E370 works) or Mirko's recorded reading says otherwise.
- **The schedule control `g` (one seed, seed 0, lower priority, the day window if the night is full):** stage A' = the E365 GT loss for 3000 steps from a random start, then a stage B identical to E370's (fresh optimiser, 12000 steps, `--init`): it separates the rule labels from the extra steps and the warm restart.
