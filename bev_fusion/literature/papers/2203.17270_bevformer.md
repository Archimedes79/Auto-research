# 2203.17270 BEVFormer: temporal self-attention over an ego-aligned BEV history

| verdict | context (the reference recurrent BEV; heavy; VideoBEV and StreamMapNet are the cheaper descendants) |

**What it does**: Grid BEV queries (200 x 200 at 0.512 m on nuScenes). Temporal self-attention: the previous BEV B(t-1) is shifted by ego motion so cells match the same world position, then deformable attention over the current queries and the aligned history, with the sampling offsets predicted from the concatenation of both. First frame: history replaced by the queries themselves. Moving objects are not linked explicitly; the paper says the deformable offsets model the temporal connection implicitly. Training uses 4 frames sampled from the last 2 s, only the previous BEV is carried at test time (recurrent).

**Results that matter** (nuScenes val): temporal 0.517 NDS / 0.416 mAP vs spatial-only BEVFormer-S 0.448 / 0.375; mAVE 0.802 to 0.394. History frames in training 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5: NDS 0.448 / 0.490 / 0.510 / 0.517 / 0.517. Without ego alignment NDS 0.510 vs 0.517; offsets from Q alone 0.513. Low-visibility recall +6.0 points over spatial-only. Latency: 130 ms with the default six-layer encoder, 25 ms with one layer, 7 ms with single-scale features, 100 x 100 BEV and one layer (GPU not stated on the page).

**Transferable technique**: concatenating query and history to predict deformable offsets (the offsets absorb object motion without a flow head); random frame sampling in training. At a 100 x 100 grid and one layer the temporal block is small, relevant for a 0.5 m grid with a 50 m range.

**Cost and fit**: Cost is the encoder stack, not the temporal block alone; no separate temporal cost is given. Needs the camera backbone and spatial cross-attention, which we do not take.

**Doubts**: Gain of ego alignment on val is only +0.007 NDS; improvement is mostly velocity (mAVE), i.e. for moving objects; no static-layer (map) result in this page; no separate latency for the temporal part.
