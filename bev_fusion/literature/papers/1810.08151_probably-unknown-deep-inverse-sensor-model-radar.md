**What it does**: A raw polar FMCW scan x (Theta azimuths x R range bins of power, Navtech CTS350x, no Doppler) goes through a polar convolutional encoder and a U-Net-style Cartesian decoder; Polar Transformer Units resample polar features to Cartesian by bilinear interpolation on a fixed polar-to-Cartesian grid at every interface. The output is a 600 x 600 grid (0.3 m, 180 m x 180 m). Per cell the network predicts a logit mean mu and a variance gamma of a latent z ~ N(mu, gamma); p(occupied) = sigmoid(mu / s) with s = sqrt(1 + (gamma sqrt(pi/8))^2) (probit approximation, no sampling at inference). So the "spread" is a Gaussian over the logit, not over position.

**Supervision**: partial lidar labels (two HDL32 lidars, ray traced): occupied = return, free = before the first return, partially observed = between first and last return (no loss), unobserved = behind (KL to a N(0, 1) prior). Loss: weighted BCE averaged over 25 reparameterised samples on observed cells plus KL on unobserved cells. Adam 1e-3, batch 16, 100 epochs, about 78 k training examples.

**Results that matter** (test IoU, own 5 h urban set): occupied 0.35 vs CFAR 0.24 (1D polar) / 0.20 (2D Cartesian); mean of occupied and free 0.63 vs 0.50 / 0.55; free-space IoU about equal (0.91 vs 0.92). Cells with high gamma are largely occluded space. 14 Hz for a 600 x 600 grid on a Titan Xp (as stated on the page).

**Transferable technique**: (1) a per-cell predicted variance (probit-moderated probability) as a learned confidence channel that comes out with the prediction; (2) a fixed-grid polar-to-Cartesian bilinear resampling inside the network, which is the same operation as our column-polar lookup; (3) the observed / unobserved split of the loss, which keeps the network from being punished where the label has no information (our FOV and occlusion masks).

**Cost and fit**: small U-Net, real time on a 2018 GPU. Needs a dense polar power image, which nuScenes does not provide (it gives processed detections, about 100 per radar). The learned thing is occupancy of a cell, not the position spread of a detection.

**Doubts**: non-public dataset; encoder kernel size is stated inconsistently (4x4 in text, 2x2 in the figure caption); no ablation of the variance head itself; one scanning radar, not automotive detection radar.
