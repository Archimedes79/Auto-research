**What it does**: predicts a per-pixel CATEGORICAL depth distribution, projects image features into 3D along the frustum weighted by that distribution (the LSS idea, here for a single camera), collapses to a BEV and detects with a single-stage head, trained end to end together with depth estimation.

**Results that matter**: first among published monocular methods on KITTI 3D detection; first monocular results on Waymo (abstract, no numbers or speed on the page).

**Transferable technique**: the single-camera categorical depth with a depth supervision loss (lidar depth, as in the paper family) is the base from which mean and variance are read for Gaussian splats; CaDDN's discretisation (linear-increasing bins) matters for far range. Details of bin spacing are not verified here (digest from memory).

**Cost and fit**: 3D-frustum volume is heavy (memory), which GaussianLSS avoids. KITTI / Waymo front camera, not nuScenes.

**Doubts**: no latency on the page; dense frustum volume does not fit "super-efficient".
