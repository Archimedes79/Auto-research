## 1. Card text
- **Statement:** training the head to put its mass on one member of the positive group (-log max_{j in G(i)} p(j|i)) removes the duplicate fan-out that the multi-positive likelihood tolerates: junction F1 +0.01 (window -0.03 to +0.04) over the champion at the head's own decode, a simpler decode possible.
- **What the max does and does not (said exactly):** for a source with a non-empty G(i) the loss is -log max_{j in G(i)} p^c(j|i) with the max taken over the in-class GT successors (the tokens near the GT successor, assigned or not); a source with two real successors (a fork) has ONE group G(i) that holds the tokens of BOTH branches (the cache builds G(i) as every token within 1.5 m of ANY GT successor of the assigned node): the max then keeps one token among all of them, so a true fork learns to deliver one branch. **This is a real cost at forks and merges; the `simpler` / junction numbers and the topo F1 show it. A fork-aware variant (the max per branch group) needs the group labels, which the cache does not carry (y is one matrix): not in this card.** The gradient reaches only the winning member (a subgradient: the max is a matching step inside the loss, D55), the others are pushed down by the softmax normalisation; the type cross-entropy, the scale loss and the empty-G terms are unchanged.
