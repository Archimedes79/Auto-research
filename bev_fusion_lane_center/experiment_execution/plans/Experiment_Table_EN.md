# Experiment table with its basis (E15 - E121)

Generated by `scripts/build_experiment_table.py` from `scripts/e2e_catalogue.py` (what, result, use tag), `scripts/e2e_basis.py` (basis) and the technique register in `reports/Loop16_Triage_Plan_EN.md` section 10.0 (links). Columns: **tag** = use in a completely differentiable end-to-end system (E learned and differentiable, P with a classical step, C classical / reference, D dropped); **SP** = stage of the sparsification chain the experiment addresses; **BB** = building blocks of the catalogue (`report_final/text/lit_extra10b.md` and the new blocks BB22 - BB30); **K** = technique and literature ids, links in the register below. The basis names what an experiment was derived from or inspired by according to the project's literature notes (loops 6 - 15); an empty basis means the experiment was designed from the data (no literature source named).

Sparsification stages: **SP0** not part of the sparsification chain (foundation, map, time, planner, analysis); **SP1** seeds and key points (peak picking, node heat maps); **SP2** line forming (dense field to lines: threshold, thinning, tracer); **SP3** graph assembly and junctions (links, connectors, assembler); **SP4** keep and verify (precision gates, verifiers, probabilities); **SP5** learned sparse extractor (step choice, vertices, events); **SP6** vertex positions of the sparse graph (refinement, keep gate); **SP7** sparse graph as planner interface (tokens, consumer).
