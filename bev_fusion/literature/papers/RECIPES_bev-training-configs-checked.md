**What repeats** [checked above]: AdamW; lr 2e-4 to 4e-4 for effective batches of 8 to 64 (NOT scaled linearly with batch); weight decay 0.01 for the transformer models, 1e-7 for the BEVDet family; a pre-trained image backbone gets lr x 0.1; warm-up of 200-500 iterations; cosine to 1e-3 of the peak, or one step; gradient clipping 5-35; EMA in the BEVDet family; fp16 with dynamic loss scale in the temporal models; gradient checkpointing only on the backbone and decoder; recurrent history detached or limited to one gradient frame; a first phase without history (CRT-Fusion 6 epochs, StreamMapNet 4 epochs).

**From search-result snippets (digest, not pages read)**: the RaCFormer paper text gives lr 2e-4 for the rest and 2e-5 for the image backbone (the config in the in-repo note says 4e-4; the two differ, take neither as final); MamBEV freezes the first backbone stage and trains the others at 10 % of the base lr; RCBEVDet++ trains in two stages and freezes the camera stream in the second; RCM-Fusion ran all its ablations on 1/7 of the nuScenes train set with CBGS: a precedent for cheap ablation protocols on a small card.
**EMA of the weights** [checked, https://github.com/Megvii-BaseDetection/BEVDepth README benchmark table, MIT licence, nuScenes val mAP / NDS; a benchmark of released configs, single runs, not a designed ablation]: BEVDepth 24e 0.3304 / 0.4355 to 0.3329 / 0.4409 with EMA; with CBGS 20e 0.3484 / 0.4805 to 0.3589 / 0.4797; BEVStereo 24e 2key 0.3456 / 0.4516 to 0.3494 / 0.4543; 24e key4 0.3427 / 0.4423 to 0.3435 / 0.4485; with CBGS 0.3576 / 0.4902 to 0.3721 / 0.4997. EMA adds +0.1 to +1.5 mAP and -0.1 to +1.0 NDS, at the price of one extra copy of the weights and no inference cost; the training command also passes `--amp_backend native` (mixed precision, not described further), `-b 8 --gpus 8`.

**Proposal for our 3060 (my inference from the table, NOT tested)**: trainable parts only (lift-reduce, radar block, temporal fusion, trunk, heads), AdamW lr 2e-4 and wd 0.01 at effective batch 16 reached by accumulation (CR3DT shows accumulation 8 on one 3090 works for a radar-camera model; the lane project's rule lr <= 6e-4 with clipping and the roll-back guard stays), 500-iteration warm-up from lr/3, cosine to 1e-3 of the peak, clip 5, EMA 0.999 evaluated and checkpointed; sequence: phase 1 dt = 0 without history (20 % of steps, as plan 8.4), then the window with the history detached beyond the K = 3 sources, one gradient frame for the camera backbone side; resume from EMA weights at a phase change as CRT-Fusion does.

**Doubts**: configs are not papers: a config can differ from the reported run (the CRN docstring and README differ in the same way, per the in-repo code note); epochs are given with CBGS or without in different files; lr, batch and hardware are not comparable between rows; none of these is an ablation.
