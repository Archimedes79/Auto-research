The system of record is NOT a pure E2E solution. ## 2. The strictest read-out and what closes it
**Attach-only 0.6616 / 0.6646 is the honest "no wiring selection" number** (hard steps R2 - R8 and the thresholds remain, named). Gap to the record 0.6981 / 0.7010: 0.0365 / 0.0364.
- **Wiring, +0.0296 / +0.0302 (81 % / 83 %).** What closes it is a learned choice of the piece end that each attached end joins: W (a head argmax, 545 parameters, E325 recipe). It does NOT remove the selection; it makes it learned, with the argmax named. A wiring without any selection would deliver the place probabilities as edge probabilities and let the scorer threshold them (class T); E325's softmax weights are not calibrated for that (E344), so this is a later card (calibration, ECE), not the cheapest next step.
- **Geometry, +0.0069 / +0.0062 (19 % / 17 %).** Sharper attached ends (the end term of E317, held). This improves the number of the E2E read-out; it does not remove a hard step.
- Expected value of W alone on the E2E read-out is small (E325 gained +0.002 / +0.003 over the rule at the snapped read-out, the oracle gate E322 shows no headroom in the choice): **the gain of W is end-to-end-ness, not accuracy**; it moves the reported system from "nearest-piece rule" to "learned head", and nothing in the table moves topo F1 up by more than a few thousandths.
