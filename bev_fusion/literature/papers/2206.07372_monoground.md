**What it does**: adds the ground plane as a geometric prior to the ill-posed monocular 2D-to-3D mapping and as an extra source of depth (a dense ground-depth branch with a depth-align training strategy and two-stage depth inference, per the search summary of the paper, not the abstract page). No LiDAR, stereo or depth maps needed.

**Results that matter**: state of the art on KITTI at the time; no numbers or FPS on the page (the abstract says "very fast").

**Transferable technique**: ground-plane depth for pixels on the road as a fixed prior channel (computed from pose), combined with a learned depth for pixels above the ground; the flat-ground error on slopes is then confined to the prior channel (derived estimate: about d^2*alpha/h, see 2005.04078).

**Cost and fit**: image-only; adds a dense branch; KITTI boxes, no maps.

**Doubts**: abstract only; sloped roads are the failure mode of exactly this prior and are not discussed on the page.
