**What it does**: Joint 3D detection and semantic occupancy from multi-view 4D radar and cameras (OmniHD-Scenes for both, View-of-Delft for detection only). Losses (checked): L_det (focal + L1, weights 2.0 and 0.25) on box annotations; L_occ = CE + two MonoScene affinity terms on the 11-class semantic occupancy labels; plus two auxiliary terms: L_aux^seg (BCE + Dice) on foreground / background BEV masks made by projecting the detection GT boxes onto the BEV plane, and L_aux^occupied (BCE + Dice) on binary occupied / not-occupied labels derived from the semantic occupancy. The radar encoder (RadarPillarNet) is trained from scratch and gets no loss of its own; the coarse voxel query generator has none either.

**Results that matter**: no ablation of the auxiliary terms was extracted.

**Transferable technique**: the same pair our section 8.4 plans for the aux head (BCE + Dice on a binary occupied map derived from the dense labels, and on a footprint map from the boxes), attached to a shared BEV feature. Their binary "occupied" map is cut from the same semantic labels used for the main loss, which is what Option A would do with Occ3D.

**Cost and fit**: free (two small heads).

**Doubts**: the auxiliary heads sit on shared (fused) features, not on a radar-only branch; no evidence on what they add; the static labels in OmniHD-Scenes come from a different annotation effort than nuScenes.
