- **Question**: is the far-range gap of the fast chain a seed-coverage artefact of the inference lattice (+-45 m) inside an evaluated box of +-48 m, rather than a feature or verifier limit?
- **Statement** (falsifiable): adding seed candidates on the square ring at +-47.5 m (the edge of the positions the builder saw in training through the lattice jitter) at inference, with the same checkpoint and K0 / M, raises R@1 far of `recall` on VAL 873 by >= 0.010, with the gain inside the ring (max(|x|,|y|) >= 45 m) and no change inside it, at Q >= 0.735 and <= 14.3 vertices per 100 m (per-piece DP basis).
- **Mechanism**: the inference seed lattice is `arange(-45, 45, 5)`; training adds a uniform jitter of +-2.5 m, so training seeds reach +-47.5 m but inference seeds only reach +-45 (plus a learned snap of at most 3.5 m), while the metrics count ground truth up to +-48 m. The far bin (>= 40 m) has 30 % of its points (78,472 of 261,042) in the ring 45 - 48 m. Measured on the cached outputs (`step0_ring.txt`, VAL 873): `recall` R@1 inner far (40 m radius, max coordinate < 45) 0.801 against the control 0.818 (-0.017), but in the ring 0.702 against 0.795 (-0.093); `fast_recall` 0.756 / 0.662. The ring explains 0.093 x 0.30 = 0.028 of the 0.040 R@1-far gap of `recall` to the control (70 %). The control (E94 trace network, whose stem initialises the builder's: the same 42 cached BEV channels; to be confirmed from its card before the run) loses only 0.023 from inner to ring, so the features carry the ring; the builder's own tree lacks it already before admission (all valid nodes at tau 0: inner 0.894, ring 0.800) and only 5.5 % of the kept seeds lie at max(|x|,|y|) >= 45 m. More seeds in the inner square do not help (E217); seeds where none are offered should.
- **Prediction** (VAL 873, cached features, the same checkpoint, against the registry entry on the same frames): `recall` R@1 far 0.771 -> 0.783 (+0.012; plausible +0.005 .. +0.025), ring R@1 0.702 -> 0.74, inner R@1 far change within +-0.003, lanes found 0.767 -> 0.773 (+0.006), Q 0.740 +-0.004, P@1 far within -0.01, vert100_dp 12.82 -> <= 13.1, topo F1 +-0.004, ms per frame +0.1 or less (80 more candidates before the K0 selection). `fast_recall`: R@1 far 0.7275 -> 0.742.
- **Decision rule** (band 0.010 on R@1 far; deterministic arms: the paired scene bootstrap of `evaluate.py --compare` decides per checkpoint; `decide.py` with the four checkpoints as paired units): KEEP arm b (registry entries `*_ring` become the standard settings of the recall family, ledger row) if on `recall` dR@1 far >= +0.010 with the bootstrap interval excluding 0, the mean over the four checkpoints >= +0.008 with the same sign on >= 3 of 4, and the guards hold on every checkpoint: Q loss <= 0.005 and Q >= 0.735, vert100_dp <= 14.3, topo F1 loss <= 0.005, false_len_frac rise <= 0.005, inner R@1 far change within +-0.005 (the built-in null: inner seeds are identical). TIE (|dR@1 far| < 0.010 on the mean): the default stays (same cost, nothing gained). CUT: a guard fails on two or more checkpoints. Arm a (shifted grid) is read only if b passes, as the cheaper / simpler form; on a tie between a and b take b (inner seeds unchanged).
