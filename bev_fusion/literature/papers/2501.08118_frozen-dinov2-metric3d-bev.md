# 2501.08118 Revisiting BEV perception with frozen foundation models: DINOv2 and Metric3Dv2

**What it does**: Replaces the image encoder of Lift-Splat-Shoot by a frozen DINOv2 and feeds Metric3Dv2 monocular depth to LSS (as the depth source) and to Simple-BEV (as a pseudo-lidar cloud).

**Results that matter** (nuScenes vehicle segmentation IoU): LSS with DINOv2 + Metric3Dv2 exceeds the baseline by 7.4 IoU using half the training data and iterations; Simple-BEV with pseudo-lidar +3 IoU over camera-only.

**Transferable technique**: a frozen DINOv2 front end cuts the trainable parameters and the data need; a second frozen head for depth can drive the lift. Pair with RB21 (LoRA-adapted DINOv2, 2409.10228) which found gains in darkness and fog.

**Cost and fit**: DINOv2-small (ViT-S/14) is cheap on a 3060, but the patch-14 stride gives a coarse 1/14 feature map for a 0.5 m BEV; Metric3Dv2 is large and would be cached or dropped.

**Doubts**: workshop paper, absolute numbers and latency absent on the page; a ViT-S vs a ResNet-18 comparison under equal latency is not shown; ECCV supplement of another paper (LetsMap) warns a frozen DINOv2 may hurt a method whose native backbone carried geometry (search snippet, not read).
