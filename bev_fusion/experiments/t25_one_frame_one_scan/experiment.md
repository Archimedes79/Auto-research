## 1 Card
- **Question.** The network gets ONE camera frame and ONE radar sweep per step and carries its past only as feature state. - **What exists, and what is not isolated.** Camera: `cam_buffer recurrent` (t21 arm R). R against P4 on the stored cards (`runs/t21_rec_s0b/eval/card.json` against `runs/t21_pair4_s0/eval/card.json`, VAL 1000): `detf` mean mAP 0.4101 against 0.4154 (-0.0053), `det` mean mAP 0.3405 against 0.3453 (-0.0048), centre-field velocity EPE 1.1364 against 1.0911 (+0.045); against the window control R lost on `det` mean mAP -0.0060 [-0.0115, -0.0002], drivable IoU@0 -0.0037, vehicle seg IoU@0 -0.0059, road_divider IoU@1 -0.0126, vehicle `ave_skill_moving` -0.0113, pedestrian AP@1 -0.0114 (`experiments/t21_one_frame_temporal/compare_rec_vs_ctl.txt`).
