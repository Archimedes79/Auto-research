**What it does** [checked]: Monodepth2-style training on 576 x 320 image triplets (ResNet-18 depth and pose nets, 14.8 M parameters for the depth net, +1.1 M for an optional radar input branch). Loss L = L_p (photometric) + L_s (smoothness) + L_v + L_r. L_v is a weak velocity supervision on the pose (pose translation magnitude vs speed x dt from odometry) and gives metric scale without any LiDAR. L_r is an L1 loss to radar depth: radar points are accumulated and Doppler-compensated, projected into the image and spread with a bilateral weight; filtered per 2D box (keep points within beta = 2 m of the box's minimum radar depth, drop the upper 50 % and outer 20 % of a box, background points, overlaps), copied vertically into the lower third and the middle of a box because radar has no elevation; pseudo target = prediction shifted by the per-point offset to preserve the depth gradient (Eq. 4); lambda_3 = 0.2, switched on after epoch 30. LiDAR is used for EVALUATION only.

**Results that matter** [checked, Table 1, nuScenes day-clear val, single raw LiDAR scans, cap 80 m, AbsRel / RMSE / delta1 %]: Monodepth2 with LiDAR median scaling 0.1398 / 6.825 / 84.82; baseline with the velocity loss only 0.1315 / 6.520 / 85.71; R4Dyn-L (radar supervision) 0.1296 / 6.536 / 85.76; R4Dyn-LI (radar supervision + radar input) 0.1259 / 6.434 / 86.97. Cars only, AbsRel: 0.1983 (Monodepth2), 0.1862 (baseline), 0.1343 (L), 0.1250 (LI); the text reports -37 % for LI against Monodepth2. So the radar's gain is almost entirely on moving objects (the photometric loss fails there); the whole-image gain of L over the velocity-only baseline is 0.0019 AbsRel.
Related (Lo and Vandewalle, same page style, nuScenes front camera, 28,130 train / 6,019 val samples, S2D RGB ResNet-18, depth cap 80 m): trained with SPARSE LIDAR delta1 0.862 / AbsRel 0.126; trained with radar only: raw radar 0.292 / 0.707, radar extended to a 0.25-2 m column 0.602 / 0.288, radar with a learned association (RC-PDA >= 0.5) 0.605 / 0.302 (cap 50 m: 0.612 / 0.294). Radar supervision alone is about 70 % of the LiDAR delta1.

**Transferable technique**: (1) metric scale from ego speed needs no LiDAR and no radar (nuScenes has ego pose and CAN speed); (2) radar helps where self-supervision is blind, on MOVING objects; (3) the radar target must be filtered by objects (2D boxes) and given a height column; raw radar as a depth target is useless (0.292 delta1); (4) for our front-camera lift: LiDAR-free depth supervision is realistic for scale and for dynamic objects, NOT for a far, static, textureless road.

**Cost and fit**: ResNet-18 depth net, cheap; needs 2D boxes for the radar filter (from GT in training); no code on the page. It trains a depth network, not a BEV depth distribution; connecting it to a depth-bin lift (BEVDepth-style) needs a conversion to bins.

**Doubts**: depth quality is whole-image AbsRel at <= 80 m, dominated by near pixels; no result at 40-60 m, which is our problem; day-clear subset only; the radar gain on all pixels is within what a seed could produce (0.1296 vs 0.1315); self-supervised nuScenes depth is generally weak (the multi-camera SurroundDepth paper 2204.03636 reports AbsRel 0.245 median-scaled, delta1 0.719; read through a summariser, nuScenes six cameras, not this setup).
