**What it does** [checked]: camera-only perception (ego-aligned accumulation before the BEV transform), prediction and planning on one BEV. Prediction: two GRU pathways, one fed the history features, one fed latent samples (diagonal Gaussians, sampled in training, mean at inference), mixed by a learned 2-channel conv gate and applied recursively over H future steps; heads decode future instance segmentation (centerness, offset, future flow), semantic maps and also PAST frames. Horizon 1.0 s past + 2.0 s future (3 past, 4 future frames at 2 Hz, 7 frames per sample). Loss: top-k cross-entropy for segmentation, L2 centerness, L1 offset and flow; "loss in future timestamps discounted exponentially" (factor not given); L = L_per + alpha L_pre + beta L_pla with LEARNABLE alpha, beta. Training: Adam, constant lr 2e-4, 20 epochs, mixed precision, 4 V100, joint end to end (no staging).

**FIERY's loss recipe** [checked, ar5iv 2104.10490]: future discount gamma = 0.95 per step (steps are 0.5 s on nuScenes), no ablation of gamma; segmentation top-k CE on the hardest 25 % of pixels; Kendall uncertainty weights on segmentation, centerness, offset, flow; KL between future and present distributions with weight 100; Adam constant lr 3e-4, batch 12, 20 epochs, mixed precision, 4 x V100-32GB, about one day. No detach mentioned. Ablation (IoU short / long range, VPQ short / long, 2 s): no temporal context 51.7 / 32.6, 40.3 / 24.1; no ego-motion transform 53.0 / 33.8, 41.7 / 24.6; no unrolling 55.4 / 34.9, 44.2 / 26.2; no future flow 58.0 / 36.7, 44.6 / 26.9; full 59.4 / 36.7, 50.2 / 29.9.

**Results that matter** [checked, ST-P3 Table 5, nuScenes val vehicles]: IoU / PQ 37.09 / 28.63 (ego-aligned accumulation + depth), + dual pathway 38.16 / 31.35, + loss for all timestamps 38.63 / 31.72. The all-timestamps loss alone is +0.47 IoU, +0.37 PQ (single run).

**Transferable technique**: (1) the discount is MILD in both papers (0.95 per 0.5 s step), our proposal in plan 8.4 is w(+-0.5) = 0.5, w(+-1.0) = 0.25 (a factor 0.5 per step): at 0.95 the dt != 0 terms carry about as much total weight as dt = 0 and dominate the gradient, at 0.5 they are a regulariser; the right value is an arm (0.5 vs 0.9), not a guess; (2) supervising every intermediate step gives a small but real gain over only the last step; (3) in FIERY each ablation removes one part from the full model: without the ego-motion transform IoU falls by 6.4 (short range) / 2.9 (long), without any temporal context by 7.7 / 4.1 (from 59.4 / 36.7), so at 2 s the correct ego warp is most of the temporal gain.

**Cost and fit**: nothing to port; the horizon (2 s) is longer than our +-1.0 s.

**Doubts**: no gamma ablation in either paper; single runs; prediction metrics at 2 Hz keyframes only; different task (instance prediction) from map layers and boxes at +-0.5 s.
