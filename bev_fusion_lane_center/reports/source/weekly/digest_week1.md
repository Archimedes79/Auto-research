## 0. Task and setup

**Predicted.** From six cameras + radar sweeps only (no lidar read), per nuScenes keyframe: a lane-centerline graph (polylines + successor edges with explicit splits/merges) in the ego BEV frame (rear axle at CAM_FRONT time, x right, y forward, +-50 m, 200 x 200 cells of 0.5 m); assigned to a map graph that carries the navigation goal (route over lanes); the goal transferred to the extracted lanes and handed to a planner with trust signals; end-to-end module output, no manual refinement (only the map input may be hand-edited) [TASK p.1; ST l.48]. Lidar experiments E01-E14 were deleted on 2026-10-13 and are not part of the project [HIST].

**Data/splits.** nuScenes first three blobs, Boston + Singapore, day and dry only (no night/rain scene in the subset) [L9 s9.1]. Scene-disjoint: TRAIN 212 scenes / 8,414 samples, VAL 22 / 873, TEST 21 / 833 (809 with a route label; blur, extrinsics, radar-off exist for every 2nd TEST sample = 409) [TASK; L9 s7.1]. 212+22+21 = 255 scenes [derived]; TASK/L9 say "323 scenes", which ST l.110 records as wrong ("new texts use 255"). 8,414+873+833 = 10,120 samples [derived]. TRAIN_EVAL = every 8th TRAIN sample (1,052), seen by the head [PE E28]. VAL = all decisions, TEST = reporting. splits.json 'test' rows are not contiguous (ST l.50). Loop 1 used another protocol: Mini (323 train / 81 val samples), then 10,120 samples = 8,414 train / 1,706 validation (= 873+833 by count), NO test set; E19 tuned on every 2nd validation sample, E26 reports on the 853 odd ones [S13 l.5; R19; R26]. "TEST used once" holds only in spirit: several finalist/ablation TEST runs exist and loop-5 monitors/thresholds were fitted on TEST frames (part 4).

**Foundation.** Frozen camera-radar BEV net; only small heads are trained. Loop 1: CRN (ICCV 2023, detection-pretrained; 128 ch at 128x128 cells of 0.8 m, PCA 32 from 300 train samples). Loops 2-9: GaussianCaR (ICRA 2026, map-segmentation-pretrained; decoder pre-head feature PCA 32 + 7 map-layer logits = 39 ch, 200x200 at 0.5 m; research-only rasterizer licence); about 0.4 s per frame on the RTX 3060 [L9 s4 E27, E43, s9.1].

**Metrics (as used).** Point F1@1 m (loops 1-5 headline): precision/recall of dense 0.5 m points, correct if nearest point of the other set is within 1 m [R24]; 1:1 F1 penalises duplicates. Lanes found = share of GT lane pieces with >= 80 % of points within 1 m; false length = predicted length > 1.5 m from any GT point; split/merge F1@3/6 m = location match of nodes with >= 2 successors/predecessors; route F = APLS-like comparison of directed shortest-path lengths between sampled reachable pairs (<= 40 m); direction accuracy; stability = F1@1 m between previous graph (warped) and current graph [PE E31 l.13, l.64; L9 s4 E41]. **Route success** (loop 5+): coverage (share of GT route 1 m points within 1.5 m of a point flagged on-route) AND precision (share of flagged points within 1.5 m of GT route) both >= 0.8 [L9 s1 glossary; PE E59 l.12]; route label = lane sequence actually driven in the next <= 6 s (not a router output). Lane F1 (loop 6+) = F1 of lane centre-line points (0.5 m) matched within 1 m; loop 8+ on kept 6 m edges with 1 m vertex spacing (clean 0.852 vs 0.856 at 0.5 m) [L9 s7.14]. Loop-5 planner proxy: lateral error of a route polyline followed at true speed, mean after 1/2/3 s (E62); AUROC of monitors with label "graph F1 < 0.6" (E64). Loop 8+: false lane length/frame, driven-path availability (kept graph covers >= 80 % of driven path within 40 m), median pose error of localisation, reference-path deviation at 10/20/30 m, route-flagged length with wrong/no map (abstention). "Map error sigma m" = rigid shift sigma per axis + yaw 2 sigma deg; "cell" = one evaluation condition [L9 s1]. Noise: scene-level intervals about +-0.035 F1, +-0.03-0.05 success; dense-head seed SD 0.0025 VAL / 0.0006 TEST (E55); DEV score SD 0.0014 (E73), 0.0011 (E78); route-head seed spread later found up to 0.03 (ST l.114).

**Compute.** One RTX 3060 12 GB, 24 GB RAM, WSL2; 2-3 GPU jobs max, OOM from dataloader workers killed extractions repeatedly [ST l.52]. Latency: loop-3/4 chain without foundation <= 41 ms (dense head 7.9 + refiner 22.2 ms GPU; tracer 6.1 + post 5.2 ms CPU) [L9 s4 E43]; loop-8 graph pipeline 0.27 s/frame on one CPU thread (tracer 17, localisation 92, edge features 137, 3 assignment nets 10, extension 0.3, ref. path 1, tracks 10 ms) + about 5 ms dense head (loop-4 figure, not re-measured) [L9 s8.2].

## 2. Story of the week

**How the work evolved.** (1) Loop 1: a detection-pretrained CRN foundation + dense distance-field head reached F1@1 m 0.670 on a validation set; the field, not the line forming, was the limit. (2) Loops 2-3: swapping to the map-pretrained GaussianCaR gave +0.243 (0.624 -> 0.867 VAL); a **dense grid head** (heat map + direction) with a rule-based tracer beat a **sparse** DETR-style extractor by 0.06 F1 / 0.18 route F; a cascade refiner, temporal fusion and a map prior added little to a lot (map prior +0.075 with exact pose, fragile without alignment). The seen-to-unseen scene shift capped everything (0.932 -> 0.820). (3) Loop 4: the topology bottleneck was attacked with learned split/merge key points (node F1 x1.6) and Bezier connectors (x2.1/1.7), at a route-F cost; foundation read-out variants failed. (4) Loop 5 reframed the task around the planner: route transfer by registration (no learned parameter, 0.926), a route-conditioned end-to-end head (0.849), and robustness: perception fails silently, so layered monitors/hold/carry-forward. (5) Loop 6: systematic fine-tuning with a clean DEV protocol; a map-trust auxiliary task + soups gave 0.920. (6) Loop 7: a simulated ADASIS-like **SD map** replaced the lane map; the pixel-level SD head reached 0.733 (geometric 0.46); training-free temporal averaging helped lanes. (7) Loop 8 inverted the order: **perception first, assignment after** on 6 m edges (0.88) with extension, tracks and a planner interface; a protocol bug (dropped empty-graph rows) was found and corrected. (8) Loop 9 made the assignment robust to map faults (per-road bias, abstention).

**Ten most impactful findings** (all numbers TEST unless noted).
1. Foundation choice dominates: CRN -> GaussianCaR +0.243 VAL / +0.237 TEST F1@1 m, far above any decoder/loss change (+0.09..0.12 for U-Net vs FCN) [E27, E17].
2. The ceiling is a seen-to-unseen scene shift of the frozen foundation: 0.932 own scenes -> 0.899 held-out train -> 0.851 VAL -> 0.820 TEST (fold heads); regularisation, mirroring, ensembling, wider read-out, instance norm, a second foundation do not remove it [E30, E49-E52].
3. Dense grid + tracer beats sparse-only (F1 0.879 vs 0.821 VAL; route F 0.765 vs 0.587); refiner/links/temporal are small (+0.001..0.007 F1) [E31, E32, E39, E41-E43].
4. Learned split/merge key points double node quality at a small route-F cost: split/merge F1@6 m 0.342/0.380 -> 0.552/0.607 (CI excl. 0), connectors 0.714/0.637 [E44, E53, E54].
5. A map is only usable after registration: unaligned 1 m error is worse than no map (0.830 < 0.887); geometric transfer after registration route success 0.923/0.883/0.782 vs 0.640/0.305/0.153 [E40, E59].
6. Perception fails silently: graph F1 0.867 -> 0.259 night, 0.433 blur, 0.648 three cameras off; head-side training/augmentation cannot restore lost information (extrinsics/radar +0.00); layered cover (monitor AUROC 0.953, hold 0.39 vs 0.60 m, carry-forward night 0.29 -> 0.69) [E57, E64, E65, E67, E68].
7. The best learned head came from an auxiliary "is this map usable?" task + weight averaging: exact-map route success 0.849 -> 0.920 [E73].
8. A navigation (SD) map carries road and branch, not lane: geometric assignment 0.46, pixel head 0.733; assigning after perception on 6 m edges gives 0.881/0.879/0.868/0.839 (oracle whole pieces only 0.454, 6 m edges 0.982) [E77, E78, E83].
9. The cheap temporal tools work only with good odometry: warped-lane average +0.042 lane F1, graph tracks 0.645 -> 0.755, learned memory +0.00-0.005; route attributes must be re-decided per frame (averaging 0.570 -> 0.400) [E79, E80, E85].
10. Train against the faults you fear: per-road bias 2 m 0.663 -> 0.847, route-flagged length with wrong map 60.7 -> 4.2 m and no map 60.1 -> 0 m; the SD-route extension returns range (night 0.276 -> 0.450, three cameras off 0.330 -> 0.633); reference path 0.308 m vs SD centre line 0.949 m at 20 m [E87, E84, E86].

**Best system at the end of week 1** (L9 s8.1): frozen GaussianCaR + map-free dense head (e37_final) -> tracer -> loose graph cut into ~6 m edges -> lane-template localisation of the SD map -> 3-seed ensemble of the 0.27 M assignment network (loop-9 recipe) giving keep/route per edge -> SD-route extension -> graph tracks -> planner hand-over (output graph with attributes, reference path V(s)+o(s), map-agreement score). Headline: route success 0.876/0.879/0.876/0.858 at map error 0/1/2/3 m (with extension at 2 m clean 0.890), lane F1 0.856, per-road bias 2 m 0.847, abstention 4.2 m / 0 m, reference path 0.308 m at 20 m, 0.27 s/frame CPU (unoptimised). Cross-check branches: geometric chain (registered map route, monitors, hold/restart/verify), E2E lane-map head v2 (0.935/0.920), pixel SD head (0.733), temporal average.

**Biggest open issues at end of week 1** (L9 s9.1, s7.15, s8.4). (1) Everything adverse and the SD map are synthetic and the SD route equals the driven route (map circularity); real navigation map and measured per-road bias not available (needs OSM download, not done). (2) Perception under sensor loss: the graph pipeline's lane F1 is below the pixel head (night 0.255 vs 0.507), the frozen foundation cannot be changed, no real night/rain. (3) Abstention weak under failure (4-25 m of route with a wrong map; gate AUROC 0.618 at night). (4) Localisation under failure: single-frame registration fails at night; held correction only helps with trustworthy odometry (hurts at 3 deg/sqrt(s)+5 %). (5) The chain (tracer, grid-search registration, tracks) is not differentiable; joint training with a planner loss gave no gain: "end-to-end" = the output graph is a differentiable interface only. (6) 21 TEST scenes, mostly single seeds, intervals +-0.03; monitors/thresholds of loop 5 are TEST-fitted; open loop only. (7) Lane count/index weak beyond one lane. (8) Latency 0.27 s unoptimised.
