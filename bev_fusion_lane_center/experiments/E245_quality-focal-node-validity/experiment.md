## 1. Card (written in P2, checked by ar-referee)
| | |

- **Statement** (falsifiable): Training the child validity nu of the builder with the quality-focal loss `|y - p|^beta BCE(p, y)` (beta 2) on the continuous target `y = exp(-d^2 / (2 sigma^2)) x chord quality` (sigma 0.5 m) instead of the hard label (`d < 0.8 m and chord within 0.6 m of a lane`) lifts R@1 beyond 40 m of the 4 u fine-tune of j1 by at least 0.03 over the equal-budget plain fine-tune j1eq, with Q at least 0.010 above it, and brings the expected calibration error of the node validity from 0.12 to at most 0.06 without a post-hoc fit.
- **Mechanism** (why it should work):
  1. The present target is a threshold: in `level_losses`, `tgt_c = (dist < 0.8) & chord_ok` (chord samples within 0.6 m of a lane) and `l_val = bce_p(nu, tgt_c)`; the ray verification target is `gd < 0.6 m and direction agrees`. The metric accepts 1.0 m (R@1, F1@1). Beyond 40 m the features localise a lane worse, so a child 0.8 - 1.0 m off, which the metric counts as a hit, is trained as a negative; the head learns a bimodal validity (E221: ECE 0.121 in the middle bins, 0.021 after an isotonic fit) and the admission threshold cuts hits that the target called misses. A graded target says "0.7 good, 0.2 marginal" and the validity ranks instead of splitting.
  2. The QFL form (GFL paper, equation of section 2.2 of the deep note) has its minimum at p = y exactly (toy test 1 of section 6 of the deep note), while our present hard-label focal-style losses are biased towards 0.5 (derivative -0.912 at q = 0.25, section 5.1 of the deep note). beta = 2 concentrates the gradient on the examples whose predicted quality is off, which is the ranking gain of the paper (beta = 2 vs beta = 0: 2.3 AP in Table 1(c)); beta = 0 is the proper scoring rule (calibration); the card tests both (arm a, arm b) because the deep note shows that ranking and calibration pull in different directions at range and that QFL is lower on small objects (the nearest analogue to far lanes) than an IoU branch.
  3. It differs from E219 in kind: no recall weight, no change of the graph terms; the pi that the assembly thresholds is a product / minimum of these nu (pi_mode min), so a better-ranked nu changes which far nodes pass tau at an unchanged vertex budget.
- **Prediction** (VAL 873 frames, fork pruning 0.4, tau as the control of E244, against arm c = j1eq / j1eqs1): Q +0.012, R@1_far +0.03 (0.688 -> 0.72), lanes_found +0.025, ECE of the node validity 0.12 -> 0.06 (arm a), 0.03 (arm b), topo_F1 within +-0.005, J not lower, vert100 within +-0.5, ms per frame unchanged. Arm b (beta 0): ECE best, Q gain smaller than a (+0.005).
- **Decision rule** (band 0.010 on Q; 2 seeds paired; paired scene bootstrap): KEEP if the clauses of section 0 hold, both seeds on the same side, bootstrap interval of Q excludes 0. The headline clause is `max(min_effect, band) = 0.010` by the tool, so the real bar is Q +0.010; a Q gain between 0.005 and 0.010 with R@1_far >= +0.03 is reported as TIE (cheaper = hard labels) and as a finding for A8 if ECE falls by >= 0.04 (calibration without a post-hoc fit is a result for the confidence, not a promotion). CUT if Q falls below c - 0.010. INCONCLUSIVE: seed 2 for a and c.
- **Test design**: arms a (2 seeds), b (1 seed), c (existing runs, reused; the plain 4 u fine-tune at hard labels; equal budget by construction), r (reference). Controls: null j1 (`fast`); strong simple baseline for the calibration claim = isotonic recalibration of the validity of c on the first half of VAL, applied to the second half (E221 gives ECE 0.021): a is judged on ECE without a post-hoc fit, and if c + isotonic reaches the same Q and R@1_far (an inference-time fit, zero training), the card reports it and the cheaper arm is c + isotonic (that is the honest alternative to the whole card). Layer 0: toy test of the deep note (argmin at p = y for y in {0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8} within the grid step; QFL(p = y) = 0; y = 1 reproduces `(1-p)^beta (-log p)`; beta 0 equals soft BCE; numpy reference to 1e-6). Layer 1 diagnostic (0.05 GPU h, cached raw nodes of VAL for j1 and j1eq): among far (> 40 m) nodes whose position is within 1.0 m of a ground-truth lane, the share with pi < tau whose distance lies in (0.6, 1.0] m. If this share is below 20 %, the tolerance mismatch is not what rejects hits and the card is killed. ECE: the E221 script (`experiments/E221_calibration-of-fork-heat-and-node-validity/code/p18_validity_calibration.py`: 10 bins, hit = within 1 m of a lane) moved into `evaluation/` as a metric (`ece` is already in decide.LOWER) with the range-binned form; no private metric.
- **Cost** (GPU h / coding days / risk): 1.25 GPU h (3 training runs of 0.4 h with the evaluation, 0.05 h diagnostic); the old control runs are reused (their cards are made in the shared set-up of E244, 0.15 h, not counted twice). Coding 0.5 day: the soft target needs `dist` and the chord distances, both already computed in `level_losses`; plus the ECE metric 0.25 day. Risk medium: with beta 2 the sigma of the target and the normalisation (the paper divides by the number of positives; here the weighted mean over `wm`) change the effective weight of the term, so the loss multiplier `val` may need a factor 2; QFL is not a proper scoring rule (the deep note), so ECE could be worse than the hard-label baseline for beta 2.
- **Kill criterion**: layer-0 toy test fails; the layer-1 share < 20 %; after 1 u the val250 Q is more than 0.02 below the start j1 or the validity collapses (mean pi of valid nodes < 0.2 or > 0.95); after both seeds of a, Q < c - 0.010 or ECE of a worse than c.
- **Differentiable path**: yes. Pixel-free, graph-based head (child validity of the tree nodes, a survival product along the ray); the target is built from detached distances (as now), the loss is a smooth function of nu; no argmax, no matching, no rule step added; gradient reaches the verification head and the survival product.

To build: loss branch in `level_losses`, spec test, ECE in `evaluation/`, registry entries `fast_qfl`, `fast_qfl_s1`, `fast_softbce` (set-up cards of the control shared with E244). New technique **K192** "quality-focal soft-target validity loss (QFL-type, continuous distance x chord target) for the node validity of the sparse graph", source 2006.04388 (idea, re-implemented from the equation), to be added to `literature/register.md` before the first run.
