## 1. Card text
- **Statement:** eight numbers of B's own maps along the chord of a candidate pair, added to the attention bias, raise the champion's topo F1 by >= +0.005 and its junction F1 by >= +0.010 over the same head on the same data path without them.
- **Mechanism, and the measured failure it attacks:** the delivered long (junction) links of the champion are mostly wrong: tp 17 - 18 %, a duplicate of a true link 13 %, a WRONG PAIRING of two real nodes 34 - 36 %, an end near no GT node 33 - 36 % (CPU anatomy on both heads, 12.5k delivered against 7.7k GT long links; E374 / E376 notes); the head's own type factor and B's saturated uout / uin evidence do not separate true from wrong (median 0.98 everywhere); only 28 - 29 % of the GT long links are delivered exactly; the long-link AP is 0.26 - 0.29. The head ranks a pair by the two tokens' states and 13 geometric numbers and never looks at what lies BETWEEN the tokens. The previous winner did: E331 removed the six evidence numbers from the E312 pair stage and lost 0.012 - 0.023 topo F1 on the differentiable read-out ('it does not pay', recorded in the E331 card); E287 had found no gain from them over the order features on GT nodes, the two results disagree and this card is the third look, on the NEW head, with maps that exist in the frozen pyramid (`centre1` 12 channels incl. the six event heats, `lines05`, `road2`: `grid/pyramid.py`). A true junction link runs through intersection area and along the centre-lane map's continuation; a wrong pairing cuts across lane dividers or road boundary or leaves the road.
- **Why not closed:** `closed_questions.md` has no row on chord evidence; E287 (TIE on GT nodes) and E331 (removal cost 0.012 - 0.023) are the evidence in the E312 stage, a different head; the new mechanism is that the champion's tokens are noisy predicted nodes where the maps' continuation is the only place the geometry-free evidence lives.
- **E2E:** yes: bilinear sampling of frozen maps is differentiable and carries no hard step; S4 (the node head trainable) would let the gradient reach B' through the maps later.
- **Cost:** needs the live path (the pyramid of the frozen B' per batch: about 13 ms per frame, 0.1 s per batch of 8; S4's `lane_live_tok.LiveSource`, tested): the step is about 0.3 s, 12000 steps = 1 h per head; four runs (z, k, two seeds) = 4 GPU h, dumps 5 min each, CPU sweeps 25 min each; budget 4.5 GPU h; inference: +5 samples x 8 maps on the allowed pairs of the 512 tokens (about 40 k pairs x 40 gathers per frame, estimated below 0.5 ms) and the pyramid maps are already computed by B' (they are not extra); the maps stay inputs the system computes anyway (B's pyramid is the system's first stage). Not measured: the pair count and the sampling time (the launch gate measures them).
- **Prediction (+0.004 on topo F1, window -0.003 to +0.012, junction F1 +0.01 window -0.01 to +0.04):** E331's removal cost (0.012 - 0.023 in the E312 stage, on a head whose evidence also replaced the maps head's own p) bounds what a re-added evidence can give; the token head already has the tokens' own node-head features (`tok`, 64 channels sampled at the node), which carry part of it. **Outcome odds: KEEP about 20 %, TIE about 65 %, CUT about 15 %.**
