Notes

- Item 11 (RAVE): two searches found no registration / fine-matching method of that name; results were unrelated (Australian vehicle register) or other lane-graph papers (e.g. LaneGraphNet, arXiv 2105.00195, not opened). Do not cite RAVE unless the user supplies a source.
- Item 2: the arXiv Comments say v2 is the published version. The paper is titled "Gaussian Splatting for Efficient Camera-Radar Fusion", not "camera-radar fusion with Gaussians for BEV map segmentation"; the abstract (via search) describes BEV segmentation.
- Item 8: CMX was accepted to IEEE T-ITS (a journal), not a conference; the arXiv page gives no year, so cite as arXiv 2022 / T-ITS.
- Item 19: MambaMap title on the page is "Online Vectorized HD Map Construction using State Space Model"; arXiv id 2507.20224 is correct as reported.
- Item 20: title on the page is "OpenLane-V2: A Topology Reasoning Benchmark for Unified 3D HD Mapping", not "Topology Reasoning for Driving Scenes" (that is an earlier/alternate name; not opened).
- Item 24: H-DETR is titled "DETRs with Hybrid Matching" (CVPR 2023).
- Item 21: both FlowNet and PWC-Net are listed; PWC-Net is the one with a cost volume.
- Item 26: the L0 paper is the one that introduces the hard-concrete distribution (title does not say "hard concrete").
- Venues marked "no venue on page" (ConvNeXt V2, DPT, Hosang, MetaBEV, STN, Pointer Nets, Mamba, MambaMap, FlowNet, sMCL, sparsemax, Scheduled Sampling) are not confirmed by the arXiv page; look up the proceedings page if a venue is to be cited. Only the arXiv year shown is certain.
- Item 28: no page opened by design; the Bishop details are from memory.
