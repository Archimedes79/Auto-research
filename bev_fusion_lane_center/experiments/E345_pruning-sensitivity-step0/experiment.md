## 1. Question, what exists, what is new
The system (E312 / E324 s, `JEndModel`): the frozen pyramid of B (2.097 M parameters, 4.677 GMACs, 4.55 ms), the junction maps head (0.444 M, 3.256 GMACs, 2.82 ms), the pair head (0.090 M, 0.022 GMACs, 9.72 ms, host-bound); E312 2.632 M and 7.955 GMACs, forward 17.09 ms (`temp/jr/timing_junction.json`, 2026-11-13); E324 s 2.741 M: B's node head (0.099 M) and the attachment (0.0096 M) sit inside the pair model, the node head reads the same pyramid features P as the maps head (`jends_train.py:42-46`), so **the pyramid points of E324 s ablate that in-model node head and the attachment candidates too** (reported as "junction view + attachment candidates"; B's delivered graph, the stored B dump, reads the intact pyramid). Nobody has measured how much each channel group, residual block and attention head matters; the sizes D99 asks for are guesses. The table is the Pareto side; the step-1 cards (sparsity term, joint fine-tuning, D99 point 3, rule of D84 point 4) take their two or three sizes from it. The `head05`, `m05` and paint-evidence points are the no-retrain preview of E341 (running): E332 found the evidence worth 0.012 - 0.023 unsnapped after retraining, so they are not expected to be FREE.
Honest limits: a removal by mean replacement is not a physical removal (GroupNorm(8) regroups after narrowing, interactions are untested, the stale calibration mean is not re-fitted), so a unit that is FREE here is LIKELY free in step 1 and a costly one may be recoverable: the table ranks, it does not bound. The pyramid is B's shared pyramid and 59 % of the 7.955 GMACs: its points are a junction view only and step 1 cannot deliver them (a pruned shared pyramid is a joint change of B and the junction heads: named gap); the table keeps two curves, heads-only (feeds step 1) and the pyramid view (reported). The six sweep numbers stand in for D99's "keypoint F1". The ms column is the idle-GPU profile of E342 part B for the blocks, and inside a conv block an ESTIMATE (block ms x MAC share), labelled.

## 2. Design: the probe, the units, the ablation, the controls
**Probe** (`evaluation/jprune_probe.py` with a selftest and a README row, straight into `evaluation/` because the card names it as how the numbers are made). It loads a stored checkpoint with `jends_eval.load_model` and runs the model over the 873 VAL frames from ONE cache of the loader's own batches: `LD.QData.DEFAULT_JUNCTION = True`, `LD.loader("val_clean", bs=8, workers=2, shuffle=False, map_mode="error", pixels=False)` as `jends_eval.py:38-39` (109 batches of 8 and one of 1, the same order), inputs as the loader yields them (fp16, then `.float()` as the dump does: lossless), the batch GT that `QEN.end_counts` reads (ent, ext, ent_m, ext_m, pairs) and tokens / rows: 873 x 39 x 200 x 200 x 2 B = 2.72 GB plus GT, about 4 GB of host RAM. `dump_frames` is FACTORED OUT of `jends_eval.dump` (shared code: `jends_eval.dump` calls it; a critic pass; a test of equality against the stored dump of the null; a copy would drift, E318 and E320 added dump fields). The probe never calls `jends_eval.dump`'s writing part (it writes the stored `ends_val.pkl` and `ends_head.json` unconditionally). The stored `ends_head.json` is at p >= 0.5; the sweep computes its own counts at 0.1, **per frame**, so any subset of scenes can be summed offline.
**Scoring a point** needs a folder per point: `runs/e345_p<NNN>_<model>/` holding a SYMLINK to the unmodified `model.pt` (no 10 MB copies; the folder is derived data, recorded by hashes of the point's `ends_val.pkl`, marked for `package.py`) and the point's `ends_val.pkl`, scored by the stored scorer (`jends_eval.py score --snap --thr 0.1`; E324 s also `--attach --snap --snap-nogeom --thr 0.1`; E312 also unsnapped `score --thr 0.1`) with a tag `e345<NNN>` (`[a-z0-9]+`, never overwritten); the stored runs' folders get no new file. The scorer writes `counts.pkl` with the per-frame counts and the scene list (`qends_eval.py:314`): the two halves (below) are summed from them.
**The two halves:** the 22 VAL scenes in sorted order; the SELECTION half = the scenes with even index in that order (11 scenes, 435 frames), the JUDGEMENT half = the odd index (11 scenes, 438 frames). (Parity of the scene id would give 21 scenes against 1: every `counts.pkl` lists ids 1, 3, 5, ..., 41, 42.) The probe keeps the six dump-level counts per frame. FREE classes, the largest FREE fraction of every unit and the order of the cumulative curve are decided from the SELECTION half only; v and the sizes are judged on the JUDGEMENT half (offline, `graph_numbers` of the subset, as `jdiag.py:187`); the full-VAL numbers of the scored points are reported next to them.
**Sweep metrics (per frame, from the same dump):** `ends_mean6` = the mean of the six dump-level numbers (entry / exit / pair recall and precision at p >= 0.1: the stand-in for keypoint F1); the matched-keypoint position error and heading error (the definition of `jdiag.py kperr`, and for E324 s the attached-end error); and the pair average precision of the logits, computed IN the probe (`jpair_pr.py` has no AP function and reads the stored dump from disk): labels from the pairs of `QEN.match_keypoints` as in `end_counts` (`qendnet.py:163-198`), keypoint threshold 0.1, exact precision at recall 0.5 by the cumulative sort of `precision_at_recall_exact`. A unit is called sweep-FREE only when `ends_mean6` AND the pair AP AND the position error all stay within their bands (0.003 / 0.003 / 0.05 m). The handle-scale head changes none of these numbers (it shapes the curves): its points are scored in full directly and its fraction in v is the largest f whose FULL class is FREE on the selection half, never decided by the sweep.
**Importance and the ablation** (no retraining, no VAL data in the ranking): s_c = mean over 201 TRAIN frames (`Subset` of the TRAIN loader with stride 42, `map_mode="error"`, `pixels=False`, `DEFAULT_JUNCTION = True`) and over positions of |a_c - mean a_c| (tokens, pair MLP and scale MLP: only valid keypoints and allowed pairs); each tensor uses its OWN per-channel calibration mean. The ablation replaces the lowest-s_c fraction f of a unit's output channels by their calibration mean (f in {0.25, 0.50, 0.75}); the variance is removed and the normalisation after it sees that (part of what is measured). Per unit type:
- **hidden width** of a block: a forward hook on the activation inside it (`ResDil.f.2`, `m1.2`, `jm.m05.2` = the post-GroupNorm ReLU; `ffn.1`, `pair.1`, `pair.3`, `scale.1` = GELU / activation; channels in dim 1 for convs, dim -1 for Linear);
- **residual block skipped** (`ResDil`, `SetBlock`): output := input (exact for `ResDil`: every block input is post-ReLU and the block is `relu(x + f(x))`);
- **one attention head** of a `SetBlock` (4 heads of 16 channels): the `out_proj` hook never fires in torch 2.8 (tested), so the edit is on a deepcopy: zero the head's V rows `in_proj_weight[2d + h*hd : 2d + (h+1)*hd]` and set the same slice of `in_proj_bias` to the head output's calibration mean; the calibration mean per head needs a manual per-head attention forward fed by a forward pre-hook on `att` (it captures h and the key padding mask); checked against a manual per-head replacement to 1e-7;
- **maps stream width** (one unit): the SAME lowest-s_c channel set (ranked on `jm.p2`'s output) replaced at `p2`'s output and at every `ResDil` block output, each tensor with its own calibration mean; f in {0.25, 0.50};
- **pyramid stage** (stem, e1, e2, e3, d2, d1, d0) and the **0.5 m pixel stem** (`pyr.s05` + `pyr.m05`: two rankings, one unit, named apart from `jm.m05`): the stage's output channels, junction view;
- **head output channel** (8 of `head1`, 2 of `head05`): the channel's calibration mean logit;
- **paint evidence inputs** (one unit): columns 145 and 146 of the 152-wide input of `je.pair.0` (c0 = 2d + NG = 141, evidence columns 4 and 5; the column test of `jends.py`'s selftest, l.71-73, confirms them) overwritten by their calibration means (a pre-hook on `je.pair.0`, and on `je.scale.0` where it reads them).
**Units and points (84):** pyramid 8 units x 3 fractions = 24; maps head: stream 2, `ResDil` hidden 4 x 3 = 12, `ResDil` skip 4, `m1` hidden 3, `jm.m05` hidden 3, `head1` 8, `head05` 2, paint inputs 1 = 35; pair head: `SetBlock` skip 2, attention heads 8, ffn hidden 2 x 3 = 6, pair MLP hidden 2 x 3 = 6, handle-scale hidden 3 = 25. 45 units (8 + 22 + 15), 20 of them with a fraction ladder. `je.kp` and the attachment are not units (named gap).
**Saved cost of a SET of removals** (`saved_macs(set)` in the probe; savings multiply, not add): the MACs of the network with the combined mask minus the MACs of the intact one, every conv / linear counted with its KEPT input and output channels (a channel is kept when no removal in the set drops it); a skipped block drops its hidden width entirely (an overlapping stream or hidden-width removal is not counted twice); the pyramid is counted analytically (physical narrowing breaks `GroupNorm(8)` for 36 and 108 channels); the test against `FlopCounterMode` on physically narrowed copies uses units of the heads whose widths stay divisible by 8 (64 -> 48 / 32 / 16, 32 -> 24 / 16 / 8), one single and one COMBINED case (stream + a hidden width + a skipped block); saved parameters likewise; units with no MACs (the pair head: 0.022 GMACs, not none) carry parameters too.
**Controls (written before the run):** (1) NULL = point 0 run twice on the GPU: against the stored cards within topo F1 2e-4 and any real-valued number 3e-3 (what E321 measured CPU against GPU: loose for GPU against GPU), and the spread of the two nulls capped at topo F1 2e-4 / any number 3e-3 too: above the cap the probe sets deterministic algorithms (`cudnn.deterministic`, `use_deterministic_algorithms`) and reruns; still above = fix the probe (kill); stored cards: E312 snapped and unsnapped; E324 s snapped, `snap_nogeom` and PRIMARY (`snap_nogeom_attach`); there is no stored unsnapped card of E324 s. (2) PLUMBING: an identity hook (writes the values back) reproduces the null to 0; a DEAD-HOOK assert on every point (the hook fired once per batch, or the edited tensor differs from the null's, the replaced channels are constant) unless the chosen set has s_c = 0; a ZEROED COPY (conv rows zeroed, GroupNorm weight 0 and bias constant, so those channels are constant by construction): mean replacement of them changes nothing (<= 1e-6 in the outputs). (3) RANDOM removal at f = 0.50 for each of the 20 ladder units, 3 fixed draws (seeds 0 / 1 / 2), quantity = the sweep loss `ends_mean6` over the whole VAL, per model-seed; a unit is TIED when both losses are 0 to 1e-6 and is not counted; 60 passes. (4) REVERSE rank at f = 0.50 (the MOST important half removed; 20 passes): must lose more than the ranked removal in more than 90 % of the non-tied units (the random control alone is weak: a variance ranking beats random by construction). (5) PROXY check: the sweep loss must rank the full-VAL loss of the scored points (Spearman >= 0.6 per model-seed), otherwise the table is read from the full-VAL points only and says so.
**Jobs (all through `run.py`; one record each, so the by-hand verdict rests on launched runs):** four GPU jobs `E345.a_e312_s0`, `E345.a_e312_s1`, `E345.a_e324s_s0`, `E345.a_e324s_s1` (the sweep passes, the dumps of the scored points, the random / reverse / null / plumbing controls; about 170 passes per model-seed at about 25 - 40 s, unmeasured: the selftest measures one pass under load; about 2 h each, the slot wait is not counted) and one CPU job `E345.s` (`--after` the four, no `--gpu`: the scorings and the bootstraps, two at a time). The E342 `_groups` patch is not installed while an E345.a job runs.

## 3. Rules (written before the run)
**Classes** (a point scored in full, the worse seed of the model, loss L as defined in section 0 on the WORSE read-out): FREE L <= 0.005 (half the noise band of 0.010), CHEAP <= 0.02, COSTLY <= 0.05, CRITICAL above. A point only swept is classed by `ends_mean6` with the thresholds 0.003 / 0.01 / 0.03 and labelled "sweep". Both are computed on the SELECTION half; the full-VAL value is printed next to it.
**Which points are scored in full (the SAME set on both seeds of a model, chosen on seed 0, applied to seed 1; 19 points per model-seed):** the null (1); v (1); the 4 units with the largest saved GMACs among those with a sweep-FREE point at their largest FREE fraction (4); 3 stratified points (one per tercile of the sweep loss over all 84 points, fixed seed 345: the proxy check) (3); the 3 handle-scale points (3); the paint-evidence point (1); the prefixes of the two curves (4 heads-only + 2 pyramid = 6). 2 read-outs each: 38 scorings per model-seed, 152 in all: about 12 h at the idle-ish 4.7 minutes of `temp/e338/score_all.log`, up to about 28 h at the 11 minutes E321 measured on a loaded CPU; bootstraps only for v and the 6 prefixes (7 x 2 read-outs per model-seed, 56 in all: 0.5 - 8 minutes each). A background chain over the weekend (D100), two scorings at a time.
**Cumulative curves (greedy, so that S_b and S_c exist):** every (unit, f) increment is a candidate, CHEAP and COSTLY fractions included; the increments are ordered by the marginal sweep loss (selection half) per saved GMAC, a unit moving from f_i to f_{i+1}; increments that save less than 0.01 GMACs (the pair head, the head output channels) come after all GMAC-bearing increments, ordered by marginal loss per saved parameter; ties are broken by unit name. **Heads-only** (maps head + pair head, 3.278 GMACs; it feeds step 1) is cut at the prefixes closest to 10 %, 20 %, 35 %, 50 % of 3.278 GMACs (S1 - S4), or at the end of the curve if the units run out before 50 %; **pyramid view** (4.677 GMACs, reported, not deliverable by step 1) at 10 % and 25 % (P1, P2). The prefixes are scored as JOINT removals, which shows the interactions the single-unit sweep does not.
**The sizes for step 1** (E312 and E324 s, the worse seed, the worse read-out, judgement half): S_a = the largest prefix with L <= 0.01 on both read-outs without retraining (the free size); S_b = the largest prefix with L <= 0.03 (fine-tuning has to win back about 0.02, D99 point 4 allows 0.01 after it); S_c = the knee (the prefix after which the loss per saved GMAC doubles). At most these three go to step-1 cards (sparsity term, joint fine-tuning, two seeds, the D84 point 4 rule, a fresh critic pass); if S_a is empty the answer to D99's question is "no size without retraining is free" and step 1 starts from the sparsity-trained network.
**Prediction (numbers, hit windows narrower than the rule; pre-registered guesses, interactions are in no single-unit sweep):** (1) v loses **0.005** on the judgement half, mean of the four model-seeds (hit window 0.002 - 0.008); (2) v saves **0.8 GMACs** of the heads' 3.278 (24 %; hit window 0.4 - 1.2); (3) at least **15 of the 45 units** have a sweep-FREE point (f >= 0.25) on all four model-seeds (counted at their largest FREE fraction, sweep and full classes reported separately; the second residual blocks, half of the pair head's hidden units and some pyramid stages are expected, the paint units and the pyramid's deep stages are not). **Falsifiable statement:** the importance ranking carries information: on every model-seed the ranked removal at f = 0.50 loses less than the random removal in more than two thirds of the non-tied units, and the reverse ranking loses more than the ranked one in more than 90 %.
**Not claimed:** anything about the fine-tuned size (step 1); anything on TEST; that a CRITICAL unit cannot be pruned after training with a sparsity term; B's pyramid and B's heads (E346); the ms of a pruned network (only MAC shares and E342's block profile); the attachment and `je.kp`.

## 4. Intervals, results, cost
The sweep is deterministic given the model and the calibration list; the four model-seeds show how much the table moves between two draws of the same recipe. Full-VAL points and prefixes carry the paired scene bootstrap (`jdiag.py boot <ablated run> <card> <null run> <card>`: its sign is A - B, i.e. minus the loss): a loss counts when its interval excludes 0; a FREE class needs the upper end of the loss interval <= 0.01. Result files: `experiments/E345_pruning-sensitivity-step0/results.md` (the table: unit, f, saved GMACs / parameters, sweep loss, full loss per read-out, class, half-wise numbers) and `table.csv`, written from the probe's json, text, committed with the card. Cost: GPU about 8 h in four jobs (budget_gpu_h 8.0, to be re-set from the measured pass under load), CPU about 12 - 28 h of scoring plus about 1 - 8 h of bootstraps over the weekend; host RAM about 4 GB.
