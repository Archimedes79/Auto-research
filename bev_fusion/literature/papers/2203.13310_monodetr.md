**What it does**: Predicts a foreground depth map from the image features, encodes depth, and runs learnable object queries through a depth-guided decoder with depth cross-attention (set prediction with Hungarian matching).

**Results that matter**: state of the art on KITTI (abstract); no nuScenes number.

**Transferable technique**: an auxiliary foreground-depth supervision on the image features sharpens the lift; the DETR head itself is not needed.

**Cost and fit**: KITTI-only; needs matching; not a good fit.

**Doubts**: no nuScenes result; monocular depth ambiguity unresolved without radar.
