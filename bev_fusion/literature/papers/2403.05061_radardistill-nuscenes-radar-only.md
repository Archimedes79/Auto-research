**What it does**: Radar points go through pillar encoding and sparse 2D convs (PillarNet-18: SparseEnc + DenseEnc, CenterHead). A LiDAR network teaches the radar network in training only: Cross-Modality Alignment (CMA) densifies the sparse radar BEV features with a small block (deformable conv + ConvNeXt V2 blocks, transposed-conv up block, concatenation + 1x1 aggregation; radar fills about 10 % of the pillars lidar does), Activation-based Feature Distillation (only where the lidar activation exceeds a threshold), Proposal-based Feature Distillation (inside proposals, with scale normalisation). The paper does not state which per-point radar attributes are used.

**Results that matter** (nuScenes TEST, radar-only; checked): RadarDistill 20.5 mAP / 43.7 NDS (mATE 0.461, mAVE 0.336) vs PillarNet baseline 8.6 / 34.7, KPConvPillars 4.9 / 13.9, Radar-PointGNN 0.5 / 3.4; car AP 54.0. Ablation on 1/7 of the data (val): baseline 5.4 / 27.3, + CMA 6.4 / 29.3, + AFD 10.9 / 33.7, + PFD 11.2 / 34.7; dropping CMA from the full model costs about 4 NDS. Fusion: BEVFusion* with RadarDistill-CR 39.6 mAP / 46.4 NDS vs 38.3 / 45.3 (note: that baseline is low). No latency; 4 x RTX 3090 for training. Text claims trailer +23 % while the table shows 0.0 to 5.5.

**Transferable technique**: (1) the order of nuScenes radar-only results is graph / KPConv point networks (0.5, 4.9 mAP) far below sparse-conv pillars (8.6) far below a distilled one (20.5): on nuScenes the dense conv on a BEV image wins over point-level networks; (2) the only part that works without lidar teacher is CMA-style densification (dilated / deformable convs on the radar BEV).

**Cost and fit**: inference is radar-only pillars + sparse conv, no teacher; the teacher needs lidar boxes and features (we have the lidar in nuScenes training data, but our product is camera + radar). Distillation as an auxiliary loss is possible offline.

**Doubts**: test-set numbers of other methods are leaderboard entries (different TTA); the 8.6 baseline is the authors' reproduction without TTA; no latency; supplementary has the CMA structure; features and compensation not listed.
