## 1. The junction map of E279 (Mirko's two marks)
Target T = `exp(-d^2 / 2)` of the distance to the nearest GT lane point (the loss of `losses.grid_loss`), p = the 1 m centre map of E279 seed 0, 88 VAL frames (every 10th), junction cells = within 8 m of a connector point inside an intersection.
- The lines are on the GT: median lateral shift of p against T 0.10 - 0.15 m on isolated lane points, also at the last 3 m before a junction (median 0.00 m left / right) and in the first 3 m of the connectors (+0.01 m); no left bias. The leftward shift of Mirko's frame is local (the centroid of p in the rows y 0 .. 2 of his circle lies up to 2.7 m left of T's).
- What is junction specific is the FILL of cells that the target leaves empty (T < 0.1, within 6 m of a lane point): p > 0.3 in 6.0 % of them inside the junction, 2.7 % at 8 - 24 m, 2.0 % farther away. It is systematic (seed 0 against seed 1: correlation 0.94 inside the junction, 75 % of the filled cells shared), worse far from the ego (correlation of p and T 0.81 / 0.75 / 0.66 at 0 - 16 / 16 - 32 / 32 - 47 m), better on TRAIN frames (0.82 against 0.71, fill 2.7 %), without a side preference (the fill lies left of the junction body in 48 / 50 / 55 % of the frames), and p is more sure than T everywhere (p = 0.9 where T is on average 0.65 - 0.75).
- **ORACLE from the GT ends** (88 frames, junction cells, correlation with T): the true ends and true pairs with ONE cubic Hermite curve each (tangent length 1.0 x chord, fixed beforehand) 0.88 (1.2: 0.93, chosen on VAL); the true ends with ALL geometrically possible pairs 0.53 (54 candidate pairs per frame against 12.7 true, 4.3 per true pair, the rule keeps 98 % of the true pairs); E279 p 0.71. The PAIRS are the bottleneck, not the ends or the shape. A classifier on the geometry of exact GT ends (HistGradientBoosting, every 3rd TRAIN frame) predicts the pairs with AUC 0.995 on VAL (precision 94 %, recall 95 %), but nuScenes holds only about 35 junction groups (frames linked at 40 m) and 747 of 825 VAL junction frames show a junction that TRAIN shows too; with whole junctions held out (TRAIN, leave-junction-out) AUC 0.969, precision 88 %, recall 84 %. The map drawn from its pairs: 0.83 (hard) / 0.84 (soft) against 0.71.

- **The failure of 'lanes found' is PARTIAL coverage, not missing lanes** (GT pieces of the lane GT, found = >= 80 % of the points within 1.0 m): tier 1 in intersections 47.5 % found / 47.8 % partial / 4.7 % missing, tier 1 elsewhere 76.4 / 22.5 / 1.1 (seed 0; seed 1 58.7 % found over tier 1).
- **The offsets are not a graph-building error** (`offset_origin.py`, 73 frames, section points): of the points 1 - 2.5 m from the GT 88 % have a real ridge of the head's own centre map under them but only 21 % have their ridge point within 1.0 m of the GT (plain road 14 %, junction zone 27 %): the map is off where the graph is. **An oracle rigid motion per frame** (translation +-4 m and rotation +-2 deg chosen with the GT, `align_oracle.py`, seed 0) removes only 14 % of the lost GT length overall (35 % on plain road): the point F1 at 1.0 m (P1, R1 of the script, not the scorer's) 0.829 -> 0.862; median |t| 0.5 m, 12 % of the frames move more than 1 m. The offset is local and shared by both seeds (the errors are systematic, correlation 0.94 between seeds): it is a limit of the frozen BEV features (and of the GT) at a 1 m tolerance, not noise and not a registration of the frame.
- **The junction ends are not clean** (`ends_quality.py`; GT entries / exits = starts / ends of the connector runs, 7.7 / 7.6 per frame): a kept node lies within 2 m (position only) of 80 % / 82 % of them, a node with the right flag (stop / exit >= 0.5) of 60 % / 55 %; of the flagged nodes only 49 % / 49 % lie within 2 m of a GT entry / exit and of the long-link sources / targets 45 % / 44 %; with 4 m tolerance: a kept node near 96 % of the entries / exits, a node with the right flag near 76 % / 71 %, of the flagged nodes 73 % / 75 % and of the long-link ends 66 % right. The heading is not the problem (30 deg or none: 49.1 against 49.4 %). The ends are placed 2 - 4 m off ALONG the lane (the event is a convention 1 - 3 m before the polygon edge).
- **The pair rule of E276 P9 / E281 undercounts the long links** (`link_strict.py`): it matches the ends to GT nodes within 2 m, so a link with a right curve and ends 2 - 4 m off counts as wrong. A strict curve-level label (>= 80 % of the curve within 1.0 m of ONE GT connector run, in its direction) calls 45.9 % / 52.4 % of the delivered long links right (the pair rule: 10.0 % / 10.7 % of them); 43 % / 46 % of the connector runs >= 8 m are found; the head's p separates strict right from wrong with AUC only 0.69 (strict precision 15 - 20 % at p < 0.2, 55 - 62 % at p > 0.8).

## 4. What the analysis says
1. **The largest loss is a local lateral offset of 1 - 2.5 m (11.5 % of the GT length) that sits in the head's own centre map**, shared by both seeds, not removed by a per-frame rigid motion (14 %) or by snapping to the map's ridge (+0.004 F1@1.0); it is a limit of the frozen cached BEV features (and of the GT) at a 1 m tolerance. A read-out cannot fix it; better features (the encoder chats), unfreezing the BEV part (the E2E goal) or targets that tolerate the offset can.
2. **The junction blur is the unknown pair of entry and exit** (oracle 0.88 / 0.53 / 0.71 for true pairs / all pairs / the network), and the geometry of CLEAN ends predicts the pairs well (AUC 0.97 - 0.995, with the caveats of section 1). The delivered ends are not clean: half of the flagged ends are not within 2 m of a GT entry / exit (73 % within 4 m). Section 5 (2026-11-10) shows that this is not simply a shift of all ends along the lane: where a flagged node exists near a GT end its along-lane position is good on the median (0.2 - 0.4 m early); what is wrong is that about a quarter of the GT ends get no flag and about half of the flags sit at no GT end.
3. **The old pair label undercounts the long links** (10 % against 46 - 52 % geometrically right), so the quality of the junction connectors is higher than 'precision 0.17 - 0.19' says; the scorer's junction numbers do not follow the strict label (p16).
4. **Closed by their written gates:** geometry features on the delivered long links with the pair-rule label (p14), the re-scored read-out (p16), ridge snapping, long-link NMS, tiny-piece removal. **Passed but not enough for a card by itself:** p15 (strict label, gain over the weak S1; only +0.01 - 0.03 over the strong current-frame set).
5. **Not tried (options, none decided, all need a card and, for the first two, GPU in Mirko's windows):** (a) a dedicated entry / exit keypoint head with targets that tolerate 2 - 4 m along the lane plus the heading, as the source of the long-link ends instead of the event flags; (b) the pair decision with the geometry of the ends inside the link head, trained on the strict label; (c) the scorer-level potential of (a) + (b) first, as a CPU oracle: a graph with GT ends, the geometry classifier's pairs and one curve per pair against the delivered graph under the E273 scorer (junction F1, topo F1, lanes found) - if the scorer does not reward it, (a) and (b) are not worth a GPU hour; (d) a tolerance-aware lane target or a lane head on features of the encoder chats once they exist.

- **The map's point is already about where the paint ends.** Painted-divider support peaks at the map's point (entries 0.57 against 0.14 - 0.18 upstream) and falls within 4 - 8 m inside the junction (entries 0.15 - 0.22, exits 0.19 - 0.29), as the 0.0 % / 11.9 % measure of 2026-11-04 said. Camera visibility is flat (0.62 - 0.69 everywhere, 0.64 / 0.67 at the stop / exit points): it cannot place a 'late' point, and the ground inside the junction is as visible as the ground before it - what is missing there is a lane that can be recognised, not sight.
- **The network puts the end slightly early, rarely late.** Median 0.2 - 0.6 m farther out than the map's point; 9 - 18 % are 2 m or more farther out; any kept node lies 2 m or more nearer the junction in 1 % of the events (a flagged node in 7 - 9 %). The centre map itself is sharp up to the junction (median lateral error 0.12 - 0.15 m at 0 - 6 m before the first connector point, `entry_and_blur.json`), blurred inside.
- **What this means for D71.** Moving the GT points later than the map's lane end has no support in the data (no paint, no recognisable lane inside; the network does not go there on its own); moving them earlier is possible but the network is already slightly early. The position along the lane has a spread (q25 .. q75 about 1.6 m wide for the flags, 57 - 63 % within 1 m, about a fifth 2 m or more off in either direction), but it is not the main defect: **the flag is missing at 25 - 33 % of the GT ends and about half of the flagged nodes sit at no GT end** (`ends_quality.json`, precision 0.48 - 0.49). A new GT version with moved points is therefore not the first lever; the end keypoint head (section 4, 5a) and the flag precision are. Open and not measured: where the map's point lies against the painted stop line and the crosswalk (the map has both as areas; the lane GT does not use them), and how many wrong-way headings there are on lanes without traffic.
