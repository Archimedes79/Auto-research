**What it does**: LSS lifts camera features into BEV voxels (200x200x16, 88 depth bins). For any 3D query point, trilinear-sampled voxel feature + sin/cos positional encoding goes through two 3-layer sine-activated MLPs (one SDF, one semantics). Supervision: lidar surface points give SDF = 0; the "Sandwich Eikonal" pushes the minimum SDF inside every occupied cell below zero and the SDF in every free cell above zero (dense constraints on both sides of the surface), plus eikonal (unit gradient), normal alignment and absolute SDF at lidar points. A joint Dice loss combines semantic logits with a free-space logit derived from the minimum SDF per voxel. Inference: marching cubes for a mesh, argmax for occupancy.

**Results that matter** (Occ3D-nuScenes val, Small = ResNet50 256x704, ablation Table 5): occupancy baseline 38.33 mIoU / depth Abs Rel 0.342; plain SDF with SIREN 31.51; SDF with LODE loss 36.66; SDF with Sandwich Eikonal 40.52; plus joint supervision 42.37 (Abs Rel 0.174). So a naive SDF is worse than occupancy (-6.8), a good SDF loss is better (+4.0). Table 3: Small 42.37, Medium 46.0, Large 53.01. Coarse labels: mIoU 42.37 at 0.4 m, 40.16 at 1.6 m GT resolution (FB-OCC 40.69 -> 26.96). Using 10 % of the lidar sampling: 41.07. Training: Adam, batch 32, lr 1e-4, ~32 epochs. Speed / memory: not reported.

**Transferable technique**: the cell-wise sandwich constraints (min SDF in an occupied cell < 0, SDF in a free cell > 0) are a dense, cheap, matching-free SDF loss from occupancy labels. Robustness to coarse labels and to sparse lidar supervision is attractive for a small system.

**Cost and fit**: needs lidar-derived occupancy labels (Occ3D) at train time; not self-supervised. Marching cubes and per-point MLP queries make inference heavier than a 2D head; for planning you can threshold voxels. Gain over occupancy is a few mIoU points, bought with extra loss weights (8 gamma values tuned).

**Doubts**: only one ablation table; Small-version numbers only; no runtime; whether the gain is the SDF or the extra joint loss is partly confounded (E vs D adds the joint term, +1.85).
