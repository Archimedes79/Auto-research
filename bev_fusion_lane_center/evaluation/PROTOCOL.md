# Evaluation protocol

Setting of every number: exact ego poses, simulated ADASIS-like SD map, nuScenes (frozen GaussianCaR features), scene-disjoint splits. State this whenever numbers leave the project.

## Data and splits
- Frames: TRAIN 8,414, VAL 873, TEST 833 (`training/data/p16_common.py`: `N_TRAIN, N_VAL, N_TEST`); scene lists in `data/splits.json`. TEST = 21 unseen scenes. The splits are scene-disjoint, **not** location-disjoint (open check: overlap of VAL / TEST locations with TRAIN, 5 m rule).
- VAL subset for early rungs of a search: 250 frames, `p16_common.val_subset()`; the full VAL (873) from rung 2 on. Never compare numbers from different frame sets.
- Out-of-fold dense heads (`e37_fold0/1/2`) give the inputs for perturbed TRAIN rows (no leakage of the head's own training frames).

## Selection and noise
- Select on VAL only. Differences below the noise band are ties. The default band is 0.010 (S and Q); since D18 (2026-11-01) a card may name a per-metric band in its section 0 (`metric, direction, band`) or in a rule clause (`band`), and `decide.py` uses it. A per-metric band cites the seed table of E240 (`experiments/E240_measurement-floor-seed-noise/seed_sd.txt`, four paired seeds, VAL 873): 3 paired SDs = Q 0.013, lanes found 0.018, R@1 far 0.019, topo F1 0.005, F1@1.0 0.007 (an SD from 4 pairs has about 40 % standard error, so these are estimates).
- Any comparison of route heads needs **two seeds**; the seed spread of the route head is up to 0.03 in route success on clean frames and 0.05-0.09 in the failure-series cells of loop 17 (regime dependent; larger than many effects there).
- Intervals: scene-level bootstrap, `sparse_lanes/bootstrap.py` (`SB.interval`, `SB.paired(variant, control)` = variant - control with the verdict against the noise band; `evaluate.py --compare` uses it, with `topo_metrics.paired` for the topology numbers; 1000 draws). Since D24 (2026-10-31) the draws and intervals of both are the one shared implementation `../../autoresearch/Evaluation/stats.py` (the same `RandomState` draws as before: stored numbers are reproduced exactly), and so are those of the loop-4 driver `drivers/boot_eval.py` (2000 draws, new random draws since D24). The loop scripts `loops/loop11/p11_eval.py`, `loops/loop17/p17_compare.py` (and `p17_extra.py`, since 2026-11-01 in `_to_delete/` and git history) keep their own old bootstraps for reproducing their numbers; do not use them for new comparisons.

## TEST discipline
- TEST is used once per finished design: the winner and its control, with the user's explicit OK, and the use is logged in `TEST_USAGE_LOG.md`. Nothing is tuned on TEST. Exploration after a freeze is validation-only (loop 16b).

## Headline metrics (from 2026-10-25; all of them come out of `evaluate.py`)
- Judge new work by **Q** at vertices per 100 m <= 14.3 (S rewards sparsity; S stays in every table for the history), **topo F1** (directed connectivity, `sparse_lanes/topo_metrics.py`), **lanes found**, **R@1 far** (recall at 1 m beyond 40 m), route success (2 seeds), ms per frame. FL and dup_frac are checks.
- Vertex count (D20, corrected by D25): the budget of 14.3 is counted on the per-piece Douglas-Peucker basis (0.3 m) and is met with and without the output stage `simplify_dp` (0.2 m, registry names `*_dp`, `assemble(dp_eps=0.2)`); the stage re-counts the same geometry and lowers only the node-id count, quoted as "nodes delivered", never against the budget; Q moves only within the noise band (champion 0.73497 -> champion_dp 0.73455, recall 0.7404 -> 0.7399); S of a `*_dp` card is not comparable with S of other cards (S divides the 14.3 oracle by the node-id count that the stage lowers: recall 0.7675 -> recall_dp 0.7789 in S, +0.0115, above the 0.010 band): `evaluate.py --compare` between a `*_dp` name and another prints a warning and sets `compare.s_not_comparable` in the card, read Q there. The stage is off by default and excluded from the `aux=True` pipelines (route head, map): they keep the unsimplified vertices.
- Paired scene bootstrap for every difference (`evaluate.py --compare`); the S band of 0.010 is the tie rule for S and Q; for topo F1 and R@1 far use the measured interval (a difference whose interval contains 0 is a tie).
- A new model is evaluated through a `models/registry.json` entry with its standard settings, never with a private driver.
- Layer 1 of the numbers protocol: `evaluate.py --golden check` reproduces every entry of `golden.json` before a loop starts and in `tools/maintenance/run_all_tests.sh`. The file holds 7 entries, not every registry name: 6 names on VAL from the cache (champion, champion_noprune, control, fast, fast_recall, recall; tolerance 0.0005) and the champion re-inferred on VAL250 without the cache (tolerance 0.0005, idle GPU). **Not guarded:** the other 46 of the 52 registry names, among them every `*_dp` entry (champion_dp, recall_dp, fast_recall_dp, recall_sp0_dp), recall_sp0, the `*_ring` entries and the four unions (ens_*): a change of the output stage, the seed ring or the union code is not caught by layer 1 on those names. Adding entries needs a grant on `golden.json` (a protected file) under a decided D<n>; until then reproduce such a number with `--expect` against its card. A golden entry changes only with a ledger row that says why.

## Metric set (see `sparse_lanes/README.md`, `lane_grid/README.md`)
Graph: F1 at 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 m, J (split / merge F1 at 6 m), vertices per 100 m, FL (false length), S (composite), lanes found, direction accuracy. Frontier: F1@Sp17 (best F1@1 with <= 17 vertices per 100 m), FL@R85. Route: route success of the route head (assigned lane on the route), route F. Planner: consumer L2 error at 1-3 s, deviation rate. Dense maps: point P / R / F1 at 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 m.

## Failure and map protocols
- Failure series (loop 5 E57, loop 17 E193+): per scene, first half clean features, second half features of one failure type, 1 s between frames. Types: night (`dark`), `blur`, `cam_front3_off` (three front cameras off), `fog`, `radar_off`, `extr3` (extrinsic error), `cam_front_off`; E209 holds out night and blur from the training of the builder.
- SD map: ADASIS-like simulator (E77); map error = scene-constant world-frame translation with sigma (m) and yaw 2 sigma (deg) plus per-frame noise (E61 / E87); modes exact, none, wrong, error 1-2 m. Map benefit is judged against the matched no-map reference (E208).
- Loop 18 additions: the map can be registered before it is used (K188 pose-grid registration with causal world-frame accumulation, `p18_regis_eval.py`) and gated by the trust of the registration (K189, thresholds 0.62 / 0.75, `--reg`, `--reg_trust` of `p17_eval.py`); report cells: exact, error 1 / 2 / 3 m unregistered and registered, none, wrong with and without the gate. Fork pruning (K190, `assemble(fork_prune=0.3)`, 0.4 for the recall variants) is part of the deployed assembly from loop 18; tables name it. Comparisons of the failure series use the paired scene bootstrap of `p17_compare.py` (F1@1) and `bootstrap.py` (S, Q, J).

## Timing protocol
Milliseconds per frame on an idle GPU (RTX 3060), batch 1, cached foundation features, after warm-up, median of the frames (`p16_timing.py`, `p18_latency.py`; the loop-17 setting is `--union 1 --fork_prune 0`); an optimised path must reproduce the graphs of the reference path (`p16_fast_check.py`: identical graphs in N / N frames).
Loop 18: the assembly path is a parameter (`fast=1` FastUnion of loop 16b, `fast=2` numba kernels, bit-identical graphs checked on all 873 validation frames by `p18_asm_check.py`); stage times with `p18_latency.py --union ... --fork_prune ...`; timing only when no other job runs on the machine (a training in parallel doubles the assembly time).
One entry for all three (W11, 2026-11-01): `evaluate.py <registry name> --route | --failure <perts> | --timing` (`eval_parts.py`) produces route success, the per-phase failure-series metrics and these timings from a registry entry into the metric card (keys in README.md, "Parts"); the drivers named above stay the code underneath. `--timing` is valid only on an idle GPU, like the driver.
