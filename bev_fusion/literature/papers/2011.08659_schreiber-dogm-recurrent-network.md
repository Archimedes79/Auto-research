**What it does** [checked]: input is a single-channel lidar measurement grid (0.15 m cells, 1001 x 1001 = +-75 m; 601 x 601 crops for training); a U-Net-style encoder-decoder with three downsamplings to 38 x 38 x 128, a two-layer ConvLSTM at the bottom and ConvLSTMs in the skip connections (cell sizes 0.15 / 0.45 / 1.35 / 4.05 m on four levels); two decoders give occupancy probability and east / north velocity per cell, with an auxiliary dynamic-cell classification. Ego-motion compensation for recurrent states at different resolutions: "input placement" shifts the input grid for small motions, "recurrent state shifting" moves all states together when the ego crosses a cell boundary of the COARSEST level (shifting states alone fails when levels have different cell sizes). Loss: Huber (delta 0.02) on occupancy, L2 on velocity and on the dynamic classification (weights 50, 0.02, 0.1), velocity terms only on cells with occupancy > 0.7. Labels: velocities from track displacements in a global frame assigned to occupied cells inside each box; occupancy labels from the particle-based DOGMa of Nuss et al. Argoverse 3D tracking: 65 training and 24 validation sequences.

**Results that matter** [checked, vs the particle baseline of Nuss et al.]: cell-level mIoU 0.8892 vs 0.6247; dynamic-cell EPE 0.1059 m vs 0.1998 m; object recall 0.8787 vs 0.8088, precision 0.8108 vs 0.2734; object-level velocity MAE 0.5173 vs 0.5264 m/s (comparable); moving-ego mIoU 0.8826 vs 0.6032; about 53 ms on a GTX 1080 Ti. 2202.04461 (abstract): raw lidar BEV with several height channels, end-to-end, outputs occupancy, velocity, semantics and drivable area; its learned inverse sensor model handles shapes and freespace better than a geometric model.

**Transferable technique**: (1) the ego-motion handling with multi-resolution states is a limitation our design does not have: we chain COORDINATES and splat once per original frame (plan.md section 7.1), so there is no state to shift; (2) the loss recipe (velocity only on confidently occupied cells, auxiliary dynamic classification) is the same as MotionHead's (L1 on object cells, zero below 1 m/s); (3) the comparison protocol (cell-level mIoU, dynamic EPE, object-level velocity MAE against a particle DOGMa) is a ready evaluation for a camera + radar velocity field if we also rasterise a classical DOGMa.

**Cost and fit**: a stationary-input design: one measurement grid per step, no radar, no Doppler, no velocity hypotheses: the network regresses ONE velocity per cell. 53 ms on a 1080 Ti for 1001 x 1001 cells.

**Doubts**: lidar only; velocity labels from tracked boxes leave the background cells at zero; no number for velocity of slow or tangentially moving objects separately.
