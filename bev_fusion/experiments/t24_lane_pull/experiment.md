## 1 Card
- **Question.** With the network, data, schedule and evaluation of `t21.ctl_s0` / `t21.ctl_s1` unchanged, does lifting the L4 lane branch's stride-4 features with lift P (pull on the same projection table, no depth weight) instead of lift PD (weighted by the DualBEV depth probabilities) raise lane divider IoU@0.5 on VAL, without breaking a guard?
- **Tried already, and what is new.** (1) The synthetic lift gate `s1a_lift_pd_vs_p` (section 5.1 of its card, in git before commit 25a6512b): lane IoU@0.5 with lift P 0.477 / 0.547 against 0.700 / 0.696 with lift PD (paired +0.223, interval 0.185 to 0.260); PD had the oracle depth there, so the gap is an upper bound for the real case, but it is a lane result in the wrong direction. (2) `t2_lift_p` on real data (`results/experiments/t2_block_swaps/results/arms.md` line 33): lane divider IoU@0.5 -0.013 with an interval that excludes 0, and worse vehicle and pedestrian AP (main lift, from scratch, 3000 steps). (3) t7 (+0.054 for the lane branch, measured WITH lift PD). **New here:** lift P only in the stride-4 lane branch added after the temporal block, as a fine-tune of a PD-trained network, on real ground paint. The prior evidence (1, 2) says P is worse for lanes; this card is a cheap test of whether the lane branch is the exception.
- **What else the evidence says.** The L4 branch of the t20 bundle lifts the stride-4 features with the main branch's depth probabilities (`lane_branch.py`), a smooth stride-16 map that modulates the AMPLITUDE of the features (the mean weight is about 1/118 of a depth bin) and also acts as the only visibility handle: under lift P a cell behind a car gets the car's feature, and the pull takes all 8 height slices (-0.75 to 2.75 m, oversample 4), of which only z = 0 is the ground. The bundle t20 did not move the lane layer (IoU@1 0.542 against 0.549 for `t16.full_s0`, not separated), and the effect of L4 alone is not isolated; D65 (Mirko 2026-11-08) orders a cheap check by the Image Encoder chat (two evaluations of `t20_base_s0`, L4 on and dropped after load) and then to leave L4 out of NEW base runs. The night CPU diagnostics on the t20 network (`temp/overview/*_result.txt`; IoU there 0.446 on 246 frames against 0.4256 on the 1000 of the card): recall of label points 0.71 with a visible stripe and 0.67 without at 5-30 m (difference +0.04, interval -0.07 to +0.14, none at 12-30 m); 0.87 for painted points at 5-12 m; 0.81 only for the post-hoc subset stripe-visible and not hidden by a vehicle. Recovering every missed painted, unhidden point would add about 5 points of recall at 5-30 m, about +0.03 IoU at most (my bound, IoU = 1 / (1 / precision + 1 / recall - 1) with 0.744 / 0.573). The stripe rate under the false cells at 0.5-1.0 m from a label (0.32) is close to that under true cells (0.37), and the labels scatter by about 0.5 m around paint (`records/diag_lane_tests_2026-11-05.md`): part of what looks like line width may be label scatter.
- **Hypothesis (a hypothesis, sign unknown).** Lift P gives the lane branch an exact ground sampling at z = 0 without the amplitude modulation of a blurry stride-16 depth map, so thin lines come out sharper, recall of painted cells and the line width improve, and lane divider IoU@0.5 rises. **Counter-mechanisms:** (i) the depth weight also suppresses clutter above the road and behind objects; (ii) the layers after the lift were trained for 6000 steps on depth-weighted features (BatchNorm in `collapse` re-adapts the scale in about 50 steps, the trunk does not), so the 1500-step fine-tune starts from a handicap the control does not have; (iii) the label scatter caps what a sharper line can gain. **My estimate that a gain of +0.015 or more appears: about 10 %.** The expected outcome is a TIE or a CUT.
- **Go / no-go before any GPU hour.** The CPU read-out `temp/overview/l4_readout.txt` (the t20 network on the 246 frames: L4 on, L4 dropped after load, lift P at step 0; written by the night's CPU agent) must show that L4 contributes: IoU@0.5 (L4 on) minus (L4 dropped) of at least +0.010, with the paired interval's lower end above 0. If the contribution is at most +0.005, L4 does nothing and a change of its lift is pointless: the card is closed as "not run" (ledger note, `closed_questions.md` not needed) and the flag file `temp/lane_tests/GO8` is never written. Between the two, the call is made by hand and written here before the chain starts. The Image Encoder's D65 check (GPU, two evaluations) supersedes the read-out when it arrives first. The chain `temp/lane_tests/chain8.sh` waits for `GO8`.
- **Prediction (written before the run; VAL 1000, dt 0, point estimates against the control of the same seed; seed 0 against `t21.ctl_s0`: lane divider IoU@0.5 0.4341, IoU@1 0.5516, AP 0.2149).** Best case, not expected value: IoU@0.5 at least +0.015 (>= 0.449) and IoU@1 at least +0.010; the lane divider AP and roc_auc not below the control by more than 0.005; non-gating pattern: a gain at 20-60 m (`.../lane_divider/all/20-40/iou@0.5`, `.../40-60/...`) and none at 0-20 m, where the recall of painted points is already 0.87. The guards as in section 1 of the t23 card: road divider IoU@1, drivable, walkway, ped crossing, vehicle IoU at dt 0, the three lane-centre rows, each not worse than the control by more than m; mAP of the fusion area by max(0.020, m); moving-centre velocity EPE not above +0.05 m/s. The seed-1 pair is scored with one evaluator.
- **Decision rule (composed by hand, label "by hand: composed from the table rows"; fixed now, before any run; d_s = IoU@0.5 of the arm minus the control of seed s from `compare`; m_s = max(0.010, half-width of its paired bootstrap, |IoU(ctl_s1) - IoU(ctl_s0)|, where the second control spread is known before the arms start because chain8 waits for `DONE7`); D = (d_0 + d_1) / 2).**
  1. **Guard clause first:** a guard (list above; rows without an interval at m = 0.010) broken by more than 2 m_s with its interval beyond -m_s in BOTH seeds -> CUT, whatever d_s is.
  2. Each seed is **up** (d_s > m_s and its interval above 0), **down** (d_s < -m_s and its interval below 0), **tie** (|d_s| <= m_s) or **open** (anything else, e.g. |d_s| > m_s with an interval that contains 0).
  3. **CANDIDATE:** both seeds up, D > 0.015, IoU@1 not below the control by more than m_s in either seed, lane divider AP and roc_auc not below the control by more than 0.005 in both seeds, every guard holds (not worse than -m_s) in both seeds. One run pair never gives KEEP; the next step (a longer fine-tune, the lift of the next base) is Mirko's decision.
  4. **TIE:** both seeds tie. The lift weighting does not matter for this recipe; the lane-branch lift idea is closed (`closed_questions.md`).
  5. **CUT:** both seeds down.
  6. **INCONCLUSIVE:** every other combination (up / tie, up / down, open, ...); the spread is reported, a third seed only on Mirko's word. A rule that fits only after the result is not allowed: this table is the rule.
- **Noise.** Lane divider IoU@0.5 over the stored cards of the t16 - t23 chain: 0.4256 - 0.4366 (SD about 0.003; siblings of one warm start, not seed twins); paired half-widths 0.003 - 0.008; the control seed pair of tonight (chain7) measures the first fine-tune seed spread. With the CANDIDATE threshold equal to the predicted effect the power of this test is about 50 % even if the hypothesis is right, and about 26 guard comparisons must all hold: a clean CANDIDATE is unlikely.
- **Kill / validity.** (1) a non-finite loss in more than 1 % of the steps; (2) `gt.json` not the signature `0.5_4_6a7aa7864a`; (3) GPU memory above 11 GB (the run's own use; the control used about 10.2 GB); (4) a resumed run is labelled and not mixed into a verdict; (5) the logged run record names `t24_lane_pull` and the loaded model's `lane.lift.use_depth` is False (logged at start); (6) at step 1000 `group.lines` above 1.2 x the control's logged value at the same step -> the run is stopped and the pair is read as CUT only if the other seed agrees, otherwise INCONCLUSIVE (t7 used a step-1000 check of this kind); (7) the go / no-go above is not met. The step-1 equality check of the t23 card does not apply to the arm (its lane contribution differs from step 1 by design); the smoke covers the code equivalence instead.
- **What it does not test.** Other lane-branch changes (resolution, features, an own line loss, the branch weight); a from-scratch base with lift P or without L4 (D65's check and a base run without L4 are a separate order); the junction mask (D66); a longer schedule. A TIE or CUT of a 1500-step fine-tune with a handicap does not prove that a from-scratch lift-P base would not be better: it says that this cheap change of an existing network does not help, and by D65 a null result changes nothing in the plan (L4 leaves the new bases anyway); only a CANDIDATE would change the plan, and that needs Mirko's word.
- **Cost.** CPU tests done. GPU, only after the go: smoke about 6 min; per seed about 1.9 h training + 0.2 h evaluation, two seeds about 4.2 h, one training at a time (about 10.2 GB). CPU: read-outs of arms and controls, about 12 min each.
