## 1 Card
- **Question.** With the network, data, schedule and evaluation of `t21.ctl_s0` unchanged, does adding a tolerance-aware BCE to the line loss of the main head raise lane divider IoU@0.5 on VAL, without breaking a guard and without it being only a shift of the operating point?
- **What the evidence says (stored files, no new run).**
  - The presence head of `t21.ctl_s0` (`runs/t21_ctl_s0/eval/scene_records_n1000.pkl`, VAL 1000, summed over scenes; `temp/lane_tests/fn_fp.py`): at tolerance 0.5 m precision 0.629 / recall 0.565 (126,206 predicted cells within 0.5 m of a GT line, 74,298 farther, 52,380 GT cells missed); at tolerance 0 precision 0.256 / recall 0.426 (200,504 predicted against 120,531 GT cells: the predicted region is 1.66 x the GT). The IoU is lost to both: far false cells are 1.4 x the misses. On TRAIN frames the baseline `t20_base_s0` has precision 0.735 / recall 0.640 / IoU 0.538 (critic's re-computation), so far false cells exist on trained data too and a good part of the VAL excess is generalisation.
  - The line loss today is a Dice with a 1-cell dilation (0.5 m) plus a Huber term on the distance field and **no per-cell BCE** (`losses.map_terms`, `line_tolerance` 1; `line_boundary`, the existing false-positive penalty `loss_extras.boundary_fp_loss`, has never been run in this project; it is a mean of distance x probability over all cells, a small, uncalibrated term, so it is the natural LATER arm, not this one). A Dice counts a false cell only through its denominator; a BCE punishes every confident cell in the background. The area layers (drivable, walkway, ...) have BCE + IoU; the lines do not.
  - Prevalence: lane divider 1.54 %, road divider 0.45 % of the cells (control curve card). Lateral scatter of the labels about 0.5 - 0.6 m (paint check `diag.paint_val`, SD 0.60 m: `records/diag_lane_tests_2026-11-05.md`): a hard 0-tolerance target is not learnable, so the BCE keeps an ignore band.
  - The ignore band is the cells farther than 0 and nearer than 1.0 m (GT distance field, continuous) from a line cell, about 1.5 cells on each side of the line cells themselves (positives are the cells with distance 0). It is wider than the Dice's 0.5 m dilation, deliberately: cells around 1 m from a line are still counted as false cells by IoU@0.5 but are not punished by the BCE.
  - Capacity is not the limit: the overfit test (`diag.overfit8_*`) took lane divider IoU@0.5 on 8 training scenes from 0.463 to 0.791 in 600 steps.
- **Hypothesis.** A BCE on the line presence (positives x3, ignore band 1.0 m, weight 10 inside the lines group) punishes the confident far false cells that the Dice ignores, and calibrates the head. Positive weight 3 is chosen on purpose: the IoU-optimal posterior threshold is about IoU / (1 + IoU) = 0.3, and a BCE with positive weight p has its 0.5 threshold at the true posterior 1 / (1 + p) = 0.25, so p = 3 puts the fixed 0.5 threshold of the evaluation near the IoU optimum. **Expected direction: recall rises, precision may fall; the IoU is the target, the two are reported, not gated.** The weight 10 is a reasoned first value (BCE value expected about 0.05 - 0.07 against a lines group of about 0.9, so the term adds roughly +0.5 to +0.7, +55 to +80 % of the group), **not tuned**.
- **What the weight does (corrected).** The Kendall weighting between the task groups is NOT scale-invariant in this fine-tune: `loss.s` is trained with AdamW at lr 1e-4 and moves at most about 0.15 in 1500 steps (control log: `s.lines` 0.472 at step 1, 0.426 at step 760), so `exp(-s)` for the lines stays about 0.62. The weight therefore raises the lines group's share of the total gradient (and of the shared trunk's gradient). This is part of the arm, and a reason for the guards on areas, objects and motion; it is also why a gain is first "more line gradient with a calibrated loss", not yet "BCE" (see the CANDIDATE step). Read from the run log: `group.lines`, `s.*`, the `grad.*` and `upd.*` rows (probe and `grad_diag_every` 500 are on).
- **Scope: the main head only.** `map_terms` is called for three heads with the same config (main `sem`, the 1 m centre-line head `sem_1m`, the camera aux head `cam_aux`); the new term is switched on only for the main head (`map_terms(..., line_bce=True)` in `BEVLoss.terms_at`), found by the critic and tested at the level of `BEVLoss` (every other term bit-equal, the camera-aux total bit-equal). The lane-centre head (1 m) is untouched. **Arm T** (`line_tolerance` 0 = a Dice without tolerance) is **dropped from this card**: it reaches the same three heads, has no sign prediction and the card expects it at best neutral; if it is ever run it needs the same head isolation and its own card.

- **Decision rule (margin m = max(0.010, half-width of the paired bootstrap of that comparison); composed by hand, labelled "by hand: composed from the table rows").**
  - **CANDIDATE:** lane divider IoU@0.5 above the control by more than max(0.015, m) with the interval excluding 0; AND every guard holds; AND the ranking is not worse (`lane_divider` AP and `roc_auc` not below the control by more than their half-widths) - otherwise the gain is labelled "operating point (calibration), not better lines" and is not a CANDIDATE; AND the step-1 check passed -> a second seed of C and of the control, plus one run with the lines group scaled to the same share without the BCE shape if the second seed holds (a gain from "more line gradient" is not yet a gain from the BCE). One seed never gives KEEP.
  - **TIE:** the lane divider IoU@0.5 difference inside +-m. A single-seed TIE or CUT is provisional until the second seed; it speaks for THESE settings (weight 10, positives x3, ignore 1.0 m), not for BCE terms in general.
  - **CUT:** lane divider IoU@0.5 below the control by more than m with the interval below 0, or a guard broken by more than 2 m with the interval beyond -m.
  - **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else -> a second seed of the arm and of the control.
- **Noise.** Lane divider IoU@0.5 over nine VAL-1000 cards of the t16 - t22 chain: 0.426 - 0.437 (SD about 0.003); paired bootstrap half-widths against the control 0.003 - 0.006; so noise alone cannot make a CANDIDATE. These siblings share their initialisation and data order, they are not seed twins; from-scratch seeds differ more (`t10.dual_s0` 0.335 vs `s1` 0.350), and the scene bootstrap measures frame sampling, not the training seed. Hence the second seed.
- **Kill / validity.** (1) a non-finite loss in more than 1 % of the steps; (2) `gt.json` not the control's signature; (3) GPU memory above 11 GB; (4) the run resumed (labelled, not mixed with an uninterrupted one in a verdict); (5) the step-1 check fails. The evaluator is unchanged since the control's card (`metrics.py`, `bev_suite.py`, `evaluate.py` identical since commit 4dba7bf649, checked by the critic), so the control's card is used as stored and not re-scored (a re-score would need `--replace`, which overwrites the stored card).
- **What it does not test.** The weight (10), positive weight (3) and ignore distance (1.0 m) are single first values (a sweep would be a second step); the `line_boundary` arm; a Dice without tolerance (arm T, dropped); a longer schedule; a label fix (registration) or a higher-resolution line head (the Image Encoder's and the lane project's questions); the recall of the zero-crossing extraction `lane_divider:zc` (recall 0.09, an extraction of the distance field, not the presence head); TEST.
