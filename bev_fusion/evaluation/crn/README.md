# evaluation/crn - CRN on our frames: the object row of the SOA table (card t26, G0)

CRN (camera + radar, R50, 4 key frames; third-party code in `~/work/CRN`, WSL, env `crn`) run on the 1706 samples of 43 of our VAL scenes that the lane project prepared (CRN info file, radar PV), its boxes converted into our ego frame and our GT, so that `bev_suite.py front` can score it next to our networks on the same 1620 frames (those with a previous and a next key frame). Why and what is claimed: `../../experiments/t26_one_long_run/experiment.md` (section 1, "References"); what was found before building: `../../records/crn_feasibility_2026-11-14.md`. The commands in order: `RUN.md`. Nothing in `~/work/CRN` is edited; the only addition there is the folder `data/nuScenes/` of symlinks (made 2026-11-15: `samples`, `maps`, `v1.0-trainval`, `radar_pv_filter`, `radar_bev_filter` -> `~/data/nuscenes_full/...`, and `nuscenes_infos_val.pkl` -> `nuscenes_infos_val_crn.pkl`; no sweeps: the infos hold none).

Pitfalls found while building: (1) CRN's "ego" is the MEAN of its six camera ego poses (the info's `ego2global_*` is averaged over the cameras, the quaternion elementwise, not unit): the converter goes through the global json and normalises the quaternion. (2) Building CRN's model runs mmdet's ResNet init, which reads the torchvision ResNet-50 weights from `~/.cache/torch/hub/checkpoints/resnet50-0676ba61.pth` (present since 2026-10-12; a missing cache would start a download: stop then). (3) `torch` 1.9.1 has TF32 ON by default for matmul and cuDNN: the launcher sets both explicitly and records the values it read back. (4) The model's own timing hooks (CRN code) switch on after sample 100 and print a block at sample 1000: harmless, the launcher measures its own ms per sample. (5) A perfect CRN (CRN's own training boxes through the whole chain, `--oracle`) scores mAP2 0.987 against the GT on the 1620 frames (AP@0.5 0.982): the ceiling of the label differences (ambulances / police cars and wheelchairs are CRN "ignore", the GT's stationary rule puts parked cars at one world pose), not an error of the chain.
