**What it does**: a 2D displacement per BEV pillar (0.25 m cells, 64 m square) for the next 0.5 s from 5 ego-compensated LiDAR sweeps, with a PointPillars-style encoder and a U-Net with separated spatial / temporal convolutions. Training needs no flow labels: (1) structural consistency, a symmetric nearest-neighbour (chamfer-type) distance between the point cloud moved by the pillar motion and the next real sweep, each point weighted by a probabilistic static mask exp(-alpha max(|F| - tau, 0)) (alpha 0.1, tau 5) so that noisy static pillars count less; (2) cross-sensor regularisation (weight 0.01): an L1 between the image flow induced by the predicted pillar motion and the camera object-motion flow (PWC-Net flow, ego-induced part removed); (3) smoothness (weight 1, small effect). The camera is training-only.

**Results that matter** (nuScenes, motion error in the page's metric, 1 s horizon, static / slow <= 5 m/s / fast > 5 m/s, mean): self-supervised 0.162 / 0.697 / 3.550; fine-tuned with labels 0.0245 / 0.229 / 0.778; MotionNet supervised 0.0239 / 0.247 / 1.011. So the label-free net is worse than supervised on static and slow, and better than the supervised pre-trained FlowNet3D / HPLFlowNet on fast; as an initialisation it beats supervised-only on the fast group. Ablation (0.5 s, fast group): structural consistency only 1.94, full 1.71. About 20 ms per sweep on a TITAN RTX; training 8 GPUs, 200 epochs.

**Transferable technique**: forward a current BEV by the predicted flow and compare it with the observed next BEV (our check (b) in plan 10.1): the weighted chamfer is the point-cloud form, a warped-raster comparison is the dense form. The static-mask weighting and the smoothness term are needed; the labels come in only as a fine-tune. The result also says what to expect: a label-free flow is good enough to initialise, not to replace GT velocity.

**Cost and fit**: LiDAR (our radar is far sparser); the camera flow term needs an optical-flow net.

**Doubts**: the page does not state units of the error table; pillar-level chamfer on 5 sweeps of LiDAR is much denser data than our radar or camera BEV.
