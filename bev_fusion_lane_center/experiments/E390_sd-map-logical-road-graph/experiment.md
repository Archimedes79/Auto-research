## 1. Card text
### 1.1 Inventory: what the simulated SD map holds (read from the code, 2026-11-21)
- **The source is the lane GT** (`GtGenerator/lane_gt.py` `load(token)`, l6 lane_center, signature 9ad1d4ce30 as the token caches), ego "lane" frame (x right, y forward, rear axle), window |x|, |y| <= 50 m. Per frame: `pieces` (lane-level, 0.5 m points in travel order, `succ` / `pred` / `left` / `right` / `opposite`, `road`, `lane_count`, `lane_index` (0 = the LEFTMOST lane: next to the opposite direction in right-hand Boston, the KERB lane in left-hand Singapore), `tier`), `events`, `roads`, `areas.intersection`, `ego_path`, `location`.
- **`roads` is already a logical road graph** (`GtGenerator/road_graph.py`): `id`, `kind` road | intersection, `lane_max`, `pieces`, `succ`, `opposite`. A ROAD node is ONE direction of travel; the antiparallel carriageway is a separate road linked by `opposite`; an INTERSECTION node = a connected intersection area plus its connectors. VAL: 873 frames, 22 scenes, 10.85 nodes per frame (max 22), 10130 successor links; TRAIN: 8414 frames, 212 scenes, 13.04 nodes (max 32), 121359 links (`E390.build4_val / build4_train`).
- **Lanes per direction:** a piece's `lane_count` = 1 + its co-directional left / right hops (a geometric estimate); `lane_max` = the maximum (it can exceed every piece's count). This card's logical lane count of a road = the length-weighted median of its pieces' counts; VAL classes 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+ lanes: 3971 / 2399 / 537 / 0 (TRAIN 54238 / 22700 / 3642 / 3). The opposite direction's count = that of the `opposite` road (none in the window: one-way, out of view, or more than the 6 m search apart). No true count exists in nuScenes.
- **Connections:** `succ` (inside the window only), `pred` by inverting it, `opposite`; junctions through the intersection nodes. **The route** = the GT's tier-1 pieces (non-causal; `lane_route_data.route_of`), the SIMULATED NAVIGATION input of the SD map as in E389 (D127 / D128) (GtGenerator/AGENTS.md: tier 1 is never an input of a perception model), lifted to the road level.
- **The existing SD paths:** `sd_lib.build_sd` / `degrade_sd` / `scene_pose_error`, `map_sim.make_map` (E273, lane-level links: must not feed an SD head, E374), E389's route samples. None keeps road-level ids or links.
- **Gaps (said plainly):** no road class, speed, width, turn restrictions or names; links stop at the 50 m window ('further roads' beyond it would need a re-crop of the global graph: not in step 1); counts are geometric estimates; no topology error model (wrong / extra links, outdated map: step 3); the route is non-causal.

### 1.2 The encoding (no perception input; `training/data/lane_sdgraph_data.py` v4, `training/grid/jsdgraph.py`)
- **Tokens:** one per node of `g['roads']` in the window (at most 64, the nearest the ego; no frame reaches the cap). **A ROAD** = its DIVIDER-SIDE EDGE line: the chain of its lanes without a co-directional neighbour on the divider side (left in right-hand Boston, right in left-hand Singapore), offset by half a lane (1.75 m) towards the divider; a two-way road's two directions share one line and the line does not move with the lane count (tested on a hand frame, with the leftmost-lane mutant caught). **An INTERSECTION** = only the stretches of its pieces INSIDE it (`segments[].in_intersection`) whose midpoint lies in ITS area (`areas.intersection`; a GT piece runs along up to five nodes and is listed on each: v3's intersection tokens held whole road pieces, v4's the stretches of a second intersection), with entries / exits; a ROAD's line likewise uses only its outer stretches and prefers the pieces of no other road; its features count APPROACHES (entries within 6 m grouped), not connectors (their number and length grow with the lane counts). Node features in the node's OWN frame, all from the INPUT map: length, heading change, curvature, kind, approaches, extent, in / out degree of the input links, near count, the input route flag and its RANK among the visible route nodes (no gap where a node is hidden), the input lane class (1, 2, 3, 4+, unknown), the opposite road's input class.
- **Relations (from the INPUT map):** successor, predecessor, opposite, junction sibling, near (within 30 m, linked or not). **Pair geometry** (rigid-motion invariant): the attach point = the closest of (an entry of b, a point of a) and (an exit of a, a point of b) (a successor can branch off the SIDE of a carriageway); 94.2 % of the VAL links lie within the 10 m gap of the P2 candidates (v6).
- **Encoder:** a token MLP (-> d 64) and 2 attention layers with a per-head bias from [relation one-hot ; pair geometry]; absent (missing) segments and padding masked. **Invariance by construction:** no absolute ego-frame coordinate enters the encoder; the ego-frame pose of each node is kept beside it for fusion.
- **The association (D135, P6 head):** the perceived side is played by PROXIES = the true lane centre points (every 4 m, max 160 per frame, the nearest the ego) with their travel direction, taken ONLY from the pieces that the GT gives to exactly one node (a piece on several nodes has several right answers): 9114 of 13080 VAL pieces (70 %), 60941 VAL proxies, 27493 of them on intersections. For each (proxy, segment) the relative geometry in the frame of the segment's nearest point (distance, heading cos / sin, along, lateral, attach position, kind) is computed; the head scores [segment embedding ; geometry] for every present segment plus an 'unassigned' slot (from the nearest segment's distance and alignment); softmax over them. The segment identity is the TARGET only; the proxies never enter the encoder. A joint rigid motion of map and proxies changes nothing (tested); a map-only pose error moves the map against them (tested).
- **Training (multi-task; the probe heads are tools of this card):** P1 (lane class), P2 (link or not over all candidates), P3 (on route over the P3 set), P6 (cross-entropy over the present segments + unassigned). TRAIN error mix, one draw per frame per epoch: count error {0, 0.1, 0.3} and 'unknown' at p 0.1 half the time; link drop {0, 0.1, 0.3}; route hide {0, 0.3}; smooth deformation {0, 1, 2} m (two plane waves per axis, wavelength 60 m: joints stay joined); Douglas-Peucker U(0, 1.5) m; map-only pose error {0, 1, 2} (m / deg); node drop {0, 0.1}; a joint rigid frame transform (+-10 m, +-30 deg). 12 epochs, Adam 1e-3, batch 32 frames, CPU 2 threads; the final model is read.

### 1.3 The probes (VAL 873 frames; ONE generator per frame for all levels (count / drop / hide / node-drop / pose uniforms first, the deformation last), so the levels are nested and paired; the same draws for g, n and the nulls; a scene bootstrap B 1000)
- **Levels:** base = count error 0.3 + link drop 0.3 + route hide 0.3; ce0 / ce01 (count error 0 / 0.1); s1 / s2 / s5 (+ smooth deformation 1 / 2 / 5 m: 5 m is beyond the TRAIN range, an extrapolation); pose1 / pose2 / pose5 (+ map-only pose error, 5 beyond the TRAIN range); nd (+ 20 % of the segments missing); d01 (link drop 0.1, reported). 'Unknown' counts are a TRAIN error only.
- **P1:** accuracy over the present ROAD nodes of exact length >= 10 m. **P2:** AUC (AP reported) on the candidate pairs (input gap <= 10 m, not an input link, both present): positives = the dropped links, negatives = the non-links; deciding set without the pairs whose two nodes both carry an input route flag. **P3:** route flags hidden on 30 % of the route nodes; AUC over the unflagged nodes within 30 m of a flagged one. **P4:** 10 joint rigid transforms per VAL frame of the base input; the largest embedding change / mean abs embedding; flips counted where the margin > 1e-4. **P5:** the bar table (paired levels). **P6:** accuracy over all proxies (the segment, or unassigned when its segment is missing); also the accuracy on the proxies with a present segment, the none recall (missing segment -> unassigned) and the false-unassigned rate.
- **Measured null pattern (v6, said before training):** the smooth deformation barely moves P1 / P2 / P3 (it keeps the relative geometry: P5 on s1 - s5 is close to vacuous for them; for P6 it matters: nearest rule 0.923 / 0.885 / 0.855 / 0.786 at base / s1 / s2 / s5); the map-only pose error moves only P6 (nearest rule 0.923 / 0.863 / 0.818 / 0.724 at 0 / 1 / 2 / 5 m; road proxies hold up, intersection proxies fall from 0.985 to 0.586); with 20 % of the segments missing the nearest rule leaves 0.189 of their proxies unassigned and puts the rest on a neighbour (the wrong overwrite D135 forbids); the logistic null almost never refuses (its fitted threshold -9.5: none recall 0.040). Bootstrap resamples left out as undefined in the nulls: 20000 = the none recall of the two P6 nulls at the 10 levels without missing segments (undefined there by construction).
