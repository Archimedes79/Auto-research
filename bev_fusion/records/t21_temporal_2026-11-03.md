# t21 one-frame temporal fusion - record (2026-11-03, Mirko's order D51)

## The question (Mirko, verbatim in D51)
"For me it looks like you do an acttive loop unrolling by feeding in 3 images over time, but on the temporal fusion I asked for one version for data from t-1 and t and another version entirelly predicting. How to reduce the buffer to one frame and to establish a recurrency from t-1 to t. ... without feeding 3 images in?" - and: "Train and test it, but rather fix it than not using it..."

## What the system does today (read from the code, `t20.base_s0`)
- Input: `n_cam 3`, `cam_stride 2`: the camera window is the current frame t and the frames t - 0.167 s and t - 0.333 s (12 Hz sweeps, every second one). `model.camera_bevs` lifts all three into BEV on every call (lift + lift2 = 28.6 ms of 102.1 ms per frame, idle GPU, three dts).
- `cam_temporal` window_memory (`temporal_fusion.py`): the current BEV + the older frames of the window, each splatted once into the current time with its own ego steps (moving content by a learned velocity), + a static world-aligned memory (the only recurrent state: `state["cam"] = {M, W, pose}`, carried from keyframe to keyframe, 0.5 s apart, in clips of `seq_len 3` with BPTT). Moving content has no memory: its history is the window.
- The motion block reads two frames (current, oldest) + radar.
- The 'three time offsets' of the timing line are a different thing: the heads decode dt -0.5, 0, +0.5 s (called three times: mover 16.5 + sem head 4.9 + obj head 8.2 ms); a deployment that needs only dt 0 saves most of that.
- So Mirko's reading is right for the input: three images are lifted per call, although a stream could lift one per step; the design intent of plan 7.1 ('each branch fuses its current planes with its predicted state, recurrent frame by frame') was kept only for the static memory, the window was added because chained map splats lag moving objects (0.74 m chained against 0.14 m from the original, plan 7.1).

## What t21 builds (card `experiments/t21_one_frame_temporal/experiment.md`)
- `cam_buffer: "pair"`: the buffer is the previous frame's raw camera BEV (t-1), carried in the state; one new image per step; the batch path with n_cam 2 is the same function (equivalence test).
- `cam_buffer: "recurrent"`: the buffer is the previous frame's fused state, moved to t by the same splat and fused with the new frame; frames of a training sample are unrolled oldest to newest inside the model (2 recurrent steps from cold), deployment streams one frame at a time.
- Arms a (pair), b (recurrent), c (control: the window of t20, same warm start, steps, lr). Warm start from `t20.base_s0`, 2000 steps.
