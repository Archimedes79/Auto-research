## 2. The BEV topics

**Lane performance.** Evidence on the transform: the synthetic oracle lift (exact depth) reaches lanes 0.99 / vehicles 1.00 / lanes at 40-60 m 0.81 (`tests/test_oracle_lift.py`), so the lift geometry is not the cap. The measured lever is the resolution of the image features: t7's stride-4 side branch (L4) raised the lane divider IoU@0.5 from 0.18 to 0.246 / 0.221 (+0.065 / +0.042, both intervals exclude 0, road divider IoU@1 +0.11); its verdict was never written. **[Q5] Mirko (2026-11-03): "You said lanes suffer from bev splat res and they need 0.5m res in world."** Checked in the code: the fused BEV grid IS 0.5 m (120 x 104) and the stride-1 detail path is on in `t16.full` (`trunk_detail` 32), so the output side has the resolution. The coarse side is the camera evidence that is splatted into it: the features come at image stride 16 (`dualbev`) and 8 (`bevfusion`), and one stride-16 pixel is about 0.57 m wide at 20 m and 1.7 m at 60 m (computed from the t7 card's 0.14 m at stride 4 and 20 m), i.e. coarser than a 0.5 m cell and than a lane line. That is the splat resolution; the measured cure is t7's stride-4 branch (L4). It is OFF in `t16.full` (`lane_branch` empty), and the model refuses it with `camera_features` 'dual' (`model.py:228` demands 'dualbev'). So the bundle changes the lift input: L4 extended to 'dual', switched on, with a test. That is the transform change; no other. Before the code: a one-hour lane diagnostic on `t16.full_s0` (CPU / idle slot): lane IoU by range (0-20 / 20-40 / 40-60 m), train against val, and the ceiling of a 0.5 m grid (GT vs GT downsampled). Rule written now: if the ceiling is below 0.8 the grid caps the number and the report says so; if the near field (0-20 m) is also poor, L4 is the lever (it is on in the bundle either way). **[Q5]**

**Multiple images, no recurrency?** The network has both. Recurrent part: a world-aligned memory carried from keyframe to keyframe (`cam_temporal` = `window_memory`, state in `BEVFusionModel.forward`, trained on clips with BPTT). Window part: three camera frames (the current one and two older, stride 2 at 12 Hz) enter each step; the motion block M needs two frames for its velocity field, and the memory holds BEV features, not pixels. The block swap `window_memory` / `ego_gru` / `none` (t2) was a TIE at 3000 steps. So a single-frame-plus-recurrence input is a legitimate simplification, but it is an ablation, not a fix: kept as is in t20 (config switch `n_cam`), no extra experiment. **[Q7] Mirko: "Why? Explain."** Why unchanged: (1) the lane weakness is a resolution problem (Q5), and three frames do not change the lane feature stride, so dropping them cannot fix it; (2) the window frames are the input of the motion block M (two frames give its velocity field), and the memory gave +0.02 outside the view in the t1 record (combined system against the window-only baseline); (3) the block swap `window_memory` / `ego_gru` / `none` was a TIE at 3000 steps (t3), so no variant is shown better; (4) the bundle must isolate the lane lever and the bug fixes, and a changed input would be a third change at once. A one-frame arm is the config switch `n_cam` if you want that ablation after the baseline.

**Heads.** Today: the 0.5 m map head already gives 5 areas, 2 line masks + 2 distance fields, 3 dense object maps, and the object head gives vehicles / pedestrians (`plan/head_design.md` section 1). So "0.5 m: lane markings, semantic map, pedestrians, vehicles" is the current head; the new part is **one pyramid level down: a 1 m centre-lane head** (my reading **[Q4]**). Own code, own GT: the head is built in `bev_fusion/training/` (a small decoder on the trunk's 1 m level: centre-line presence, offset, direction bins). It is NOT taken from the lane project: that is a separate codebase (a head for the frozen GaussianCaR features) and the two projects do not depend on each other (Spurmitte lead chat after Mirko's word, 2026-11-03; its grid head `training/grid/` and the E271 jobs on GPU slot 0 are theirs and are not touched). Its GT, **[Q3] Mirko: "Yes, but ist it there already? Saw it. If you want to recalculate to 1m, then do it for now like before on 0.5m"**: it is there in part. autogt v8 has the centre-line mask, one averaged direction and the exact polylines (`autogt/nuscenes_gt.py` lines 291-306), but no tokens and no links. End to end (D47): no rule step on the training path. **[Q4] answered by Mirko (chat, 2026-11-03), verbatim: "There is a lane Center Head on CaR. Do not Touch. You have a grid based in your project. Touch it."** My reading: the lane project's centre-line head on GaussianCaR stays untouched (as in section 3a); the grid-based head of THIS project is the one to change. That head is the dense map head (`SemanticHead` on `TimedHead`, `training/bev_trunk.py`, layout `losses.layer_spec`) with its unfinished centre-line part (`centrelines=True` = mask + (cos, sin); the CentreHead of plan 8.5 / D26 is not built, `head_design.md` section 5 gap 1). So the 1 m centre-lane head is built INTO this grid-based map head, not beside it: `layer_spec` gets the centre-line channels at the trunk's 1 m level (presence, distance field, direction bins, split / merge heat), the loss goes into `BEVLoss`, the model option replaces the old `centrelines` design (the old option stays readable for old runs, as `hygiene_2026_11_02` does). The code of the lane project is only read as a model (copying a function into a bev_fusion file is allowed, the workspace rule on duplication); nothing in `bev_fusion_lane_center/` or on GaussianCaR changes.

**Head spec by kind (Q4 rest). Mirko: "Why explizit channels? maybe think abstract?"** Right: not a hand-written channel list. The head reads a layer table, one row per output = (layer, kind, level), and the kind decides channels, target and loss, so a new layer is a row, not new code. Kind `area`: 1 logit (BCE + soft IoU, as today). Kind `polyline`: presence + truncated distance field + direction (the axis as cos 2a, sin 2a; or B bins, B a config number), with the dilated Dice + Huber + direction loss; one function for lane dividers, road dividers and centre-lines (a divider row can leave the direction out). Kind `dense_box`: the footprint, as today. `level` (0.5 m or 1 m) says where the head sits and from which resolution the target is pooled, so "the 1 m centre-line head" is one row `(centreline, polyline, 1 m)` and the heads that exist are rows at 0.5 m. `losses.layer_spec` is the start of this (one channel per entry); the change is that an entry carries kind and level and channels, targets and losses are built from the kind. Old runs keep their layouts (they evaluate from their pinned code). If a graph with splits and merges ever comes, it is one more kind (`graph`), not a special case.

**Bug fixes in the bundle.** The correctness items of `records/open_findings_2026-11-02.md`: #1 strict dt, #2 `vel_known`, #3 `w_obj` as a weight, #4 dead radar-slot branch and `radar_stationary` off (the head layout changes once, at this baseline), #6 float32 splat positions (all built behind `hygiene_2026_11_02`, now ON); #5 BatchNorm over dt (5-minute GPU measurement first; if the statistics are a mixture, per-dt statistics); #7 resume details (RNG saved, code-snapshot check); #8 radar cache signature. NOT in the bundle: the motion block's own learning rate (t18.fix was CUT by its rule; a tuning, not a bug). Whatever the short training finds goes in as well.
