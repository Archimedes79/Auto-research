# 2006.11275 CenterPoint: center-based 3D detection and tracking

**What it does**: Objects are points. A heatmap head finds centres on the BEV map, regression heads give sub-cell offset, size, orientation and velocity at the centre cell. Tracking is greedy closest-point matching on the predicted velocity. An optional second stage refines with point features.

**Results that matter**: nuScenes, single model 65.5 NDS and 63.8 AMOTA (lidar; abstract page). No camera or radar numbers.

**Transferable technique**: the head is independent of the sensor; it works on any BEV feature map with 0.5 m cells (the heatmap is class-wise Gaussian, peaks found by 3x3 max pooling, no NMS). Velocity regression per centre uses two frames in the lidar case; radar Doppler would give it directly. Used as the head in BEVFusion (SN20), CRN and BEVerse-style detectors.

**Cost and fit**: a few 3x3 convs per head; negligible against the trunk. Fits our "no matching" rule if the peak extraction is max-pool based.

**Doubts**: the Gaussian radius and the heatmap focal loss need tuning for small BEV objects (pedestrians at 0.5 m cells span one or two cells); camera-only versions are weak in depth.
