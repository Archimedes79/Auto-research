**What it does** [checked]: occupancy-flow head for a camera network. Voxel features from 0 to 4 m height are averaged into a foreground-focused BEV feature and downsampled. The PREVIOUS frame's BEV feature is warped to the current frame with the camera parameters (the page does not mention the ego pose), then each current feature is compared with the warped features at predefined hypothetical offsets by cosine similarity: a cost volume. It is concatenated with the current BEV feature and the flow head predicts from the result; the previous features are cached, so no second inference. Flow is predicted as classification + regression: the flow range is split into bins from the training min / max, the head gives a probability per bin and the flow is the probability-weighted sum of the bin centres; loss = L2 + cosine similarity on the regression, cross-entropy on the GT bin. Not iterative: one feed-forward pass.

**Results that matter** [checked, tables 2 and 3]: OpenOcc (occupancy + flow): ALOcc-Flow-3D Occ score 43.0, mAVE 0.556, mAVETP 0.481, RayIoU 41.9; ALOcc-Flow-2D 42.1 / 0.537 / 0.427 / 40.5. Occ3D without mask: ALOcc-3D RayIoU 43.7, mIoU 38.0. Speed RTX 4090 batch 1: 6.0 FPS (3D), 8.2 (2D), 30.5 (2D-mini). It placed second in the CVPR24 Occupancy and Flow Prediction Competition (page text).

**Transferable technique**: (1) offset hypotheses with a similarity score = the dense counterpart of our hypothesis scores, but the offsets are a fixed set around zero after the ego warp: it cannot reach large displacements, ours spans v_m learned per cell; (2) classification into bins + expected value (flow = sum p_b c_b) is the "soft hypotheses" idea of our softmax over v_m, except that ALOcc collapses the distribution to its mean before warping (a car and its occluder at two speeds average out), while our splat keeps all hypotheses weighted; (3) the cosine-similarity term in the loss is cheap and direction-aware.

**Cost and fit**: 2D-mini 30.5 FPS on a 4090 is a hint that the cost-volume part is light; camera only, no radar, no Doppler.

**Doubts**: flow evaluated as mAVE per occupied voxel against GT from tracked boxes; no ablation of the cost volume alone in the part read; the ego-pose statement is missing in the text (the warp uses "camera parameters").
