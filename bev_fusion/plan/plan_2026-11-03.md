## 2. The BEV topics

**Lane performance.** Evidence on the transform: the synthetic oracle lift (exact depth) reaches lanes 0.99 / vehicles 1.00 / lanes at 40-60 m 0.81 (`tests/test_oracle_lift.py`), so the lift geometry is not the cap. The measured lever is the resolution of the image features: t7's stride-4 side branch (L4) raised the lane divider IoU@0.5 from 0.18 to 0.246 / 0.221 (+0.065 / +0.042, both intervals exclude 0, road divider IoU@1 +0.11); its verdict was never written. My proposal: **no new BEV transform**; the lane lever of the bundle is the L4 branch plus the 1 m centre-line head. Before the code: a one-hour lane diagnostic on `t16.full_s0` (CPU / idle slot): lane IoU by range (0-20 / 20-40 / 40-60 m), train against val, and the ceiling of a 0.5 m grid (GT vs GT downsampled). Rule written now: if the ceiling is below 0.8 the grid caps the number and the report says so; if the near field (0-20 m) is also poor, L4 is the lever (it is on in the bundle either way). **[Q5]**

**Multiple images, no recurrency?** The network has both. Recurrent part: a world-aligned memory carried from keyframe to keyframe (`cam_temporal` = `window_memory`, state in `BEVFusionModel.forward`, trained on clips with BPTT). Window part: three camera frames (the current one and two older, stride 2 at 12 Hz) enter each step; the motion block M needs two frames for its velocity field, and the memory holds BEV features, not pixels. The block swap `window_memory` / `ego_gru` / `none` (t2) was a TIE at 3000 steps. So a single-frame-plus-recurrence input is a legitimate simplification, but it is an ablation, not a fix: kept as is in t20 (config switch `n_cam`), no extra experiment **[Q7]**.

**Heads.** Today: the 0.5 m map head already gives 5 areas, 2 line masks + 2 distance fields, 3 dense object maps, and the object head gives vehicles / pedestrians (`plan/head_design.md` section 1). So "0.5 m: lane markings, semantic map, pedestrians, vehicles" is the current head; the new part is **one pyramid level down: a 1 m centre-lane head** (my reading **[Q4]**). Reuse, do not rewrite: the lane project's grid head (`bev_fusion_lane_center/training/grid/head.py, targets.py, losses.py`, oracle G0 passes) is exactly a 1 m centre-lane head; E271.a_s0 is training it on the builder features right now (GPU slot 0, since 07:49, 4000 steps; not touched). In bev_fusion it reads the trunk's 1 m level. Its GT: the lane graph in the BEV frame. autogt has no such layer (D47 (b) asked for one); the lane project's `make_graph_gt.py` builds it in the lane frame (x right, y forward, +-50 m). Step: an autogt sidecar `lane_graph` (new GT version, `versions.csv`) converting to the BEV frame (x forward 0-60, y left +-26) with the conformance test of D47 (b): converted back, it reproduces the lane project's `graph_gt` on the 873 VAL frames. End to end (D47): no rule step on the training path; sparsification stays in the lane project (E271 stage 2).

**Bug fixes in the bundle.** The correctness items of `records/open_findings_2026-11-02.md`: #1 strict dt, #2 `vel_known`, #3 `w_obj` as a weight, #4 dead radar-slot branch and `radar_stationary` off (the head layout changes once, at this baseline), #6 float32 splat positions (all built behind `hygiene_2026_11_02`, now ON); #5 BatchNorm over dt (5-minute GPU measurement first; if the statistics are a mixture, per-dt statistics); #7 resume details (RNG saved, code-snapshot check); #8 radar cache signature. NOT in the bundle: the motion block's own learning rate (t18.fix was CUT by its rule; a tuning, not a bug). Whatever the short training finds goes in as well.
