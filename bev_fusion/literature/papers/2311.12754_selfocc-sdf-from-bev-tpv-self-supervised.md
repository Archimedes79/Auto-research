**What it does**: Image features are lifted to a BEV or TPV (three-plane) feature map. A two-layer MLP over the feature sampled at a 3D point (BEV: separate MLP per height slice; TPV: broadcast-sum of the three planes, one MLP) outputs a signed distance, a colour and optional semantic logits; points off the grid use bicubic interpolation. SDF is turned into rendering weights and rendered to depth and RGB of the previous and next frames (MVS-style: photometric dissimilarity of warped neighbour frames over several depth proposals, weighted by the rendering weights). Occupancy at inference is the sign of the SDF (0-threshold); rendering is not needed at test time.

**Results that matter** (Occ3D-nuScenes, self-supervised, ResNet50, 384x800): TPV variant IoU 45.01 / mIoU 9.30, BEV variant IoU 44.33 / mIoU 6.76. Loss weights: eikonal 0.1, Hessian 0.1, colour 0.1, sparsity 0.001, semantic 0.1 (only with pseudo-labels). Ablation Table 5 (half schedule, rows as extracted, tick layout ambiguous): occupancy IoU 18.90 -> 36.68 -> 38.43 and mIoU 2.81 -> 6.10 -> 6.94 over three loss configurations; the text says SDF beats density for occupancy and depth but is worse for novel-view depth synthesis (smoothness prior). 12 epochs on 8x RTX 3090, nuScenes under two days. Inference speed, params and memory: not reported.

**Transferable technique**: (1) the decoder pattern "BEV feature + height -> small MLP -> SDF" with the Eikonal L_E = | ||grad s|| - 1 |, a Hessian L1 smoothness term and a one-sided sparsity term max(-s,0) as the geometric regularisers; (2) rendering only as a training loss. For a front-camera + radar BEV: the MVS photometric term needs neighbour frames and poses (available in nuScenes).

**Cost and fit**: training is heavy (8 GPUs, ~2 days); on a 3060 it needs fewer rays and smaller images. No lane relevance directly. Absolute mIoU (9.3) is far below supervised methods (RenderOcc 2D-label 23.9), so self-supervision from photometry alone is weak.

**Doubts**: rays per image and samples per ray not stated; ablation table could not be mapped to loss rows from the extraction; pose from ground truth in the main results; no downstream seg / detection benefit shown.
