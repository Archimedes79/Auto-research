Reading (BEV system chat, mine, not Mirko's words; he can correct it): (1) camera: ONE image per step is the only image input; the past enters only as the state of the previous step at the level of BEV features (the fused output of the previous step, moved with the ego motion), no second image, no window of 3 frames. In the code this is `cam_buffer: "recurrent"` (model.py, t21 arm R); the pair arm (P / P4: current frame plus the previous raw BEV as a second input) is the control, not the target. (2) radar: ONE scan = the sweep of the five radars at the camera time (`radar_window` about 0.08 s) instead of all sweeps of the last 0.5 s; the radar past enters the same way, recursively at feature level (`rad_temporal`: radar memory with the Doppler static gate, built but never trained successfully). (3) At run time the network gets one frame and one scan per step and carries its state (stream mode).
Confirmed: 2026-11-13, Mirko in chat, answer to the two points of the reading ("does the recursive form replace P4 as the target, and does 'one scan' mean one sweep of all five radars at the camera time?"): "Ja, beides". The reading stands as written.
Measured the same day (studies/radar_sweep_density.py, records/radar_sweep_density_2026-11-13.txt, nuScenes mini, 384 keyframes, 5,138 observed vehicles / pedestrians in the grid): one sweep carries 54 radar points in the grid per keyframe against 300 for 0.5 s (5.6 times fewer); a vehicle has at least one point in 64 % of the cases (0.5 s: 82 %), 1.7 points on average (8.9); a pedestrian 29 % (55 %), 0.5 points (2.8). So the radar memory has to rebuild what the 0.5 s accumulation gives today.
Consequence: nothing applied yet, no training started. Next: a card (hypothesis, prediction, rule) before any run; a CPU measurement of what one sweep carries against 0.5 s of sweeps; the control and the new arm are scored on the current GT (front v13, lane l6, D83), so the control is re-evaluated or retrained, not read from old numbers. Points to confirm with Mirko are listed in the chat reply of 2026-11-13: the recursive form replaces P4 as the target, and "one scan" means one sweep of all five radars.
