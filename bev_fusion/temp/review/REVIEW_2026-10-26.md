## Checked and fine
lift_pd depth-bin convention (bin k = [1 + 0.5 k, 1.5 + 0.5 k) = DualBEV's label rule floor((d - 1) / 0.5)); radar column indices and
the sensor -> current-ego chain; backward ego steps keep v and omega physical with negative dt; box corner construction in gt_bev;
instance layer dtype (int32, survives the float16 cache); fusion modality dropout never zeroes both; TemporalFusion re-centring,
memory read geometry and update; the S5c refiner (residual targets, IoU quality, zero start); metrics (matching, AP, TP errors).
