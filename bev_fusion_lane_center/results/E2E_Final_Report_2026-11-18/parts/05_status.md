# 5. End-to-end status: what is differentiable and which hard steps are named

**In short.** The delivered graph of the champion is one network read out by thresholds: the frozen lane head B' makes the tokens, one token head with three self-attention layers wires them (a softmax per source with a none slot) and types them (a softmax over seven classes), and a loss on the output reaches the head. Five hard steps of the previous end-to-end system (E324 s) are gone; what remains is named below and is **not** hidden: a compute budget, a gate, four output thresholds and the pyramid of the lane head, which the loss does not reach (the node head of B' is trained in the loop since D120: two stages). Status of this chapter: S4 measured (E372, S4-HOLDS), the champion since D120.

**What "end to end" means here, and what it does not.** From the cached camera-and-radar features to the graph, every learned step is differentiable and trained on a loss at the graph level, except that the pyramid of the lane head B' is frozen (trained earlier on its own loss) and its node head keeps that loss in stage 2 (the champion is trained in two stages). The three hard items that remain in the forward path are the top-K, the gate and the thresholds of the read-out; each has a price that is measured or named (sections 7 and 13). A rule step in the delivered graph is reported every time: there is none besides these.

**S4, the step that closes the staged gap (E372): measured, S4-HOLDS on both seeds, the champion since D120.** The node head of B' runs inside the training step, the loss of the token head reaches it, and B' keeps its own loss so that it does not drift; three arms (z: the same fine-tune with B' frozen; b: B' on its own loss only; s4), two seeds, VAL 873 frames, topo F1 read live at the D115 cell: z 0.6685 / 0.6763, b 0.6663 / 0.6787, s4 0.6682 / 0.6813, the staged champion 0.6679 / 0.6762. s4 - z -0.0003 / +0.0049 (the interval of seed 1, [+0.0020, +0.0079], is above 0, that of seed 0 is not), s4 - staged champion +0.0002 / +0.0051; junction F1 +0.0021 / +0.0029; the loss of the head made 0.19 - 0.40 of the updates of the node head, the tokens moved on every frame (median 5 cm). By the written rule of the card the verdict is S4-HOLDS (quality kept, the gradient reaches the node head), not S4-GAINS (it needs s4 - z of at least +0.01 on both seeds); the lead took it as the champion (D120, with D109: the end-to-end variant at a tie).
