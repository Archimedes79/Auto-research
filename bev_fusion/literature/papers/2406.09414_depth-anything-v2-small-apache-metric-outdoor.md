**What it does** [checked]: monocular depth model (the DINOv2-based encoder and DPT-type decoder are from memory, not on the pages read). Three changes against V1 (abstract): synthetic images replace labelled real ones, a larger teacher, student models trained on large-scale pseudo-labelled real images; "much finer and more robust depth predictions"; more than 10x faster than recent Stable-Diffusion-based models (no ms figure). Sizes: Small 24.8 M, Base 97.5 M, Large 335.3 M parameters, Giant 1.3 B (checkpoint "coming soon" on the README). The four pretrained models give RELATIVE depth (README: "robust relative depth estimation"; affine-invariant inverse depth is from memory, so a scale and shift is needed for metric use). Metric depth: six fine-tuned models in three sizes, indoor trained on Hypersim (max_depth 20 m) and outdoor trained on Virtual KITTI 2 (max_depth 80 m); the outdoor Small one is the candidate for us (80 m cap covers our 60 m BEV).

**Results that matter**: the abstract and README give no accuracy numbers. Related, from existing notes and one new page: (a) 2501.08118 (existing note): frozen DINOv2 + Metric3Dv2 depth in LSS gives +7.4 vehicle IoU and Simple-BEV with a Metric3Dv2 pseudo-lidar cloud +3 IoU over camera-only (abstract only). (b) Metric3D v2, https://github.com/YvanYin/Metric3D (README read): code under a 2-clause BSD licence, weights' licence not stated; checkpoints v2-S (DINOv2-reg ViT-Small), v2-L, v2-g and v1-T / v1-L; input crop (512, 1088), canonical focal length 1000 px, depth range (0.3, 150) m; KITTI AbsRel 0.044 (ViT-L) and 0.039 (ViT-giant2), NYU 0.047 and 0.045; no nuScenes numbers, no ms.

**Transferable technique**: three uses, cheapest first. (1) Frozen teacher for the depth head of the lift: its dense metric-ish depth replaces the sparse lidar target (BEVDepth-style CE over bins on the teacher's depth, as in 2501.08118); it is dense, so far road pixels get a target (lidar has almost none at 40-60 m on thin lines). (2) Frozen features (DINOv2-Small ViT-S/14 is stride 14, coarse for our stride-8 need; the existing note says so). (3) Scale alignment of a relative model with sparse metric anchors (radar points as in 2401.04325, or the ground-plane height h = 1.5 m for road pixels: depth = f*h/(v - v_horizon) is exact on a flat road).

**Cost and fit**: Small is 24.8 M, offline caching of teacher depth for the training frames costs one pass (no run-time cost at inference if used as a teacher); frozen use at run time adds a second backbone.

**Doubts**: nothing here is measured at 40-60 m, where monocular models are least reliable (my expectation, not verified); metric checkpoints are trained on synthetic Virtual KITTI 2 (domain gap to nuScenes unknown); the Apache-2.0 statement covers the Small model, the metric checkpoints' licence is unverified; Metric3D v2 weights licence unverified; no paper found that distils Depth Anything into a BEV lift without LiDAR (a search snippet of FreqPDE 2510.15385 mentions dense pseudo depth together with sparse LiDAR; not read).
