# 2602.23894 SelfOccFlow - label-free occupancy flow with a feature cosine-similarity cue in BEV

| | |

| groups | A5: 3 (the only label-free BEV flow cue that avoids a pretrained optical-flow net) |
| code / licence | no code link on the page |
| verdict | watch (the mechanism is simple and portable; evidence is a single preprint with a heavy pipeline) |

**What it does**: camera-only occupancy with separate static and dynamic signed-distance fields (the split from Grounded-SAM semantic masks, not from motion); fields of t-1, t, t+1 are ego-aligned and averaged, the dynamic field sampled at positions warped by the predicted flow (the flow is learned implicitly through the rendering losses). The explicit flow cue: BEV dynamic features at t are compared by cosine similarity with the features at t+-1 inside a 35 x 35 window; the arg-max displacement becomes a pseudo-label for the flow head (L1, weight 5.0), down-weighted where the forward-backward check disagrees.

**Results that matter** (nuScenes occupancy flow, RayIoU / mAVE): 41.39 / 1.31 against OccNet (3D-supervised) 39.66 / 1.61 and LetOccFlow-L 40.48 / 1.45; SemanticKITTI RayIoU 50.20 vs 47.06 (LetOccFlow). On KITTI-MOT the flow is worse than LetOccFlow (EPE 6.79 vs 3.49). 32.4 M parameters, 3.78 FPS on a V100 (LetOccFlow 253.3 M, 1.04 FPS). The paper states that removing the similarity loss prevents flow from being learned at all; no quantitative ablation of it.

**Transferable technique**: a flow pseudo-label from feature matching in a small window with a forward-backward consistency mask. On our synthetic gate it is the 'self-supervised (b)' arm without any optical-flow net: the features of the two frames are already in BEV, the window search is a local correlation volume (RAFT-like, one level).

**Cost and fit**: the window search is 35 x 35 correlations per cell (1225 channels at full grid), heavy at full resolution; at 0.5 m 120 x 104 a 9 x 9 window (+-2 m) would cover 15 m/s at 12 Hz (1.25 m per frame; scout estimate, not from the paper).

**Doubts**: needs LiDAR range supervision in the paper; semantic masks decide which field is dynamic; flow metrics on KITTI-MOT are weaker than the baseline; no code.
