# A. How to repeat the result

This chapter is reused in part from the report of 2026-11-16 (data, splits, metrics); the network, the training recipe and the numbers to hit are written for the champion when it exists.

### A.1 Data and splits
- **nuScenes v1.0-trainval** with the map expansion (four locations; raw data, the manifest and the download / check tools: `data/NuScenes/README.md` of the repository, the data itself is not in git). Frames are keyframes (2 Hz). **TRAIN 8,414, VAL 873 (22 scenes), TEST 833 (21 scenes)** = 10,120 keyframes of 255 scenes: the cached features exist for the first three download parts of the sensor data (split name `b123`, `gcar_pipeline2.sh`: validation = all 1,706 VAL + TEST frames, training = the rest), while the GT generator (chapter 1) covers all 34,149 keyframes of trainval; training and scoring use only the 10,120. Scene-disjoint, **not** location-disjoint (open check). Lists in `data/splits.json`. Select and tune on VAL only; TEST once per finished design with a logged OK (used once, E312).
- **Foundation, frozen:** GaussianCaR (the camera + radar BEV network of the project; it is run once to make the cache, its checkpoint and code are paths of the project, not part of this package). It is never trained here (D50). Its output is **cached once per frame** in `~/exp_gcar_b123/feats` (WSL; `data/README.md`): `dec32` = the 32 PCA channels of the decoder features (PCA basis from `gcar_tools.py pca`), `logit7` = the 7 map-segmentation logits (`fused32` is also stored, not used by the head) = **39 channels on a 200 x 200 grid, 0.5 m cells, 100 x 100 m around the ego**. `BuilderData` (`training/data/p16_data.py`) is the dataset class that serves one frame of a split (`train` / `val_clean` / `test_clean`) and normalises the channels with `refiner_stats_dec32_logit7.npz`. The head reads `x[:, :39]` only. Cache scripts: `training/data/prep/extract_features.py`, `gcar_tools.py`, `gcar_pipeline1.sh`, `gcar_pipeline2.sh` (long on one RTX 3060). **Cost of the foundation:** because it is cached, no millisecond figure of this report contains it; its own cost per frame was not measured in this project.
- **GT:** the lane GT l6 (chapter 1), derived data: junction GT (`jgt.py`), node / link targets (`qtargets.py`), junction map targets (`qjunction.py`), the simulated SD map (`map_sim.py`).

### A.2 Metrics: what the numbers mean
- **Topo F1** (TOPO-style directed reachability, `sparse_lanes/topo_metrics.py`): seeds every 5 m on the GT; from each seed the GT points reachable along the travel direction within 30 m ("holes") against the predicted points reachable from the matched predicted point ("marbles"; snap 2 m, direction within 60 degrees, one-to-one matching within 1 m); in plain words see "Terms used in the tables" on the start page. Responds to broken lanes, cut links, false branches, reversed direction. **The headline.**
- **Own lane (tier-1 F1):** the same on the GT points of tier 1 that are visible. **Lanes found**, **junction F1**, **far recall R@1** (recall at 1 m beyond 40 m), **dup_frac** (share of predicted length that runs parallel within 0.75 m of another piece) are the checks (`sparse_lanes/README.md`).
- **Maneuvers realised** (`jmaneuver.py`): a GT maneuver is one entry -> exit connection; it is realised when at least 90 % of its GT points (0.5 m spacing, at least 3) have a delivered polyline point within 1.5 m whose direction differs by at most 40 degrees ("is the lane drawn there, in that direction"). 10,991 maneuvers, counted over the 826 VAL frames with at least one GT maneuver ("junction frame, maneuver definition", E310, `jmaneuver.py`; the other 47 frames have none; chapter 3 uses another, node-count definition for its situation strata); "maneuvers realised" in percent is the share of these 10,991.
- **Protocol:** VAL only for selection; paired scene bootstrap (1000 draws) for differences; a difference inside the noise band is a tie; milliseconds per frame on an idle GPU, batch 1, after warm-up, median; every optimised path must reproduce the reference graphs. `evaluation/PROTOCOL.md` has the full rules.

### A.3 The network, the training recipe and the numbers to hit at each stage

**Modules (chapter 2).** Frozen lane head B' (`runs/e292_a_s<S>`: pyramid, node head, soft Matrix-NMS layer) -> cached tokens (top-K 512 by existence: node token 64-d, position, heading, existence, 8 flag logits, scales) -> embedding d = 64 -> three self-attention layers (4 heads, FFN 128, relative-geometry bias from 13 features of the pair, key bias log of the existence weight) -> the wiring layer (two heads: short and long chords, per-source softmax over the allowed destinations and one none slot) and the 7-way node-type softmax. The head has 0.127 M parameters.

**Training recipe.** One stage of the head on the cached tokens: `bash tools/env/_py14.sh jtoken_train.py --name e373_cb_s<S> --seed S --ident e292_a --none-weight 0.25 --steps 12000 --bs 8 --lr 1e-3 --workers 4` (the champion; the first recipe is the same command with the default none weight 1.0; AdamW, 5 % warm-up, cosine, roll-back guard, a checkpoint every 500 steps with resume). Loss per source and class: minus log of the summed probability of the positive destinations (the none slot when there is none), none weight 0.25 (champion) or 1.0 (first recipe), plus 0.5 x node-type cross-entropy plus 0.5 x L1 of the handle scales on the long GT links.

**Read-out and decode.** `jtoken_eval.py dump --run e373_cb_s<S> --seed S`, then `jtoken_eval.py score --run e373_cb_s<S> --seed S --link-thr 0.2 --keep-thr 0.6 --no-none-gate` (the first recipe: link 0.1); **the final decode of the champion** (E375, D115) is per class: the same `score` with `--link-thr 0.3 --link-thr-long 0.2 --keep-thr 0.6 --no-none-gate` (short lane links 0.3, long connector links 0.2; `sweep --grid perclass` makes the 36-cell table, `--ref-cell 0.2,0.2,0.6,off` adds the paired intervals). The single thresholds were chosen on the 291-frame TRAIN sample by the written rule of E366 (`jtoken_eval.py sweep --split train --every 3`). Timing: `jtoken_eval.py timing --run e365_s1_s0 --b-run runs/e292_a_s0`.

**Numbers to hit (VAL 873 frames, seed 0 / seed 1).** Scorer reproduction of the stored plain-B base: 0.6259 / 0.6249 to 1e-6 (`check-layer1`); the first recipe at the decode of record: 0.5486 / 0.5743 (E365); at its chosen decode: 0.6617 / 0.6767 (E366), fresh head seeds 2 / 3: 0.6618 / 0.6733 (E371); the none weight 0.25, fresh seeds 2 / 3, cell 0.2 / 0.6 / off: 0.6698 / 0.6769 (E373); **the champion at its final per-class decode (0.3 / 0.2 / 0.6 / off): 0.6679 / 0.6762, junction F1 0.252 / 0.245 (E375)**; whole path 16.83 / 16.57 ms and 2.323 M parameters. **Checks and pitfalls.** The token cache must match its manifest (the fixture compares the cached tokens with the stored dumps); the head seed and the cache seed are different things (E371 needed a `--cache-seed` option); runs resumed after a crash are not bit-reproducible; the GPU may stall when two trainings share the card.
