# 1. Introduction: purpose and status

**The task, in plain words.** A driver-assistance function needs to know **where the lanes are and how they connect**: a line along the centre of every lane with its travel direction, where a lane forks or merges, and the curved connectors that lead through a junction from an entry lane to an exit lane. This is the *lane-centre graph*. We compute it **from camera and radar only**, for the 100 m x 100 m around the car, once per keyframe (nuScenes gives one every 0.5 s). No HD map is needed; a coarse road-level map may be added as a hint.

**The goal (Mirko, D113).** A good, simple, cheap, *end-to-end differentiable* champion: "grid -> nodes -> pruning -> self-attention -> cross-attention to the past -> cross-attention to the map -> node classification", with a softmax wherever a hard selection can be a softmax. A system with rule steps is not in the solution set and never blocks. The work is done when such a champion exists at an *okayish* level; the "okayish" gate was written down before the first run (chapter 10).

**What we did and what we found (so far).**
1. We built the ground truth that the task needs from the nuScenes map: directed centre lines, junction connectors, valid entry-to-exit pairs, events and what the cameras can see (chapter 3).
2. We started from a frozen camera + radar network (GaussianCaR) with a lane head B on top and made it end to end step by step: a junction layer, a soft attachment of the connector ends, a learned wiring head, a soft duplicate suppression. That ladder reaches topo F1 0.6912 / 0.6948 and still has five named hard steps (chapters 4 and  10).
3. We then replaced the whole junction machinery by **one token head**: the predicted nodes of the lane head are tokens, a soft none-slot prunes, and the per-source softmax of the last self-attention layer *is* the wiring (chapter 2). Its first stage, S1, **missed** the written gate at the decode of record (0.5486 / 0.5743 against floors of 0.6359 / 0.6349), but the head ranks the links well: read at a decode chosen on the TRAIN sample (link threshold 0.1, keep threshold 0.6, no none gate; chosen before the single VAL score) the same heads reach **0.6617 / 0.6767** and **pass the quality half of the gate on both seeds** (E366). Two heads trained afterwards with fresh seeds (E371) land within 0.004 (0.6618 / 0.6733). **One loss weight then changed**: the none target and the none class are weighted 0.25 instead of 1.0; on two fresh head seeds this reaches **0.6698 / 0.6769** (E373) and finds clearly more lanes (0.727 against 0.68), and by a rule written before the data it **replaces the first recipe as the champion** (E373). **The final decode is per class** (E375, adopted by D115 on a rule written before the run): short lane links are kept at p >= 0.3, long connector links at p >= 0.2, with the same head and weights; this lifts the junction F1 from 0.20 to 0.25 and leaves topo F1 unchanged inside the band (**0.6679 / 0.6762**, F1@1.0 0.846 / 0.847, lanes found 0.719 / 0.721). The whole path takes 16.6 - 16.8 ms against 19.7 ms for the previous system. The champion is labelled *staged, lane head B' frozen* and is stage S1 of the design; the first recipe is the confirmed runner-up (a tie inside the band).
4. Two follow-ups of the last hours (chapter 10, B3): a head that starts from the one-line rule and trains on the ground truth does not stay there (E370, MISS: the loss pulls it to the conservative point within 500 steps, so the operating point is the lever, not the start), and a per-class decode of the champion (a stricter threshold for short lane links) is the first attempt on the weak junction F1 (E375: ADOPT-DECODE: the junction F1 rises from 0.20 to 0.25 on both fresh heads, topo F1 stays within 0.002, lanes found falls by 0.006 - 0.008; the plain head's 0.27 is not reached).
5. What S1 taught us: at the decode of record the head is precise (0.86) and conservative (recall 0.40 - 0.43), and a one-line rule on the same tokens is better than it (0.6265 / 0.6281); at the looser decode the recall is recovered at a price (precision 0.68, more vertices, the junction F1 0.20, 0.25 after the per-class decode, still below the plain head's 0.27): the junction links remain the weak part (chapters 10, 6).

**Status.** A champion exists (D114, decided by the written rule of D113, its final decode by D115 on the written rule of E375; neither by the owner: he can overrule them). S2 (cross-attention to the past) is measured: a TIE by its written rule, the champion takes no past block (E368: +0.004 / +0.002 topo F1 against the bar +0.010; chapter 10, section B4). S2b (the past fused by an MLP and a gate) is measured as well: a TIE (E387: f - z topo F1 +0.0051 / -0.0009; the fusion adds nothing over the residual block; chapter 6). S3 (cross-attention to a road-level SD map) is measured too: a TIE, the champion takes no map block (E374: +0.003 / +0.003 topo F1 against the bar +0.010; chapter 10, section B7). S4 (a joint fine-tune so that the loss reaches the node head of the lane head) is measured: S4-HOLDS on both seeds by the written rule of the card (E372): topo F1 against the same head with B' frozen -0.0003 / +0.0049, against the staged champion +0.0002 / +0.0051, junction F1 +0.0021 / +0.0029; the lead took it as the champion (D120, by the card's rule and D109, the end-to-end variant at a tie; the owner can overrule it; chapter 10, section B9). One table is not computed: the split by crowded junctions (chapter 10).

**Terms used in the tables.** *Keyframe, BEV:* one camera + radar snapshot, bird's-eye view = the top-down feature grid. *GT / VAL / TEST / seed:* ground truth; VAL = 873 frames of 22 scenes, used for choosing; TEST = 833 frames of 21 scenes, used only with the owner's recorded OK (untouched here); every trained system runs with two seeds (s0 / s1). *Topo F1:* seeds every 5 m on the true graph, follow the lane direction up to 30 m, match the points to the delivered graph within 1 m, combine precision and recall (1.0 = perfect). *Token, soft pruning, none slot:* a token is one predicted node of the lane head; a token's existence weight biases the attention instead of removing it; the none slot is one extra destination per source whose probability says "no outgoing link from here". *End to end (E2E):* a loss on the final output sends gradients to every learned module; hard steps are allowed only on the output or as a named budget, and are named.
