**What it does**: Adds a perspective (image-space) 3D detection head whose proposals feed the BEV head (two stages), so modern backbones without depth pretraining optimise faster. It does not change the camera-to-BEV lift to use a depth distribution; the BEV lift remains BEVFormer's spatial cross-attention.

**Results that matter**: none on the page read (state of the art on nuScenes is claimed).

**Transferable technique**: only the idea of a perspective-space auxiliary loss on the image backbone; for us this is the depth head's supervision, already covered by BEVDepth.

**Cost and fit**: transformer pipeline; not useful for the PD lift.

**Doubts**: not read beyond the abstract page; the brief's guess that it carries a depth-weighted backward lift is not borne out by the abstract.
