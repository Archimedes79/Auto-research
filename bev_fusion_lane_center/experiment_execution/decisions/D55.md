## Decided

Decided: 2026-11-04
Decision: D55 (Mirko, in chat on 2026-11-04, answering my report that the lane-graph literature (TopoNet, MapTRv2, LaneGAP, CGNet) trains query-based graphs end to end with Hungarian matching in the LOSS only, and that PROBLEM.md says "no matching"; his message, verbatim: "forget the no matching rule, I am not sure what it means, the rule is e2e differentiable"). My reading, which he can correct: the rule of the lane project is "end-to-end differentiable": the forward path from the BEV features to the delivered output (including the map assignment and the classification) has no rule step and a loss on the output reaches every module. Matching (bipartite, one-to-many, Sinkhorn) INSIDE THE LOSS, to decide which prediction is compared with which ground-truth element, is allowed; it is not part of the forward path. Argmax / thresholds stay allowed on the OUTPUT only and are named. Consequence: the words "no argmax, no matching, no rule step" in PROBLEM.md line 8 and in CLAUDE.md "Experiments" are replaced by the rule "fully end-to-end differentiable forward path (soft outputs up to and including the assignment and the classification); matching in the loss is allowed; hard selection or rules only on the output, named" (PROBLEM.md through a grant under D55); the matching-free designs of the E273 card (anchors, ports, slots) are alternatives, not requirements, and CGNet-style query decoders (Apache-2.0 code `xiaomi/cgnet`) are admissible as the graph stage; the closed question on the Poisson set loss (E244 / E255: matching-free was worse at an equal budget) stays as it is.
