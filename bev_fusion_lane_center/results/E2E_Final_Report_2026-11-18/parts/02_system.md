# 2. The system: architecture and description

**The design (Mirko's D113: "grid -> nodes -> pruning -> self-attention -> cross-attention to the past -> cross-attention to the map -> node classification", and "softmax would also be better").** One cached keyframe in, one lane-centre graph out. Stage S1 is built and measured (card E365, section 1); S2 and S3 are measured (both a TIE: no past block, no map block), S4 is PENDING. The chain:
1. **Frozen GaussianCaR camera + radar BEV features**, cached (D50).
2. **B', the frozen lane head with the soft Matrix-NMS layer** (the lane head B of E273, its pyramid and node head on a 2 m grid, with the layer of E292 instead of the hard close-node step): per cell an existence logit, a position, a heading, 8 flag logits and a 64-dimensional node token. In stage 1 it is cached and no gradient reaches it; in stage 2 (S4, E372, the champion since D120) its node head runs inside the training step and the loss of the token head reaches it, while B' keeps its own node loss (the head's loss was 0.19 - 0.40 of the updates of the node head).
3. **Tokens = the top-K 512 cells by existence** (K was fixed by the manifest: coverage of GT nodes at K = 384 was 0.9878, below the written 0.99). The top-K is a named compute budget; no gradient goes through it.
4. **Embedding and three self-attention layers** (d = 64, 4 heads, FFN 128). The attention logit between token i and token j is the scaled dot product plus a learned bias from 13 relative-geometry features of the pair (turn angle, chord in the frames of both headings, ...) plus log of the key's existence weight. **That weight is the soft pruning:** no token is dropped inside the network; a token with a small weight is simply a weak key. For the first 1000 steps and in layer 1 the weight is sigmoid(e) of B'; afterwards it is 1 - p_none of the head's own node-type softmax.
5. **The wiring is the attention of the last layer** (two heads: short links with a chord up to 8 m, long links above 8 m = junction connectors): for every source token a **softmax over the allowed destination tokens and one learned "none" slot**. The none slot sees the whole candidate set; it replaces the keypoint threshold, the wiring rule and the close-node step of the earlier system. Two handle scales per pair (a small MLP) give the connector curve.
6. **Node types: a softmax over seven classes** (none, lane point, fork, merge, junction entry, junction exit, boundary); p_none is the head's own pruning weight.
7. **Stages after S1 (each its own card, two seeds, VAL, a fresh critic):** S2 cross-attention to the past (the tokens of the previous keyframe, ego-warped, one frame per step, D91), S3 cross-attention to the map, S4 a joint fine-tune. Status: S2 (E368) is measured: the block is built and the head reads the past, but it adds +0.002 to +0.004 topo F1 (bar +0.010), a TIE, so it is not taken; S3 (E374, a road-level SD map) is measured and a TIE as well (+0.003 / +0.003 topo F1, bar +0.010), S4 (E372) is measured: S4-HOLDS on both seeds by the written rule of the card (E372): topo F1 against the same head with B' frozen -0.0003 / +0.0049, against the staged champion +0.0002 / +0.0051, junction F1 +0.0021 / +0.0029. By the card's rule and D109 (the end-to-end variant at a tie) the lead took it (D120): **the champion is the two-stage recipe, stage 1 = the head on the frozen cache (S1), stage 2 = the head and the node head of B' trained together through the token loss (S4), without a past block and without a map block**; the pyramid of B' stays frozen.

**The named hard steps of S1** (everything else is soft and a loss on the output reaches the head):
- the top-K 512 of the tokens by B''s existence (a compute budget, no gradient through it, none to B');
- the allowed-pair gate of the wiring (`jtok.pair_geometry`: turn angle, minimum and forward chord; the attention only ranges over allowed pairs); at K = 512 the gate and the token set let 94.3 % of the GT links through (both ends covered and inside the gate, E365 manifest, VAL, B' seed 0);
- B' itself: frozen and cached; its soft layer and its own existence threshold of 0.05 for the token mask;
- on the output only (the decode): a node is kept when p_none is below the keep threshold, a link when p is above the link threshold (and, optionally, above p(none)) and both ends are kept. The decode of record of stage S1 is keep 0.5, link 0.3 and the none gate on; the **decode of the champion is keep 0.6, link 0.2, none gate off**, chosen by a written rule on the head's own TRAIN sample of 291 frames before the single VAL score (the first recipe's cell was link 0.1, keep 0.6); the delivered graph is read section-free (every node section 0, chains cut at forks and merges).

**Not in the system:** keypoint peaks with top-K 32, the snap, the attachment, the wiring rule, the close-node step (the five hard steps of the earlier champion E324 s that this design removes).

**Training of S1.** One stage, 12000 steps, batch 8, learning rate 1e-3 (5 % warm-up, cosine), AdamW. Loss: per class and per source the negative log of the summed probability of the positive destinations (every token within 1.5 m of a GT successor; the none slot when there is none), with the none target and the none class weighted **0.25 in the champion** (1.0 in the first recipe, the runner-up), plus 0.5 x node-type cross-entropy, plus 0.5 x L1 of the handle scales on the long GT links. Matching inside the loss (the static assignment of tokens to GT nodes) is allowed (D55).

**Stage 2 (S4, E372, the champion since D120).** 3000 joint steps, batch 8, learning rate 1e-4 for the head and the node head, per-group clip: `jlive_train.py --head-run e373_cb_s<S> --train-nodes 1 --live-inputs 1 --steps 3000 --bs 8 --lr 1e-4` (the token cache is replaced by the live output of B''s node head). Training step about 0.29 s against 0.24 s of the head alone. The inference network is the same as in stage 1 (B' + the head; only the weights of B''s node head changed), so ms per frame and parameters do not change by construction (not re-timed in the card). A new token cache from the s4 B' is needed before a head-only experiment builds on this champion.

**Cost counted in every result:** parameters, GMACs and ms of B' alone, of the head alone and of the whole path. S1: 2.32 M parameters in all (B' 2.196 M + the head 0.127 M) against 2.741 M for E324 s; the whole path takes a median of 16.83 / 16.57 ms (B' alone 11.41 / 11.37 ms, the head alone 6.70 / 6.64 ms with 0.86 GMACs; measured on the head of the first recipe, the champion has the identical architecture) against 19.7 ms for E324 s (chapter 10, section D).
