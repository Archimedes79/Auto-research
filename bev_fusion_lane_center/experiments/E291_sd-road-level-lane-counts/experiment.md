## 1. The input (what is in it, what is not) and the step 0 numbers
Built on the lane side from the lane GT, by location (`jgt.junction_gt(g)["arms"]`, computed in `QData.__getitem__` for TRAIN and VAL frames; the staged data path puts the arm list into the batch, padded, with ONLY the fields `c` (centre), `t` (heading into the junction), `n_in`, `n_out`; `spread_in`, `spread_out` and the zone are NOT copied). **What it really is (critic finding 2):** `n_in` / `n_out` are the numbers of clustered GT END points of an arm (1.5 m / 40 degree clusters of connector-run starts and ends), `c` and `t` are means of GT end centroids and headings: a fan-out lane counts once, a lane cut by the window is absent, a short run is absent; this is not a lane tag of a road map (a real `lanes` tag counts lane pieces; `sd_lib.build_sd` of this project does that, as a road raster, without an arm table: not reused because the pair head needs positions per arm, and the nuScenes map API gives no per-direction count). So **the 0 % row is an oracle on the end targets and the arm geometry**; the report says so; a real-tag variant is a follow-up. Not in the table: connectors, lane ids, successor / predecessor / left / right relations, lane index, dividers, crossings, stop lines, the pair identities. **The corruption model (what a real map does wrong), a pure function of (frame token, zone, r), per frame (a scene-constant shift would need the ego pose; `sd_lib.scene_pose_error` is not used):** each arm's count (per direction) is wrong with probability r (the read-outs at 10 % and 30 % add NO unknown arms; the +-1 read-out has rate 0.3: every count moves by +-1 with probability 0.3): replaced by a random count in {0, ..., 5} different from the true one (a gross error, not only +-1; a +-1 read-out is added, table only), and, in b's TRAINING only, 15 % of the arms are marked UNKNOWN (count 0, flag 1: the unknown flag and the absent flag are the SAME channel) and 20 % of the junctions are 'counts unknown, geometry kept' (so that the U read-out is in distribution for b); the arm centre shifts by up to 2 m and the heading by up to 5 degrees (iid per frame), applied ONLY when r > 0 (b's r = 0 junctions equal the clean read-out); the rate r is computed inside the data path from the zone index (hash of token and zone; arms with zone -1 share one r) and the zone index is not carried by the table; an arm is dropped with probability r / 3 (a road missing in the map); a frame with no junction GT (`jg is None`) gets an empty table: every keypoint absent. The 20 % junction dropout is OFF at read-out (the "0 %" row has no absent maps). **Step 0 (CPU, no GPU, before anything is trained; the ceiling for the gain):** on the 873 VAL frames with the stored E312 dumps (`runs/e312_p_s*/ends_val.pkl`) and the `junction_gt` arms of the same frames: per arm and kind, the number of REAL keypoints (peaks of the heat, `valid & real`) within 6 m of the arm centre and heading-aligned against `n_in` / `n_out`: the shares of arms with deficit (found < n), exact, surplus (found > n), and the GT pairs lost behind a deficit (a pair head cannot add a missing end) and the false pairs behind a surplus; **the gain a count can give is bounded by these shares**; result in this card before the build starts (section 3).

## 2. Design
- **The input, differentiable and gated (D88):** per keypoint of the pair head (entries and exits) the features `[n_arm / 4, unknown / absent flag, distance to the nearest arm axis / 20 m, heading agreement (cos), (the keypoint's rank among the arm's keypoints by lateral offset) / max(n, 1), (real keypoints found in the arm - n) / 4]` through a small MLP (`6 -> 16 -> 1` gate logit and `6 -> d` features) added to the keypoint token before the SetBlocks as `token += sigmoid(g) * f`; `g`, the output weights of `f` and their bias are initialised so that the term is ZERO: **the model starts as E312 (checked with the E312 checkpoint loaded, `strict=False`)**; the module lives INSIDE `JEnds` so that the trainer's parameter set contains it. **Real peaks only (critic finding 3):** `JEnds.forward` injects GT end cells as keypoints in training (`gt` given, score 2.0) and not at evaluation; the "found" and rank features use `valid & real` (as `rank_matrix` does) and the count compared is kind-specific (entry keypoints against `n_in`, exit keypoints against `n_out`); a test proves the features are identical with and without the GT injection on a frame with no real peak. A keypoint with no arm within 10 m is absent (features 0, flag 1); **the association and presence radius is 10 m, not 6 m (delta critic B4: `c` is the midpoint of the entry and exit centroids, up to 14 m apart (`jgt.ARM_JOIN_DIST`); 13.0 % of the entry ends and 13.2 % of the exit ends lie more than 6 m from `c`, 5.6 % / 3.2 % more than 8 m, 1.0 % / 0 % more than 10 m)**. Batch-wise torch (cdist to the padded arms, same-arm masks), no host loop.
- **Named hard steps on the input side (D88; critic finding 7):** the nearest-arm `argmin`, the 6 m presence gate, the rank sort, and the DETACHED keypoint positions: the gradient of the prior path stops at the keypoints, so the map cannot shape the heat in this card (a follow-up: a soft association by a softmax over the arms, zero parameters). The output side is unchanged (the delivered graph's named steps stay).
- **Arms and read-outs:** c = E312 stored + the recipe twin (flag off); a trained clean (+ 20 % absent), b trained with corrupted counts (as above); each of a and b read at: clean (0 %), 10 %, 30 %, +-1, **U (ALL counts unknown, geometry kept; read for b only)**, **S (the table of ANOTHER frame, rate 0)**; each read-out as a dump into its own run directory `runs/e291_<arm>_<run>_<readout>/` (a copy of `model.pt` and `config.json` and `ends_val.pkl`, written by the staged `jends_eval.py dump --prior-noise r --prior-mode ...`; `qends_eval.py score` reads `runs/<run>/ends_val.pkl` and is frozen: it is called on those directories with unique `--tag`s, so no stored card is overwritten); snapped (`--thr 0.1 --snap`) and unsnapped (`--thr 0.1`) cards for each.
- **Why two arms and many read-outs, not a training run per noise level (default):** the questions "what does a clean map give" and "what does a wrong map cost" are read-outs of two models. Attribution: a and b both have the gate; the robustness of b is attributed to the CORRUPTED TRAINING, the contrast a-at-30 % against b-at-30 % is the measurement of it.
- **What it needs in code (staged copies, new files where possible):** the arm table in the batch (staged `lane_gt_data.py` / `qjunction.py`), `training/grid/jprior.py` (new: the corruption, the features, the gate; imported by the staged `jends.py` with a `prior` flag, default off = bit-identical to E312), `jends_train.py` flags `--lane-prior --prior-noise {0, mix}`, `jends_eval.py dump` flags, the leak and identity tests, the analysis code of the splits, `jdiag.boot_pooled` (new function in the not-frozen `jdiag.py`: asserts identical scene lists of all four counts files, one scene draw per resample for both seeds, the mean over seeds of the topo F1 difference, percentiles of the 1000 pooled values and the two per-seed differences), a hardening assert in the staged `jends_train.py` (`float(os.environ.get('BFLC_SIG', '1.5'))` equal to the sigma stored in the maps checkpoint's args: a missing `BFLC_SIG` in any launcher stops the run), `temp/e291/start_arms.sh` (exports `BFLC_SIG=1.0`; the other environment variables of the E312 launch are checked from its run record: `BFLC_RASTER` defaults to legacy, which matches). **Staging mechanics:** `jends_train.py` inserts the real folder into `sys.path` at index 0, which shadows PYTHONPATH: the launcher is the meta-path finder of `temp/e347/stage_launch.py` (E347's), `temp/e347/run_staged.sh` sets `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""` (CPU only), so a GPU variant `temp/e291/run_staged_gpu.sh` is built; after the first start the run's tag is checked for `temp/e291/stage`; the GPU slots depend on D102 (open) and D57 (lane work first).
- **Prediction (numbers, a guess, not derived; written before the run):** clean map (b, G): **+0.008 topo F1** snapped; the U read-out about **+0.002** (the geometry alone gives little); a at 30 % wrong **-0.02**; b at 30 % wrong **-0.003**; the gate norm falls with the noise. **Power (recomputed after the delta critic):** two seeds of the same recipe differ by 0.0040 (E312 snapped 0.6825 / 0.6864) with a 95 % scene interval [-0.0077, +0.0164] for one pair (half-width about 0.012); pooling two seeds gives about 0.009 (to be measured). A gain of +0.008 therefore cannot show, G+ needs about +0.011 or more; R+ needs the point to be at least about 0.000 to +0.002 at 30 % wrong. The card is a MEASUREMENT read through the pooled interval, the twin and the U / S read-outs: INCONCLUSIVE is the likely token at the stated prediction and it is reported as that, not as a failure.
- **The discussion the numbers feed (Mirko's question):** PROS: the count per direction is cheap and nearly always in a road-level map; it says how many entries / exits an arm has (a missed far lane, a duplicate); it costs no compute (a table lookup). CONS: maps have gross errors (construction, new markings, wrong tags), a wrong count is trusted by a network that learned from a clean map (a at 30 % shows the size), the count says neither WHICH lane nor WHERE (no connections) and a stale map misleads; the gate and the corrupted training of b are the E2E answer (learned doubt) instead of a rule; and the clean row here is an oracle, the real gain with a real tag is smaller (step 0 bounds it).
- **Not claimed:** anything about lane-level maps (stay out: Mirko's correction), an exact-map upper bound as a RESULT (the clean row is labelled an upper bound), TEST (not used), the maps head as a consumer of the prior (a follow-up), real OSM tags of nuScenes (the table is built from the lane GT and corrupted by a model of map errors).
