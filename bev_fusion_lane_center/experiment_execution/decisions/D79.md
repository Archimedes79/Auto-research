My reading, which he can correct: the choice of the judging metric for the entry / exit keypoint head (E283 option 5a / 5b, D71) is delegated to the lead chat of the lane project; the rule is written into the card of that head before any run (hard rule 4) and he can overrule it at any time. The choice made now:
- **Headline (the one number that gives KEEP / TIE / CUT):** `topo_F1` of the delivered graph (lane suite function through `evaluation/qgraph_eval.py`, VAL 873 frames, paired scene bootstrap, two seeds on the same side). KEEP above +0.02 over the control, TIE inside +-0.02, CUT below -0.02 or a guard broken.
- **Guards (must hold):** `F1@1.0` not lower by more than 0.01; `dup_frac` not higher by more than 0.02; vertices per 100 m (Douglas-Peucker basis) not more than 1.0 above the control; `junction_F1` not lower by more than 0.03; the assignment gain (`lane_acc` with the map error) not lower by more than 0.01.
- **Reported, not judged:** `lanes_found`, `Q`, topo P / R, junction P / R, ms per frame, and the head's own numbers (recall and precision of entry and exit points with a tolerance of 3 m along the lane, 1.5 m across and 30 degrees of heading; share of pairs whose curve lies within 1 m of the true connector).
- **Why:** topo F1 is the connectivity measure and the one that responds: in the CPU oracle of E283 section 7 (VAL 873, both seeds) GT ends with true pairs raise it by +0.10, with the classifier's pairs by +0.04 to +0.05, and the negative control (a curve for every possible pair) lowers it. `lanes_found` rises with every added curve (0.91 for all pairs) and can be gamed; `Q` mixes sparsity and position; `junction_F1` hardly moves even with perfect ends (+0.03, its precision stays at 0.16 - 0.18 in every variant) and is therefore a guard, not a judge.
Consequence: no code, GT or training change. The open point 1 of the HANDOVER is closed by this entry; the card of the end head (written after the next CPU step, the oracle with the measured end error) takes the rule as it stands here and may only tighten it. The band of 0.02 is the one of E274; the two seeds of the oracle differ by 0.001 in topo F1.
