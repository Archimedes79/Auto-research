# 2101.03697 RepVGG: making VGG-style ConvNets great again

**What it does**: Trains a multi-branch (3x3 + 1x1 + identity) block and re-parameterises it into a single 3x3 conv per layer for inference, giving a plain stack of 3x3 convs and ReLU.

**Results that matter**: over 80 percent ImageNet top-1; on a 1080Ti 83 percent faster than ResNet-50 and 101 percent faster than ResNet-101 at higher accuracy (abstract).

**Transferable technique**: the same re-parameterisation can be applied to the BEV trunk or heads (a conv-only BEV encoder that fuses into plain 3x3 layers for the 3060). Candidate for a small BEV encoder after the lift.

**Cost and fit**: plain 3x3 stack fuses well in TensorRT/ONNX; weights are larger than MobileNet-style nets.

**Doubts**: no BEV or segmentation result here; ImageNet speed is on a 1080Ti, not a 3060 with mixed precision.
