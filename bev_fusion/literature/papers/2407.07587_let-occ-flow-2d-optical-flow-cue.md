**What it does**: TPV features from images, ego-aligned history fused by a backward-forward attention module; two MLPs give an SDF and a 3D flow field, rendered along rays with NeuS weights so that depth and flow are weighted sums. Dynamic flow = optical flow minus the static flow implied by the rendered depth and the ego pose; GroundedSAM masks give movable objects. Losses: photometric (L1 + SSIM), flow (weight 5e-3), optional LiDAR range, Eikonal, Hessian, edge-aware smoothness. Two stages (static geometry, then SDF + flow).

**Results that matter** (nuScenes, RayIoU / mAVE): camera-only 30.12 / 1.42; camera + LiDAR 40.48 / 1.45; OccNet (3D-supervised) 39.66 / 1.61. KITTI-MOT camera-only EPE 3.529, Fl-10% 0.084 (OccNeRF 6.986). Optical-flow ablation (supplement, caption looked mislabeled): without flow supervision EPE 3.649, foreground EPE 9.663; with 3.488 and 7.510. 8 A100, under 2 days on nuScenes.

**Transferable technique**: (1) the decomposition flow = observed 2D flow minus the flow the static scene would produce; the ego-induced part is known exactly in our case, so only the residual is learned; (2) a pretrained 2D flow net is a label substitute but gave a modest gain (foreground EPE 9.7 to 7.5).

**Cost and fit**: 253 M parameters per SelfOccFlow's table; far outside our budget.

**Doubts**: pseudo-labels from a pretrained flow model whose own training data is not driving; evaluation mixes datasets.
