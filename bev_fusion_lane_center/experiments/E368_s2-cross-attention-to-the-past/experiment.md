## 1. Card text
- **Statement:** a head that sees, besides the tokens of the current keyframe, the states of the previous keyframe's tokens in the current lane frame decides weak tokens somewhat better than the same head with one frame only: topo F1 +0.004 (window -0.006 to +0.020) over the matched control on VAL, both seeds.
- **Mechanism:** B' is conservative per frame; 72 % of the GT nodes it misses at t have a weak token (0.05 < p < 0.5) near them, 28 % have none. The previous keyframe saw the same lane 0.5 s earlier from about 3 m further back; a lane that was delivered there and lies under a weak token here is evidence a single frame lacks. The block is the cheapest form of it: keys / values = the previous token states, queries = the current tokens, the ego pose is data, the warp is a rotation and a translation, the softmax over the previous tokens plus a none key is the soft form of 'is there a past token here at all'.
