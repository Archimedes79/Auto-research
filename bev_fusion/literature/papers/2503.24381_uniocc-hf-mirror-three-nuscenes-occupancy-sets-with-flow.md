**What it does**: A unified benchmark and toolkit for occupancy forecasting and prediction over nuScenes, Waymo, CARLA and OpenCOOD. For nuScenes it re-packs existing labels: folders `NuScenes-via-Occ3D-2Hz-{mini,train,val}`, `...-via-OpenOccupancy-2Hz-...`, `...-via-SurroundOcc-2Hz-...` (checked on the file list). Every `.npz` has semantics (`occ_label`), and `occ_flow_forward` / `occ_flow_backward` of shape L x W x H x 3 in units of voxels pointing to the voxel's position in the next / previous frame; flow is given for dynamic agents and static environment alike (card); the project page lists no flow for the Occ3D nuScenes entry (page).

**Format and coverage**: nuScenes at 2 Hz; resolution 0.2 m / 0.4 m; range not stated on the pages read; dataset total 776 GB (card), per-split sizes not shown (per-file `.npz` of 1.7 to 2.2 MB are visible in commit diffs for the OpenOccupancy variant; digest).

**Results that matter**: none for radar.

**Transferable technique**: nothing to port; the value is availability: if Google Drive / Baidu are not reachable, the three label sets can be fetched from Hugging Face.

**Cost and fit**: 776 GB total makes it a pick-folders-only option; per-variant sizes unknown, so check the Files tab before any download; adds nothing over the originals for a 2D static layer.

**Doubts**: re-packaged labels may differ from the originals (a 2025-07-17 fix of instance tokens is mentioned on the card); the licence line of the card is for the toolkit and may not cover the nuScenes-derived labels.
