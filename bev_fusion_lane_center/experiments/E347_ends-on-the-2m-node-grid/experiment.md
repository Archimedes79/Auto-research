# E347 - the junction ends on the 2 m node grid: can one grid carry the ends (no 1 m branch), and does a one-to-one target make the peak rule unnecessary?

Card of 2026-11-14 19:25, revised 19:45 after the first critic pass (REVISE: the controls were not the one-change references, the stage-1 gate could not see the offset, the duplicate bar was unreachable, a missed gate was read as a structural verdict, three numbers and one command line were wrong) and 19:55 after the second (REVISE: arm o's loss weight would make it fire in clusters, the gate was read at a fixed threshold, the stage-2 choice ignored the duplicates, the data path was missing from the to-build list). Written by the lane lead on Mirko's order of 2026-11-14 ("Plane 2 Experimente"; his goal: head only, end-to-end differentiable, simple, small, cheap, good). Plan card: nothing is started; the lane experiments chat owns the code.

**Why:** in a head that outputs ONE graph the connector ends are nodes of the same node set as the lane nodes, and that set lives on the 2 m grid of B (E346). Today the ends come from a second, finer system: the junction maps head reads the 2 m, 1 m (and 0.5 m) features and predicts the entry / exit heat at 1 m (3.256 GMACs, 1.779 without the paint branch of E332 / E341), the keypoints are 3 x 3 peaks of that heat (a named rule on the training path of the pair head) and are joined to B's pieces afterwards by the snap. The only earlier try with ends on B's grid (E284) read B's FROZEN node tokens with the old end labels and stopped at an entry recall of 0.57 / 0.58; the gain since then came from the end GT of D82 and the narrow heat of E307 (keypoint F1 0.630 / 0.642 -> 0.723 / 0.705), and the resolution was never varied. On the VAL ground truth 0.2 % of the GT ends share a 2 m cell with another end of the same kind (E344 section 4b: a scratch measurement with its caveat, up to 0.4 %; re-run as a check in this card). Two questions:
1. **One grid (arm g):** the end head as a 2 m head. This is a package, not one factor: the output at 2 m, no 1 m features and no `m1` block, the pair tokens read from the 2 m features, a regressed sub-cell offset in place of the soft-argmax, and a 3 x 3 peak window that is 6 m wide instead of 3 m. The question of the card is the package (can the 1 m branch go); the free read-outs below say which part costs.
2. **Duplicates through the loss (arm o):** the heat target is one cell per GT end, every other cell a negative, and the read-out is the threshold alone, no peak rule. The cheap twin of the close-node question, and different from its two negative results (E344 section 1b): E277 / E279 and E292 damp the existence of B's nodes with a step or a layer AFTER the node head (soft NMS; E277 broke the recall guards, E279 could not be adopted next to the junction heads, E292 was not adopted); here nothing follows the head, the target itself is one-to-one, and the targets are points (ends), not nodes whose position along the line is the grid phase (E289).

## 1. Card text
- **Statement:** an end head on the 2 m grid with a regressed sub-cell offset and a one-to-one target feeds the pair stage as well as the 1 m head does (topo F1 within 0.01 of E341 on both read-outs), without a peak rule.
- **Mechanism:** the E307 result says the ends were limited by the target (a narrow heat separates neighbouring lanes), not by the cell size; with a one-to-one target neighbouring ends in adjacent cells are both positives, which a peak rule on a 2 m grid cannot deliver. An end is a point target, so the offset is well defined (B's node offsets are not a precedent: E289 found the nodes locked to the grid because their target is defined by the grid phase); the precedent is the E284 head, 2 m with an offset, mean end error 1.18 m.
- **What it saves when adopted:** the 1 m block `m1` of the maps head (and with E341 the 0.5 m branch), and it puts the ends on the grid of B's nodes, the precondition for "the end IS a node" (wiring by index, no snap), which is the card after this one. D86: simple (one grid), little compute, end to end (arm o removes the peak rule from the training path of the pair head).
- **Inputs:** ready: `training/grid/jmaps.py` (`keypoints` has `res`), `jgt.fields` (has `n`, `res`), `training/train/jmaps_train.py`, `evaluation/jmaps_eval.py`, `jends.py`, the end GT of D82, the frozen pyramid of B.
