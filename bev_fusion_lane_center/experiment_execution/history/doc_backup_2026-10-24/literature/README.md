**Reading depth.** A = abstract page, M = method section, C = code page. Loop-16 cards (301) are M or M+C for 284 of them; loop-17 cards (103) were read through page digests (method sections queried, equations not read by the author of the note) - the caveat is in `sources/lit17/*.md`, section 1. Numbers in the notes are author-reported on mixed protocols and must not be compared across papers or with ours. Facts not seen on a page are marked "from memory" or "not found" in the notes.

**The project in one paragraph.** Camera + radar BEV features of a frozen foundation (GaussianCaR) -> dense lane field -> sparse lane graph (vertices, edges, junctions) -> temporal memory -> assignment to an SD map route -> navigation attribute per lane -> planner. Goal: all of it differentiable, short and correct graphs, fast. Experiments: [../experiments/README.md](../experiments/README.md).

## What the literature gave us, by topic

**1. Foundations (camera-radar BEV).** Everything reads frozen features; a map-pretrained network carries lane structure far better than a detection-pretrained one (our E27: F1 0.624 -> 0.867 on VAL), and radar adds little to lane geometry (BEVCar [SN04](papers/SN04_bevcar-camera-radar-fusion-for.md), TopoBDA [SN09](papers/SN09_topobda-the-only-camera-radar.md) are the only camera-radar lane results found; CRN [SN05](papers/SN05_crn-radar-assisted-view-transformation.md) was the first foundation, [SN01](papers/SN01_gaussiancar.md) GaussianCaR the one we use). No paper trains lane heads on frozen cached BEV features or measures the seen-to-unseen gap of a foundation (ours: 0.932 on its own training scenes -> 0.820 on TEST). Nearly every lane / vector-map repository pins Python 3.8, PyTorch 1.9 and mmcv-full 1.x: ideas are reusable, code is not.

**2. Dense field -> sparse graph (growth, links).** Growth agents (RNGDet [GR01](papers/GR01_rngdet-rngdet.md), CenterLineDet [GR02](papers/GR02_centerlinedet.md), iCurb [GR03](papers/GR03_icurb.md), RoadTracer [GR04](papers/GR04_roadtracer.md), DAGMapper [GR05](papers/GR05_dagmapper.md), DeH4R [GR06](papers/GR06_deh4r.md)) are trained by behaviour cloning or DAgger; none passes gradient through the selected children, none selects its step length by verifying intermediate samples, none reports fork resolution. Link papers verify a *given* segment ([GR10](papers/GR10_sam-road-sam-road-linegraph2road.md) SAM-Road, [TP01](papers/TP01_topologic.md) TopoLogic, [TP04](papers/TP04_lanegap.md) LaneGAP, [TP07](papers/TP07_topoenhance.md) TopoEnhance, [TP13](papers/TP13_copo.md) CoPo). On a frozen foundation, learned vector-level structure (key points, Bezier connectors) gained; re-learning decisions the rules already make (edges, node-conditioned heads) did not (experiments E31-E56).

**3. Verification along rays, sampling, survival (loop-16 builder).** Survival / discrete-time hazard along a ray ([VS01](papers/VS01_discrete-time-hazard-network-with.md), [VS02](papers/VS02_drsa-deep-recurrent-survival-analysis.md), [VS04](papers/VS04_pondernet-and-act.md), NeRF transmittance [VS06](papers/VS06_nerf-volume-rendering-alpha-compositing.md)), deformable and bounded-offset sampling ([VS20](papers/VS20_deformable-detr-reference-points-learned.md) Deformable DETR, [VS19](papers/VS19_dino-look-forward-twice-mixed.md) DINO), cascades and line verification ([VS12](papers/VS12_l-cnn-junction-proposals-line.md) L-CNN, [VS26](papers/VS26_clrnet-lane-priors-refined-over.md) CLRNet). No perception paper trains a hazard along a ray with censoring from range or occlusion, and the calibration of a product of conditional probabilities is untested anywhere read.

**4. Several hypotheses, matching-free training.** Winner-take-all family ([MH04](papers/MH04_relaxed-wta.md), [MH05](papers/MH05_evolving-wta-and-sampling-fitting.md), [MH06](papers/MH06_mcl-and-stochastic-mcl-oracle.md)), anchor / slot classification, quality-argmax labels, sparsemax / entmax ([MH35](papers/MH35_sparsemax-and-alpha-entmax.md)), perturbed top-k ([MH37](papers/MH37_differentiable-perturbed-optimizers.md)), Gumbel-top-k ([MH39](papers/MH39_stochastic-beams-and-the-gumbel.md)), straight-through ([MH46](papers/MH46_straight-through-estimators-gumbel-softmax.md)). No paper trains a b-ary geometric tree with a beam through the selection; Hungarian-free assignment for crowded configurations (parallel lanes, junction mouths) has no remedy except matching.

**5. Online vector maps and temporal memory.** StreamMapNet [VM01](papers/VM01_streammapnet.md), MapTracker [VM04](papers/VM04_maptracker.md), SQD-MapNet [VM02](papers/VM02_sqd-mapnet.md), PredMapNet [A14](papers/A14_predmapnet-per-instance-raster-history.md), Neural Map Prior [A20](papers/A20_neural-map-prior-learned-global.md), state-space memories ([A02](papers/A02_sigma-lane-gated-mamba2-temporal.md) SIGMA-Lane, [A12](papers/A12_mambamap-bev-and-instance-query.md) MambaMap). Gaps that matter to us: no validity memory with Bayesian (log-odds) fusion on vertices, no Hungarian-free temporal training, no streaming memory for a lane graph with explicit edges under modality failure, pose-error robustness of memories almost untested.

**6. Sensor failure and robustness.** Post-fusion repair modules and gates between a frozen fusion and the head ([RB01](papers/RB01_post-fusion-stabilizer-identity-initialised.md) PFS, [RB02](papers/RB02_modalpatch-learned-history-based-feature.md) ModalPatch, [RB03](papers/RB03_safemap-robust-online-hd-map.md) SafeMap, [RB04](papers/RB04_robomap-what-matters-for-robust.md) RoboMap, [RB06](papers/RB06_grace-bev-trust-gate-failsafe.md) Grace-BEV, [A24](papers/A24_metabev-modality-arbitrary-bev-queries.md) MetaBEV). Evidence is mostly synthetic corruption suites for detection or map segmentation, so we defined our own failure series for lane graphs (E57, E193+); head-side remedies restore only part of what the frozen foundation loses. [RB14](papers/RB14_gaussiancar-no-failure-evaluation-exists.md) notes that no failure evaluation of GaussianCaR exists.

**7. SD map, route and planner interface.** Lane-level assignment of an SD route to perceived lanes ([C01](papers/C01_online-navigation-refinement-mat.md) / [PL01](papers/PL01_online-navigation-refinement-sd-roads.md) MAT, [PL41](papers/PL41_smart.md) SMART, [C03](papers/C03_navmapfusion-sd-map-cross-attention.md) NavMapFusion), planners that consume lane tokens with an on-route flag ([PL03](papers/PL03_plantf.md) PlanTF, [PL04](papers/PL04_pluto.md) PLUTO, [PL05](papers/PL05_diffusion-planner.md) Diffusion Planner, [PL08](papers/PL08_vad.md) VAD, [PL11](papers/PL11_uniad.md) UniAD), uncertainty into planning ([PL09](papers/PL09_uncad.md) UncAD). No paper measures the closed-loop effect of a perceived lane graph with topology in a rule-based or nuPlan-style planner, and none back-propagates planning into topology (our E119 / E121 / E202: no gain).

**8. Evaluation and data.** OpenLane-V2 [TP29](papers/TP29_openlane-v2-data-and-metrics.md) scores are not comparable across devkit versions (the same model: TOP_ll 4.1 vs 10.9). nuScenes-based online-map numbers are inflated by location overlap (85 % of nuScenes validation locations also occur in training; MapTR drops from 48.7 to 20.9 on a geographically disjoint split [VM46](papers/VM46_geographically-disjoint-splits.md)); our splits are scene-disjoint, not location-disjoint - a location-overlap check is cheap and still open. Fork / merge landmark protocols use tolerances 0.5-5 m; our J uses 6 m, so report a curve next to it. Licences: nuScenes is non-commercial; GPL-3 repositories and repositories without a licence file are "read and re-implement" sources (list in `repos/README.md`).

**9. Vision-language-action models.** Ablations show little average gain from language, non-LLM planners are on par on NAVSIM ([PL20](papers/PL20_navsim-v1.md)), cost and fragility are high: not needed for this module (E-row `VLA`).

## What the literature did not provide (open, ranked by use for the next loops)

1. A differentiable selection of hypotheses (tree / beam) with gradient to the dropped nodes for *geometric* nodes (only discrete or latent states are demonstrated).
2. Validity memory on vertices and a temporal consistency metric for graph outputs (mAS / C-mAP need instance identity or GT pairing).
3. Calibration of per-vertex / per-fork probabilities (no paper measures it).
4. Map trust / abstain for wrong or missing SD maps beyond training with noise (dustbin-style assignment [C30](papers/C30_superglue-sinkhorn-optimal-transport-with.md) SuperGlue, [C31](papers/C31_lightglue-matchability-scores-instead-of.md) LightGlue is the closest tool).
5. Closed-loop or planner-aware map-quality metric for lane graphs.

## How this folder is maintained

- New paper note: one `.md` in `papers/` named `<ID>_<slug>.md` (ID = cluster prefix + number, e.g. `GR13`), fields as in `sources/lit16/SCHEMA.md`; add a K id to `register.md` before a technique is used in an experiment.
- No downloads without asking; reading pages is fine. Mark every fact "checked" (page seen), "digest" (page seen through a summarising tool) or "from memory".
- `tools/restructure/build_literature.py` is the script that built `papers/`, `repos/` and `register.md` from the original notes on 2026-10-24; later notes are added by hand.
