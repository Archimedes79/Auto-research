## 1. Card text
- **Statement (falsifiable):** a single ego-warped output state of the previous keyframe, fed through a zero-start gate, raises the pair precision at a fixed recall of the E312 head by more than 0.02 without losing topo F1, because 20 % of the true pairs flip between keyframes (E290) and the previous frame saw the same junction 0.5 s earlier.
- **What the state is, and what it cannot do:** the state is OUTPUT-space: for the previous keyframe the keypoint positions, headings and probabilities and the pair logits of the network itself (not B's or E312's frozen tokens: those live in another embedding space, are in-sample on TRAIN rows and embed the unwarped position). The keypoints are heat peaks of the CURRENT frame; the layer can only re-score pairs among the current peaks. A flip caused by a MISSING end in the current frame cannot be fixed by it, and the tokens carry no pair evidence of their own; the ceiling of the idea is therefore the share of the pairs delivered at t-1 and missed at t whose two ends are present at t (a logit-only flip). Step 0 measures it first.
- **Step 0, CPU, no training (the kill):** the E290 replay rewritten for predicted-keypoint association on the dump of E312 (`runs/e312_p_s<S>/ends_val.pkl`: `kp_pos`, `kp_dir`, `kp_p`, `logit`, `allowed`, `scale`): (a) the ceiling: the pairs delivered at t-1 (p >= 0.1) and missed at t, split into logit-only flips and end-miss flips (the end of the pair has no keypoint within 4 m / 30 degrees at t); (b) the zero-parameter replay: the pair logit averaged with the ego-warped logit of the associated pair of row - 1 (associated by the predicted keypoints of both frames, no GT in the averaged logit), weight **0.5 pre-registered**, a sweep of the weight labelled IN-SAMPLE on VAL; read out with the snapped E312 read-out. Decision: step 0 passes when the logit-only flips are at least 25 % of the missed-at-t pairs AND the replay at weight 0.5 raises the pair precision at c's recall (jpair_pr) by at least 0.01 on a seed with snapped topo F1 not below c - 0.005; otherwise the previous frame is a weak witness at 2 Hz and the card is not run. E290's own unread kill is read with it (the warp must map at least 95 % of the GT ends of one frame onto the next). The scratch `temp/e290/pair_flip.py` is rewritten and PROMOTED into `evaluation/` with a test when it is used for this card (the git rule).
- **Rows, poses, scene starts:** TRAIN rows: `poses_train.npz` (`prev`, `R`, `t`, `dt`) gives the previous row (row - 1 for 8202 of 8414 rows, 212 scene starts); `QData` filters rows by the stored GT (`lane_gt_data.py:67`), so the previous row is fetched by its row index, not by the position in the filtered list; the poses must be checked to be the CAM_FRONT lane frame of `qgraph_view._infos()` before use (a loader test with a known pose); VAL: `qgraph_view._infos()` and the cached order. A scene start has no state: the layer is skipped (zero state, mask).
- **Training the state:** in each step the network's own forward under `torch.no_grad()` on the previous row (about +40 % step cost, estimated), the state warped by the relative pose, CenterTrack noise (position jitter, 10 % false entries, 40 % missing), the gate initialised at zero; no backpropagation through time: the state is a constant input and there is NO gradient through time (named hard step of the end-to-end claim: differentiable inside a frame only). The teacher is the network itself, never a stored dump, and is not saved in `model.pt`. The recursive and the 'cross-attention' readings are then the same trained model: the stream buffer holds the previous output, the cross-attention reading recomputes it from the previous frame (+3.26 GMACs only if the pyramid output is not buffered); an eval-time switch on one checkpoint, no second training.
- **Where the layer sits and what feeds it:** after the set blocks of `JEnds` on the keypoint tokens (`jends.py`), keys / values from the warped state entries (position, heading, probability, the K x K pair logits row), additive to the pair features; the heat of the state is NOT used in this card (outputs only).
- **Controls and the null:** c2 (flag-off retrain on the current trainer) is the reference of the rule; the null checks run on the SAME checkpoint at inference: state off, the state of ANOTHER scene, the ACAUSAL state (row + 1): the gain must vanish for the first two and the third must not exceed the causal one by a suspicious margin (it would show that the head exploits the future correlation). The first-frame null covers only about 22 VAL frames and is reported, not relied on.
- **Prediction (numbers, written before the run; a hit is within +-0.02):** precision at c2's recall +0.02 (logit 0 and -2.2), snapped topo F1 +0.003, dup_frac 0, junction F1 0, lanes found +0.005; step 0: logit-only flips about 40 % of the missed pairs (a guess: no number exists yet), the replay at weight 0.5 +0.01 precision.
- **Cost (reported next to it):** the layer: one cross-attention over at most 32 keypoint tokens against 32 state entries, about 1 - 2 MMACs (the critic's count) against 22 MMACs of the whole pair head (E330) and 7.96 GMACs of the system; parameters about 15 k (estimate); a stream buffer of the previous output; training: +40 % per step for the no-grad forward, 2.6 s per step x 3000 = 2.2 h per run; budget: r x 2 seeds 4.4 h, c2 x 2 seeds 3.2 h, scoring, dumps and the sequential eval driver: about 9 GPU hours. The precision numbers of c (0.743 / 0.724) come from `jpair_pr.py` (the `ends_head.json` values 0.674 / 0.665 are another definition); E316's precision gain at E312's recall is +0.041 / +0.018.
- **Hard steps (D55, named):** the choice of the previous row, the top-K peak choice of the CURRENT frame (an index choice), the association by the warp (soft attention, no hard association), the p >= 0.1 threshold and everything after it as in E312; the state is detached (no gradient through time).
- **Not claimed:** any gain; a recursive end-to-end training through time; anything on TEST (D84 covers only E312).

## 5b. Step 0c (2026-11-14, CPU, no training): the heat-level replay, the one arm step 0b did not measure (rule written BEFORE the run; GO of the lane lead, D90)

**Combination (pre-registered, zero parameters):** p_c = 1 - (1 - p_t) (1 - w p_w), **w = 1.0**, noisy-OR (the probability that at least one of the two looks saw the end; two looks of 0.07 each give 0.14 > the peak threshold 0.1). Matching to the GT ends: `jwiring.match` (4 m / 30 degrees, one to one), the GT only labels, it never enters the combination. The sweep w in {0.25, 0.5} is reported labelled IN-SAMPLE and does not decide.
**Quantities per seed (851 consecutive pairs of frames, the 16 200 GT ends of t of step 0b):** GT ends matched by the baseline (tp0) and by p_c (tp1); GAINED = matched by p_c and missed by the baseline; LOST = matched by the baseline and missed by p_c; **net gain share = (tp1 - tp0) / GT ends**; gross gain share = GAINED / GT ends; NEW keypoints = keypoints of p_c with no counterpart (`counterpart`, 3 m / 30 degrees) among the baseline keypoints of t; TRUE-new = new keypoints that match a GT end the baseline missed, FALSE-new = all other new keypoints; end recall / precision of the baseline and of p_c.
**Rule:** the heat-level state (E336 arm r) is justified only when on BOTH seeds (a) the NET gain share is >= 0.05 AND (b) TRUE-new >= FALSE-new (the same two bars as step 0b, the net share because a state can also lose ends). **Limits (named):** one fixed combination, not a learned one; the heading of a new peak comes from frame t's heading map, which may be poor where the end is faint; the E312 maps were trained to produce peaks in ONE frame, a state trained for accumulation could behave differently; the replay uses E312's maps head with its own pair training in the weights (the maps are the ones of the system of record).

**5b amendment (2026-11-14, BEFORE the run, after the light cards pass: REVISE, six lines added; the design, w = 1.0 and the two bars are unchanged).**
1. **Null arms (same quantities, they decide nothing alone):** N1 = the heat of t noisy-ORed with ITSELF (p_c = 1 - (1 - p_t)^2: no time information, only the lowered effective peak threshold); N2 = the previous heat NOT warped. **Reading:** if the net gain share of the real arm (w = 1.0) exceeds max(N1, N2) by less than 0.02 on either seed, the gain is not temporal and bar (a) counts as FAILED even when the raw number is >= 0.05. **Closure wording:** a failure of (a) closes only the zero-parameter noisy-OR accumulation (w = 1.0, bilinear warp); it is no ceiling for a learned heat-side state. The heat-visible ceiling is reported next to it (not a bar): the share of the baseline-missed GT ends that have a warped previous heat value >= 0.05 within 4 m (any heading), against the same count of random places in the same frames (RandomState 1234).
2. **Baseline sanity (a stop, before any combination):** the baseline extraction on the CPU reproduces `ends_val.pkl` on the stored frames (number of valid keypoints per kind and frame equal in >= 99 % of the frames, position difference <= 0.05 m), and the baseline end recall / precision over the 851 pairs equal those of step 0b (0.787 / 0.620 seed 0, 0.790 / 0.602 seed 1) within 0.003; otherwise stop and report, nothing is read.
3. **Definitions:** per kind, `jwiring.match` against the full GT of t; tp0 / tp1 = distinct GT ends matched by the baseline / by p_c; GAINED = matched by p_c and not by the baseline; LOST = the reverse; tp1 - tp0 = GAINED - LOST (asserted). NEW = a p_c keypoint with no mutual-nearest counterpart (`counterpart`, 3 m / 30 degrees) among the baseline keypoints of t. Also reported, not deciding: VANISHED = baseline keypoints without a counterpart in p_c (and how many of them were matches), valid keypoints per frame and keypoint precision of both, the per-arm numbers of N1 and N2. 4. **Extraction as `JEnds.forward`:** position = soft-argmax over the 3 x 3 window of p_c (cell centres c = -50 + (i + 0.5), dim 1 = x, dim 2 = y), heading = the unit heading map of frame t at the peak cell of p_c (entry channels 4:6, exit 6:8), top K = 32 per kind by p_c. 5. **Warp test:** the selftest of `temp/e299/heat_replay.py` checks identity, a 5 m shift and a 90-degree pose against `jtime.warp`.
