**What it does**: Fuses surround-view 4D radar and cameras for 3D semantic occupancy with a Radar Height Self-Attention module for the sparse radar. Datasets: OmniHD-Scenes (200 clips, 11 semantic classes, six 4D radars; the static-element GT comes from manual annotation of lidar points, per the page) and, for generalisation with conventional radar, SurroundOcc-nuScenes. Pseudo labels (Grounded-SAM masks combined with lidar and 3D boxes) supervise the occupancy output as a whole and are not radar-specific.

**Results that matter**: no radar-only or static-class number extracted. Loss (Eq. 10): cross-entropy (weight 1) + Lovasz-softmax (5) + geometric scene-class affinity (1) + semantic scene-class affinity (1). "The radar branch receives no direct supervision"; it trains end-to-end through these losses.

**Transferable technique**: none to port; the loss recipe (CE + Lovasz + two affinity terms) is the standard occupancy loss that OccFusion, Doracamom and RadarOcc also use.

**Cost and fit**: needs 4D radar data we do not have.

**Doubts**: only the loss part was queried; no ablation of radar supervision.
