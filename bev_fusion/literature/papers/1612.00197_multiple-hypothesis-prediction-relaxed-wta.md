**Verification of the from-memory citation**: "MCL" is Guzman-Rivera et al. (NIPS 2012, not looked up here); the deep-network version is sMCL, 1606.07839 (Lee, Purushwalkam, Cogswell, Ranjan, Crandall, Batra; 2016-06-24; "minimises loss with respect to an oracle", independent of architecture and loss, no extra parameters). The multiple-hypothesis-prediction (MHP) paper for deep nets that is easiest to cite is 1612.00197 (Rupprecht, Laina, DiPietro, Baust, Tombari, Navab, Hager; 2016-12-01, v3 2017-08-22).

**What it does** [checked]: M hypothesis outputs per input. Meta loss: only the hypothesis whose Voronoi cell contains the label gets the base loss (extension of Lloyd's method to gradient descent). Hard assignment starves hypotheses (if all start far from the labels only one gets gradients), so the RELAXED version gives the closest hypothesis weight 1 - eps and each other eps / (M - 1) with eps = 0.05, and drops whole predictions at random with about 1 % probability; the paper says non-selected predictions "will slowly evolve until their Voronoi regions contain some training samples". The paper has NO separate score network: the selection is implicit; mixture density networks (with mixing coefficients) are discussed as the alternative and criticised because components with coefficients near zero lose their gradients. Side effects: faint "ghost" predictions from eps; blurring of single-hypothesis models (average of modes) is avoided.

**Results that matter** [checked]: LSP pose, oracle-selected PCP: 1 hypothesis 59.7 %, 5 hypotheses 61.2 %, 10 hypotheses 62.8 %; VOC2007 mAP 83.8 (single) -> 85.1 (9 hypotheses); COCO mAP 65.2 -> 67.8 (5); VOC2012 segmentation 4 hypotheses 70.3 % mIoU vs 69.1 % for MCL with 134.9 M vs 539.6 M parameters; NTU future frame min MSE 960.6 -> 728.5 (10 hypotheses). The oracle picks the best hypothesis, so these are best-of-M numbers.

**Transferable technique**: our block has the learned score s_m in a softmax mixture instead of a WTA: the splat output is a weighted MIXTURE of the hypotheses, trained end to end on the final loss, so every hypothesis gets gradient in proportion to pi_m (no Voronoi assignment); the failure to watch is the same one the paper names, a hypothesis that never wins gets pi_m -> 0 and stops learning (mixture-density collapse). Cheap guards from the paper: eps-relaxed weights (a floor on pi_m early in training, e.g. pi_m <- (1 - eps) pi_m + eps / M, annealed to 0), whole-hypothesis dropout, and logging the entropy of pi per range bin as a health metric; for velocity supervision (MotionHead.loss) a WTA over M hypotheses against the GT cell velocity trains the f_m without touching the score.

**Cost and fit**: nothing to install; 5 lines in the loss.

**Doubts**: oracle (best-of-M) metrics hide calibration; future-frame experiment is NTU video, not driving.
