## 2. Three project questions

**(a) Differentiable registration of a perceived graph to the SD map (translation + yaw).**
- Already in the project and not re-read here: OrienterNet probability volume over (x, y, yaw) with soft-argmax (K99, PL46), HOLO, U-ViLAR, MapLocNet, EgoVM histogram solver with covariance, UMPE SE(2) micro-alignment (C02, C24-C29).
- New in this pass: BEV-DWPVO (arXiv 2502.20078, 2025, checked): a differentiable weighted Procrustes solver on BEV keypoints reduces pose to 3 DoF and trains end to end with pose supervision only. No 2025-26 paper that registers a vector graph (not a raster or image) to an SD map was found.
- Design suggestion (our inference, not from a paper): render the perceived graph as a soft distance / heat raster, correlate with the SD-map distance field over a small pose grid (about +-5 m, +-10 deg, a few thousand poses; rotate the map K times, then one batched convolution), softmax over poses, expectation (K08 / K113), optional weighted Procrustes on soft vertex-to-polyline matches; the pose covariance is the trust input for (b).

**(b) Map trust / abstain for wrong or missing maps.**
- Already in the project: dustbin (SuperGlue C30), matchability (LightGlue C31), existence-aware matched / outdated split (RTMap C08), tri-mode training (Uni-PrevPredMap C06), noise-as-diffusion (NavMapFusion C03), presence gate (UMPE C02). MapEx (arXiv 2311.10517) is in `lit7/lit7_b.md`.
- New: ArgoTweak (arXiv 2509.08764, ICCV 2025, checked): dataset with realistic outdated priors made of atomic element-level edits; the abstract reports a smaller sim2real gap than synthetic priors (no numbers on the page; licence CC BY-NC-SA). Gated Attention (row 5) gives a query-dependent "attend to nothing" without a dustbin.
- Gap: no paper trains an explicit, calibrated "this map is wrong" output for lane-level assignment. Suggested recipe (ours): a learned null column in the vertex-to-SD-edge assignment, ArgoTweak-style deletes and warps as training noise, the output gate, and a route-success curve at 0 / 25 / 50 % deleted edges plus a wrong map.

**(c) Fast CPU/GPU graph assembly (vectorised path-union, NMS-free graph building).**
- Nothing found for 2025-26 on lane-centre-line graph assembly (three searches). Nearest: Work-Efficient Parallel NMS Kernels (arXiv 2502.00535, posted Feb 2025, journal 2022, checked): about 1 ms for 1,024 candidates on Tegra X1 / X2, valid for Soft-NMS style variants; GraphMorph (arXiv 2502.11731, NeurIPS 2024, checked): branch-level graph decoder + SkeletonDijkstra to align a centreline map to the graph (sequential, not a speed result).
- Matching-free training is covered by row 8 (Beyond Hungarian); NMS-free inference by YOLOv10 / Polar R-CNN (MH21, VS28, in project).
- Our own K186 (CUDA graphs) and K187 (vectorised union, 50 -> 15 ms idle, identical graphs) are ahead of everything found: build further in-house; no paper to import.

## 3. Ranked top-5: try first

1. **Output gate in the route-head cross-attention (Gated Attention, 2505.06708)**: one sigmoid per head, differentiable, and the first cheap test of "abstain on a wrong or missing SD map" with the route-success curve of (b).
2. **LapSum exact-k soft top-k (2503.06242; 2608.06912 if n grows)** for selecting tree / beam children: attacks open literature gap 1 (gradient to dropped hypotheses) without argmax on the training path.
3. **Posterior-weighted merge + one EM step on saved WTA hypotheses (2606.26424)**: post-hoc, no retraining, and it targets the unmeasured calibration of fork probabilities (gap 3).
4. **minGRU- or gated-delta-style per-vertex state (2410.01201, 2412.06464)**: smallest recurrent cell with an erase gate, trainable in parallel on cached windows; aims at the missing validity memory on vertices (gap 2) and costs microseconds.
5. **Strip-convolution block in the dense lane-field head (2501.03775)**: a one-file swap suited to thin elongated lanes; judge it by ms at batch 1 on an idle 3060 (2607.01984 is the reminder that FLOPs mislead) and by S against the 0.010 noise band.

Next in line (not top-5): weight-shared builder refinement with intra-loop self-distillation and a learned stop score (ELT 2604.09168 + LoopVLA 2605.09948), bounded-distribution vertex offsets (D-FINE 2410.13842).

## 4. Gaps and caveats

- Hazard / survival along a ray: no new perception paper; the register's K02 / K41 stand.
- Convnet and SSM candidates are validated on ImageNet, language or ARC, not on frozen BEV features; our own E27 (map-pretrained foundation 0.867 vs detection-pretrained 0.624 F1 on VAL) says the foundation mattered far more than the head, so expect small head-side gains.
- WebSearch is a US-only snapshot with summarised results; a missed paper is possible. Paper ids come from fetched landing pages, except the two "from memory" rows (verify before use).
- No file other than this one was written; no GPU job, tool script or project file was touched.
