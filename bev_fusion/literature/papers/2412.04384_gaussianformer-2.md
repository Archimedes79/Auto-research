**What it does**: each 3D Gaussian is read as the probability that its neighbourhood is occupied; geometry is the probabilistic product of these probabilities (empty = product of (1 - p)), semantics an exact Gaussian mixture. A distribution-based initialisation module learns pixel-aligned OCCUPANCY (not surface depth) so that Gaussians start in non-empty regions. Depth uncertainty: the distribution over where along the ray content exists is learned directly.

**Results that matter**: abstract says state of the art on nuScenes and KITTI-360 with as few as 8.9 % of the Gaussians of GaussianFormer; no IoU, FPS or memory numbers on the page.

**Transferable technique**: occupancy-probability superposition is a way to merge overlapping splats from many pixels without double counting: BEV cell value = 1 - prod(1 - p_i) instead of alpha-blending by depth order; relevant if the Gaussians of a front camera overlap heavily at range.

**Cost and fit**: attention-based Gaussian updates with sparse CUDA ops, heavier than a one-shot splat; six cameras, 3D semantic occupancy.

**Doubts**: no latency read; query-based refinement is not the cheap lift we want.
