## 1 Card
- **Question.** With the lifts, trunk, heads and radar side of `t20.base_s0` unchanged, does a student encoder that outputs the same three tensors keep every guard inside its margin and cut the image side to a fraction of its time? It does NOT ask for a lane gain: nobody published a stride 4 / 8 / 16 ablation for thin or far lanes, and in our chain the stride-4 branch alone did not move the lane divider (t20: 0.542 against 0.549).
- **Strides (the question "4 or 8").** The depth distribution and the main context are at **stride 8** (118 bins + 80 channels, 32 x 88 cells at 256 x 704): this is the finest level the camera-only BEV map work lifts (BEVFusion camera-only seg, GaussianLSS / GaussianCaR, Simple-BEV, PointBeV; LSS, BEVDet, BEVDepth and our DualBEV stay at 16; plan 2.7 / 2.8). A **stride-4 detail level** carries context only (no depth: lifted with the stride-8 depth, as the `res4` branch does today): 64 x 176 cells, where one pixel is about 0.14 m at 20 m. A depth head at stride 4 would cost 4 x the cells of stride 8 (118 bins each) for no proven gain (Fast-BEV's stride-4 map gave no detection gain). Strides 16 / 32 live inside the encoder and feed the DualBEV-convention output. Our own evidence: oracle lift stride 16 keeps 0.17 of the 40-60 m lane lines, stride 8 with 4 sub-heights 0.81; t7's stride-4 branch +0.042 / +0.065 lane divider IoU@0.5.
- **The student (drop-in outputs, so everything downstream and the warm start stay valid).** Encoder: `timm` EfficientViT-B1 (`features_only`: strides 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 with 32 / 64 / 128 / 256 channels; 4.6 M parameters without the classifier; weights `models/pretrained/imagenet/efficientvit_b1_r256_in1k`, Apache-2.0 tag; probed on CPU 2026-11-04). The control encoder is ResNet-18 (strides 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 with 64 / 128 / 256 / 512 channels, 11.7 M, weights present). A light FPN with 128 channels (top-down only) and three heads: **H16** = 118 depth logits + 80 context at 16 x 44 in DualBEV's cell-centre convention (target: the cached `cam_feats` outputs); **H8** = 118 + 80 at 32 x 88 in BEVFusion's frustum-cell convention (target: the cached `cam_feats_bevfusion`); **H4** = the 256-channel map of ResNet-50 layer 1 at 64 x 176 (target: the frozen layer 1 run on the same JPEG, what `res4` reads today). Depth heads are light (a 3 x 3 conv with 128 channels, then 1 x 1, as the plan's candidate), not DualBEV's 35.6-GFLOP DepthNet. Registered as a new entry of the `CAMERAS` table (`model.CAMERAS`) with a live path for the current key-frame image (`data/cache/img_front`: 19,398 JPEGs, about 1 GB). A single-source student (one lift, no `cam_merge`) is a second step (t22b) if this one stands.
- **First try (Mirko, 2026-11-04: "stride 8 and following your suggestion on the selection. Lets just try the candidate with the best chances and then refine it").** Only the main candidate: EfficientViT-B1 (arm S), stride 8 as the main level, outputs **H16 + H8** (the two cached teacher interfaces, so the chain and the warm start stay valid) with the `res4` branch left as it is (frozen ResNet-50 layer 1 on the JPEG, small next to the rest); one seed; no ResNet-18 arm yet; the control is the t21 arm Mirko takes.
