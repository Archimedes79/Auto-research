## 1. The design
### 1.1 The question, the facts, why a gate
The association has two parts: WHICH segment a token belongs to (the zero-shot P6 head does this well: `seg_acc` 0.913 / 0.903 on the tokens that have a present segment, `nonfalse_acc` 0.895 / 0.891 with the missing-segment tokens) and WHETHER it has one (a false token has none, a token on a missing segment has none in the map). F1 learned both together. The gate separates them: the P6 head assigns, an existence classifier says 'unassigned' or not. **Facts of the stored data** (recomputed by the lane chat and the critic from `runs/e391_assoc/*.json`, `train_s<S>.pkl`, `runs/e391_F1_s<S>/card_*.json`): of the SCORED tokens (delivered and labelled or false) 33.4 % / 33.1 % are false on VAL (44 401 of 132 921, 43 637 of 132 009; of all delivered tokens 24.0 % / 23.7 %), 23.5 % / 24.2 % on the TRAIN holdout, **68.5 % / 68.1 % on the fit frames as E391 used them (mask missing)**; missing-segment tokens are 6.9 % of the VAL scored set; p6gate rejects 78.2 / 78.0 % of the false tokens at false_unassigned 0.16 - 0.17, F1 75.2 / 76.2 % at 0.128 / 0.132; the zero-shot head's own 'unassigned' slot already rejects 15 / 18 % of the false tokens and its false_unassigned is 0.014 / 0.030 on VAL (it eats part of the guard). The bars equal null + 0.25 x (1 - null) with the null p6gate. **What a gate must reach:** with the P6 argmax on the accepted tokens and the VAL composition, the final rejection of the false tokens must be about 0.72 - 0.80 at a total false_unassigned of 0.04 - 0.05 (the range depends on whether the dropped true tokens would have been right and on the recall of the missing segments, 0.75 - 0.90); the tree has to lift the ROC strongly, not by a few points.
**The oracle gate** (a perfect existence decision, then the P6 argmax) reads assoc_acc 0.9479 (seed 0) / 0.9420 (seed 1) on VAL: the ceiling of the decode.

### 1.2 Data (E391's; nothing of E391 / E390 is edited)
`evaluation/mapassoc_probe.AssocData` (the cached B' tokens of cache seed S, the E390 road graph under the map draw of the level, the labels), the encoder `runs/e390_g7_s<S>` and the zero-shot head `heads6.assoc` (frozen), the level **dec** (pose 1 m, count 0.1, link drop 0.1, route hide 0.3, segments missing 0.1; fixed draws by (391, frame)); part A's `collect` uses 2 loader workers, part B trains and reads with E391's own 4. **The delivered set** (the champion's p_none < 0.6): stored in the labels for VAL and the holdout; for the TRAIN fit frames computed by `lane_mapassoc_data.e373_pnone` (the E373 stage-1 head `runs/e373_cb_s<S>` on the CPU, every TRAIN frame once, about 10 min) into a NEW file `runs/e391_assoc/exist_pnone_s<S>.pkl` (the shared pickles are not edited), asserted equal to the stored mask on the holdout frames (the kill). **The fit set is therefore delivered by the E373 head on frames that head was trained on (in-sample, as the holdout is for B'), and the E373 stage-1 head's p_none is a PROXY of the delivered set of the S4 champion (whose B' was trained jointly): said, also for the p_none input of g0 / g1n / g1.** The scored set of a frame: the delivered tokens with an association label (`delivered & assoc >= -1`). Kinds: 0 a present segment, 1 its segment missing from the map, 2 a false token; the gates' target: `assoc == -1` (kind 1 or 2, for all three arms alike). New code: `evaluation/mapassoc_exist.py` (it carries its own `selftest`, as `maperror_probe.py` does) and a README row.

### 1.3 The features, the classifiers, the threshold
- **Own outputs (g0, g1n, g1):** sigmoid(e), sigmoid(flags) (all columns), tok (all columns), and p_none (the champion's, from the file above: for VAL from the stored E373 dump).
- **P6 summary (g1 only):** from the zero-shot logits (softmax over the present segments and the unassigned slot): p_unassigned, the largest and the second segment probability, their margin, the entropy. **Token-map geometry (g1 only):** from `pa` (D135 proximity features: column 0 the distance / 10 m clipped at 5, column 1 the cos of the heading difference, column 7 = 1 for a present segment; absent and padded segments are zeros and are masked by `valid`): the smallest distance to a PRESENT segment in m (50 when the map has none), the cos there (0 when none), the number of present segments within 2 m and 4 m. The P6 summary is partly redundant with the geometry (the head's own 'none' slot is a function of the nearest distance, cos and lateral offset): said. Nothing label-dependent (kind, assoc, gt_id, the lane target) is a feature.
- **Classifiers:** g0 `LogisticRegression(max_iter=3000)` on standardized features; g1n / g1 `HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=200, learning_rate=0.1, max_leaf_nodes=31, l2_regularization=1.0, early_stopping=False, random_state=0)`, values fixed here, not tuned. The fit set: 2000 TRAIN frames outside the holdout (generator (3913, S)) at the level dec, delivered tokens only (about 280 k tokens, about 210 k of them in the fitted scenes), a quarter of the SCENES held out for the threshold, never fitted.
- **The threshold:** on the validation scenes, the value on a grid of 0.02 steps of the predicted probability of 'unassigned' that maximizes the VAL-composition-re-weighted assoc_acc subject to the re-weighted false_unassigned <= 0.04 (a margin to the guard 0.05; the zero-shot head's own false_unassigned shifts by +0.004 / +0.013 (seed 0 / 1) from the holdout to VAL, so the guard is NOT expected to hold with certainty on VAL); none -> the arm cannot meet the guard (its stop rule fails).
- **The accepted tokens** (a token whose probability of 'unassigned' is below the threshold) get the P6 argmax, which can itself be the head's own 'unassigned' slot (as in p6gate).

### 1.4 The re-weighting (the holdout is not the VAL composition)
The holdout reads are in-sample for B' and 22 scenes. **Re-weighted metrics:** the per-kind rates (kind 0: assigned right; kind 1 and 2: left unassigned; kind 0 left unassigned = false_unassigned) measured on the holdout, combined with the kind shares of the VAL dec level of the same seed (`nulls_val_s<S>.json`, `levels.dec.counts`: tokens, absent, false; present = tokens - absent - false: 0.597 / 0.069 / 0.334 for seed 0). This corrects the composition, NOT the shift of the features: **measured on the stored files, a learned gate's re-weighted holdout assoc_acc is optimistic against VAL by +0.014 / +0.022 (p6gate) and +0.017 / +0.020 (F1)**, while the zero-shot head goes the other way (-0.016 / -0.007); the holdout's interval is about +-0.026. The stop rule therefore carries a margin of 0.02.

### 1.5 The rule, written before any run
- **Stop rule (the holdout, both seeds, per arm):** an arm goes on to the VAL read only if on BOTH seeds its re-weighted assoc_acc >= the VAL bar of E391 section 5.1 + 0.02 (0.8627 / 0.8597) AND its re-weighted false_unassigned <= 0.05. The step reads VAL only if **g1** passes. If g1 fails on either seed, part A CLOSES, in one of two cases: (i) **no arm passes on both seeds: 'not shown separable at the bar with these inputs'** (a noisy point estimate on 22 scenes; a one-seed miss closes it too; it does not say that no head can, and not that E391's NO FUSION stands: the population of E391 was wrong), with the report of the re-weighted operating curves of g0, g1n and g1 and what is missing (token context, the live B' features, a temporal state); (ii) **g0 or g1n passes on both seeds while g1 fails: 'the separation exists without the map inputs'** (g1's P6 and geometry inputs are in-sample at fit and out-of-sample at hold, so g1 can fail where its subset passes): reported, no VAL read of g1 by this rule; `outcome` prints the case. g0 and g1n are reported at the holdout in any case.
- **VAL read (once, only if g1 passed; at the level dec; g0 and g1n are read on the same draws as the no-map nulls whatever their stop rule says):** per seed, **clause A** for g1: assoc_acc >= 0.8427 / 0.8397 (E391's bars, written before E391 ran), the paired scene-bootstrap interval of assoc_acc against g0 AND against g1n above 0 (the map has to add something to a no-map gate), false_unassigned <= 0.05. **Lanes (reported, no clause):** the lanes read through the association with z as the fall-back (for the unassigned tokens and also for a token assigned to a segment that is not a road or has no known lane class; p6gate keeps E391's class 1) cannot clear E391's L bars by construction (the oracle association read through the map's input lane classes gives 0.8935 / 0.8939 against the bars 0.9182 / 0.9044, the map's classes carry a 10 % count error); lanes_acc of g1, of z and of the p6gate read-through are printed. **Route (R) is not read by a decode rule** (the oracle read-through of the map's route flag is 0.4745 against the bar 0.8771). Minimum counts (assert): 20000 labelled tokens, 3000 false, 2000 tokens of missing segments. The VAL numbers of E391's p6gate are quoted from `nulls_gate_val_s<S>.json`, not recomputed.
- **Outcomes (in this order; both seeds):** **CLOSED** (g1 failed the stop rule: no VAL read; the two cases above); **NO-MAP GATE SUFFICES** (the SAME arm, g0 or g1n, meets assoc_acc >= the bar AND false_unassigned <= 0.05 as a point estimate on both seeds: the existence decision is separable WITHOUT the map, so the map adds nothing at this decode; reported as a finding about E391's population, not as a fusion); **NO FUSION** (g1 fails clause A on both seeds); **INCONCLUSIVE** (g1 passes A on one seed only, or passes on both but by less than the band 0.01 above the bar on a seed; E390 / E365 have no seed 2, so 'a third seed' cannot be run: it stays INCONCLUSIVE); **GATE WORKS** (g1 passes A on both seeds, each at least 0.01 above its bar): goes to the owner, nothing is adopted; it would motivate a differentiable existence term in a trained head (E391c), not the tree.
- **What a verdict supports and what not:** VAL has been read by E390 (three times) and E391 (once): part A is the fifth read of the same 873 frames and part B (below) the sixth (the p6gate reproduction check and the null read `nullread` touch VAL too); the VAL LABEL COUNTS (the kind shares) set the thresholds and the stop rule (`val_weights`); a null that cannot meet the guard on the TRAIN validation scenes is read at the threshold 0.5 and flagged (`threshold_fallback`); TRAIN and the holdout are in-sample for B'; only `dec` is read; the 28 % of delivered tokens that are unlabelled (split / merge stretches, 51 898 on seed 1) are not scored, so a gate that rejects true tokens there is not penalised; tokens within a frame and a scene are correlated (the scene bootstrap); E390's P6 head was trained on the non-holdout TRAIN frames, which are the fit frames, so the g1 geometry and P6 inputs are in-sample at fit time and out-of-sample at hold and VAL (a source of shift of its own, measured by the holdout stop rule). The tree and a threshold are rule steps (design R1: a candidate that adds a rule step is no solution; D145 (2): winners are learned end-to-end systems only): E391b is a feasibility probe; its GATE WORKS or CLOSED is information for the next card, not an adoption. **A reference without cost:** the oracle gate (above) and g0 re-thresholded by the guard rule are the matched operating-point references in the reports.

- **PART B, the rule (E391 section 5.1, unchanged, so that the two cards are comparable):** arms P, F1, F2, F3, seeds 0 and 1 (cache seed = E390 encoder seed = S), the ONE change = the loss and the fit only on the delivered tokens. The bars are E391's, written before E391 ran (VAL assoc_acc >= 0.8427 / 0.8397 with the paired interval against p6gate above 0 and false_unassigned <= 0.05; lanes_acc >= 0.9182 / 0.9044; route_f1 >= 0.8771 / 0.8750; holdout bars for the choice 0.8472 / 0.8499, 0.9266 / 0.9282, 0.8664 / 0.8553); the nulls are E391's stored files (their p6gate was itself fitted on the wrong population, so the bar is, if anything, EASIER than a corrected null would give: **the corrected no-map null g0 of part A is printed beside the verdict**; a PASS of a fusion arm while g0 alone meets the association bar does not show a map effect, said in the report). The choice on the TRAIN holdout is E391's mechanical rule (`choose_d`: the association arm F1 if it beats P by more than 0.01 on both seeds, else P; the attribute arm the first of P, F1, F2, F3 that passes the holdout association and attribute bars on both seeds, none: the best attribute mean among those passing the association bars, none: the association arm), then ONE VAL read of the chosen arm(s), and the verdict in E391's order (`verdict_d`, code with a case table): **NO FUSION** = the association arm fails clause A on both seeds; **INCONCLUSIVE** = a deciding clause passes on one seed and fails on the other (no seed 2 exists); **PARTLY** = the association arm passes A on both seeds, the attribute arm fails A, L or R on both; **PASS** = the association arm passes A on both seeds and the attribute arm passes A, L and R on both. A killed arm fails every bar on its seed (a missing card is NaN). **An arm that was NOT RUN (the time cut of D145: no GPU start after 2026-11-25 03:00) leaves part B INCOMPLETE: no choice and no verdict until it has run in the next window; the missing arm is never read as a failure** (`choose_d` refuses missing cards; `--allow-missing` exists only for the owner's explicit decision to read an incomplete set).
- **What each outcome means for the E391 record (written before any run):** part B **PASS or PARTLY** (the association arm passes clause A on both seeds with the population corrected): **E391's NO FUSION is REVERSED UNDER E391's BARS**: the fit population, not the architecture, was the problem; the E391 text stays as the record of what was run, the E391b verdict is the current reading, DESIGN.md R9 changes only by the lead on that verdict. **The record text must say, from the VAL read of the corrected no-map null g0 (`nullread`, point estimates, not paired): 'map effect over the corrected no-map gate: not shown' if g0 meets the association bar and the guard on both seeds, else 'g0 reaches <x> / <y> below the bar'.** (E391's p6gate null was itself fitted on the wrong population, so its bar is, if anything, easier than a corrected one; a pass of a fusion arm by less than the band 0.01 above the bar is reported with its margin: part B uses E391's point bar unchanged, for comparability.) Part B **NO FUSION**: **E391's NO FUSION is NOT REVERSED by the delivered-mask loss**: it stands as recorded; this is 'confirmed' only in that sense, because a residual shift remains (the delivered TRAIN false share 23.5 - 24 % against 33 % on VAL, features in-sample for B' and the E373 head, no prior correction in the argmax decode) and is named in the report; part B **INCONCLUSIVE** (a seed split, or a kill on one seed): **UNRESOLVED**, E391's NO FUSION stays the recorded verdict with its caveat. Part A does not change E391's record by itself: NO-MAP GATE SUFFICES or GATE WORKS say that the false-token decision is separable on the right population (pointing the same way as a reversal); CLOSED says nothing about E391's verdict. Nothing is adopted by any outcome (D145).
- **Differences from E391 other than the population (said, because 'ONE change' is the rule of comparability):** (1) part B uses E391's 4 loader workers and the same `torch.manual_seed`, and `AssocDataD` draws no extra random number, so the TRAIN map draws are plausibly E391's (not verified: the optional pairing below would show it); the time per arm is E391's about 9 min only while the GPU is not shared; (2) the masking also removes the lanes and route targets of the tokens that are not delivered, so F2 / F3 see about 3 times fewer supervised tokens per step at the same 3000 steps: 'under-trained' is a rival reading of a part B NO FUSION, named; (3) arm z (the no-map lanes / route head that sets the L and R bars) and E391's p6gate, nearest and logistic fits were trained on the old population and are not refitted: the L and R bars carry that flaw (part B's clause A is the deciding one); (4) the optional pairing of F1d against E391's F1 on the same draws and a prior-corrected read of F1d (a threshold fitted on the TRAIN validation scenes as in part A) are NOT part of this card.

### 1.6 Prediction (a guess, before any run)
With the population right, the logistic gate alone (g0) gains over E391's p6gate: assoc_acc about 0.82 on VAL (F1 on the wrong population read 0.822 at a 13 % true-token loss); the tree on the same inputs adds a few points, the map-side inputs more; the guard false_unassigned <= 0.05 is the likelier failure than the accuracy (the zero-shot head eats 0.014 - 0.030 of it). Stop rule: g1 passes on both seeds 45 %, g1n 30 %, g0 8 % (g0 would need a re-weighted holdout assoc_acc of 0.8627, the measured holdout optimism is +0.017 to +0.022: a logistic gate at about 0.82 on VAL is below that). Outcomes of part A (g1 passes the stop rule on both seeds 45 %; given a pass: GATE WORKS 40 %, NO-MAP GATE SUFFICES 10 %, NO FUSION 20 %, INCONCLUSIVE 30 %): CLOSED 55 %, NO-MAP GATE SUFFICES 5 %, NO FUSION 9 %, INCONCLUSIVE 13 %, GATE WORKS 18 %. **Part B** (a guess): with the prior corrected F1 loses far fewer true tokens (false_unassigned about 0.05 - 0.08 at a similar false rejection) and reads assoc_acc about 0.85 on VAL, F2 / F3 about 0.86; E391's outcome is REVERSED (PASS or PARTLY) 40 %, NO FUSION not reversed 30 %, INCONCLUSIVE 30 % (a seed split, or the guard 0.05 failing on one seed); route (R) stays the weak clause (F2 / F3 near the bar on the holdout, 0.85, in-sample).
