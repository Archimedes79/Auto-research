Raw shards are made by code from a seed and named by hashes in a manifest. The truth store is made from the annotations and named by the signature of the rasteriser. The trainer uses the general engine `trainkit`; a run goes through `Training` and the launcher `run.py`. The Runner stores one mask per frame, the evaluation counts hits and misses, and `decide.py` applies the rule of the card to the metric cards. One step is a rule on the output path: a pixel is the disc when its logit is above zero.
