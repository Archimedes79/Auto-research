**What it does**: Builds per-keyframe dense voxel labels from nuScenes lidar without us touching lidar. Pipeline in three steps (checked): (1) voxel densification: dynamic objects and static scene are separated; points of dynamic objects are aggregated in their box frames and concatenated, static points are aggregated in the global frame; non-keyframes get labels by nearest-neighbour; non-ground surfaces are meshed with VDBFusion (TSDF), ground by local surface fitting; (2) occlusion reasoning: ray casting from the lidar origins marks voxels as occupied / free / unobserved (mask_lidar), and the camera visibility (first occupied voxel along each camera ray) gives mask_camera; (3) image-guided refinement with semantic masks sets voxels in front of the first matching voxel to free.

**Format and coverage** [checked on the data card]: 200 x 200 x 16 voxels of 0.4 m over x, y in [-40, 40] m and z in [-1, 5.4] m; 18 values: 0-16 as nuScenes-lidarseg (0 others / general object, 1 barrier, 11 drivable surface, 12 other flat, 13 sidewalk, 14 terrain, 15 manmade, 16 vegetation, vehicles and pedestrians in between; id-to-name order is memory, verify on the data) and 17 free. Per frame `gts/<scene>/<sample_token>/labels.npz` with `semantics`, `mask_lidar`, `mask_camera`; `annotations.json` holds `train_split` / `val_split` scene lists and per frame timestamp, ego_pose, prev / next. Frames: mini 404, train 28,130, val 6,019, test 6,008 (data card) = the keyframes of the 850 trainval scenes (the OpenOccupancy paper lists the same 28,130 / 6,019) at 2 Hz (datasheet). Hosting: Google Drive, Baidu, OpenDataLab; sizes on the card: mini about 440 MB, trainval about 32 GB (folder holds imgs + gts + annotations.json; the gts part alone is not stated), test about 6 GB. The paper's own split text is inconsistent (600/150/150 vs 700/150/150 scenes).

**Results that matter**: none for radar. Radar evidence from others: OccFusion (2403.01644) reports a drop when radar is added on Occ3D and blames the label extent and the visibility mask; REOcc (2511.06666) and HyDRa (2403.07746) train their shared occupancy head on it without a radar-specific loss.

**Transferable technique**: (1) the static / dynamic split before accumulation (static in the world frame, objects in box frames) is the same recipe as SurroundOcc, OpenOcc and RadarOcc, so a static-only layer can be taken from the semantics (barrier, manmade, vegetation, terrain ...) and parked vehicles added from boxes with speed < 0.5 m/s; (2) use mask_lidar, not mask_camera, as the "defined" mask of a radar target (mask_camera hides what the lens does not see, which is where radar adds information); (3) collapse z by a per-column rule (structure voxels from 0.3 m above the highest ground voxel to 3 m) instead of fixed slice numbers, then resample 0.4 m to our 0.5 m cells by area coverage.

**Cost and fit**: no lidar download. Our grid x 0..60 m, y -26..26 m: Occ3D covers x 0..40 only (66.7 % of the area, computed), y fully. The far third, where radar helps most, has no label. Labels are in the ego frame at the keyframe time; ours is the ego frame at the front-camera time (shift by the ego poses, at most about one cell at 15 m/s; the lidar-camera offset is memory). The 510 scenes of parts 1-6 should be inside the trainval split (inference from the frame counts; check with annotations.json).

**Doubts**: dense labels in the 25-40 m ring come from sparse lidar plus Poisson/TSDF filling; nuScenes ego poses are noisy for accumulation (an ECCV 2024 supplement says so, digest); parked and moving cars are not separated in the semantics; the camera-visibility step needs the images (camera-defined, not radar-defined); the class "others" mixes unknown static things with debris.
