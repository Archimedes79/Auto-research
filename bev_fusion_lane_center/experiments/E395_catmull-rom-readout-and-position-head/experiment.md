## 1. Card text
### 1.1 The change in detail
- **Catmull-Rom curve (uniform, tau = 1/2):** a segment from node P1 to P2 with the ghost nodes P0 (before P1) and P3 (after P2): `C(t) = 0.5 * ((2 P1) + (-P0 + P2) t + (2 P0 - 5 P1 + 4 P2 - P3) t^2 + (-P0 + 3 P1 - 3 P2 + P3) t^3)`, t in [0, 1]: it passes through P1 and P2, the tangent at P1 is (P2 - P0) / 2, the curve is linear in the nodes. A missing ghost is the reflection (P0 = 2 P1 - P2, P3 = 2 P2 - P1). With unequal chords the uniform curve can overshoot; the centripetal one (knot intervals |ΔP|^0.5) cannot form cusps or loops. Step 1 reads both; the LOSS of step 2 uses the uniform curve and only links with comparable chords (below).
- **Step 1 read-out (`jcr_eval.step1`, CPU, no training):** for every delivered link (i -> j) of the prediction (`jtoken_eval.frame_prediction` at the D115 cell): P0 = the delivered predecessor k of i (a link k -> i) with the smallest turning angle between (P_i - P_k) and (P_j - P_i), else the reflection; P3 = the delivered successor l of j with the smallest turning angle between (P_l - P_j) and (P_j - P_i), else the reflection. **This choice is a hard selection on the output (a rule step, named under R1)**; the curve is sampled with max(2, ceil(chord / 0.5 m) + 1) points uniform in t and given to the scorer as `links['curves']` (`qgraph_eval.readout` takes a given curve instead of the Hermite one, line 88). Modes: **H** the record (curves None), **P** the straight chord sampled at 0.5 m (the null: does the curve shape matter at all), **CR** uniform Catmull-Rom, **CRC** centripetal, **CRS** Catmull-Rom for the short links (chord <= 8 m) and the record's Hermite connector for the long ones (the long links are where the node heading and the learned handle scales carry the curvature). Oracle modes on the cache dump (the tokens carry `gt_id`): **OLAT** every delivered token with a GT node (`gt_id >= 0`) moved along its normal (the rotation of `dirv` by 90 degrees) onto the GT node's line, **OFULL** moved onto the GT node, each read with the connectors H and CR. The oracle uses the lane-GT node positions, the scorer scores against the old graph GT: OFULL + H is the sanity that the two agree (it must score near the top of the range; the number is reported).
- **Step 2 position head (`jtoken.JTokenHead(pos_head=True)`, default off = the champion bit for bit, no extra parameter, no extra output key):** built LAST: `dpos = 3 m * tanh(MLP(xn) / 3 m)` on the final features after the last layer (an MLP d -> d -> 1, GELU, the last Linear zero-initialised: it starts at the B' position), `out['dpos']` (B, K); the refined position `P' = pos + dpos * n`, n = the rotation by 90 degrees of the token's `dirv` (an input, no gradient into it). Only the normal offset moves: sliding along the lane changes the curve little and the lateral error is the large one (rms 0.48 m, longitudinal 0.30 m). The offset is applied to EVERY token, also to the ones that no GT node was assigned to (duplicates, false tokens): those are not supervised in position and follow what the head generalises; `nodeerr` reports their share and the size of their offsets.
- **Curve loss (`jtoken_loss(curve_w=)`), exactly:** per frame, per GT link (u -> v) of the frame's GT table with its GT ghost nodes (the GT predecessor of u and the GT successor of v that continue most straight, the reflection when missing) and with the token ASSIGNED to every GT node (`gt_id`, the cache's one-to-one greedy assignment in the order of existence, within 1.5 m and cos >= 0.5 of the direction): a link whose node u or v has no assigned token is left out (so the loss trains on well-detected regions: the share of GT links with both nodes present is reported); the GT predecessor of u is the first one in the table order among those with the smallest turning angle; a ghost is AVAILABLE when its GT node exists and has an assigned token, otherwise it is a reflection in BOTH curves (the reflection contributes no chord). The refined positions P' of the (up to) four tokens give the predicted curve, the four GT nodes the GT curve (both uniform Catmull-Rom); at t in {0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1}: `rho((C_pred(t) - C_gt(t)) . n_gt(t))`, n_gt(t) the unit normal of the GT curve at t, rho = smooth L1 with the knee 0.5 m; a sample whose GT tangent is shorter than 1e-6 is dropped; per frame the mean over the used samples, then the mean over the frames that have one. **Only links with comparable chords enter:** the link chord |G_v - G_u| <= 8 m and the ratio of the largest to the smallest of the chords between the GT positions of the AVAILABLE nodes among (pre-u, u-v, v-suc) <= 2; the share left out is reported (`n_links`, `n_excl`); a frame without a used sample contributes nothing (a finite zero with a graph). **What it is:** at t = 0 and t = 1 it is plain node regression `(Q - G) . n_gt`; at the other t a fixed combination (weights sum to 1) of the lateral errors of four nodes (the curve is linear in the nodes), so in the quadratic regime it is a positive semi-definite quadratic form in the node errors whose minimiser is the same as the node loss's; only the weighting of neighbouring errors differs, and the target is the Catmull-Rom curve through the GT nodes, not the dense lane. The weight 0.5, the knee 0.5 m, the five t and the chord rule are ONE set of values, not tuned. `--node-w W` instead: `rho((P'_g - G_g) . n_g)` per assigned token (t = 0 only, n_g = the normal of the GT node's direction).
- **Data (`lane_gtgeom_data`, derived data under `runs/e395_gtgeom/`, not in git; `lane_ptok_data` item keys, new only):** per frame `gt_pos`, `gt_dir`, `gt_btok` (the assigned token of every GT node, -1 = none), `gl` (u, v, pre, suc) of every GT link; label side only, no model input reads them.
- **Dump and scoring:** the dump carries `dpos` (additive key); `jcr_eval.py step2 --run p0_s<S> --seed S` scores the SAME dump with the standard scorer at the D115 cell as **U** (nodes unmoved, the head's own topology), **M** (nodes moved by `dpos` along the token normals) and **M+CRS** (moved, Catmull-Rom for the short links), connectors H otherwise; `nodeerr` reads the node errors (below).
- **Not changed:** the wiring read, the types, the decode thresholds (D115), B', the caches, the data order.

### 1.3 The verdicts (by hand, written before any run; this is the whole rule)
- **Step 1 (connector X in {P, CR, CRC, CRS} against H on the same dump; metrics F1@0.5, F1@1.0 and topo F1; the code is `jcr_eval.py verdict step1`, tested on the case table):** per seed, per dump: **LOSS** when any of the three differences X - H is <= -0.010; **GAIN** when F1@0.5 and F1@1.0 are both >= +0.010 with the paired scene-bootstrap intervals above 0 (and no LOSS); **TIE** otherwise. **The verdict of a dump** (both seeds): the class when both seeds agree; GAIN + TIE = TIE; LOSS on one seed with GAIN or TIE on the other, or GAIN + LOSS = INCONCLUSIVE. **The deciding dump for any claim about the champion is the S4 live dump** (`e372_s4`); the c and r2 dumps are the consistency read (a class on S4 that c or r2 contradicts is reported as 'not consistent'; coded in `verdict step1 --arm all`, which also prints H05 / H10 / B05 / B10 per seed and checks the OFULL sanity); the oracle headroom exists only on c and r2 (cache dumps). X 'replaces the Hermite connector at no cost' ONLY when D05, D10 and the topo F1 difference are each >= -0.003 on both seeds of EVERY dump. The oracle modes give the HEADROOM, no verdict: **H05 = F1@0.5(OLAT + H) - F1@0.5(H)** and **H10 = F1@1.0(OLAT + H) - F1@1.0(H)** on the control's own dump (c for p0 / p0n, r2 for p2), per seed, the headroom FOR MATCHED NODES (a measured quantity, not a bound: unmatched tokens stay unmoved in the oracle although the head moves them); sanity: OFULL + H must not read below OLAT + H by more than 0.010 in F1@0.5 and F1@1.0.
- **Step 2 (arm X in {p0, p0n, p2}, per seed; connectors H; the code is `jcr_eval.py verdict step2`, tested on the case table including the cases below):** the PRIMARY contrast is the same run's dump moved against unmoved: **D05 = F1@0.5(M) - F1@0.5(U), D10 = F1@1.0(M) - F1@1.0(U)** with paired intervals (same dump, wiring and decode: only the node positions differ). **The bar:** **B05 = max(0.25 x H05, 0.010)** and **B10 = max(0.25 x H10, 0.010)** (H of the arm's own control, seed by seed, from the record step-1 runs; written into section 3 BEFORE any training). The topology effect is separate: **T = U(X) - control** on topo F1, lanes found, junction F1. **Guards** (on the total M(X) against the control c / r2; 'a guard fails' = ANY of the three): topo F1 not below the control by more than 0.010, lanes found by more than 0.010, junction F1 by more than 0.015.
  **'The bar is met' includes the paired intervals above 0 (D05 >= B05 and its interval above 0, D10 >= B10 and its interval above 0). A GAIN seed = D05 >= 0.010 with the interval above 0 and D10 >= 0.** **Evaluation ORDER, the first that applies:** **0. CUT** when a CUT kill fires on any seed (saturation; the refined nodes worse than the unmoved ones). **1. INCONCLUSIVE** when a number is not finite; an INCONCLUSIVE kill fires; the bar is met on exactly ONE seed; the two seeds differ by more than 0.020 in D05 or in D10; or both seeds are GAIN seeds but a guard fails on exactly one seed. **INCONCLUSIVE is final for this card** (reported, treated as TIE for any adoption claim); adding head-seed twins is an addendum with its rule written before its runs, as E392 section 1.9. **2. CUT** when D10 (or D05) is <= -0.010 on one seed and the other seed's value is not >= +0.010; or a guard fails on both seeds. **3. KEEP-quality** when the bar is met on both seeds and no guard fails on either seed. **4.
