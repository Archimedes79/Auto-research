## 1. Card text
- **Statement:** a head that attends from the perceived tokens to the SD route tokens learns the registration under the protocol's map errors and assigns the tokens to the navigation route far better than the same head without the map (route F1 at the road level +0.10 or more), uses the map where it decides (route forks), and ignores a missing or wrong map.
- **Mechanism:** the route is a polyline of road-centre samples with a direction and a lane count in the vehicle frame with the map's pose error; a token on the carriageway of the route is near the polyline (the offset is small against the carriageway half width n * 1.75 m + 0.5 m) with a similar heading; a cross-attention with METRE-SCALE pair features makes that a learned soft test and learns the offsets the registration needs from training on the error mix; the none key lets the head ignore an absent map. At forks the route decides which branch is on the route: only the map knows. The ego-lane label is the same decision at the lane level; the SD route loses the lane choice, so it is learned from the tokens' own states (ego-lane prior) plus the road-level hint.
- **Inputs (what is built; the E374 / E368 machinery is reused where it exists):**
  - **Route GT per frame (derived data, new; `training/data/lane_route_data.py`):** the new lane GT already carries the driven route: per point of the pieces a `tier` (1 = the driven ego path, extended along the map to 40 m ahead: `GtGenerator/lane_tiers.py`), per piece `road` (the road id), `lane_count`, `succ`, and the frame has `roads` (id, pieces, succ, lane_max) and `ego_path`. Route pieces = the pieces with tier-1 points (no frame is dropped; a stopped ego still gets a route to 40 m). Per E357 table node (`piece` = the index in the frame's pieces): `on_lane` = its piece is a route piece; `on_road` (v3 of the card, built and tested in the data layer) = the node lies within n x 1.75 m + 0.5 m of the EXACT SD route polyline (the one the route tokens are made from, before the map error; n = the lane count at the nearest route point, clipped to 1 - 5) with a heading cosine >= 0.7 to the route direction there, OR on_lane (a lane of the route is on its carriageway by definition); the diagnostic `on_road_id` (the node's piece is in the piece list of a road that lists a route piece) is kept in the cache but is NOT the label: a junction road lists all its connectors, so it also labels the non-taken turn paths and blurs the forks (the share 55.9 % against 40.0 % for the band on delivered VAL nodes). Per token: the label of its assigned GT node (`gt_id`; -1: none). The SD route = `sd_lib.build_sd(adapted g, route piece indices)['route']` (the centred path of the route pieces, lane choice lost; the adapter: `pts` = the piece polylines, `edges` = the successor pairs by index). The old `make_route_gt.route_for` on the future path is an optional cross-check of the fixture only (85 of 873 VAL frames would get no route by it).
  - **Route tokens:** the SD route resampled every 2 m within +-50 m: pos, the unit direction of travel, the lane count (1 - 5, 0 unknown), valid; at most **160 samples** (v3 of the card, D128: with 64 samples 36 % of the VAL and 44 % of the TRAIN frames were truncated and no head could find the road nodes on the cut-off part: the exact-route rule reached only F1 0.941 (0.993 uncapped); the longest raw route is 136 samples on VAL and 171 on TRAIN, so 160 cuts 0 of 873 VAL frames and 0.04 % of the TRAIN frames; the samples kept are the nearest the ego; cache version v4); FIXED KEY SET (pos, dir, count, valid; a piece id, a road id, the route piece list, a lane index or a node label is a leak: a check refuses any other key). The map errors are the E374 draws applied to the route (shift + yaw + `sd_lib.degrade_sd`: DP sparsification 0.5 - 2 m, count errors 0.1); modes exact / error (a FIXED draw per frame seeded by the frame token: shift 1 - 2 m, yaw sigma 1 degree) / train (the E374 mix) / none / wrong (the route of another scene: a fixed plan frame (i + 97) mod N moved to another scene) / gross (error + 5 m). The labels never change with a map error.
  - **`RouteHead`** (`training/grid/jroute.py`; the pair features are new, at metre scale (`pair_features`: turn cos / sin, offsets along / across the token and the route heading, distance, lane count; the archived S2 `cross_geometry`, offsets / 20, is NOT used)): inputs per token = the cached B' token features (`tok`, pos / 50, dirv, existence, flags_p; the option `geo` drops `tok` = the geometry-only control); one pre-norm cross-attention to the route tokens (4 heads of 16, the pair-geometry bias, one learned none key per head) PLUS metre-scale registration features pooled by the attention (the attention-weighted along / across offset in metres / 5 clipped to +-3, the distance, the heading cosine, the lane count of the keys, and the offset to a sharp soft nearest key): the plain `cross_geometry` scales offsets by 1/20 and only biases the attention, which is too coarse to decide 'within 2 m'; an MLP -> two logits (road, ego lane); 33,738 parameters (21,130 for `geo`; pinned in the selftest); no self-attention among the tokens: a token's output depends on its own row and the route only. Arm z: the same head with every route token invalid. Loss: BCE of the road and lane logits on the labelled tokens, positive weight by the TRAIN frequency; 3000 steps, batch 8, lr 1e-3, AdamW, cosine; the stage-1 head is NOT in the training loop (the dump of the champion's delivered set is read at evaluation only).
  - **Evaluation** (`evaluation/jroute_eval.py`): dumps p_route per token for the VAL frames in each map mode, the covered-node F1 metrics, the fork frames, the paired bootstrap, the reference rows (flag-all, straight prior, geometric rule).
- **The data fixture (before any training; CPU; thresholds fixed here), on 200 VAL frames:** (a) the route is plausible: the pooled p90 of the distance from the driven path (`ego_path`) to the SD route centre polyline <= 2.5 m (the per-frame distribution is printed); the wrong conventions (a mirrored path, the ego path of ANOTHER frame, the reversed path) must FAIL; (b) the labels: 100 % of the on_lane nodes are on_road (by construction), the share of covered nodes with on_road / on_lane and the number of frames with a route are reported (the critic measured on_road 32.5 % on VAL); agreement of tier-1 with `make_route_gt.route_for` is reported; (c) the leak check: the route token table has exactly the 4 keys; the SD route is the carriageway mean on frames with >= 2 parallel lanes (not the lane centre path); (d) TEST guard: VAL rows = `data/splits.json['val']`, TRAIN rows = `splits['train']`, `poses_val.npz` also holds TEST rows: none is read (asserted); (e) the reference rows (flag-all, straight prior, geometric rule exact / error) are MEASURED by `jroute_eval.py rules` on the covered delivered nodes of the v4 VAL cache and printed in section 3 before any run of m (v3 of the card: the critic's older numbers 0.490 / 0.778 / 1.000 / 0.959 were made with other labels on 788 frames and are not reproduced by the band label; they are measurements, not gates).
- **What would make it fail:** the head does not learn metre-scale registration in 3000 steps (the learnability sanity step catches it before the full runs: it is a design failure, not a result about A6); under the wrong map the head follows the map (trust clause; a wrong route of another frame is often straight ahead, so the wrong-map F1 is also read on fork frames); the champion's delivered set on TRAIN is cleaner than on VAL (E388: B' tokens of TRAIN are in-sample): z and m share this shift, the absolute F1 is affected, the differences are what the rule reads.
- **Not claimed:** anything about TEST; the S4 champion's stage 2 (the head reads B''s cached tokens); a real navigation system's route (the map is GT-derived and simulated; the route extends 40 m ahead along the map); a joint training with the champion's head; lane-level association beyond the ego-lane prior.
- **Starts from:** the cached B' tokens and the E373 delivered set (D113 / D115), the E374 map-error draws (code behind the tag `archive/s2-s3-blocks-2026-11-18`), `sd_lib`, the GT's `tier` / `road` / `roads` (GtGenerator v13). **Lens / group / sources / status:** A6 / A7, D127; E374 (a map block inside the token head, TIE for topology: the map did not help the graph; this card asks the association question); loops 5 - 19 (route heads of the old builder); status v2, critic pass 1 taken in.
