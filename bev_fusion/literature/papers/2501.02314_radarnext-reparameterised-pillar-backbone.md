**What it does**: PointPillars-style pillarisation of one 4D radar (0.16 m pillars, 64 channels, VoD 320 x 320 grid; features on VoD: x, y, z, RCS, relative and absolute radial velocity, timestamp; TJ4D: x, y, z, relative radial velocity, range, SNR; compensation not described). Single-path backbone built from MobileOne re-parameterised depthwise convs (multi-branch in training, one path at inference), neck MDFEN: one DCNv3 deformable conv at the largest scale (adaptive receptive field, not attention) + PAN + FPN, anchor-free CenterHead (PillarNeXt). Five scans on VoD.

**Results that matter** (VoD val 5 scans, mAP; checked): RadarNeXt (MDFEN) 50.48 (car 37.44 / ped 41.83 / cyc 72.16) with 1.58 M parameters; baselines in the same table: SMURF 50.97, RadarPillars 50.70, PointPillars 48.72, SECOND 39.39. TJ4DRadSet test 32.30 (SMURF 32.99, PointPillars 27.17). Backbone ablation (VoD): dense backbone + FPN 48.89 mAP, 4.73 M params, 72.3 FPS; RadarNeXt backbone + FPN 47.98, 0.90 M, 83.6 FPS; dense + MDFEN 48.23 but RadarNeXt + MDFEN 50.48 at 67.1 FPS (RTX A4000) and 28.4 FPS (Jetson AGX Orin). DCNv3 position matters: 46.96 to 50.48 depending on where it is placed.

**Transferable technique**: a 1-1.6 M parameter conv-only radar BEV network matches the 5 M dense one; the gain comes from a deformable (offset-predicting) conv that lets the receptive field follow the radar's position error - the conv-only analogue of a learned footprint. This supports the hypothesis that a conv stack on a rasterised radar image can absorb position uncertainty (one place is worth +1.6 mAP, but only at one level).

**Cost and fit**: 15 ms per frame for the whole network on an A4000 (a 3060 is slower than an A4000; scout estimate, not measured). DCNv3 needs a custom CUDA op (not export friendly); depthwise re-parameterisation is plain convs at inference.

**Doubts**: VoD only; the dense-vs-reparam ablation changes the backbone only with FPN neck; no seeds; DCNv3 placement ablation looks noisy (46.96 to 50.48); features differ between VoD and TJ4D so there is no feature ablation.
