# 6. Qualitative results: good cases and issues

**How to read a picture.** All pictures have the same layout: the front camera with the lane ground truth projected into it (top left; it is the GT, not the delivered graph), the numbers of this frame (top right), and below three bird's-eye panels, ego at the origin, 48 m each way: the **plain lane head B**, the **champion** (the token head with none weight 0.25, seed 0 of the confirmation, at its final per-class decode of E375: link short 0.3 / long 0.2, keep 0.6, none gate off) and, for context, **E324 s**, the previous system with its rule steps (its snapped read-out: it is not an end-to-end result and no bar). Grey with arrows = ground truth (travel direction); a pale band = the lane the car really drives; delivered points are green within 1 m of the truth, orange within 2 m, red further away; **blue = truth that nothing covers**. All pictures are VAL, no TEST frame.

**Which decode the pictures show.** The final per-class decode of E375 (short 0.3 / long 0.2 / keep 0.6 / gate off), on the same head (`e373_cb_s0`, seed 0, VAL). The graphs were recomputed from the stored dump of that head; their pooled topo F1 is 0.6679, equal to the E375 card. The frames were chosen again by the same written rules, so two of the six differ from the single-threshold version of the earlier draft (the typical frame and the loss frame); the other four frames are the same.

**How the frames were chosen (rules, not by eye).** From the per-frame topo F1 and maneuver counts of the 873 VAL frames: (1) the champion's topo F1 nearest to its 75th percentile, (2) the largest gain over the plain head, (3) the largest loss against the plain head, (4) the lowest share of realised maneuvers among frames with at least four, (5) the frame where the champion realises the most maneuvers fewer than the plain head, (6) the lowest topo F1 of the champion. Rules 4 and 6 select the same frame, shown once.

**The counts behind the pictures (873 VAL frames).** The champion is better than the plain head by more than 0.02 topo F1 on 461 frames, worse by more than 0.02 on 230 and within 0.02 on 182; the median gain is +0.027. Of the 10 991 ground-truth maneuvers it realises 7 003 (63.7 %), the plain head 5 773 (52.5 %) and E324 s with its rule steps 8 121 (73.9 %). On 150 of the 826 frames that have maneuvers it realises fewer than the plain head: this is the junction issue of the abstract.

**What the pictures say, with the numbers.** The champion usually finds more of the lane graph than the plain head (461 against 230 frames with a clear difference) and covers lanes and branches the plain head misses; it pays with extra lines at the edges (topo precision 0.71 on the whole set at the final decode, 0.68 at the single threshold), with isolated breaks in a lane (the loss picture) and, at crowded crossings, with connectors it does not deliver (junction F1 0.25 at the final per-class decode against the plain head's 0.27, 0.20 at the single threshold; 150 frames with fewer maneuvers than the plain head). The previous system E324 s, which has a pair head and rule steps for the connectors, realises 8 121 of the 10 991 maneuvers, the champion 7 003: the junction machinery is what the token champion does not yet replace at the same level.
