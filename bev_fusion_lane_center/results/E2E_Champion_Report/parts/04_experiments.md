**Reading the table.** The first five rows are the *ladder* (chapter 5): each step removes or replaces one hard step, and the cost is small. The oracle-node rows (E357 - E362) test the token design on ground-truth nodes, so they are not comparable with the ladder: they answer whether the wiring is learnable at all (it is; the weak output is the node-type head). E365 is the first test on predicted nodes.
