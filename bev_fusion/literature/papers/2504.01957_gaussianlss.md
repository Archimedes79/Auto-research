**What it does**: LSS-style per-pixel depth probabilities over 64 bins in (1, 61) m. From them it computes the soft depth mean mu and variance sigma^2; a soft range [mu - k sigma, mu + k sigma] (k = 0.5) bounds an ellipsoid, and the probability-weighted 3D mean and covariance of the unprojected bins give one 3D Gaussian per pixel (1/8-scale features). A small CNN predicts feature, opacity and depth; the Gaussians are alpha-blended onto the BEV plane and rendered at 50x50, 100x100 and 200x200 and fused (multi-scale to hide mean-inconsistency between neighbouring pixels). Depth uncertainty is thus NOT a learned extra head: it is the variance of the depth categorical, and it sets the footprint of the splat (far or blurry pixels smear over more cells). BEV grid 200x200 over [-50, 50] m = 0.5 m cells (our grid).

**Results that matter** (nuScenes val, 6 cameras, EfficientNet-B4): vehicle IoU 38.3 (224x480) / 40.6 (448x800) without visibility filter; 42.8 / 46.1 with filter. Projection-based PointBEV: 44.0 / 47.6 (visibility filter), i.e. about 0.4 to 1.5 IoU behind it but best unprojection method. Speed: 80.2 FPS vs 32.0 FPS (PointBEV), memory 0.33 vs 1.26 GiB; total 11.22 ms, view transform 3.06 ms (vs 13.47 ms), timing on an RTX 4090 (appendix D; Table 3 names no GPU). Map segmentation (appendix C): drivable 76.3, ped. crossing 46.3, walkway 50.2, divider 38.7. 3D detection NDS 34.0 / mAP 26.6.

**Transferable technique**: (1) take mean and variance of the per-pixel depth distribution and splat an ellipse in the ground plane instead of one-hot or full-frustum pooling: cost per pixel is O(1), not O(depth bins); (2) multi-scale render + upsample + fuse (50/100/200); (3) variance as the "range blur" for distant lane pixels, directly the distance-uncertainty we want. A single front camera needs no code change: drop the camera loop, only a wedge of the BEV is covered.

**Cost and fit**: 11 ms for six cameras on a 4090; one camera would be a fraction, but the number is not stated for 1 camera and a 3060 is about 3x slower in FP32 (our estimate, not measured). Images only, no radar. Depth supervision is not stated in the page text read (a lidar depth loss is likely, not verified). Lane classes: divider IoU 38.7 only (no centre line).

**Doubts**: never evaluated with one camera; the paper's speed claim differs between versions (2.5x/0.4 vs 2x/0.7 points); GPU for the headline FPS not named; code licence unknown; the Gaussian is a function of the depth variance only (isotropic in the ray perpendicular), so lateral uncertainty (pose, calibration) is not modelled.
