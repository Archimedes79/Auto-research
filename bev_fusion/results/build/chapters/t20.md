### What was built and trained

The bundle of order D48 in one run (`t20.base_s0`): the stride-4 lane branch L4 (a frozen ResNet-50 stem and first layer on the current frame, lifted with the DualBEV depth and added to the dual camera BEV), a 1 m centre-line head inside the grid-based map head (it pools the 0.5 m centre-line and direction layers), the correctness fixes of the open-findings list, and the dead radar-stationary channel removed. Everything else is the delivered network: the full network `t16.full_s0` with the motion block and the delta-slot radar, 6000 steps on 428 training scenes, ground truth v8, one seed. The validation loss of the run fell from 11.9 (step 2000) to 8.9 (step 4000) and 8.5 (step 6000); the training ended without a stop or a non-finite value (a short bug-finding training and a crash-and-resume test came first and fed no verdict).

### What the numbers say

- **The lane lever is not confirmed.** The card predicted a lane divider IoU@1 of 0.60 (+0.05 over `t16.full_s0`). The run gives 0.542 against 0.549: the paired interval contains 0, and the reading rule of the card (inside plus or minus 0.01 = not confirmed) applies. IoU@0.5 is equally flat (0.426 against 0.429). The bundle does not improve the lane layer of the full network. The three changes were bundled by order, so the report cannot say which of them did nothing or whether one helped and another hurt; the evidence of the earlier side-branch test (t7: L4 helped on a weaker model) did not carry over to the full network.
- **The 1 m centre-line head works as a first output**, with no reference in this system: IoU@1 0.643 (the floor of the card was 0.35), IoU@0.5 0.423. Its direction is poor: the mean absolute angle error is 0.73 rad (about 42 degrees), so the head finds where the centre lines are, but not yet which way they run.
- **The other outputs are level.** Drivable area is slightly better (+0.009, interval above 0), walkway slightly worse (-0.017, interval below 0). Detection (mAP in the fusion area 0.409 against 0.416) and the velocity at moving box centres (1.104 against 1.112 m/s) are not separated from the control. The velocity error on static cells and on vehicle cells is higher (0.228 against 0.189 and 0.641 against 0.613 m/s, intervals above 0): the cause is not isolated (the bundle changed the trunk input, the loss hygiene and the radar channels at once). The cost is 102.1 ms per frame at batch 1 on an idle GPU against 96.4 ms (the card allowed 105): L4 and the 1 m head cost 5.7 ms.
- **Verdict (by hand: D48, one seed).** The guards of the card hold (maps mean IoU +0.0015, mAP -0.0066, moving-centre velocity -0.008, all inside their bands), the training ended without a stop and the gates are green, so `t20.base_s0` is accepted as the reference baseline for the next experiments. The prediction for the lane divider was missed.

### Weak spots after this run

@@WEAK@@

The weakest outputs are the same as before the bundle: pedestrian crossing, car park, walkway and the lane markings (0.43 at 0.5 m, 0.54 at 1 m tolerance); every map layer scores higher on its own training scenes than on validation (the gap column), and the lane markings are far from solved even on the training scenes, so the limit is the fit as well as the generalisation. Which part of the lane problem is the image side and which is the supervision (the lane paint is thin and its map registration is uncertain) is not separated by this run; that is the open question the next lane step has to answer before another module is added.
