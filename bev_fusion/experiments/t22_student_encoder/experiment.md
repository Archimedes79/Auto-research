## 1 Card
- **Question.** With the lifts, trunk, heads and radar side of `t20.base_s0` unchanged, does a student encoder that outputs the same three tensors keep every guard inside its margin and cut the image side to a fraction of its time? It does NOT ask for a lane gain: nobody published a stride 4 / 8 / 16 ablation for thin or far lanes, and in our chain the stride-4 branch alone did not move the lane divider (t20: 0.542 against 0.549).
- **Strides (the question "4 or 8").** The depth distribution and the main context are at **stride 8** (118 bins + 80 channels, 32 x 88 cells at 256 x 704): this is the finest level the camera-only BEV map work lifts (BEVFusion camera-only seg, GaussianLSS / GaussianCaR, Simple-BEV, PointBeV; LSS, BEVDet, BEVDepth and our DualBEV stay at 16; plan 2.7 / 2.8). A **stride-4 detail level** carries context only (no depth: lifted with the stride-8 depth, as the `res4` branch does today): 64 x 176 cells, where one pixel is about 0.14 m at 20 m. A depth head at stride 4 would cost 4 x the cells of stride 8 (118 bins each) for no proven gain (Fast-BEV's stride-4 map gave no detection gain). Strides 16 / 32 live inside the encoder and feed the DualBEV-convention output. Our own evidence: oracle lift stride 16 keeps 0.17 of the 40-60 m lane lines, stride 8 with 4 sub-heights 0.81; t7's stride-4 branch +0.042 / +0.065 lane divider IoU@0.5.
- **The student (drop-in outputs, so everything downstream and the warm start stay valid).** Encoder: `timm` EfficientViT-B1 (`features_only`: strides 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 with 32 / 64 / 128 / 256 channels; 4.6 M parameters without the classifier; weights `models/pretrained/imagenet/efficientvit_b1_r256_in1k`, Apache-2.0 tag; probed on CPU 2026-11-04). The control encoder is ResNet-18 (strides 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 with 64 / 128 / 256 / 512 channels, 11.7 M, weights present). A light FPN with 128 channels (top-down only) and three heads: **H16** = 118 depth logits + 80 context at 16 x 44 in DualBEV's cell-centre convention (target: the cached `cam_feats` outputs); **H8** = 118 + 80 at 32 x 88 in BEVFusion's frustum-cell convention (target: the cached `cam_feats_bevfusion`); **H4** = the 256-channel map of ResNet-50 layer 1 at 64 x 176 (target: the frozen layer 1 run on the same JPEG, what `res4` reads today). Depth heads are light (a 3 x 3 conv with 128 channels, then 1 x 1, as the plan's candidate), not DualBEV's 35.6-GFLOP DepthNet. Registered as a new entry of the `CAMERAS` table (`model.CAMERAS`) with a live path for the current key-frame image (`data/cache/img_front`: 19,398 JPEGs, about 1 GB). A single-source student (one lift, no `cam_merge`) is a second step (t22b) if this one stands.
- **First try (Mirko, 2026-11-04: "stride 8 and following your suggestion on the selection. Lets just try the candidate with the best chances and then refine it").** Only the main candidate: EfficientViT-B1 (arm S), stride 8 as the main level, outputs **H16 + H8** (the two cached teacher interfaces, so the chain and the warm start stay valid) with the `res4` branch left as it is (frozen ResNet-50 layer 1 on the JPEG, small next to the rest); one seed; no ResNet-18 arm yet; the control is the t21 arm Mirko takes. Refinements, one at a time and only after the first result: H4 from the student (replaces `res4`), the ResNet-18 control encoder, the seed twin of S and of the control, a single-source student, higher input resolution.
- **Design.**
  - **Stage 0, no training, minutes of an idle GPU (needs Mirko's slot):** image-side milliseconds, batch 1 and 3, 256 x 704, fp32 and fp16 autocast, CUDA-synchronised median (`evaluation/metrics.ms_per_frame`, the script `temp/timing/time_image_sides.py` extended): (a) DualBEV heads, (b) BEVFusion side, (c) both, (d) the `res4` layer 1, (e) EfficientViT-B1 student with H4 / H8 / H16 (random weights), (f) ResNet-18 student. The reported 90 - 100 ms per frame of the chain excludes (a) - (d). INVALID if another process uses the GPU. These numbers fix the cost prediction below BEFORE any training.
  - **Stage A1, bring-up with the backbone FROZEN on the small set (Mirko, 2026-11-04: "first short training with frozen weights on a small set, then a full training allowing to refine all weights"):** the `small` split (60 / 10 scenes, 2,283 train and 378 val samples, every cache exists), the ImageNet EfficientViT-B1 backbone frozen, only the FPN and the H16 / H8 heads trained, about 5 epochs (about 11 k samples, 2 - 5 min at 40 - 100 samples/s). It proves the plumbing end to end (JPEG -> student -> both heads in the two conventions -> losses against the cached teacher outputs), measures samples per second and GPU memory (this fixes the budget of A2), and gives the floor of "heads on frozen ImageNet features": agreement numbers of gate A (i) on the 378 val samples. If the floor already passes the stand-in test, A2 still runs, because the aim is the refined encoder; if the loss does not fall, stop and fix before A2.
  - **Stage A2, the full distillation with ALL weights trainable (per encoder, 1 run each):** AdamW, the backbone at 0.1 x the head learning rate, the key-frame JPEGs of the `large` split (428 train scenes; the 82 val scenes are held out), depth KL on H16 / H8 (the student's probabilities pooled to the teacher grid where the strides differ, as plan 2.7), context cosine + MSE, H4 MSE to the frozen layer 1. Budget set from stage 0 (target: at most 2 h per encoder on the idle box; batch, learning rate and steps are written in the smoke test). The DualBEV / BEVFusion caches and the teacher weights are read only. Early-feature matching is the weak form of distillation (KD360): it is a warm start, and the ImageNet-only start is the other arm of the comparison between B1 and R18 only through the fine-tune below.
  - **Gate A (layer 1 / 2, no training of the chain):** (i) teacher agreement on the VAL key-frames: depth KL and context cosine per head, reported per range bin; (ii) **stand-in test:** `t20.base_s0` evaluated with the student's outputs in place of the cached teacher outputs, frozen weights, `bev_suite.py ... --velocity eval --n 1000`; pass if lane divider IoU@1, mAP (fusion area) and drivable are each no worse than the reference by 0.05 and vehicle AP@2 at 40 - 60 m by 0.08 (loose on purpose: nothing was fine-tuned yet); else extend the distillation once, then stop and report.
  - **Stage B, in-chain fine-tune, 1500 steps, accumulation 2, batch 3, warm start from `t20.base_s0` (exactly the t21 protocol),** arms **S** (EfficientViT-B1 student) and **R** (ResNet-18 student), seed 0 each. The student trains at 0.1 x the learning rate with BatchNorm in eval mode; a distillation anchor to the cached teacher outputs on the current frame (weight decaying to 0.1 at half the steps) keeps the depth from drifting (no depth GT: the plan's depth caveat). The camera input is the current key-frame image only, so stage B runs on the one-frame temporal chain of t21 (the window needs images of the older frames, which the cache does not have; a sweep-image cache would be 58 k JPEGs, about 3 GB, a separate step). **Default chain: P4 (`t21.pair4_s0`), Mirko's pending choice between P and P4;** the control follows his choice.
  - **Controls:** the t21 arm Mirko takes (`t21.pair4_s0` by default; same warm start, steps, GT, evaluator and frames), the teacher-fed chain itself (stage-A stand-in numbers), ResNet-18 as the control encoder (R), and `t20.base_s0` for the long-recipe row. Stored numbers are re-scored with the final committed evaluator in step 0 (same rule as t13); the GT is v8 (`c643a7539d`) as for the controls, checked from `gt.json`.
- **Gate (layer 0, CPU, before the smoke test).** The student module's shapes and conventions on synthetic tensors (H16 in cell-centre, H8 in frustum convention: the ramp-feature geometry test `tests/test_dual_camera_geometry.py` run on student outputs, G7a/b/c < 0.5 / 0.5 / 1 px); `tests/test_slots.py` with the new kind (a gradient reaches every trainable parameter, the frozen teachers get none); `tests/equivalence.py` clip mode: with the student flag off the chain reproduces `t20.base_s0` (max abs diff 0); `check_config` refuses the combinations the student cannot take (a window chain without older images); `training/startable.py`. Smoke test (`smoke.d32` recipe) checks: `gt.json` = v8 signature, a finite loss, a gradient above zero on every student parameter.
- **Prediction (written before the run; against the control chain, VAL 1000 frames, point estimates of one seed).** Quality: lane divider IoU@1 and @0.5, drivable, walkway, mAP (fusion area), thresholded vehicle IoU at dt -0.5 / 0 / +0.5 s, static EPE: within -m of the control (S) and within -2 m (R); velocity EPE at moving box centres within +0.05 m/s; vehicle AP@2 at 40 - 60 m (the far range, where depth sharpness matters) within -0.03 for S and within -0.05 for R. Upside, not predicted: a lane gain from the BEV-loss fine-tune of the encoder. Cost: the image side (a) + (b) + (d) falls to at most 0.5 of its time on the idle GPU for S (FLOPs ratio about 0.1: the plan's student at 256 x 704 is 15.8 GFLOPs for ResNet-18 and about 6 - 7 for EfficientViT-B1 (my estimate from 0.69 GMACs at 256 x 256, not a plan number) against 71.4 for the DualBEV side alone; milliseconds fall less because of small kernels at batch 1), parameters of the image side from about 85 M frozen (DualBEV side 54.6 M, BEVFusion side about 30 M) to roughly 5 - 10 M (S) or 15 M (R), my estimate; to be restated from stage 0 before stage A. All margins m = max(0.010, half-width of the paired scene bootstrap of the comparison itself) because the reference has one seed; the floor is the project's noise band.
- **Decision rule (per metric by `decide.py`, `--lower-better` for the EPE rows; composed by the text, labelled "by hand: composed from the table rows").**
  - **STAND-IN (candidate):** every guard of S is not below the control by more than m (point estimate and the interval's lower end not beyond 2 m) AND the cost row holds -> the student replaces the frozen encoders in the product path after a seed twin of S and of the control (2 seeds each; one seed never gives KEEP); by the tie rule the cheaper variant wins.
  - **BETTER:** additionally a map or line guard (lane divider IoU@1, walkway, crossing) beyond +m on the point estimate with the interval excluding 0 -> report as a quality gain, still needs the twin.
  - **CUT:** a guard below the control by more than 2 m with the interval entirely below -m, for S -> S is cut; then R is read alone: if R holds its guards, R is the candidate (simpler, same recipe), otherwise the student route is closed for this chain and the frozen encoders stay.
  - **R vs S:** R is preferred only if it holds every guard AND is not slower on the 3060 (stage 0); otherwise S.
  - **INCONCLUSIVE:** an interval straddles a margin -> a second seed of S and of the control (+ 2 x 2 h), not adoption.
- **Kill / validity.** (1) gate A fails twice; (2) a non-finite loss in more than 1 % of the steps; (3) `gt.json` not the control's signature, or the student receives teacher outputs at training time other than the cached ones; (4) GPU memory above 11 GB or free host memory under 3 GB at the start (one training at a time); (5) a resumed run is labelled "resumed" and not mixed with an uninterrupted one in a verdict; (6) the stage-0 timing INVALID (another GPU process): not used.
- **What it does not test.** A lane gain from the stride (see the question); the window temporal chain with live older frames; depth supervision beyond the cached teacher (radar ranges, road plane: Mirko's decisions); MobileNetV3/V4, EfficientNet and the real-time segmentation nets (closed by Mirko's narrowing to ResNet-18 / EfficientViT-B1); a single-source student; higher input resolution (448 x 800, which would need rescaled teacher targets); the long budget; TEST.
- **Cost, first try only (one encoder, one seed; estimates, none measured on this box):** stage 0 about 10 min of an idle GPU; stage A1 (frozen backbone, small set) 2 - 5 min plus setup, then stage A2 (all weights) about 1 h (cap 2 h; 16 - 17 k training key-frames, 6 - 8 epochs, between 40 and 100 samples/s through the two cache reads and the JPEG decode); stage B about 2 h (the t21 arms took 1.8 - 2.0 h for 1500 steps at 3.5 - 4.5 s per step; the step is host-bound, so one live image per sample adds little) + about 0.5 h evaluation; GPU memory 9 GB for the control chain plus the student's activations (to be measured, limit 11 GB).
