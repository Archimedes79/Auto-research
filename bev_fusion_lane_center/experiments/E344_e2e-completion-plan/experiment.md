## 4. Track B: Q-Lane, one query head on the 1 m grid
**Idea (what we talked about before: E294 "nodes from the 1 m maps", E295 "a position spread per node", E297 "soft pruning", D82 "stay differentiable"):** every cell of the 1 m grid (100 x 100 = 10 000, on the same frozen pyramid as today, so comparisons stay fair) is a query. No Hungarian matching: the queries are anchored at their cell like the 2 m nodes of B and like the end heat of E307 (CenterNet style), which keeps the training easy on 8 414 frames; free DETR-style queries (TP30, TP05, TP06 in the corpus) are kept as the later option B2 (the claim that they converge slowly on 8 414 frames is from the literature, not measured here). **Read section 1b first:** the pyramid already has a dense 1 m `centre1` head whose node F1 (0.612) was far below B's 2 m heads (0.772 / 0.776, E273); that is the reason B has 2 m cells, and Q-Lane has to say what is different.
**What a query outputs:** an existence logit; a type (normal, split, merge, entry, exit, as B has them); a sub-cell position mean and a position uncertainty (sigma along and across the line: the Laplace NLL of E318, the heat target of sigma 1.0 m of E307); a heading with a concentration; and a successor distribution over a fixed set of forward slots in the query's own frame plus a dustbin ("no successor here"), computed densely by a convolutional head. Open points that Q0 decides, because they are not details: (a) **one query per cell, one heading, one successor distribution cannot hold crossing connectors** (1.9 % of the lane cells at 1 m, 3.5 % of the connector cells hold connectors more than 45 degrees apart; section 1b); (b) **connector length against the slot radius:** 73.5 % of the GT connectors are longer than a 12 m slot radius, the median is 19 m; (c) **how a slot becomes an edge:** the softmax is normalised over positions, not nodes; B's soft walk (`sections.py:17-33`, a backward walk over the incoming-link logits of candidate pairs plus a "no predecessor" option, a dense K x K `linalg.solve` with K = 512, 2.80 ms) needs node-to-node edges, so a slot needs a nearest-node choice or a soft attach (the selection problem of E320 / E325 again), and a dense walk over 10 000 queries is impossible (a 10^4 x 10^4 solve; at 1 m density K rises to about 500 - 650 and the cost scales as K^3): **an active set (a top-K and a threshold) is unavoidable and stays a named rule step**; (d) what happens to B's assigner (`qassign.py`, 8.33 ms, the biggest B head, reads B's node tokens).
**Junction connectors: two representations, both tested by Q0, decided by its numbers:** (i) chains of ordinary nodes through the junction (every 1 - 2 m; about 300 extra nodes per junction frame at 1 m, 150 at 2 m, against 159 nodes per frame for all lanes in B; crossing connectors share cells), or (ii) the connector stays a Hermite link between two sharp ends (as B's long links and as E312 / E324 s do) and only its interior carries a large sigma (section 4b); (i) and (ii) contradict each other in the first version of this plan; B's own rule since E273 is (ii) ("connectors carry no nodes").
**Output:** a probabilistic graph: nodes with existence, type, mean, sigma, heading; edges with probabilities. The planner (and the time fusion of D90 / D91) reads probabilities and uncertainties; what is left is the consumer's thresholds T and the active set of the walk.
**One loss for everything** (the "optimises smoothness and connections and everything" part). What exists and what does not: existence: BCE with pos_weight 20 + dice (E307, `jmaps_loss`; focal lives in `qendnet.py` / `qgraph.py`); type CE; position: the Laplace NLL exists for ends only (`jends.py`, E318 running); heading cosine; successor CE with the dustbin (new); **duplicate suppression as a one-to-one loss: only E292's damping layer `qmnms.py` exists, and it is a hypothesis against two negative results (section 1b): the card of Q1c must say how a one-to-one matching differs from that damping when the GT neighbours are 1 m apart**; **smoothness (curvature of the soft path, C1 continuity of the tangent): no code exists**; connector geometry (the Chamfer loss of E317, running); the order / non-crossing penalty (E287); a keep-gate budget (`qkeep.py` is "to write" in E297; only E271's `soft_lines.sparsify` exists).

### 4b. Variant B0 (Mirko's sketch of 2026-11-14): points on the 2 m grid, information pooled by attention, then soft pruning
"First design points on a 2 m grid, let information pool, prune the irrelevant points; the points at junctions get a large position uncertainty; every point estimates position, position uncertainty, direction, type and connection after the self-attention and pooling steps; the position estimate is itself improved by the self-attention." Read as a cascade: (1) propose one point per 2 m cell with a first position, sigma, heading, type; (2) neighbourhood self-attention (a 30 m window, 15 x 15 cells, about 70 MMACs per layer; global attention over the 2 500 cells would be 0.8 GMACs per layer) plus coarse pooled tokens from the 4 / 8 m levels for the long range; (3) soft keep gate (existence x importance, the chord cost of E297); (4) every attention layer adds a zero-initialised residual to the position and to log sigma (iterative refinement, the look-forward-twice and deformable-reference-point ideas of VS19 / VS20 in the corpus); (5) the connection (successor) after the last layer. It is Q-Lane on the 2 m grid of B instead of the 1 m grid, with B's node head as the first stage, so it is the cheaper start. Measured on the VAL ground truth on 2026-11-14 (scratch `temp/_session_scratch_s292/gt_end_grid.py`, `gt_arm_reg.py`; two caveats: `gt_end_grid.py` counts the ends beyond the first per cell, so the share of ends in a shared cell is up to twice the printed 0.2 %, and `gt_arm_reg.py` reports a standard deviation along the heading, which for a pair of ends is half their gap; both measurements become part of the Q0 card, with the command written in it): (a) **the 2 m grid is not a resolution obstacle for the ends**: 0.2 % of the GT entry / exit ends share a 2 m cell with another end of the same kind (0.0 % on a 1 m grid); the nearest other end has a median distance of 4.8 m (entry) / 5.2 m (exit), 2.0 % / 1.3 % are closer than 2 m and 26 % / 17 % closer than 3.5 m (neighbouring lanes); the position has to come from the sub-cell offset, as in B (median 0.2772 / 0.2747 m on the nodes in E294 / E273, but locked to the grid, E289; E295 quotes 0.28 m as an RMS, not a median); (b) **the ends of one arm are regular, which is what a self-attention can use to improve the positions**: in the 3 818 groups of two or more ends (mean size 2.2) the spread along the heading (the stop-line alignment) has a median of 0.47 m (90th percentile 2.1 m) and the lateral gap between neighbouring ends a median of 3.43 m (10th - 90th percentile 2.81 - 4.00 m). The keypoint ends of E312 are 1.36 / 1.32 m from the GT ends on VAL (E324 l.92; E324 s own keypoints 1.41 / 1.39 m, the baseline of the prediction below; the 1.44 m quoted earlier is E308 seed 0, E310 l.54; 0.6 m on the training frames, which contain injected GT cells, E318 l.60), and the pair head's set blocks already attend over the keypoints but only feed the pair logit: the position is the soft-argmax of a 3 x 3 window of the heat and nothing else. The first test of the idea is therefore cheap and needs no new architecture: **a zero-initialised position / log-sigma residual after the set blocks of the pair head, supervised by the end loss of E317 / E318** (a card after E317 / E318 have run; prediction: the end error of E324 s own keypoints 1.41 / 1.39 m -> at most 1.1 m with a bootstrap interval; topo F1 is reported, a +0.005 would sit inside the 0.010 band and could neither pass nor fail). Q0 gets the grid size as a parameter (2 m and 1 m) so that B0 and Q-Lane are decided by the same ceiling measurement. Risks named: a large sigma is an escape hatch of the NLL (beta-NLL and the calibration ENCE are the guards, the position loss keeps a term that does not depend on sigma); long junction connectors (10 - 30 m) as chains of uncertain 2 m points cost vertices, so the connector stays a Hermite link between two sharp ends and only its interior carries the large sigma; a hard drop of tiny points for speed is a named internal selection, the delivered weights stay soft.
