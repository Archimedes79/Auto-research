**What it does**: Camera-only 4D occupancy forecasting on nuScenes (and Lyft). Occupancy of general movable objects (GMO: bicycle, bus, car, construction vehicle, motorcycle, trailer, truck, pedestrian) is built from the 3D boxes, inflated to the full box volume, with instance association and a 3D backward centripetal flow; the general static objects (GSO) and free space come from nuScenes-Occupancy (OpenOccupancy). Instances with visibility below 40 %, appearing only in the future, or leaving the range are dropped; missing intermediate instances are filled by constant velocity.

**Format and coverage** [checked]: 0.2 m voxels, [-51.2, 51.2] m in x, y, [-5, 3] m in z, 512 x 512 x 40; 700 of 850 scenes train; 23,930 train and 5,119 test sequences (length-7 windows at 2 Hz).

**Results that matter**: none for radar.

**Transferable technique**: the split itself: take the static layer from a released occupancy set, the movable layer from boxes. For us: static structure = released semantics (barrier, manmade, vegetation ...) and parked boxes (speed < 0.5 m/s), movable = box footprints we already have.

**Cost and fit**: nothing to run.

**Doubts**: a label recipe, not a method; the GSO layer inherits every limitation of nuScenes-Occupancy.
