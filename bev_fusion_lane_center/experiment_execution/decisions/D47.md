My reading, which he can correct: (1) **D26 clause (7) is withdrawn**: "a dense head first for lanes and lane centre lines, the D7 graph builder only later", and with it the design line "a dense head trained with the others, a rule graph at inference" (`bev_fusion/plan/head_design.md` section 4, D26 row; `plan.md` 8.5). The rule for lanes and lane centre-lines is now: trained end to end, no rule step on the training path, the gradient reaches every module; a head may produce dense maps, a sparse graph or both, dense maps are allowed as auxiliary targets, probes and side outputs but are neither a stage that must come first nor the delivered output. D26 clause (8) (trainable image features) stands. (2) **Regions are data, not code**: every system names its region (frame, x / y range, resolution) in its own configuration; the lane project is x right, y forward, +-50 m, the BEV system x forward 0 - 60 m, y left +-26 m; the ground truth is stored once as vectors over a window larger than both; each consumer crops and converts to its own frame; a head-to-head comparison scores both systems on the intersection of the regions (the front sector); the existing tools stay one per project (`run.py` with `AR_PROJECT`, `evalsuite` with one `Source` / `Subject` / `Task` per project), a cross-system comparison is one more `Task` with a region argument. (3) **Sparse lanes go into the shared ground-truth set as a vector layer** (pieces with the nuScenes lane / connector token, polyline, incoming / outgoing links, kind), in the ego frame at the front-camera time, over a margin window; window-dependent items (open ends, kept edges) and dense fields (distance, direction bins, split / merge heat) are derived by each consumer from the vectors with its own function and its own grid; nobody has to read the layer, and it carries no loss weight, no mask policy and no visibility rule. (4) Losses, policies (what is supervised where: D36 - D38) and code stay separate per system.
