**What it does** [checked]: LSS-style multi-camera detector (image encoder, view transformer, BEV encoder, CenterPoint-like head) whose gain over its modules comes from augmentation and Scale-NMS. Image-view augmentation (IDA): flip, scale, rotation in [-5.4, 5.4] deg, horizontal random crop. BEV-space augmentation (BDA): random flip, rotation in [-22.5, 22.5] deg and scale in [0.95, 1.05] applied to the view transformer's OUTPUT features and to the detection targets. Why it is needed: the view transformer maps pixel by pixel, so image-view augmentation does not regularise the BEV encoder or the head; BDA does, and it requires the two stages to be decoupled.

**Results that matter** [checked, nuScenes val mAP, peak / final epoch, BEVDet-Tiny]: no augmentation 23.0 / 17.4; IDA only 20.5 / 17.8; BDA only 26.2 / 23.6; IDA + BDA 31.6 / 31.2 (NDS 39.2). So IDA alone does not help and hurts the peak, BDA alone adds 6 points of final mAP, together 13.8 points over none; the gap between the peak and the final epoch (23.0 vs 17.4 without augmentation) suggests overfitting. Scale-NMS: mAP 29.5 to 31.2, pedestrians +4.8 AP, traffic cones +7.5.
Recipe [checked]: paper: AdamW, grad clip, lr 2e-4, total batch 64 on 8 RTX 3090, ResNet step schedule x0.1 at epochs 17 and 20, 20 epochs, CBGS; Swin: cyclic policy 2e-4 to 1e-3 over the first 40 % then linearly to 0. Config dev2.1 (R50): AdamW lr 2e-4, weight decay 1e-7, step [24], linear warm-up 200 iterations (ratio 0.001), clip 5, 24 epochs, 8 images per GPU, input 256 x 704 from 900 x 1600, EMA hook (init_updates 10560), with_cp=True in the backbone, fp16 off (commented).

**Transferable technique**: BDA on the BEV tensor with the GT transformed by the same matrix. For us it must be applied (a) identically to every frame of a sequence (the memory and the splat live in world / ego axes), (b) to the ego motion inputs (v, omega: a flip changes the sign of omega), (c) to the radar Doppler vectors and to the velocity targets (rotate / flip the vector, not just the position), (d) with a flip only if the map semantics allow it (driving side: a left-right flip makes right-hand traffic into left-hand traffic; lane directions flip), so flip = off or an arm, rotation <= 22.5 deg and scale 0.95-1.05 as the default arm; (e) a rotation of 22.5 deg moves the front-camera field of view out of the grid sector, so the unknown mask must follow. The regulariser acts on the trainable part only, which is our lift-reduce, radar block, fusion and trunk.

**Cost and fit**: a grid_sample / affine on the BEV tensor and the targets, negligible; the camera heads are frozen, so image-view augmentation has little to regularise except the lift-reduce.

**Doubts**: peak vs final difference shows the 20-epoch setting is overfitting-dominated for the weak baseline, the BDA gain for a model with pre-trained frozen heads and far fewer trainable parameters may differ; no seeds; the paper page lists no weight decay or EMA.
