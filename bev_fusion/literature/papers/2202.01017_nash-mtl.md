# 2202.01017 Nash-MTL: multi-task learning as a bargaining game

**What it does**: Treats the per-task gradients at the shared parameters as players in a bargaining game and picks the update direction by the Nash bargaining solution, with a convergence guarantee. The related CAGrad (2110.14048) is already catalogued.

**Results that matter**: abstract claims state of the art on several MTL benchmarks; no numbers on the page.

**Transferable technique**: if GradNorm and uncertainty weighting both fail to keep the lane head alive next to the detection head, per-task gradients at the BEV trunk can be combined. Cost is one backward per task.

**Cost and fit**: 2 to 4 backward passes through the trunk per step, acceptable only when the trunk is small.

**Doubts**: gains in later vision benchmarks are small versus tuned fixed weights (memory, not checked).
