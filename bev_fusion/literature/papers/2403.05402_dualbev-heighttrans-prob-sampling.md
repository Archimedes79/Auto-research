**What it does**: Two view transforms in one stage. HeightTrans (3D-to-2D, backward): in every BEV cell predefined 3D points over height -5..3 m (13 points: 0.5 m steps inside +-2 m, 1 m outside) are projected with p_2d = K T p_3d, the image feature is sampled bilinearly and multiplied by P_img (an instance-mask weight) and by P_proj, then summed per cell. P_proj is "a trilinear grid sampler on the depth map D, treating depth as the third dimension", i.e. the per-pixel categorical depth distribution is read at the point's projected (u, v, depth); the full per-pixel distribution is kept (checked). The 2D indices are a lookup table, run with the BEVPoolV2 CUDA operator. Prob-LSS (2D-to-3D) is the usual LSS lift times a learned BEV cell probability P_bev; the two are merged by channel attention. Whether the trilinear sample is taken at the point's own depth is not stated explicitly in the text (inferred from the notation D(u, v, d)).

**Results that matter** (nuScenes val, detection, no segmentation): P_proj alone 37.0 mAP / 48.8 NDS; without P_proj (uniform depth, P_img + P_bev only) 27.0 / 40.0; all three 38.0 / 50.4 (multi-frame setting). View-transform operation comparison on a BEVDet base (Table 4c): SCA_da (FB-BEV's operation) 29.5 / 36.6 at 12.50 ms; plain bilinear sampling (Simple-BEV type, no depth) 30.5 / 37.6 at 7.15 ms; BEV pooling (LSS) 30.7 / 38.2 at 0.32 ms; Prob-Sampling 30.6 / 39.0 at 0.32 ms. Latency on one NVIDIA 3090; whole view transform including depth net 11.7 ms vs 10.2 ms for BEVDepth. Input resolution of Table 4c not stated on the page. Test: 55.2 mAP / 63.4 NDS (V2-99).

**Transferable technique**: the formula V(cell, z_k) = c(u, v) * p(z_cam | u, v), with the depth distribution sampled by a trilinear grid sample, is exactly lift PD and is an established design, not our invention. Evidence that the depth weight matters: the row without P_proj (uniform depth) is 27.0 / 40.0 mAP / NDS against 37.0 / 48.8 for P_proj alone (Table 4b, multi-frame); in Table 4c plain bilinear sampling without depth is 30.5 / 37.6 against 30.6 / 39.0 for Prob-Sampling, so the single-frame gain of the weight over the unweighted pull is +1.4 NDS. Plain sampling at 7.15 ms is also slower than the fused operator. Fixed per-cell heights with a precomputed table (13 points) match our 8 elevations. Take the design; the ablation also gives our "null" (P without depth).

**Cost and fit**: table + one CUDA op (BEVPoolV2), 0.32 ms on a 3090 for the operation; the repo is mmdet3d / Docker-based (heavy to read). Detection only; the paper states BEV segmentation as future work. Six cameras, 128 x 128 val grid.

**Doubts**: speed is the pooling operator's, not a plain-PyTorch gather; the 0.32 ms excludes the depth head; no segmentation and no thin-structure result; depth quality limits it (authors say so); the instance-mask term P_img is an extra supervised head we would not have; Table 4c has no stated resolution.
