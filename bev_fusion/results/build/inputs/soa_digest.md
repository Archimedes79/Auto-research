## 3 Our current numbers in the same classes

Source: `runs/t16_full_s0/eval/by_n/table_n1000.txt` (baseline `t16.full_s0`) and `runs/t17_data/eval/by_n/table_n1000.txt` (`t17.data`, 700 train scenes); both: VAL, 1000 frames of 82 scenes, GT v8, dt 0, EMA checkpoint at step 6000, one seed, cold memory. Values marked [json] are not in `table_n1000.txt` but in `metrics_n1000.json` of the same folder (same run, same frames).

Our protocol, in one place: the grid is x 0-60 m forward by y +-26 m (120 x 104 cells of 0.5 m; `evaluation/bev_fusion_area.py` RES, X0, Y0). Inputs: ONE front camera (CAM_FRONT, three frames) + five radars, not six cameras. Maps are scored on `seg_fov` = the grid cells the front camera sees (about 62 % of the grid; `evaluation/bev_suite.py` REPORT_NOTE). IoU is a boundary-tolerant IoU: `iou@0` is the plain IoU, `iou@0.5` / `iou@1` accept a predicted cell within 0.5 m / 1 m of a GT cell (`evaluation/metrics.py` docstring). GT is autogt v8 from the nuScenes map expansion: `drivable_area` is the devkit `drivable_area` layer, lines (`lane_divider`, `road_divider`) are 1-cell masks (`autogt/layers.py`, `autogt/nuscenes_gt.py`). Objects: groups vehicle (car, truck, bus, trailer, construction vehicle), pedestrian, two_wheeler; AP at 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 4 m; `mAP` = mean over the four distances (`evaluation/metrics.py` line 305). `det` = front field of view up to 60 m, boxes without a lidar / radar point dropped from the GT; `detf` = the fusion area (camera x radar fan x not hidden, about 60 % of the grid), GT boxes with footprint share f >= 0.5 ("care"), detections on neutral boxes ignored (D37 / D38; `evaluation/bev_fusion_area.py`). 82 of the 150 val scenes, 428 of 700 train scenes, 6000 steps x 18 keyframes (about 108 k samples).

## 4 How to read this

Today the only fair statements are within our own protocol: (a) on identical front-camera frames and our metric, the camera path equals a SOA camera detector (DualBEV-front 0.250 vs 0.242, S4), and the dual-feature chain reached 94 % of GaussianCaR on drivable area and 50-76 % on walkway and the dividers, 26-34 % on carpark and crossing (S2, old GT, 400 frames, 3000-step chains); (b) the t16 / t17 numbers are our new baseline and may not be set beside leaderboard values: range, cameras, classes, GT and scoring differ. That experiment was done on 2026-11-02 (report section 6.3): re-score GaussianCaR's cached logits (`pitch.maps_lfl` pipeline) and the front-only DualBEV port (`bev_suite.py front`) on GT v8, the same frames and `seg_fov` / `detf`, next to `t16.full_s0`; CRN on the same 1000 frames would add the radar+camera detector.
