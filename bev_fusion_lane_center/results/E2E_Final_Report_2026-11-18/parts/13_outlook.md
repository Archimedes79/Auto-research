# 13. Open items and conclusion

**What the work shows.**
- Making the lane graph end to end can be done in small steps with small cost: from the plain lane head 0.6259 / 0.6249 to 0.6912 / 0.6948 at the end-to-end read-out, with a learned wiring head 0.6931 / 0.6964.
- On ground-truth nodes the token design with a per-source softmax learns the wiring (0.8427 / 0.8367 at 16000 steps), with the node-type head as the weak output.
- On the predicted nodes of the frozen lane head the first stage S1 misses the written level at its decode of record (0.5486 / 0.5743) but passes its quality half at a decode chosen on the TRAIN sample (0.6617 / 0.6767, +0.036 / +0.052 over the plain head): the head ranks links well, the operating point was the problem. One loss weight (the none target at 0.25) then gives the champion, 0.6698 / 0.6769 on fresh seeds with more lanes found (0.727). It costs precision (0.68), vertices (27.4 - 27.8 per 100 m) and junction F1 (0.20). A per-class decode (short links 0.3, long links 0.2) then lifts the junction F1 to 0.25 with topo F1 unchanged inside the band (E375, 0.6679 / 0.6762): the final decode of the champion, at the price of four tuned output thresholds.

**What is still hard.**
- The junction (long) links: at S1 only 51 % of the sources with a ground-truth long successor get a long link.
- Pruning and recall: the head keeps 86.8 % of the tokens assigned to a GT node but adds no recall over the lane head.
- The train-validation gap: the lane head is cleaner on TRAIN than on VAL (node recall 0.926 / 0.923 against 0.862 / 0.864).
- The token set: at most 94.3 % of the GT links have both ends covered and inside the admissible-pair gate.
- A start from the one-line rule does not help (E370, MISS): the ground-truth loss decides the operating point.
- No TEST number exists for any system here; the junction F1 of the champion (0.25 at the final decode, 0.20 before) is still below the plain head's 0.27.

**How to improve the learned wiring (proposals of the experiments chat; the loss proposal was run as E376: NO; in this order).** The diagnosis of E365: short links are wired well (86 % of the sources with a ground-truth successor get a link), junction links only to 51 %; of the missed links, half miss already the source node and 40 % every target node. A later token-level count of the experiments chat (CPU, VAL, the fresh heads of E373; a scratch measurement that is not yet on a card, so no numbers are quoted here) says that the delivered long links are a mix of true links, duplicates of a true link, wrong pairs of two real nodes and ends near no ground-truth node, and that the signals the head already has (the type factor entry x exit, the lane head's own long-link evidence) do not separate the true from the wrong ones. The reading: the long wiring lacks **information**, not a better normalisation.
- (1) **Evidence.** S3 (the road-level map: lane count and direction of the roads) was a candidate for the missing information: E374 is a TIE (the head reads the map, it gains recall of weak tokens, +0.012 / +0.017 lanes found, but not junction precision). S2 (the past) did not bring it either: E368 is a TIE. What is left on the evidence side is S4 (the loss reaching the lane head, E372).
- (2) **A loss matched to the metric.** The negative log-likelihood with a none slot trains calibrated probabilities; the metric is an F1 at a threshold. The none weight (1.0 to 0.25) and a decode chosen on the TRAIN sample lifted the head from 0.55 to 0.67. Two cheaper candidates were taken out on the card before any run (a class-specific none weight for the long class is a threshold shift; a soft expected-F term acts as a very large none weight), and the max-positive likelihood (E376) was run and is a NO for the junction F1. The placement of the none push was run as E377 and is TARGETING-ONLY: the push reaches the false junction points (kept none tokens next to real nodes) but no none weight beats the champion's balance of junction F1 against recall.
- (3) **Junction links as a soft two-sided assignment** entry <-> exit (Sinkhorn normalisation on both sides), instead of a softmax per source over about 500 targets in which "none" wins. Its rationale was duplicate fan-out of links; E376 found that few sources carry two links above 0.3 (3.8 % and 3.0 % on the two seeds), so this proposal is weaker than it was and does not repair wrong pairings; a typed prior alone would not help on these inputs. Not run.
