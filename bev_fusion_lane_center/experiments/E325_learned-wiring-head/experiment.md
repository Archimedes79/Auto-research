## 1. Card text
- **Statement (falsifiable):** a head with about 0.5 k parameters that sees the geometry of each candidate place and the pair it belongs to reproduces the nearest-piece choice of the snap at equal topology (snapped topo F1 within 0.005 of c on both seeds), so that the last selection rule of the delivered graph has a learned, differentiable replacement.
- **What G0 says and does not say:** the choice among the delivered pieces in reach has no accuracy headroom for a head that imitates the rule at the GT end (E322 section 2: o - c -0.0007 / +0.0013, a wrong choice on purpose costs 0.03, reach 4 m is the best). So this is an end-to-end card, not an accuracy card. G0 does not cover ties among co-located ends (12 - 14 % of the oracle ends), wiring both of them, or a dustbin. Note on G0: in its oracle arm the long-link cover test used the swapped piece ends, so o - c is not a pure choice effect; w keeps the keypoint pairs (the cover test sees the same pairs as in c) and is not affected.
- **Places, not pieces (ties):** several candidate piece ends can sit at the SAME place (co-located ends of a fork or a merge). The head chooses among distinct PLACES (candidates grouped by position within 1e-6 m) and keeps the rule's list order within a place, so that the labels of co-located ends, which are arbitrary list-order labels, cannot be learned or unlearned; agreement is counted per place, as `jwiring.py` does. Reported: the share of ends whose label place is not among the keypoint's candidates (it caps the attainable agreement).
- **The head `Wire` (new module `training/grid/jwire.py`):** per connector end i and candidate place c in reach (the same gate as the rule: 4 m and 45 degrees, at most 8, an index choice): 15 features: distance d, offsets along and across the end's heading, the heading difference (cos, sin), the kind of the piece (lane / link), the logarithm of its length, the existence and link probabilities B gives its end node, the keypoint's kind and probability, and the partner end (chord length, heading difference of the pair, kp probability of the partner), one end-kind flag. A 1-hidden-layer MLP (hidden 32): 15 x 32 + 32 + 32 + 1 = **545 parameters, about 0.55 kMACs per candidate**; the two-layer variant would have about 1.6 k parameters. Per frame about 27 ends x up to 8 places x 545 = at most 0.12 MMACs (VAL: 26.1 / 27.1 ends per frame), against 7.96 GMACs. The softmax over the places is the weight of each place. **Dustbin:** no learned dustbin in this card; the fixed dustbin logit -R^2 / (2 sigma^2) = -5.556 (R = 4 m, sigma = 1.2 m) is only the case 'no candidate in reach' and is excluded from the argmax (the twin ties it exactly at d = R, and an unconstrained learned MLP does not guarantee that it never wins). Without a dustbin target the weights are conditional on a correct piece existing, they are NOT edge probabilities of 'is wired'; calibration is judged on labelled ends only, against the twin's softmax.
- **Label and training population:** the label of an end is the place the rule picks at the matched GT end (G0's oracle; matching as `qendnet.match_keypoints`). Ends that match no GT end or find no piece at it (26 % + 25 %) get the rule's choice at the (jittered) keypoint as their label (so the head keeps the rule where the oracle is silent and the population is all ends with at least one candidate); the share of each kind is reported. Cross entropy over the places. The head is trained on frozen inputs; joint training with the pair head is the follow-up.
- **Data, corrected:** B's delivered pieces and E312's keypoints are needed on TRAIN frames. `jtest_eval.py` supports only the VAL and TEST splits and `jwiring.gt_ends` is VAL-only with the junction flag (paint sidecars); neither is edited. A new module (`training/grid/jwire_data.py`) runs the existing models on the TRAIN loader (8414 frames, no missing lane GT) with the loader settings of `jends_train.py`, computes the candidate features per frame immediately and stores only the compact arrays (about 8414 x 27 x 8 x 16 floats = 0.1 GB per seed), not B's full dump (146 KB per frame, 1.2 GB per seed). **Time, from the measured loader (0.12 s per frame on TRAIN):** about 17 minutes per pass at one worker and about 6 minutes with 3 workers; the CPU passes of the VAL took 841 / 608 s (about 2.2 / 1.6 h on TRAIN), so the dump runs on the GPU in the window: about 0.5 GPU h per seed for B and E312 together, the head itself minutes; **budget 1.5 GPU hours.**
- **The in-sample guard (three steps before anything else is built):** (1) a pilot on 200 TRAIN frames measures the TRAIN against the VAL keypoint error (`jdiag kperr` statistics: VAL mean 1.35 m, median 1.15 m, p90 2.57 m) and the rule's agreement with the oracle label on TRAIN; a keypoint jitter (HEAVY-TAILED, a mixture or Student-t matched to the VAL quantiles, not an isotropic Gaussian: sigma = 1 gives p90 2.15 m only) is calibrated until the rule's agreement on TRAIN plus jitter reproduces the VAL values (84.2 % over all ends, 75.0 % on real choices); B's piece ends and probabilities are also in-sample on TRAIN (E312 saw 3000 steps, about 2.9 epochs): the pilot reports how much better they are than on VAL. (2) A diagnostic scene-wise cross-validation on VAL (the head trained on the labelled ends of a part of the VAL scenes, judged on the rest) separates 'no learning' from 'shift' before the TRAIN dumps are built; its numbers are IN-SAMPLE on VAL (labelled so, never a claim) and only decide whether to build step 3. (3) The ablation 'distance-only head' (the features reduced to d and the offsets): the head must beat it by the margin below, otherwise the extra features add nothing. The head sees no VAL frame in training and no TEST frame. The jitter constants are two to three numbers derived from VAL error statistics (a summary of VAL, no VAL label enters the head).
- **Read-out, fixed:** `add_connectors(wire_choice=...)` (default off = the rule, bit for bit): `wire_choice` is a list with ONE entry per pair (`len(wire_choice) == len(pairs)`, checked) of (index of the entry piece or None, index of the exit piece or None), indexing the `ends` list of the KEPT pieces, i.e. AFTER the long-link cover drop (`qends_eval.py:69 - 76`); the features and the labels are therefore computed on the same kept list by a helper `kept_ends(...)` that `add_connectors` and `jwire` both call (the G0 helper `jwiring.piece_ends` works on the list BEFORE the drop and is not used for this); a chosen piece that the cover drop removed raises. It is NOT appended to the pair tuple (a longer tuple triggers `curve_s`).
- **Named hard steps that remain:** the argmax of the head per end, the candidate gate and its cut at 8, the p >= 0.1 threshold, the keypoint peak choice, the close-node step and the rest of B's read-out (E292), the Hermite sampling and Douglas-Peucker in the scorer.
- **Controls and confound statement:** c (rule) against w (head) on the SAME E312 dump and the same graph, so only the choice differs; t (the twin) proves the read-out path; the oracle o and the positive control `second` from E322 are the ceiling and the floor (topo F1 c, o 0.6817 / 0.6878, `second` 0.6513 / 0.6563 on E312); the head is trained with 3 head seeds per E312 seed (cheap) and the mean and range are reported next to the arm; the bootstrap of the scene resamples is the interval.
- **Prediction (numbers, written before the run; a hit is within +-0.003 for topo F1 and +-0.02 for agreements):** w - c = 0.000 topo F1 (an imitation of a label that ties the rule); place-level agreement with the oracle label on the real choices: rule 0.750 / 0.745, head **0.790 / 0.785** (+0.04: the head also sees heading, length and the partner end; the window 0.77 - 0.81 excludes the rule's 0.75, so a head that only equals the rule falsifies the prediction without being killed); the distance-only head 0.75 +- 0.01; lanes found, F1@1.0, dup_frac, junction F1 within 0.003 of c.
- **Price (to be filled by the run):** 545 parameters, at most 0.12 MMACs per frame; ms: the head is batched over the ends of a frame, the host-side candidate lists are the cost (the pair head is already host-bound at 9.7 ms for 0.02 GMACs); measured on the CPU and, in the window, on the idle GPU with `training/ab_timing.py`.
- **Not claimed:** any gain; anything about ties, a dustbin target, or k = 2 pieces per end (wiring both co-located places is a read-out variant, struck by D88; it could come back only as a differentiable term); anything on TEST (D84 covers only E312).

## 3. Risks
(1) The in-sample TRAIN inputs make the head overconfident on VAL: the pilot, the heavy-tailed jitter, the ablation and the VAL-only judgment are the guard, and the cross-validation separates shift from no learning before the kill can fire. (2) Features that leak the label (the distance to the GT end is not a feature). (3) The head learns the rule and nothing else (agreement equal to the twin's): not a kill (D87); the replacement is kept as the documented twin and the prediction of +0.04 is falsified, which is a finding. (4) The host time of the candidate lists may exceed the rule's: stated as price. (5) The place-level choice cannot express wiring both of two co-located pieces (a follow-up card).
