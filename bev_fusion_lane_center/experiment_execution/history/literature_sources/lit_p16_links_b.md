## Notes

- Only one real problem: SAMRoadPP = 2411.16733 does not carry the name "SAM-Road++" in its page title. The title is "Towards Satellite Image Road Graph Extraction: A Global-Scale Dataset and A Novel Method" (first author Pan Yin). From memory (not shown by the abstract fetch) this paper may be the one that proposes SAM-Road++; check the abstract/PDF before citing it under that name, or cite it by its real title.
- Several keys are method nicknames that do not appear in the page title (TrajTopo, ADMLP, BEVPlanner, CGNet, GeMap, BeMapNet, MapBench); the arXiv ids match the intended papers by title and topic. First authors for BeMapNet (Limeng Qiao) and VADv2 (Bo Jiang on the page) differ from what some citations list first; use the page order.
- Venues are copied from the arXiv "Comments" or "Journal reference" fields. "arXiv only" means no venue was shown on the page, not that the paper was never published.
- Dates in the table are partly in the future relative to older knowledge (Uni-PrevPredMap RA-L Oct 2026, MapTCL IROS 2026, UMPE ICRA 2026, VADv2 ICLR 2026); they are as displayed on the pages.
- No PDFs were downloaded. Six ids (HIMap, GeMap, PivotNet, BeMapNet, MapNeXt, VADv2) came from web search results and were then verified by opening the abs page. No id was invented.
