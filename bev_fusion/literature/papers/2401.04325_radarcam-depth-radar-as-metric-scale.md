**What it does**: Four stages: monocular depth prediction, global scale alignment with the sparse radar points, learned quasi-dense scale estimation (radar-to-image-patch association), and local scale refinement with a scale-map learner. Radar supplies metric scale rather than a dense depth input.

**Results that matter**: mean absolute depth error 25.6 % lower than prior radar-camera depth methods on nuScenes and 40.2 % lower on the authors' ZJU-4DRadarCam set (abstract). No numbers in metres on the page; no speed.

**Transferable technique**: the radar is used as a scale anchor for a (frozen) monocular depth prior; for a front-camera BEV system this is the alternative to a lidar-supervised depth head: a monocular depth foundation + radar range points for scale. Which radar attributes are used (RCS, Doppler) is not stated on the abstract page.

**Cost and fit**: monocular depth backbone plus a refinement net: moderate; ZJU-4DRadarCam is a 4D radar set that we do not have (ignored), nuScenes numbers are given.

**Doubts**: abstract only; no ablation of radar attributes known.
