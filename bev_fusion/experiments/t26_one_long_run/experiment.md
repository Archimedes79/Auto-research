## 1 Card text
- **Statement** (the claim under test). Trained once the way published systems are trained, the D91 network is at or above GaussianCaR on the six map rows and above CRN on front-sector objects, on identical VAL frames, GT v13 and our metric code, inside the area both systems cover.
- **Why one run and not five** (Mirko, 2026-11-14: "just one experiment to rule them all, write card"). The levers that the stored results point at are put into one training: the input of D91, an image encoder that learns the task, image augmentation, more data, and a budget of the size published systems use. The price is named under "What it does not test": the run says how far the bundle gets, not which part carried it.
- **Mechanism, with the evidence for each part.**
  - *Budget.* The base `t20.base_s0` saw every labelled training key frame about 6.6 times (6000 steps x 18 key frames per step / 16,289 samples of split `large`, `splits/README.md`); published camera + radar systems train 20 passes or more (the lead's recollection of the papers, not re-read). The validation loss still falls at the last step of every long run (t20, `runs/t20_base_s0/log.jsonl`: total 11.9 -> 8.86 -> 8.51 at steps 2000 / 4000 / 6000; the groups fall by 6 to 15 % over steps 2000 -> 6000, the total by 29 % because it carries the learned Kendall weights). 36000 steps on `xl` are 24.2 passes (36000 x 18 / 26,730); the fall-back of 24000 steps is 16.2.
  - *Encoder.* All camera features today are cached outputs of two frozen detection networks (DualBEV, BEVFusion). A maps-only run with them closed about a tenth of the map gap, and the old chain's learning curve needed 4 to 10 more doublings (t6 report, tag `archive/cards-before-delete-2026-11-04`): budget alone on frozen features is not expected to be enough. The trainable student exists and was reviewed (t22: EfficientViT-B1, 5.3 M parameters, 8.8 - 9.3 GB and 6.5 s per step with the recomputed backward; `experiments/t22_student_encoder/experiment.md`). At 1500 steps, current frame only, it stayed 0.013 `detf` mAP below its teachers (INCONCLUSIVE); whether a long task training takes it past them is the bet of this card.
  - *Prior result for "the student on every frame" (t22 stage B2, CUT).* The same distilled student on the older frame too, older frame under no_grad, fine-tuned for 1500 steps from a base trained on teacher features: mAP of the fusion area -0.0769 [-0.0972, -0.0529], lane divider IoU@1 -0.0600, drivable -0.0286; static-cell velocity EPE = control (the source-mismatch effect was real), the fine-tune recovered only 30 / 25 / 51 % (lane IoU@1 / mAP / drivable) of the stand-in's loss (`LEDGER.csv`, t22 card). **What is new here:** the base is trained from scratch with the student on every frame from the first step (no base that learned on teacher features and then meets a weaker encoder), 24 times the budget, jitter. **What is not new, and the first thing to read:** the older frames are again under no_grad (their features carry no gradient to the student, only the newest frame of each key frame does): if the older-frame student features stay weaker than the newest one's, the recursion leans on them and the maps pay again. The kept checkpoints and the step-12000 read-out are the early look.
  - *Data.* 700 instead of 428 training scenes tied with frozen features at 6000 steps (t17, archive tag); with a trainable encoder and 24 passes the larger set is the guard against overfitting, not a lever of its own.
  - *Input (D91).* One sweep at inference without training costs 0.087 `detf` mAP on the stored P4 network; zero radar costs 0.191 (`records/t25_p4_readout_2026-11-14.txt`, 246 VAL frames). The network of this card is trained with one sweep from the first step, so the 1500-step adaptation handicap of t25 does not apply.

Geometric jitter (per-sample `ida`) changes every projection-table cache key of the lift (`lift_pd.lift_key`): not in this run; its cost is measured on the CPU by the coder and written in section 2.
