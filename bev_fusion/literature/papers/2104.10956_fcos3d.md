# 2104.10956 FCOS3D: fully convolutional one-stage monocular 3D detection

**What it does**: Transforms 7-DoF boxes into per-pixel 2D-centre offset, depth, size, rotation and attribute targets; scale-assigns objects to FPN levels by 2D size, samples by projected 3D centre, and redefines centre-ness as a 2D Gaussian around the 3D centre. No 2D detector needed.

**Results that matter**: top vision-only entry of the NeurIPS 2020 nuScenes challenge (abstract); mAP / NDS not read.

**Transferable technique**: a monocular pretraining or auxiliary head on the image features (pixel-wise centre, depth) before the lift; its weights are commonly used to initialise BEV detectors (PETR, BEVFormer use FCOS3D backbones, memory).

**Cost and fit**: ResNet-101 + DCN in the standard config, too heavy; a ResNet-18/34 re-run is possible.

**Doubts**: monocular depth errors dominate; no radar.
