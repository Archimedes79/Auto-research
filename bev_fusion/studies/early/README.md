# studies/early - the studies of October 2026 that used to be named `test_*`

Each file here answers a question by training or measuring something on the synthetic world or on nuScenes mini and prints numbers; none of them is a regression check, so none is in `tests/run_gates.sh` (moved here from `tests/` on 2026-11-03, D48, with the `test_` prefix dropped; a name that would shadow a module of `training/` got a suffix). The gates with a known answer stay in `../../tests/`.

Run from the `bev_fusion` folder: `wsl ~/miniconda3/envs/gcar/bin/python studies/early/<file>.py [args]`. Their helpers are `../../tests/support/` (`synthetic_world.py`, `flow_viz.py`) and, for three of them, `tests/test_oracle_lift.py`. Output and caches go to `temp/` (scratch). `temporal.py` (from `training/`) and `radar_recon_test.py` (from `temp/radar/`, a study that three others import) came here on 2026-11-10 because only the studies use them; they are at the same level so that a study finds them first on `sys.path` (a script's own folder). The numbers in the third column are the ones recorded in the old `tests/README.md` and the plan, not re-run on 2026-11-03.

"Imported by" lists who still imports the file; a doc or card that only names it is not an import.
