**What it does** [checked abstract]: three modules: Multi-View Fusion (radar and image features fused in the camera view and in BEV), Motion Feature Estimator (pixel-wise velocity plus BEV segmentation), Motion Guided Temporal Fusion (MGTF: aligns and fuses BEV features across timestamps, recurrently). Improvement over the previous best radar-camera method on nuScenes: +1.7 % NDS and +1.4 % mAP (abstract).

**Digest from our notes** (code note + plan 7.2): R50 val 57.2 NDS against CRN 56.0; night 33.0 vs 30.4 mAP; 14.5 FPS RTX 3090; the temporal warp is a forward push (scatter to 4 neighbours, colliding sources overwrite), fixed dt 0.5 s, six history frames stacked in channels; the velocity GT recipe (box footprints, ground velocity, zero below 1 m/s). The released velocity shift line writes into a copy in PyTorch (checked by our script, plan 7.2), and the paper has no isolated ablation of the shift: the gain of a velocity warp is unproven in the literature.

**Position vs our block**: has: a supervised per-cell velocity, a velocity-guided warp, camera + radar, nuScenes numbers. Lacks: Doppler as evidence (radar only as input channels into the estimator), hypotheses / multimodality (one velocity per cell), chained ego coordinates (ego motion applied separately), mass / unknown channel, learned decay. It is the "regress a velocity and push" baseline our arm B vs A is meant to beat.

**Doubts**: velocity warp isolated gain unknown; the note on the copy-assignment bug rests on the released code as read.
