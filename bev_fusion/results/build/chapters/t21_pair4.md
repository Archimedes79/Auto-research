### What was built and trained

Version P4 of order D51: the pair with the previous frame 0.333 s back (camera stride 4) instead of 0.167 s. It was added as the fix candidate for the one cost the first two versions share: the velocity block reads the current frame and the buffered one, and was trained on a 0.333 s baseline without a time input, so with a buffered frame 0.167 s old its gain is miscalibrated and the displacement it sees is halved. Everything else is as in the chapters before (warm start from `t20.base_s0`, 1500 steps, equal-budget control, one seed). The price is stated with every number of this arm: a buffer 0.333 s old means the stream runs at 3 Hz with one buffered frame, or at 6 Hz with a ring of two raw BEVs; it is not "one frame at 6 Hz".

### What the numbers say

- **The velocity loss is gone.** Against the equal-budget control the velocity error at moving box centres is 1.091 against 1.084 m/s (+0.008, interval -0.0002 to +0.0161: not separated from the control), where the pair at 0.167 s had +0.049 and the recurrent version +0.053. The direct paired comparison of P4 against P gives -0.041 (interval -0.058 to -0.026) on the validation frames and -0.052 (interval -0.084 to -0.021) on whole warm scenes. This is consistent with the halved time baseline of the velocity block as the cause of the loss of the first two versions; it is not isolated (no window arm with the velocity block on the previous frame was run, and P4 also changes the age of the frame that the camera temporal block fuses).
- **By the point estimates the other guards hold.** mAP in the fusion area 0.4154 against 0.4117 (+0.004), lane divider IoU@1 0.5495 against 0.5516 (-0.002, interval -0.008 to +0.004); on whole warm scenes 0.5427 against 0.5496 (-0.007, interval -0.025 to +0.002: the lower end is well past the band of 0.010). Velocity on static cells is better than the control (0.217 against 0.228, interval below 0), so is the walkway (+0.006), and against P the moving-vehicle speed skill over the whole area (+0.010; in the fusion area +0.004, not separated).
- **Separated losses on other metrics** (one seed, about forty metrics per comparison: hints, not findings): the F1 of the lane-line zero crossings is lower than the control by 0.018 and than P by 0.029 (warm: 0.035 and 0.054, intervals below 0), and on whole warm scenes the road divider IoU@1 is lower by 0.015 (interval -0.032 to -0.003). The card has no guard on them and the lane IoU does not follow the zero-crossing F1, but the lane layer is the weak spot of the system, so they are named here.
- **Cost.** The batch path takes 90.0 ms at batch 1 on an idle GPU against 102.5 ms for the window control (-12.5 ms; the card allowed 92: met; the ms exclude the two frozen image encoders). The stream path runs the same operations as that of P (57.1 ms at dt 0, 77.4 ms for three time offsets, synthetic timing measured for P; only the age of the buffered frame differs), but needs the older buffer.
- **The stream and the batch computation agree** within 3.4e-3 in the semantic output over 48 frames (velocity within 3e-5; one scene, on the CPU; not bit-identical, see the chapter on P) and the stream drifts away from a cold start like the other versions (mean difference 0.20 at the first comparable step, 0.51 at 48). The memory command (400 validation frames, no intervals) shows warm against cold gains outside the camera view of +0.033 (drivable area), +0.038 (walkway) and +0.050 (lane mask), below those of the window control, which has the same world memory.
- **Verdict (by hand: D51, one seed).** By the point estimates no guard is worse than its band and the cost is lower: P4 is a tie with the three-frame window on the written guards and the version that keeps the velocity. Which of P (one frame, 6 Hz stream, velocity +0.049 m/s worse) and P4 (a 0.333 s buffer, velocity level with the window, two named losses on secondary metrics) the system takes is a decision for Mirko; the report recommends P4 if the stream may run at 3 Hz or keep a ring of two raw BEVs, P otherwise.
