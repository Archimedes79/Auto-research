## 1. Card
| | |
|---|---|
| lens | system review (ROADMAP P10, expected saving "about 30 % of search GPU hours") |
| group | A9 evaluation, data, metrics (A4 training regime) |
| sources | ROADMAP P10, E219, E240, runs/e123/*/log.txt, runs/e123_ladder/cards/e18_*.json, runs/e18/rc_eval/*.json |

- **Statement.** The VAL metrics of a 50 % checkpoint of a 4 u fine-tune (scored with the final assembly, fork pruning 0.4) already show the sign and at least half the size of the final recall effect: j1rc is above j1eq on lanes found by at least +0.02 in every pairing, so a run that is more than 0.02 below its control on lanes found at 50 % can be cut with no wrong cut in this set; Q does not separate at 50 % (inside +-0.01), so a Q-based early kill is not supported.
- **Mechanism / what the records say today.** P10 as written ("replay on loop-16 / 18 curves: fewer than 1 in 10 wrong cuts") cannot be replayed: `p16_train.py`, the trainer of every fine-tune of loops 16 - 18, writes `model.pt` once at the end, has no checkpoint, no resume and no mid-run evaluation, and its log holds only the smoothed training loss every 100 steps (CLAUDE.md asks for a checkpoint every 400 - 500 steps with resume). What can be checked now from the stored cards (10 fine-tunes of loop 18, final models): (i) the training loss is useless as a curve: Spearman with the final VAL Q is 0.35, because the loss contains the recall weight (j1eq 3.03, j1rc 3.52, j1rc6 4.07 at equal Q); (ii) the VAL250 evaluation at the end of training ranks the runs like VAL 873 on lanes found (Spearman 0.93) and F1@1 (0.92) and S (0.89), but on J it is blind (Spearman -0.07; J 0.19 on VAL250 against 0.41 on VAL 873) because the training-time assembly has no fork pruning; (iii) the schedule is OneCycle with a 10 % warm-up and cosine decay, so a 25 % checkpoint is still at 93 % and a 50 % checkpoint at 59 % of the peak learning rate: early checkpoints carry optimiser noise that the final model does not. (iii) is the reason for suspecting that early Q is noisier than the 0.008 effect of the weight.
- **Prediction.** At 50 %: lanes found j1rc - j1eq = +0.045 (0.02 - 0.065) on VAL 873 in each of the 4 cross-pairings (seed 2 / 3 of j1rc against seed 2 / 3 of j1eq); Q difference between -0.01 and +0.01 (not separated); pooled SD over checkpoints at 50 % about 1.5 times the final SD of E240. Cut test, applied to both orientations (variant below control by more than 0.02 on lanes found at 50 %): the correct cut fires for j1eq-against-j1rc in at least 3 of 4 pairings; a wrong cut (j1rc against j1eq) fires in 0 of 4. At 25 % the lanes-found difference is smaller than +0.02 in at least one pairing, so 25 % is too early.
- **Decision rule.** KEEP (adopt P10 for lanes found, with the metric named, the checkpoint at 50 % and the threshold 0.02 on VAL250 + pruning 0.4) only if the wrong-cut count is 0 of 4 and the correct-cut count at least 3 of 4; with n = 4 pairings this proves "no wrong cut in 4", not "fewer than 1 in 10" (the upper 95 % bound of 0 / 4 is 0.53), so KEEP means "worth a larger replay", not "switch the tick on": the tick may cut on the lanes-found rule only after 12 pairings (E240 plus the next seed pairs of loop 19). CUT = one wrong cut: P10 is restricted to catastrophic runs (NaN, loss rising, derailed) and the 30 % saving is withdrawn from the roadmap. TIE (Q does not separate, as predicted) is reported as a finding in its own right: early kill applies to recall-type metrics only.
- **Test design.** No new training: the four E240 fine-tunes (j1rc and j1eq, seeds 2 / 3) save `<name>_p25`, `_p50`, `_p75` with the config card (so `evaluate.py` can load them through registry entries). Controls: equal-budget pairing as in E240; null = the base j1 (cut test of "no better than j1 at 50 %" gives the false-negative rate for a run that does nothing: j1 against j1eq at 50 % should not separate); final-checkpoint cards of E240 as the reference of the order. Seeds 0 / 1 cannot be replayed. Fidelity rung: layer 3 (VAL250) for 25 / 75 %, layer 4 (VAL 873) for 50 %. Statistics: four pairings are not independent (each run enters two pairings): report them as a table, no p-value.
- **Cost.** 0.5 GPU h (3 min per VAL 873, 1 min per VAL250, 4 + 8 evaluations), 0.3 coding days for `--save_at` (writes `net.state_dict()` and the card at the given fractions; one `ar-reviewer` pass because `training/` is shared). Risk: an evaluate.py registry entry per checkpoint (16 small entries; write them from a loop in a throw-away script, not by hand). The saving if it works: lanes-found screening fine-tunes of 13 min stop after 6.5 min, about 50 % of the search GPU hours of recall-type experiments (12.9 GPU h in loop 18, of which 7 runs of E219 were recall fine-tunes).
- **Kill criterion.** See the table.
- **Inputs.** To build: `--save_at` flag (p16_train.py), registry loop. Ready: E240 command lines, `evaluate.py`, VAL250 subset. CPU-only part, done now from run records: the three observations (i) - (iii) above (script of this card: proxy comparison of runs/e123_ladder/cards against runs/e18/rc_eval; cheap to repeat).
- **Referee** (2026-10-25, ar-referee): ACCEPT as a piggyback on E240, conditional on `--save_at` being in the E240 launch (otherwise void, as the card says). Honest about its power (4 non-independent pairings, KEEP only means "worth a larger replay"), and observation (iii) about the OneCycle LR (pct_start 0.1: 59 % of peak at 50 %) checks out against `p16_train.py`. Skeptical points to carry into the summary: (1) the test is nearly built to pass, because the recall weight changes lanes found from the first steps; a pass says nothing about changes of architecture or regularisation, so state the scope "loss-weight type effects" and keep P10's 30 % saving unclaimed; (2) the saving for 13-minute fine-tunes is 6 minutes: the real use is long runs, which are not tested here; (3) the JSON lists seeds [2, 3] but the arms list [0, 1, 2, 3] and seeds 0 / 1 cannot be replayed: fix to [2, 3]; (4) decide.py judges mean and sign, not "every pairing": the pairing table is manual, say so; (5) checkpoint registry entries are written by a loop in a throw-away script (card says so). The `--save_at` flag is shared training code: default off, ar-reviewer pass, about 30 - 45 min, and it must land before E240 starts. Cost: about 0.4 GPU h of evaluation (VAL250 x 12, VAL873 x 4), partly CPU assembly.
