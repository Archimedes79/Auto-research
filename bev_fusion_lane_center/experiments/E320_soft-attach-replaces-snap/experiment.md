## 1. Design and prediction
**What changes (one factor, with its supervision):** the connector end is no longer the keypoint but an ATTACHED end. For every keypoint `Attach` (`training/grid/jends.py`) takes the nodes of B's frozen node head on the same batch (`qnodes.NodeHead`: the 2 m grid, existence, 8 type flags, long-link and section scores, position, direction, token; the head is loaded from the base run `runs/e273_p_s<S>`, exactly as the E308 option does) that lie within 4 m and 45 degrees of the keypoint and exist (probability above 0.05), the nearest 8 of them (the reach and the angle of the delivered wiring, `qends_eval.WIRE_POS / WIRE_ANG`). A small MLP scores each candidate from (distance, along / across offset and heading agreement of the node, its existence / flag / long-link / section probabilities, its token, the kind of the keypoint, the keypoint's token); a learned 'no attachment' (from the keypoint token) competes in the softmax. The end = w0 x keypoint + sum w_m x node_m, the heading the normalised weighted mean, so the curve starts / ends ON a node of B when the weight is concentrated and falls back to the keypoint when nothing is in reach. Differentiable in the scores, the keypoint position and the heading; the pair logits, the keypoints and the handle scales are untouched.
**Supervision (written before the run):** (1) the geometry loss of E317 on the FINAL curve: `--geom-w 0.5` is part of this recipe (the attached curve must be supervised as a shape; E317's own run is held by D85, the CODE is reused): the Bezier curve with the attached ends, headings and the learned handle scales against the GT connector polyline of the pair, symmetric point-to-polyline distance, smooth L1 1 m, plus the position of every keypoint (here: its attached end) against the nearest GT end within 4 m / 30 degrees; (2) a cross entropy over the candidates + the dustbin for every keypoint that belongs to a GT end: target = the candidate node nearest to that GT end within 2.5 m, else 'no attachment'. The cross entropy is part of the geometry term and shares its weight 0.5.
**Read-out:** `jends_eval.py dump` writes `att_pos`, `att_dir` (the attached end and heading of every keypoint) and `att_p` (1 - the dustbin weight) next to the keypoints; `jends_eval.py score --attach` and `qends_eval.cmd_score` take the connector from the attached ends (`pairs = (att_pos[i], att_dir[i], att_pos[k + j], att_dir[k + j]) + the learned handle scales`, the same Bezier sampling as today, `QEND.curve_s`) and do NOT snap; the pieces are wired by the default rule of `add_connectors` (every delivered piece end within 4 m / 45 degrees of the connector end, the E284 / E287 way), so a connector that is attached to a node of B touches the pieces that start / end at that node. `jmaneuver.py` has the variant `p0.1a` for the same. Everything the loss sees is soft; **the named hard steps** are the candidate set (the nearest 8 gated nodes: an index choice, the gradient flows through the values), the keypoint peak choice and the read-out threshold p >= 0.1 (as in E312), and the proximity wiring of the pieces at the read-out (`add_connectors`, as for every unsnapped read-out). That the snap is gone is true for the geometry of the connector, not for the wiring rule.
**Differences to the snap that the result must be read with:** the candidates are the NODES of B's head (grid cells, a node that the close-node step of the E275 spec merges away may still be chosen: the piece end is then at most 1.5 m away, inside the 4 m wiring reach), the snap moves the end to the delivered LINE end after the hard close step; the snap wires exactly one piece per end, the attached read-out wires every piece in reach; the training target is the node nearest to the GT end (not the delivered piece end, which does not exist inside the training loop).
Two seeds, VAL 873 frames, TEST untouched. **Prediction (a number):** topo F1 of arm a is 0.005 below E312 snapped (a TIE inside the band: +0.0516 / +0.0565 over the plain graph) and about 0.034 above the unsnapped one; attachment accuracy `att_acc` of the training log above 0.8 at the end; the attached end error `geom_end_d` below the keypoint error `att_kp_d`. If a is far below c and near u the attachment did not learn what the snap does (the candidate target or the node source is wrong: then the delivered line ends, not B's nodes, must be the candidates, which needs the hard graph inside the forward).
