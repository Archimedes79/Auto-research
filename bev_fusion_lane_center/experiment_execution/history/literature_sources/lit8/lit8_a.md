Date of scan: 2026-10-16. Method: every row was opened on its arXiv abstract page (WebFetch); "(html)" = numbers read from the arXiv/ar5iv HTML full text, "(abs)" = abstract page only, "(search)" = search-result summary only. ## Design implications for our architecture (tied to the six steps)

1. Steps 1-2: copy the SAM-Road template: dense BEV heatmaps (centerline, junction/endpoint, direction) -> vertex NMS -> edge classifier. Add TopoPoint's lesson that endpoints must be detected as first-class vertices. Its edge probability is the graph confidence.
2. Step 2 operating point: choose thresholds for a stated precision, not best F1. SAM-Road picked best-F1 on val and already shows P 90.47 vs R 67.69; RoadTracer reports recall at a fixed 5% error rate. Report "graph length covered at <=5% wrong length" for our sparse graph.
3. Abstention: no lane-graph paper reports a risk-coverage curve. Use pixel-level evidential or calibrated probabilities (ICLR 2025 BEV UQ benchmark) and propagate along edges (min or product, my choice); a path can stop at its first uncertain edge. RATopo/SeqGrowGraph suggest keeping redundancy or an explicit stop and pruning later.
4. Step 3 association: no E2E paper learns lane-to-SD-road assignment; ONR (lit7) is the only task definition. Cheapest route is to reuse the step-2 edge classifier on (lane edge, SD road) pairs.
5. Step 3 extension: SGC-Lane (endpoint clustering), SAM-Road++ (extended line) and the lane-graph prior (2307.13344) are three ways to complete short lanes; mark extended parts as "map-derived" with a lower confidence class, never as perceived.
6. Step 3 prior fusion: UMPE's recipe (log-confidence attention bias, SE(2) alignment, presence gate, source dropout 0.3) is the only one validated through a planner (VAD 0.72/0.22 -> 0.42/0.12), but it has no perturbed-prior test.
7. Step 4 navigation: three independent results say a bare command is ignored: TransFuser on NAVSIM 84.0 (original) vs 84.7 (random command); Hydra-MDP NAVI 69.6; SSR CM 49.9. Put the route on the lanes (per-lane on-route flag, lane index/count, maneuver) and supervise it (NaviHydra's BCE head on route lanes; command-diverse data; SNG adds noise to the path points).
8. Step 6 interface: make each lane a token (20 points + confidence + route attributes) and let the ego/plan query cross-attend it, as VAD, SparseDrive and GraphAD do (about 100 map queries x 20 points). GraphAD's kNN agent-to-map edges are the nearest published graph form. A lane-connectivity adjacency as attention mask is NOT done by any planner I found.
9. Differentiability: NMS/threshold are not differentiable. Keep soft confidences in the token (UMPE bias, MP3 map spread) and use hard pruning only at inference or for the output graph. VAD-style training-only constraints (direction, boundary) let graph losses reach the planner cheaply.
10. Schedule: UniAD (6+20 ep), SparseDrive (perception then joint) and MP3 (planner weights last) all train in stages; expect graph head first, then planner, with an ablation of freezing vs joint.
11. Step 5 temporal: nothing new for graphs here; keep lit7 plan. MP3/SUPER-AD show dense uncertain rasters are accumulable.
12. Evaluation: open-loop nuScenes L2/Col is weak (arXiv 2312.03031, CVPR 2024: models with ego status rely mainly on it; arXiv 2305.10430: an MLP on past trajectory and velocity alone reduces average L2 by about 20%, perception methods keep an edge in collision rate). Add a command-sensitivity test (NAVI-style) and NAVSIM/Bench2Drive-style evaluation.

## What NOT to claim (not verified)

- No paper jointly trains a perceived lane graph (vertices, connectivity edges, per-lane route attributes) with a planner; all B rows pass query features or polylines. Say "combination of known parts".
- Do not attribute UMPE's gain to lane graphs (BEV feature fusion; no perturbed-prior or planning-per-source ablation; nuScenes open-loop caveat).
- "Command not mentioned" (DiffusionDrive, DriveTransformer, LAW, GoalFlow, UMPE, Hydra-MDP) means not found in the text read; NAVSIM itself supplies a left/straight/right one-hot (NAVSIM html, 2406.15349).
- SAM-Road/Sat2Graph/RNGDet numbers are aerial imagery, not BEV; RNGDet++ and Sat2Graph values are as tabulated by SAM-Road. TopoRoad was not opened. No accuracy numbers on the SAM-Road++ page.
- Venues not shown on the fetched page and not asserted: UniAD, VADv2, SparseDrive, Hydra-MDP, TransFuser, GoalFlow, GenAD, HDMapNet. SGC-Lane has no arXiv entry found. MapUQ (ICML 2026 poster, no arXiv found) was not used.
- Not read: ECE tables of the BEV UQ paper; NTT (2406.08349), NavigScene (2507.05227, text-based navigation), GenAD interface, LMDrive details beyond text tokens and a two-stage training; none gives usable lane-graph evidence.
- No paper found with per-lane on-route flag, lane index or lane count predicted from BEV (only classic ego-lane index work with a known lane count, search snippets only). No conformal or abstention study for lane graphs found.
- Internal inconsistencies seen: GraphAD Table 7 vs Table 1 numbers; MP3 text ("diverges a third of the times") vs table (14.63% off-route); L2 header "mm" in SparseDrive/GraphAD tables; NaviHydra ignore rates for SSR/TransFuser (37.2/32.5%) are the tool's inference (100 - NAVI), only Hydra-MDP 30.4% is stated.
