- **Statement** (falsifiable, one sentence): The uniform weight average ("soup") of fine-tunes of the same start `e123_D7_r3_j1` with the same recipe (j1rc: recall weight 3, 4 u) and different seeds, with the stem BatchNorm statistics re-estimated on TRAIN frames and evaluated with the `fast_recall` registry settings (192 / 96, tau 0.7, fork pruning 0.4), raises lanes found and R@1 far over its parent seeds by at least +0.010 each on VAL 873 (two disjoint two-seed soups, each against the better of its two parents), with Q loss <= 0.005, topo F1 loss <= 0.005 and per-piece DP vertices <= 14.3 per 100 m, at unchanged network cost (same architecture, same ms per frame).
- **Mechanism** (why it should work): the far verdict of the j1rc fine-tune is dominated by seed variance, not shared bias: in step 0, 26.0 % of the far (>= 40 m) ground-truth points are hit by one to three of the four seeds (near range 9.4 %), the union of the four seeds reaches 0.802 against a mean single-seed recall of 0.681 (approximate metric of step 0, not the evaluate.py number); the seeds moved 15.3 - 15.5 % of the weight norm away from the shared start and are 16.2 - 16.5 % apart (cosine of the displacements about 0.43), a third of the seed-to-seed distance in the first seed-MLP layer (192 x 2014) and about half in the stem BN statistics. Averaging fine-tunes of one start keeps the shared displacement (the recall-weight signal) and averages out the seed-specific part; the soup paper shows that fine-tunes of one pre-trained model lie in one basin and their uniform average beats the members at zero inference cost, and the project's own E73 found the same on the trust head. A weight average is the only ensemble the tree builder admits without a graph-fusion rule (each seed grows a different tree, so the outputs cannot be averaged node by node; E264 tests the output-side ensemble). Why the far range: the far nodes are the ones whose validity sits near the threshold, so a lower variance of the score moves more true far nodes over tau than near ones. What it cannot do: lanes that no seed finds (19.8 % of the far points) stay missed; the recall family (rc1) cannot be souped usefully, rc1 and rc1s1 are 0.59 % apart (step 0), so this card upgrades the fast chain (j1rc, no time recursion, 21.1 ms in E253) towards the recall chain (rc1 with recursion, about 27 ms) at about 6 ms less.
- **Prediction** (VAL 873, `evaluate.py`, registry settings of `fast_recall`): four-seed soup (arm a4) against the mean of its four members (Q 0.7391, lanes found 0.7388, R@1 far 0.7337, from `runs/eval/e24_j1rc_s{0..3}/val.json`): lanes found +0.012 (to about 0.751), R@1 far +0.015 (to about 0.749), Q +0.003, topo F1 +0.002, false_len_frac within +-0.003, node-id vertices per 100 m within +-0.3; two-seed soups (a01, a23) about two thirds of that (lanes found +0.008, R@1 far +0.010). Class: "tie or small keep"; a loss barrier (soup worse than both parents) is the main failure mode.
- **Decision rule** (band 0.010; seed SD lanes found 0.004, R@1 far 0.003, Q 0.0025 from E240; the soups are deterministic given their members, so the twin is the second disjoint pair): KEEP if both pairs gain (a01 vs the better of s0 / s1, a23 vs the better of s2 / s3, "better" by lanes found) and the mean gain over the two pairs is >= +0.010 in lanes found or in R@1 far, with Q loss <= 0.005 against the parents' mean, topo F1 loss <= 0.005, false_len_frac <= +0.003, per-piece DP vertices (E257 basis) <= 14.3; then register a4 as `fast_recall_soup` if it holds the same clauses against its four members' mean (the deliverable of tonight). TIE (|mean gain| < 0.010): weight averaging does not buy coverage; keep the single seed (simpler provenance, same cost). CUT if a guard fails. INCONCLUSIVE (one pair gains >= 0.010, the other loses): a third pair cannot be formed from four seeds; report and keep the single seed. decide.py: variant cards a01, a23; control cards the better parent of each pair (pairs by identity, `deterministic: false` because the members are trained seeds).
- **Test design** (arms; controls; budget; rung): arms, all from existing checkpoints (no training): a01 = soup(s0, s1), a23 = soup(s2, s3), a4 = soup(s0 .. s3), each with the stem BN statistics re-estimated (forward-only, BN layers in train mode with cumulative averaging, 2,000 TRAIN frames, no gradient, other layers in eval); a4n = soup(s0 .. s3) with the BN buffers simply averaged (does the re-estimation matter?). Controls: matched reference = the four member cards on the same 873 frames (`e24_j1rc_s0` .. `s3`, layer 1: re-score them first and reproduce their stored Q and lanes found to 4 decimals); procedure null c0r = member s0 with the same BN re-estimation and no averaging (isolates the effect of the re-estimation from the effect of the averaging); equal budget: every arm and control uses zero training steps and the same frames; seed twin: the two disjoint pairs; reference ceiling: the output ensemble of E264 on the same seeds. Paired scene bootstrap through `evaluate.py <soup> --compare <parent>`. Rung: VAL 873 (layer 3 - 4); no TEST.
- **Cost** and **kill criterion**: GPU about 35 min in total (BN re-estimation 5 x 3 min, VAL inference 5 x 4 min for the new raw caches; more on a loaded machine), CPU minutes, coding 1 h (one throw-away script temp/loop22/e263_soup.py (not written: E263 deferred): average state dicts, re-estimate BN, write `runs/e123/<name>/model.pt` and a card json with `init` = list of members; five registry entries `e26_j1rc_soup01` / `soup23` / `soup4` / `soup4n` / `e26_j1rc_s0_bn`); ms per frame unchanged by construction (identical network). Kill: a01 below the worse parent in lanes found or Q more than 0.020 below the parents' mean (a loss barrier: the seeds are not linearly connected; then no a4); c0r moves s0 by more than 0.005 in Q or lanes found (the re-estimation is a confound: then judge the averaged-buffer soups only, a4n and two-seed versions with averaged buffers).
- **Inputs** (ready / to build): ready: checkpoints `runs/e123/e18_D7_j1rc`, `e18_D7_j1rcs1`, `e24_D7_j1rcs2`, `e24_D7_j1rcs3` (same architecture, 121 tensors) with cards in `runs/e123_ladder/cards/`; VAL cards of the members; the TRAIN loader `p16_data.loader("train", with_prev=False)`; `evaluate.py` (a new registry entry computes the raw cache once on the GPU). To build: the soup script above (spec test: soup of a member with itself and no re-estimation reproduces the member's raw outputs bit for bit on 20 VAL frames). Training path untouched; the soup is weight arithmetic after training, no rule step is added to the delivered graph (the existing assembly rules stay as they are).
- **Referee (2026-11-01, ar-referee)**: REVISE (not tonight; not G2-ready). The mechanism contradicts its own step 0: a weight average approximates an averaged score, i.e. a soft majority of the seeds, and step 0 gives far majority (>= 3 of 4) recall 0.653, below the mean single seed 0.681; the union (0.802) is E264's mechanism, not this card's. E73's soup was a trust classifier, not a builder with hard top-K0 seed selection. The two-seed prediction (+0.008 lanes found) lies below the card's own KEEP bar (+0.010): the expected verdict is TIE. The OR clause (lanes found OR R@1 far) lets either metric carry a KEEP: name one main metric. There is no dup_frac guard. Even at its prediction the deliverable (lanes found about 0.751) stays below `recall` (0.767), so it is no prototype candidate for D28, and its 35 GPU-min compete with E262 / E266 in a 2-h window. Fix: restate the mechanism against the majority number; a cheap kill first (a01 with averaged BN buffers on val250, kill if below the worse parent); one main metric; dup_frac a - c <= +0.010; schedule after tonight. Cache note: every soup and the c0r null need their own run directory, because `evaluate.builder_raw` keys the raw cache by run / M / K0 only.
