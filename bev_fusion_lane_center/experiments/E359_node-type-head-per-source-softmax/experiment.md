**The one question.** E357 (T0: the ground-truth nodes of the 2.5 m lattice as tokens, ONE pair head deciding every candidate edge independently with a balanced binary loss) FAILED by its written rule on both seeds (topo F1 0.4255 / 0.3928 at p >= 0.5; best rule 0.7011). Its diagnosis (stored dumps, in-sample on VAL, next to the verdict): (a) the lane links are ranked right (the highest-p successor is a GT link for 99.6 % of the sources; with a one-successor decode the short-link F1 is 0.992) and only over-produced by the independent threshold; (b) the head does not know WHICH nodes carry a junction link (it gave long links from 27 911 nodes, 4.3 per source, for 6 256 true sources) and even with the source known its top-1 junction link is right for only 68.5 %. E359 asks whether two changes that the design always contained and that E357 left out fix this: (1) a **node-type head** (an auxiliary output of the same token set: does this node start a junction link, does it end one, does it start a lane link) and (2) a **per-source softmax** (each source node distributes its probability over its candidate successors; the type probability is the budget that the "none" option takes). All soft and differentiable (D55 / D88); the one hard step added is the decode at the end (an argmax per source, named). **What is new against E273 v6** (E355 section 2: a single row softmax with a "none" in B's link head, reverted: junction F1 0.122, topo F1 0.467; not verified by me, cited from E355): oracle nodes instead of B's learned ones, a type head as the source gate, short and long candidates in separate groups, a multi-positive likelihood (forks), and the destination type in the ranking. **What is NOT isolated:** the card changes the loss (the balanced pair BCE and the dual softmax of E357 are gone), the decode and adds the type head at once; the attribution below rests on two numbers (type head, oracle-source top-1), not on an ablation.

**What stays exactly as in E357** (so that the two cards are comparable): the nodes (GT node table of B's code on the 2.5 m lattice, positions and headings), the features (B's frozen pyramid `runs/e273_p_s<S>`, sampled at the node positions), the token encoder and the two set blocks, the pair head's MLP and the scale head, no node flags or piece ids as input, the allowed gate (both headings forward along the chord, turn at most 130 degrees, chord 0.3 - 100 m), the training data and schedule (TRAIN only, 3000 steps, batch 8, lr 1e-3, `--workers 4`, step 3000 scored, nothing chosen on VAL), the section-free read-out of record, the scorer, the three rules and the GT links (E357's stored cards `runs/eval/e357_{lo,lo_near,lo_all,hi}_sec0/val.json`), seeds 0 and 1, VAL 873 frames, TEST untouched.

**The model and the exact likelihood (written before the code).** One `Linear(d, 3)` on the token after the set blocks gives three logits per node: long_out `a_i`, long_in `b_i`, short_out `c_i`. The pair logit `z_ij` is E357's. Candidates of a source i are the allowed pairs (gate) split by chord: short (chord <= 8 m = `jtok.SHORT`) and long (chord > 8 m). For the LONG class the destination type enters the ranking: `z'_ij = z_ij + logsigmoid(b_j)`; for the short class `z'_ij = z_ij`. Per class, `s_ij = softmax_j(z'_ij)` over the candidates of i (masked with the -1e4 fill and the `rows` mask convention of `qendnet`, float32, log space) and `p(i -> j) = sigmoid(type_i) * s_ij` with type_i = a_i (long) or c_i (short); `p(none) = 1 - sigmoid(type_i)`. It is a proper distribution over {none} + candidates. Loss per source i that has at least one candidate of the class (nodes without any are masked), with G = its in-gate GT successors of the class: **G non-empty: -[ logsigmoid(type_i) + logsumexp_{j in G} z'_ij - logsumexp_{j in candidates} z'_ij ]** (the marginal likelihood that the drawn successor is one of the GT successors; at a fork the softmax mass splits between the successors, so the individual edge probabilities stay below 0.5 there); **G empty: -logsigmoid(-type_i)**. The type BCE therefore IS the none part: no separate BCE on the source-side logits and no "none target" in the softmax (no double counting); the pair logit gets gradient only from the sources that have a GT link. Plus a plain BCE on `b_j` over the nodes that are a candidate destination of at least one long candidate (target: destination of an in-gate GT long link), plus 0.5 x L1 on the two handle scales of the long GT links. GT links outside the gate are masked from every term and counted. Normalisation: the mean over the valid sources of each class per frame, then over the frames of the batch (short and long weight 1 each); BCE(b) and the scale term likewise. Positives are rare for the source flags (about 5 % of the nodes), so the plain BCE learns the prior first and 0.5 is a calibrated cut; no class weight (E357's balanced weights are what made its 0.5 meaningless).
