## 1. Design and prediction
Code (committed with this card): `training/grid/jmaps.py` `distill_loss`, `training/train/jmaps_train.py --teacher / --distill-w` (default off: loss, parameters and RNG order of the E312 / E333 command lines are unchanged; the saved `args`, `config.json` and `status.json` gain the keys `teacher: null` and `distill_w: 1.0`, which every loader ignores through `.get`). The teacher is the junction head of the named run on the SAME frozen pyramid (an assert checks `joint` and `base` of both runs), built from its `args['d']` / `args['ctx']`, strict `jm.` load, never saved, never trained, forward under `no_grad` on the pyramid features of the student's own forward.
**The loss (deviation from the scan, with the reason):** the entry and exit heat logits of the student against the teacher's heat PROBABILITIES as soft targets, plain BCE (its minimum is the teacher's own logit; with the ground-truth loss's positive weight 20 the minimum would move from 0.01 to 0.17), summed over the cells and divided by the number of peak cells of the teacher (heat probability above 0.3, the same mask as the heading term: the number of positives, as CenterNet / FCOS normalise; a mean over the 10 000 empty cells would dilute the gradient of the ends to nothing, and the probability mass, the first draft, makes the scale depend on the teacher's background calibration: about 146 at a background logit of -7, about 1000 at -5, against 300 - 480 of peak mass per batch: critic), + the heading cosine to the teacher's heading at the cells where the teacher's heat probability is above 0.3 (the headings are trained at the ground-truth ends only, so elsewhere the teacher's heading is noise: critic C2), weight 0.5 as in the ground-truth loss. Channel-wise distillation (a spatial softmax per channel) was built first and dropped on the critic's toy case: at temperature 1 a weak end (logit -0.5) next to a strong one (+6) gets 0.15 % of the softmax mass, at temperature 4 the empty cells get 99.7 %; on a sparse end map it transfers only the strongest peak. What is transferred here is the teacher's probability at every cell, including the uncertain ends; this is not a claim that the soft targets help (that is what the arm measures). Weight 1.0, ONE value, no sweep (a second value would be a tuning on VAL); both terms are in the log every step (`k_bce`, `j_heat`). **The weight 1.0 is not an equal pull:** per peak cell the distillation gradient is about (student - teacher) / (number of peak cells), the ground-truth heat BCE is a mean over about 160 000 cells with the positive weight 20, so the soft term probably dominates the heat gradient (estimate, not measured: the logged values have different normalisations and do not show the gradient ratio); the arm is then mostly imitation of the teacher plus the ground-truth dice, headings and zone / core / paint terms. One value, written before any number.
**Where the teacher acts, and what that costs (critic point 3):** in stage 1 only. The joint pair stage is E312's recipe unchanged (`--e2e --maps-w 1.0`: the maps head trains on the ground-truth maps loss and the pair loss, no teacher term), the same as in E333's plain arm; 3000 steps there may wash out part of what stage 1 taught. This is the likely reason why the expected effect is small, and a distillation term in stage 2 is a variant that is not in this card.
**Tests:** `python jmaps.py` (BCE minimal at the teacher's own logits; the teacher gets no gradient although its forward kept the graph, so the detach is tested, not the requires_grad flag; the heading term has zero gradient where the teacher sees no end and the cosine values 0 / 2 / 1 for the same / opposite / orthogonal heading; a width-32 student follows a width-64 teacher, loss down by more than 20 % in 40 steps); eleven mutants (no detach, soft heading weight, pos-weighted BCE, reversed heading sign, exit heat dropped, exit heading read from the entry channels, mask threshold 0.05 and 0.9, mean instead of sum / peaks, mass instead of peaks, peaks of the entry channel only) are all killed by a fixed one-frame pair with both kinds and a hand-computed loss value (scratch run, `temp/_session_scratch_s292/mutants_distill.py`). CPU smoke of the real trainer (20 steps, d = 32, teacher E307 seed 0): finite, k_bce 5.9 against j_heat 1.2 at step 20 (the student starts far from the teacher). A student checkpoint (extra args) loads strictly in `JEndModel` and in `jmaps_eval.py`, `jmodel_cost.py`, `kill_check.py` (read as json).
**Prediction (a number):** topo F1 -0.006 against E312 on both read-outs, given that this arm only runs when the plain width 40 lost more than 0.01 (E333's own prediction was -0.004: the distilled head is expected to win back about half of a loss of that size); maps keypoint F1 -0.02 against E307 (0.7233 / 0.7051). Honest risk: the teacher saw the same TRAIN frames, so on them it is near the ground truth and adds little to it (no VAL information is involved); the stage-2 washout above; a student cannot copy what its layers cannot hold.
Cost: maps stage (the teacher forward adds 3.256 against 3 x 1.967 GMACs of the student's forward and backward, +55 %; training is host-bound, compare s / step with E333's log) + pair stage 1.15 - 1.6 h + post 0.25 h: about 2.4 h wall, 4.8 slot hours. Start (not started): `temp/e339/chain.sh`, called by `temp/day17/day17.sh` (run-once guard, window rule of `chain_lib.sh`); single stages `temp/e339/start_maps.sh <seed>`, `start_pairs.sh <seed>`; `temp/e339/READY` carries the chain. The width is the constant 40 in `start_maps.sh` (no file).
